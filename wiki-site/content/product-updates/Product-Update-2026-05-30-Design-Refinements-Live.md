# Product Update: Design Refinements Live

**Date:** May 30, 2026

## What Shipped

We've completed design refinement passes across three critical dashboards:

- **Skills Taxonomy Browser**: Rebuilt the 3-column skills interface to match our final design mockup, improving readability and navigation flow.
- **Weekly Performance Dashboard**: Refined the full dashboard layout to align with design specifications, making progress tracking clearer and more intuitive.
- **Unlock Plugin**: Redesigned the unlock interface to our current design system, streamlining the experience of discovering and activating new capabilities.

All three interfaces now reflect consistent spacing, typography, and visual hierarchy across the TI Skills Economy platform.

## Why It Matters

As a survivor building clarity and critical thinking, you deserve tools that work as predictably as you do. When dashboards look polished and feel intentional, you can focus on what matters—your growth, your choices, and your progress—instead of navigating confusing interfaces.

These design refinements reduce cognitive load and build confidence. You're no longer decoding unclear layouts; you're engaging with a platform that respects your time and attention.
