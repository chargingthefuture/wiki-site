# Product Update: Workforce Tracker, Mobile Chat, and Blocking Tools

**Date:** June 26, 2026

## What Shipped

**Workforce is now a read-only live tracker.** It shows population-scale demand and gaps across the survivor economy in real time. You can see where help is needed without admin edit access.

**Mobile Foundation Direct Line.** Android now has the same one-to-one chat paired with a Foundation quote request that iOS had. You can chat directly with a member handling your quote.

**Mobile member blocking.** Android members can now block other members, bringing Android to parity with iOS.

**ServiceCredits wallet history on mobile.** Check your wallet history and see how your ServiceCredits moved over time.

Behind the scenes: fixed profile fetches and audit trails, cleaned up stray merge markers, removed dead admin sync buttons, and tightened API security on workforce report routes so data stays private.

## Why It Matters

You get a clearer picture of where the survivor economy needs hands. Direct Line on mobile means you're not stuck waiting to chat about a quote. Member blocking gives you control over who can contact you. Wallet history lets you track your credits without guessing.

These are small, concrete tools. They do one thing each and do it cleanly.
