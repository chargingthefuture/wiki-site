# Product Update: July 16, 2026

## What Shipped

**Commons composer now accepts line breaks.** Write in the Commons the way you'd write anywhere else—hit Enter to start a new paragraph. Posts preserve those breaks when they display, so your formatting stays intact.

**Delete your own Commons posts.** Posted something you want to take down? You can now delete your own message. No edit function yet, just removal.

**Directory takedown requests.** If your name appears in the Directory, you can now request that your listing be removed. Quora URLs tied to your name are suppressed from the Directory by default.

**Directory rail shows honest copy.** The sidebar that points to the Directory no longer makes claims that aren't supported. Privacy language is accurate.

**Verified Community label removed.** The Commons no longer marks any post or user as "verified." Desktop mentions chip moved inline so it doesn't clutter the interface.

**Commons posts from peers stay plain.** When someone else posts in the Commons, inferred action buttons no longer attach themselves to their message. Posts are just posts.

**Admins can post longer.** Post length limits for admins in the Commons are now raised.

**Signed-out Commons uses subtle row highlights.** When you view the Commons without logging in, posts show faint per-author row highlights instead of colored bubbles. Cleaner, less distracting.

**Community Planning rosters added to Workforce.** If you're coordinating a Workforce Community Planning team, you can now see and manage your rosters on web and Android.

**Public endpoints have rate limits.** Traffic from any single IP to public endpoints is now rate-limited. Bots and scrapers get robots.txt rules to follow.

## Why It Matters

Writing in the Commons should feel natural. Paragraphs matter. Deleting your own post gives you control over what you've shared. Takedown requests in the Directory put you in charge of your own listing. These changes are small but they add up to a platform that respects your choices and doesn't make false claims about what it is.
