# Product Update: Feed Retirement & Mood Refresh

**Date:** June 9, 2026

## What Shipped

Two updates landed on main this week:

1. **Feed app retired** – We've hidden the retired Feed app from the navigation to reduce clutter and focus your attention on active tools.
2. **Mood web shell redesign** – The Mood tool now features a comic-inspired theme that makes mood tracking more engaging and visually intuitive. Mobile layout on Chyme has been fixed to display correctly on smaller screens.

## Why It Matters

Simplicity builds trust. By removing tools that no longer serve you, we reduce cognitive load—something critical when you're working to maintain clarity. The Mood refresh makes daily check-ins feel less clinical and more human. A tool that feels good to use is a tool you'll actually use.

These changes reflect our commitment to keeping the platform lean, functional, and respectful of your time.
