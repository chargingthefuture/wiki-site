# Product Update: LightHouse Currency Display & Mobile Polish

**Date:** July 6, 2026

## What Shipped

LightHouse listings now show prices and accepted currencies based on your location. When you browse a listing, the price displays in your local currency, and you can see which payment methods the host accepts.

The mobile loading screen now centers properly and fills the entire viewport on all devices.

Country and state dropdown menus on the LightHouse web host form now work consistently across the platform.

## Why It Matters

You can compare pricing and payment options at a glance without doing mental math or guessing what currencies a host accepts. The mobile experience is cleaner and easier to read. Setting up a LightHouse listing is now smoother, whether you're on desktop or mobile.
