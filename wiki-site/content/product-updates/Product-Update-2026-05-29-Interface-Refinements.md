# Product Update: Interface Refinements

**Date:** May 29, 2026

## What Shipped

We completed web pixel pass refinements across three core interfaces:

- **Skills Taxonomy Browser**: Rebuilt the 3-column layout to match design mockups, improving visual hierarchy and scanning patterns.
- **Weekly Performance Dashboard**: Refreshed the full dashboard interface for consistency and clarity.
- **Unlock Plugin**: Redesigned the `/plugin/unlock` interface to align with our design system.

We also fixed a timestamp issue that was recording product update dates in UTC instead of US Eastern time.

## Why It Matters

Consistent, predictable interfaces reduce cognitive load when you're navigating the platform. Clear visual design reinforces our commitment to calm, respectful user experience—especially important when you're rebuilding clarity and agency after harassment.

These refinements are part of our ongoing effort to make every interaction on Charging the Future feel trustworthy and intentional.
