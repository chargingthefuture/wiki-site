# Product Update: June 11, 2026

## What Shipped

This week's updates focus on clarity during your commute and platform reliability:

- **Enhanced weather readouts**: City and state are now announced in point weather reports, so you get location-specific context without fumbling with your phone.
- **Clearer report headers**: Road and route weather reports now use plain, consistent language for easier scanning.
- **Smarter alert routing**: Only weather alerts relevant to driving conditions now influence your route verdict, reducing noise and decision fatigue.
- **App update stability**: Fixed CI/CD workflows and startup processes so Charging the Future updates deploy smoothly without interruption.
- **Improved accessibility**: Removed redundant per-line announcements so screen readers deliver information clearly and concisely.

## Why It Matters

Survivors navigating daily life deserve tools that respect their attention and cognitive load. When you're managing safety, work, and clarity simultaneously, every unnecessary detail or system glitch adds friction. These changes prioritize signal over noise—weather information that's actually useful, app stability you can rely on, and accessibility that works the way you need it to. You stay focused on what matters: moving forward with confidence.
