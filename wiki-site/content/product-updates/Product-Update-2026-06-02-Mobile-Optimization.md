# Product Update: Mobile Optimization and Schema Stability

**Date:** June 2, 2026

## What Shipped

We've expanded mobile support across five key plugins:

- **ServiceCredits and Peer Programming** now have responsive phone layouts that adapt to smaller screens without sacrificing readability or functionality.
- **TrustTransport, Foundation, and ClickLog** now display optimized interfaces on mobile devices.
- **AI Consent Modal** button layout now stacks vertically on phones, making actions clear and accessible without cramped spacing.
- **Schema stability improvements** to prevent data drift in production environments, ensuring reliable database operations behind the scenes.

## Why It Matters

Survivors access Charging the Future across different devices—sometimes on the go, sometimes from home. Mobile-first design means you have the same clarity and control whether you're on a phone or desktop. The schema improvements ensure your data stays consistent and safe, reducing the technical friction that can interrupt your workflow.

These changes reinforce our commitment to **Live, Work, and Prevail** by making the platform work for you, wherever you are.
