# Product Update: Navigation and Contributions

**Date:** July 1, 2026

## What Shipped

Fixed the contribute action on the Contributions page so it's visible and easy to find. Added the uniform left rail to the account area, so navigation looks and works the same on mobile and desktop.

Also aligned the mobile app with Expo SDK 55 to keep everything running smoothly.

## Why It Matters

When the contribute button was hard to spot, you had to hunt for how to add something new. Now it's right there where it should be. The consistent left rail means you don't have to relearn navigation when you switch devices—the account area works the same way everywhere you access it.

Small fixes like these reduce friction and let you move through the platform with confidence.

## Technical Notes

- Merged fixes to contributions visibility and account area rail alignment
- Updated test documentation for trust manual testing
- Aligned mobile React Native and React with Expo SDK 55
