# Product Update: Skills Hunt Polish & Admin AI Status

**Date:** June 21, 2026

## What Shipped

Three fixes landed on main:

- **Skills Hunt skill picker no longer jumps the page.** When you select a skill to nominate, the page stays put instead of scrolling unexpectedly.
- **Long skill names display fully.** Skills with longer taxonomy labels now show in their entirety without being cut off in the Skills Hunt nomination flow.
- **Admin landing shows AI engine status.** Admins can now see at a glance whether the chat AI is running on RunPod or Ollama.

## Why It Matters

Small friction points add up. When a tool jumps around or hides information, it breaks focus and trust. These changes keep the Skills Hunt nomination smooth and predictable. For admins, knowing which AI engine is active removes guesswork and speeds up troubleshooting.
