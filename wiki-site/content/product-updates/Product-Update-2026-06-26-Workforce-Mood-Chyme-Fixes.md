# Product Update: Workforce, Mood, and Chyme Fixes

**Date:** June 26, 2026

## What Shipped

Three plugins received stability and accuracy updates:

**Workforce Dashboard** — Fixed a crash that happened when the skill-match query ran. The dashboard now loads without errors.

**Mood Plugin** — Corrected the privacy notice to accurately describe the account-keyed cooldown. What you see now matches how the feature actually works.

**Chyme** — Mobile users can now raise their hand in room chat and have it stay raised. Added a presence heartbeat so the app keeps your connection alive while you're actively chatting. Also fixed a limit check on message count so Chyme stays within its contract bounds.

**Plugin Registry** — Removed duplicate rows left over from plugin renames, so renamed plugins (like level-up and socket-relay) no longer show twice in the directory.

**LightHouse Routes** — Hardened route definitions to close code-review findings.

## Why It Matters

When tools crash or show incorrect information, they break your trust. These fixes remove friction from the work-matching, mood-tracking, and chat surfaces so you can focus on what you're doing, not on fighting the platform.
