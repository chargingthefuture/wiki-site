# Product Update: Route Safety, Bug Reports, and Community Contributions

**Published:** June 10, 2026

## What Shipped

We've released several features focused on your safety and your ability to shape the platform:

- **Route Weather with Hazard Alerts**: Real-time weather and driving conditions integrated directly into route planning. The system now provides live-location look-ahead forecasts, driving verdicts, and hazard alerts to help you make informed travel decisions.
- **Expanded Road Coverage**: Added opt-in feeds from 511.org (SF Bay Area) and new coverage across Canada and Mexico for road event data.
- **In-App Bug Reporting**: A private triage pipeline lets you report issues directly from the app. Reports are securely collected and reviewed by the team without public exposure.
- **Contributions Plugin**: A new way to support Charging the Future. Members can see fundraiser campaigns, track contributions, and access admin tools if they choose to help lead community efforts.
- **Voice-Friendly Output**: Route weather information is now available as plain-text, voice-friendly output and includes truck-optimized ETA defaults.

## Why It Matters

Survivors deserve tools that prioritize safety and control. Route hazard alerts reduce travel uncertainty. The private bug-reporting system ensures your feedback reaches us securely, without fear of public judgment or exposure. And the Contributions plugin lets you decide how—or whether—to invest in the platform's future. Every feature respects your agency and privacy.

Web and Android now have feature parity across all new capabilities, so your experience is consistent no matter which platform you use.
