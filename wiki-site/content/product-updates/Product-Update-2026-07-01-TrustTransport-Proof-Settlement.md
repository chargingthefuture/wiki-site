# Product Update: TrustTransport Proof Capture & Settlement

**Date:** July 1, 2026

## What Shipped

The platform released three TrustTransport updates:

1. **Proof capture**: Providers can now log a short reference for a trip—a photo reference, a confirmation code, or a note—and the app timestamps it automatically. No images or location data are captured; it's a redacted text reference kept for dispute evidence.

2. **Automatic settlement**: Service credits transfer automatically from requesters to providers upon trip completion, eliminating manual steps.

3. **Earnings and payouts**: A new dashboard displays TrustTransport earnings over time, allowing users to request payouts whenever desired.

Additional fixes included consistent admin back buttons across devices, navigable contributions pages, improved author color indicators in community posts, and clearer comic engine status badges.

## Why It Matters

Proof capture gives users control over documentation of events. Automatic settlement removes friction from credit transfers, and the earnings surface provides transparency about compensation and payout options.
