# Product Update: Beacon Streaming, Community Stats, and Mobile Fixes

**Date:** June 24, 2026

## What Shipped

**Beacon recording timing fixed.** When a host starts publishing to Beacon, HLS streaming and recording now activate immediately. Previously, there was a delay between when a host went live and when the platform began capturing the stream. You won't miss the opening moments of a session anymore.

**Service Credits in community stats.** The weekly community stats digest now includes Service Credits earned during that week. You can track how many credits you've accumulated alongside other community activity metrics.

**What Works suggest button on mobile.** The suggestion button in What Works is now properly displayed on mobile layouts. If you're on a phone and want to suggest a resource, the button is there.

## Why It Matters

These changes make Beacon sessions more reliable to watch and record. Tracking Service Credits in your weekly stats gives you visibility into your contribution history. Mobile access to the suggest feature removes a friction point for members browsing on phones.
