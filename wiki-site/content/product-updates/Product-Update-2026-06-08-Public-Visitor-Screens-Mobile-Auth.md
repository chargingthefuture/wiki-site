# Product Update: Public Visitor Screens & Mobile Authentication

**Date:** June 8, 2026

## What Shipped

We've completed two major capability rollouts:

### Public Visitor Screens
All plugins now have bespoke public visitor pages. This means you can preview what each tool does—like Skills Hunt, Mood, Peer Programming, and Trust Signal—without creating an account first. Explore at your own pace and decide what's right for you.

### Mobile Sign-In
Mobile users now sign in through Clerk OAuth, an industry-standard authentication method that's faster and more secure than previous approaches. No more friction getting started on phone or tablet.

## Why It Matters

Survivors deserve agency over how they engage with tools built for them. Public preview screens respect that agency—you can review functionality without commitment. For mobile users, streamlined authentication removes barriers to accessing support whenever and wherever you need it.

These changes reflect our commitment to calm, predictable access: low pressure, clear paths forward, full control in your hands.

## Technical Notes

- Trust Signal backend now supports visibility controls and admin verification
- CI/CD pipelines now securely source secrets from Infisical
- Expo workflows configured for production mobile builds
