# Admin Platform Build-Out Complete

## What Shipped

We've finished building out real, functional administrative interfaces across the TI Skills Economy platform. This includes:

- **ServiceCredits Admin UI**: Full approve/reject workflow for managing community credits on web and Android
- **LevelUp Admin**: Grant-only credit management interface for skills advancement tracking
- **Directory Admin Page**: Dedicated management screen with delete confirmations and real authentication
- **Weekly Performance Admin**: Complete tracking and reporting for program participation metrics
- **Peer Programming Admin**: Full management UI for coordinated learning sessions
- **Skills-Hunt, LightHouse, Workforce Admin**: Dedicated Android screens bringing mobile parity to all major programs
- **Platform Operations**: TrustTransport, SocketRelay, and GDP currency rate admin tools
- **Comic AI Review Console**: Updated to match current design specifications across web and mobile

## Why It Matters

Administrative clarity reduces friction and builds trust. When coordinators have straightforward, responsive tools to manage resources and track progress, survivors get faster approvals, clearer feedback, and more predictable access to opportunities.

The shift from prototype to real, platform-wide admin infrastructure means the TI Skills Economy is moving from experimental to operational. This is foundational work that enables scaling—coordinators can now focus on supporting survivors rather than working around broken tools.

Cross-platform consistency (web + Android) also respects different work environments. Whether someone manages the community from a desk or on the move, they have the same reliable interface.
