# ServiceCredits Monetary Policy Update

## What Shipped

ServiceCredits now includes a full monetary policy layer. You can:

- **View circulation metrics**: See how many credits exist, how many are in use, and track the flow of value through the economy.
- **Set mutual-credit limits**: Each participant has a flat, equal limit on how much mutual credit they can use at once. No credit scores. No social ranking.
- **Use the rail selector**: Choose whether to earn credits through work, redeem them for services, or move them between accounts.
- **Monitor the GDP boundary**: The system enforces an upper limit on total credits in circulation to prevent inflation.

The dashboard gives you a clear view of all this. The debug agent now also surfaces structured error logs so you can see exactly what's happening when something goes wrong.

## Why It Matters

A healthy economy needs transparency. When you can see how money moves and what the rules are, you're not guessing. You have control. These tools let you understand the system you're part of and make real decisions about how you participate.
