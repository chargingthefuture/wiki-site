# Product Update: July 2, 2026

## What Shipped

**TrustTransport Android earnings and payouts.** The earnings screen and payouts history are now live on Android. You can see what you've made and request withdrawals from the app.

**TrustTransport Android trip flow.** Trip progression, proof capture, and the Direct Line chat now work on Android. Accepting offers on the Track tab works too.

**Chyme graceful audio fallback.** When WebRTC isn't available, Chyme no longer breaks. The app stays usable while audio loads or connects.

**Mobile Account & Data link.** Terms & Privacy is now linked from the Account & Data screen on mobile, so it's easier to find.

**Navigation polish.** The back arrow on public plugin landing pages now stays inline in the header instead of overlapping other elements. Works better on all screen sizes.

## Why It Matters

Survivors using TrustTransport on Android can now track and manage earnings without switching to desktop. Chyme stays stable even when audio setup takes time. Cleaner navigation means less friction when moving through the app.
