# Chyme Unified Experience

## What Shipped

We merged Chyme chat and directory copy to a single design standard across Charging the Future. This means:

- Plugin-context naming is now consistent wherever Chyme chat appears
- Directory labels and help text align with our core voice principles
- Removed redundant or conflicting terminology that could confuse users
- Simplified onboarding so new survivors see one clear language system

## Why It Matters

When your platform speaks with one voice, you can trust it. Survivors deserve clarity—not confusion that adds friction when you're already navigating a lot. Unified language reduces cognitive load and reinforces that Charging the Future is predictable, calm, and built with your experience in mind.

This is part of our larger commitment to plain language and trauma-informed design. Every word choice matters when trust is on the line.
