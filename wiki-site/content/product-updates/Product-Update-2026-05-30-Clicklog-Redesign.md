# Clicklog Interface Redesign

## What Shipped

We've rebuilt the clicklog web pixel interface to align with the Charging the Future design system. The redesign focuses on clarity and usability without changing any underlying functionality. Your activity logs are now presented in a cleaner layout that makes it easier to navigate and review your data.

## Why It Matters

A clear interface means you can spend less time figuring out how to use the tool and more time understanding your own activity patterns. When survivors can easily review what's being logged, you maintain better control over your data and can make informed decisions about what to track. This redesign reinforces our commitment to tools that work *with* you, not *for* you.
