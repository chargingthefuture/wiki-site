# Product Update: June 20, 2026

## What Shipped

Two fixes landed on main:

**Chyme guest stage count**: The on-stage participant count in Chyme audio rooms now displays accurately when guests join. Previously, the view could show an incorrect number.

**Normalized URLs in Unlock admin**: The Unlock admin queue now shows normalized Quora URLs, so URLs with different formatting appear consistently and match up correctly with submissions.

## Why It Matters

Chyme audio rooms are where survivors connect in real time. An accurate on-stage count means you see exactly who's in the call without confusion.

In the Unlock queue, consistent URL handling means admin tasks run faster—no wasted time puzzling over whether two URLs are the same entry or different ones.

The code is open and lives at https://github.com/chargingthefuture/chargingthefuture.
