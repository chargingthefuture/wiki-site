# Product Update: Admin Demo Banner Mobile Fix

## What Shipped

Fixed a layout issue on mobile devices where the demo banner was covering parts of the admin navigation. The banner now reserves its own space instead of overlapping the menu, so all navigation items stay visible and clickable.

## Why It Matters

Admins managing the platform on phones can now access their tools without the banner getting in the way. Navigation stays clear and predictable, whether you're on a phone, tablet, or desktop.
