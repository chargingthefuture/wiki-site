# Product Update: Peer Programming Cohorts and Live Video

## What Shipped

Peer programming cohorts now support live video sessions. When you start a cohort, you and your group members can join a video call to work together in real time.

We also made navigation smoother across the platform:
- Mobile users now see a back bar on every plugin page so you can return to the app list or main menu without scrolling.
- The Weekly Performance view now always shows the current week, so the page is never blank when you open it.
- The Directory browse section is now responsive on mobile phones and tablets.
- The What Works panel includes a back button so you can return to your apps.

## Why It Matters

Cohorts work best when you can see and hear each other. Live video brings that connection to the platform without needing to jump to a separate tool.

Mobile navigation fixes mean you spend less time hunting for buttons and more time doing the work that matters to you. Whether you're on your phone or at a desk, moving between sections should feel natural.

## Technical Notes

Live video uses Stream, our video provider. Please check your Stream quota before starting a session with your group. The code for these changes is at https://github.com/chargingthefuture/chargingthefuture.
