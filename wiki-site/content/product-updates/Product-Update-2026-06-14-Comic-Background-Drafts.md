# Product Update: Comic Background Drafts

## What Shipped

The @comic chat now generates draft responses in the background instead of making you wait. When you ask @comic a question, the chat stays responsive while the draft is being created. You can continue the conversation or ask follow-up questions without any delay.

We also moved our AI processing from shared infrastructure to RunPod serverless endpoints. This gives us faster and more reliable response times.

## Why It Matters

When a tool blocks you while it thinks, it breaks your flow. Now @comic works alongside you instead of stopping you. The faster infrastructure means responses come back quicker overall.

These changes are small but they add up: the platform feels snappier, you stay in control of the pace, and everything just works without friction.
