# Product Update: Bug Reporting Launch

**Date:** June 11, 2026

## What Shipped

We've built and deployed a dedicated bug-reporting interface for web and Android users. The "Report a Problem" feature lets you submit issues directly from the app with minimal friction. When you encounter something that doesn't work as expected, you can now document it, describe what happened, and send it to our team for investigation.

The interface is straightforward: open the menu, select "Report a Problem," describe the issue, and submit. On both web and Android, the experience is consistent and accessible.

## Why It Matters

Survivors deserve tools that work reliably. When something breaks or behaves unexpectedly, we need to know—and we need to hear from you directly. This feature closes the feedback loop and puts you in control of how we improve.

By reporting problems early and often, you help us catch issues before they affect others. Your experience matters, and this tool ensures your voice reaches our development team.

## Technical Notes

- Bug reporting is now live on the web platform and Android builds
- Mobile app icons have been corrected and validated
- expo-updates dependency added to support future over-the-air updates
- Build stability improved across both platforms
