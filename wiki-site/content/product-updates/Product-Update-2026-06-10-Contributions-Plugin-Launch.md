# Product Update: Contributions Plugin & Copy Clarity

**Date:** June 10, 2026

## What Shipped

We've released the **Contributions plugin**, a new foundation that tracks and celebrates how you participate in the TI Skills Economy. The plugin supports:

- **Drives**: Organize and contribute to community initiatives
- **Gift card contributions**: Track and acknowledge resource sharing
- **Engagement contributions**: Document your participation in skill-building and mutual aid
- **Credit thank-you grants**: Recognize and reward contributors with transparent credit allocation

We've also updated app copy across the platform to be more accurate and honest. We removed claims about encryption and "safe space" designations that we couldn't reliably guarantee, ensuring every statement reflects what the platform actually does.

## Why It Matters

Survivors need tools that honor their agency and effort without overselling capabilities. The Contributions plugin gives you visibility into your own participation—a form of autonomy in how you engage with community.

Accuracy in our language builds trust. By removing vague or unverifiable claims, we're committing to plain, specific communication about what Charging the Future provides. You deserve products and language that don't inflate promises or obscure limitations.

These changes support our core mission: helping you Live, Work, and Prevail with clarity and authenticity.
