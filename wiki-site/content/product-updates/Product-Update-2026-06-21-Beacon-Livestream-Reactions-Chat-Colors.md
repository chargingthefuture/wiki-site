# Product Update: Beacon Livestream, Emoji Reactions, Chat Colors

**Date:** June 21, 2026

## What Shipped

**Beacon livestream plugin (web).** Admins can now broadcast live video to the community. Broadcast from your phone via RTMP or share your desktop screen. The stream goes out to all members in a one-way feed—viewers watch but can't interrupt the broadcast.

**Emoji reactions in the Commons.** Posts in the Commons now support emoji reactions. Tap an emoji to add your reaction or see who reacted to a post.

**Chat bubble color convention.** Your own messages now appear in gray. Messages from others show the color of their plugin (LightHouse blue, SocketRelay green, etc.). This makes it easier to follow who's saying what in a crowded conversation.

## Why It Matters

Beacon gives admins a way to reach the whole community at once—for announcements, skill shares, or group check-ins—without needing a separate video tool. Emoji reactions let you acknowledge a post without typing. The chat color change makes conversations easier to read, especially when many people are talking at once.
