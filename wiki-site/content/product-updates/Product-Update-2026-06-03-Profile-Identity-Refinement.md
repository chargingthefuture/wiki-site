# Product Update: Profile Identity Refinement

**Date:** June 3, 2026

## What Shipped

We've standardized how usernames and full names display across Charging the Future. Your username now appears consistently in:

- Person surfaces throughout the platform
- Skills Hunt nominee displays
- Profile identity layers

We also removed unused display name fields that created confusion about which identity was active. The result: clearer, more predictable identity representation everywhere you interact.

## Why It Matters

Survivors deserve clarity about how they're represented online. When identity displays are inconsistent or confusing, it undermines your sense of agency and control. By standardizing usernames and removing redundant fields, we're making it easier for you to:

- Know exactly how others see you
- Build consistent reputation within the community
- Trust the platform's authenticity layer
- Focus on your work without identity confusion

This is part of our commitment to make every surface of Charging the Future predictable, respectful, and under your control.
