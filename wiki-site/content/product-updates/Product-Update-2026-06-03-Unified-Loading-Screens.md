# Product Update: June 3, 2026

## What Shipped

We merged several updates focused on consistency and mobile usability:

- **Unified loading screens**: All loading states now display the same "Exit Their Economy / Exit The Psyop" message across the platform, creating a predictable experience.
- **Improved mobile detection**: Viewport settings and CSS breakpoints now work together, so the platform adapts correctly to phones and tablets.
- **Chyme username clarity**: Chyme now identifies authors by username instead of display name, making conversations easier to follow.
- **Better form layout on phones**: The What Works suggest form now stacks vertically on smaller screens, reducing friction when submitting ideas.

## Why It Matters

Consistency reduces cognitive load. When the platform behaves the same way everywhere—whether you're on your phone at home or a desktop at a library—you can focus on what matters: connecting with community, documenting what works, and building clarity.

Small improvements to form layout and author attribution might seem invisible, but they compound. Every moment we save you on friction is a moment you reclaim for healing, growth, and prevailing.
