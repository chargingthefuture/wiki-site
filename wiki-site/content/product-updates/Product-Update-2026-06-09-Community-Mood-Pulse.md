# Product Update: Community Mood Pulse & Comic Theme Rollout

**Date:** June 9, 2026

## What Shipped

We've released two major features:

### 1. Mood Community Pulse
A real-time, anonymous aggregation system that lets survivors understand how their community is feeling without compromising individual privacy. The pulse fetches mood data from participants who opt in, displaying trends and patterns that help you feel less alone and more aware of shared experiences.

### 2. Comic Theme Across All Platforms
We've refreshed the visual design of plugin shells, web chrome, and mobile interfaces with a cohesive comic-inspired theme. This includes updates to the Chyme chat interface, community shell, account and data screens, AI review console, LevelUp Trainers, Achievements, and Credits Wallet on both web and mobile.

## Additional Improvements

- **Chyme Live Audio Rooms on Mobile:** Stream Video SDK integration enables real-time audio conversations directly on your phone.
- **Exit Their Economy Loading Screen:** A dedicated mobile interface that reinforces your agency and control during transitions.
- **Cleaner UI:** Removed off-brand placeholders for Direct Messages and Notifications to reduce noise and confusion.
- **Viewport Fixes:** Improved responsive design on authed and shared web views with no mobile bottom gap.

## Why It Matters

The Mood community pulse acknowledges a fundamental need: survivors benefit from knowing they're not isolated. By aggregating anonymous data, we give you real insights into your community's collective experience while keeping individual stories protected. You choose whether to participate, and you always know what data is being shared.

The visual refresh signals that Charging the Future is evolving. A cohesive, intentional design across all surfaces—web, mobile, chat, training, and economy screens—builds trust and makes navigation predictable.

These updates embody our core principle: **Live, Work, and Prevail with clarity, critical thinking, and authenticity.** You now have clearer visibility into your community and a more consistent, empowering experience across every part of the platform.

## What's Next

We continue refining the mood pulse based on how survivors use it. We're also building out the LevelUp economy and credits system to give you measurable ways to invest in your recovery and support others.

Stay tuned for more updates.
