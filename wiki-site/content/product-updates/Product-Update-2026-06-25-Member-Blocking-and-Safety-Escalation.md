# Member Blocking and Safety Escalation

## What Shipped

Two features rolled out together:

1. **Block a member**: Use the block option in the Commons or any Direct Line. Once blocked, that member can't send you messages or see your posts in shared spaces. You can unblock anytime from your Manage settings.

2. **Safety escalation**: When you block someone, you can optionally note that the block is tied to a safety concern. Moderators will see the flag and can review the context.

## Why It Matters

Control over who reaches you is part of staying safe. Blocking is immediate and private—no one else sees your block list. If a block involves harassment or a threat, flagging it helps moderators spot patterns and keep the platform safer for everyone.

## How to Use

**To block a member:**
- In the Commons: find their post or profile, tap the menu (three dots), select "Block."
- In a Direct Line: tap the member name at the top, select "Block."

**To escalate a safety concern:**
- When you block, a checkbox appears: "This block involves a safety concern." Check it if the reason involves harassment, threats, or suspicious behavior.
- Moderators receive the flag with your block. You don't need to add details unless you want to.

**To unblock:**
- Go to Settings → Manage → Blocked Members. Find the person and tap "Unblock."
