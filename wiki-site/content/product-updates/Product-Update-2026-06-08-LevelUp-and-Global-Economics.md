# Product Update: June 8, 2026

## What Shipped

**LevelUp Trainers, Achievements, and Credits Wallet**
We've launched three interconnected features in the TI Skills Economy:
- **LevelUp Trainers**: Named mentorship and skill-building guidance
- **Achievements**: Milestone tracking that recognizes your progress
- **Credits Wallet**: A transparent ledger of your earned credits and economic participation

These tools give you concrete visibility into your growth within the platform.

**Real GDP World Map**
Our world map now displays live economic data instead of a placeholder. You can see how the Psyop-Free Economy is distributed globally and understand where survivors are building alternative economic systems.

**Simplified Interface**
We removed the Direct Messages stub and Notifications bell from the navigation rail. These changes reduce visual clutter and focus the interface on active, working features.

## Why It Matters

Survivors need clear feedback on their progress and agency over their economic participation. The new Achievements and Credits Wallet create that clarity—you can see exactly what you've earned and what it means. LevelUp Trainers connect you to expertise without requiring you to navigate unclear hierarchies.

The real GDP map grounds the TI Skills Economy in tangible, global reality. You're not working in isolation; you're part of a movement.

Removing dead UI elements respects your attention. Every component now serves a purpose.
