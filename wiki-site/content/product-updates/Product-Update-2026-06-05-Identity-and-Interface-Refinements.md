# Product Update: Identity and Interface Refinements

**June 5, 2026**

## What Shipped

This week we merged three focused improvements to how you control your identity and experience the interface:

### SocketRelay @username Visibility
Your @username in SocketRelay is now displayed publicly by default. Collaborators see who you are—no more Anonymous placeholders. This strengthens accountability and trust in shared work.

### Live Badge Removal
We've hidden the "Live" badge that previously appeared on available plugins. This reduces visual noise and lets you focus on the tools themselves rather than status indicators.

### Tiered Design Strategy
We've adopted a tiered approach to how features and terminology appear across the platform. This ensures the interface stays calm and predictable while educational content has room to explain concepts fully.

## Why It Matters

For survivors building work and community, identity matters. Knowing who you're collaborating with builds trust. At the same time, a calm interface free from unnecessary badges and signals helps you stay focused on what you're creating.

These changes reflect our commitment to give you control over how you present yourself while keeping the platform respectful and clear. You decide what you share and how you're seen.

## Next Steps

If you're using SocketRelay for collaboration, you'll see your @username displayed to your network. If you have feedback on these changes, reach out in the Commons chat in the app.
