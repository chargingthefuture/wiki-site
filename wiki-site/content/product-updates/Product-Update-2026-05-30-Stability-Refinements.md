# Product Update: Stability Refinements

**Date:** May 30, 2026

## What Shipped

Our team completed a focused round of technical improvements across multiple core services:

- **Data Integrity**: Fixed duplicate entry handling in our readiness tracking system, ensuring accurate progress visibility.
- **Service Alignment**: Refined API contracts and runtime behavior across Mood, GentlePulse, SocketRelay, TrustTransport, GDP, and Peer Programming services.
- **Code Quality**: Standardized internal patterns and decomposed complex logic into clearer, more maintainable units.
- **Review Process**: Codified our CodeRabbit self-triage labeling and changelog conventions to keep communication consistent and transparent.
- **CI/CD Stability**: Improved automated review workflows to reduce noise and focus feedback where it matters most.

## Why It Matters

These updates are foundational work—the kind that doesn't always announce itself, but makes a real difference in how smoothly the platform runs. When your tools work reliably, you can trust them. When data is accurate, you can make clearer decisions. When our code is clean, we ship fixes faster and introduce fewer bugs.

For survivors building lives of clarity and authenticity, reliability is respect. You deserve tools that work the way they should, without hidden surprises or silent failures.
