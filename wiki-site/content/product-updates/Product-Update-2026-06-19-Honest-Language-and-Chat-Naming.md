## What Shipped

We removed false claims from two plugins:

**Foundation** no longer says providers are vetted or background-checked. Foundation connects you with people who offer services. We don't run background checks. That's the honest description.

**TrustTransport** no longer claims to offer safety features or live tracking it doesn't have. TrustTransport coordinates rides. It doesn't have real-time location sharing or safety alerts. We took those words out.

We also named the two chat spaces in the platform:

- **The Commons**: the home chat where anyone can post to everyone. It's the many-voices space.
- **Direct Line**: the one-to-one chat paired with a plugin — like when you match with a LightHouse provider or request a SocketRelay.

Other fixes:
- The Skills Taxonomy left rail now works instead of sitting there decorative.
- Directory sector filter now matches the sectors you've mapped in your skills, not just a separate profile field.
- Service Credits earn model got corrected and the UI chrome cleaned up.
- Skills Hunt admins can now create a new round and reach the admin surface on mobile.
- New /account hub gathers your identity info in one place.
- Chyme room names and message timestamps were restored.
- Sign-in now redirects straight to chat.
- Public Commons is readable signed out.

## Why It Matters

Accuracy builds trust. When we say what a tool does and doesn't do, you can decide if it fits what you need. Naming the two chat spaces makes it clear which one you're in — Commons for shared talk, Direct Line for your one-to-one connection with someone helping you.
