# Product Update: Hub Posts and Loading Improvements

**Date:** June 3, 2026

## What Shipped

Three changes landed on main to improve Hub reliability and clarity:

1. **Hub posts now show author usernames.** When a peer shares a post in your Hub, their @username appears at the top. This makes it immediately clear who's contributing to the conversation.

2. **Fixed a crash on app load.** The TrustTransport system was crashing when it tried to read the API list shapes. We've fixed the way it processes that data so the app loads smoothly.

3. **Synced deployment secrets.** Updated how the app references its Render service secrets so deployments stay reliable and consistent.

## Why It Matters

When you're building trust and community with other survivors, knowing exactly who's posting matters. Clear authorship makes conversations more transparent and easier to follow.

A crashing app creates friction and breaks your flow. By fixing the TrustTransport load issue, we're making sure you can open Charging the Future and get to work without barriers.

These fixes are small but foundational—they remove obstacles so you can focus on what matters: connecting with others, building skills, and moving forward.
