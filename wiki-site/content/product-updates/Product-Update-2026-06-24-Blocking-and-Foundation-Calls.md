# Product Update: June 24, 2026

## What Shipped

**Member blocking.** You can now block other members. When you block someone, they can't contact you through Direct Line or the Commons. The block is one-way — you won't see their messages, and they won't be able to reach you.

**Foundation instant call settings.** Providers using Foundation can now set up 1:1 calls that start right away. When you request a connection, you'll see what the provider has agreed to share before you're connected — so there's no surprise about consent or what the call will cover.

**Clearer plugin names.** Two-word plugin names now read as one phrase throughout the app instead of being split up. This makes navigation and plugin descriptions easier to scan.

## Why It Matters

Blocking gives you control over who can reach you. It's a straightforward boundary tool — use it when someone isn't respectful or safe to interact with.

Foundation's call settings and consent preview mean you know exactly what you're getting into before you connect. No guessing, no after-the-fact surprises.

Clearer naming means less friction when you're looking for something or learning what a plugin does.

The code is at https://github.com/chargingthefuture/chargingthefuture.
