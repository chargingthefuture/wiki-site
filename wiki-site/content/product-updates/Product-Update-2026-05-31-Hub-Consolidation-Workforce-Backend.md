# Product Update: Hub Consolidation and Workforce Backend Finalization

**Date:** May 31, 2026

## What Shipped

We've consolidated feed announcements into the Survivor Hub home page, creating a unified channel for important updates. This means you'll see all critical information in one consistent place instead of scattered across multiple surfaces.

Our workforce backend is now fully finalized. This infrastructure supports the skills economy features that help you build and monetize your capabilities. The backend handles everything from service credits to skills discovery—all working reliably so you can focus on what matters.

We've also refined our internal processes: CodeRabbit review policies now give project owners clear control over when changes are ready, and our team uses descriptive branch names for better transparency.

## Why It Matters

After harassment, clarity and predictability reduce cognitive load and help you regain a sense of control. Consolidating your announcements into one hub means fewer places to check and less chance of missing critical information.

The finalized workforce backend is infrastructure built for you—it's the foundation that lets you Live, Work, and Prevail. When systems work reliably, you can trust the platform and focus energy on recovery and building the future you want.

## Technical Details

- Feed announcements now backed by Survivor Hub channel architecture
- Workforce backend status moved from yellow (in progress) to green (complete)
- Service credits web pixel pass and skills-hunt rule-116 decomposition finalized
- CodeRabbit draft/ready policy now owner-controlled
