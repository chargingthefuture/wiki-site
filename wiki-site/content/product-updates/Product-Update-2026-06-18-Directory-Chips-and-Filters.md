# Product Update: Directory Chips and Filters

**Date:** June 18, 2026

## What Shipped

We fixed several things in the directory and home chat:

- **Directory search and filter chips now work.** When you click on a sector chip or search chip in the directory, it actually filters the results. Before, the chips were there but didn't do anything.
- **Concierge chips stay visible.** The "ask what you need" suggestion chips now stay in your home chat view so you can use them anytime, not just once.
- **Better bio and skills display.** We backfilled survivor bios and skills from older data so profiles are more complete. If your profile was missing information, it should show up now.
- **Chip styling improved.** The concierge chips now scroll horizontally and use a clearer color so they're easier to spot.

## Why It Matters

The directory is how you find people and services in the TI Skills Economy. When chips don't filter, the directory becomes harder to use. Now you can narrow down results by sector or search topic and actually see what you're looking for.

The home chat suggestions are there to help you get started. Keeping them visible means you can explore what the platform offers without hunting for help.

Completed bios mean better matches and clearer connections with other survivors.
