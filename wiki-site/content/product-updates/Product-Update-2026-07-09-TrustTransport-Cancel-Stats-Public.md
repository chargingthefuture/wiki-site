# Product Update — July 9, 2026

## What Shipped

**TrustTransport cancel control.** The Tracking tab now has a cancel button for active TrustTransport requests. If you need to stop a request in progress, you can do that from the same place you track its status.

**Community stats accuracy.** The stats display now counts posts, not people. Before, the numbers were confusing because they mixed two different things. Now a count is a count.

**Recurring Activity public landing.** Signed-out visitors can now see what Recurring Activity is and how it works. The page lives at the public landing alongside other product introductions.

**Contributions public landing fixes.** Removed made-up numbers. The page now shows only real data or nothing at all — no guessing.

**SkillsHunt cleanup.** Fixed issues found during code review. The test scripts and internal behavior are more reliable.

## Why It Matters

Cancel control gives you full agency over your TrustTransport trips. If plans change, you're not stuck.

Accurate stats mean you can trust what you see. No confusion, no math that doesn't add up.

Public landings help people outside the platform understand what each tool does before they join. The Contributions landing now shows honesty instead of numbers that don't exist.
