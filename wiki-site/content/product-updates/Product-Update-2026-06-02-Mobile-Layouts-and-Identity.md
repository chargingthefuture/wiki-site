# Product Update: Mobile Layouts and Identity Improvements

**Date:** June 2, 2026

## What Shipped

We've completed a comprehensive mobile redesign across the TI Skills Economy plugins:

- **Mobile layouts** for What Works, Skills Taxonomy, Workforce, Skills Hunt, LevelUp, Weekly Performance, ServiceCredits, Peer Programming, TrustTransport, Foundation, and ClickLog
- **Fixed mobile display issues** in the What Works suggest form and AI consent modal to prevent layout flashing and button stacking
- **Identity improvements** in the Directory to use first and last name fields instead of display names
- **Infrastructure stability** updates to monitoring and schema consistency

## Why It Matters

Survivors access Charging the Future from all kinds of devices and situations. A phone that works reliably—without jarring visual shifts or cramped forms—means you can engage with your skills, your peers, and your recovery journey without friction. Clear identity options in the Directory help you present yourself authentically and build trust within the community.

These changes reflect our commitment to making every interaction calm, predictable, and under your control, no matter where you are.
