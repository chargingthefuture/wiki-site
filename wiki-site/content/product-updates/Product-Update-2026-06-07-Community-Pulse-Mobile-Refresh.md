# Product Update: Community Pulse, Mobile Refresh, and Chat Improvements

**Date:** June 7, 2026

## What Shipped

### Mood Community Pulse from Anonymous Aggregates
We've launched real community pulse tracking that aggregates anonymous data about how survivors are doing. This gives you visibility into shared emotional trends without exposing individual responses. The data comes from you and others who choose to participate—completely optional and completely private.

### Mobile App Cleanup
We removed 14 dead mock screens from the mobile app. This makes the app lighter, faster, and easier to navigate. You'll notice cleaner interactions and fewer confusing dead ends.

### Comic Theme Foundation
We've built the foundation for a comic-style visual theme with a persistent per-user toggle. You can now customize how the interface looks in a way that works for you.

### Hub Chat Suggestion Improvements
Chat suggestion chips now fill, focus, and scroll the composer properly. This means the chat interface responds the way you'd expect when you're composing messages.

## Why It Matters

These updates reinforce three core principles we're committed to:

**Privacy-first insights:** Community pulse data is aggregated and anonymous. You get to see trends without individual data being exposed—because your safety matters.

**Clarity and control:** A cleaner mobile app means less confusion. Customizable themes mean the platform adapts to you, not the other way around.

**Responsive chat:** When chat works smoothly, you can focus on what you're saying, not fighting the interface. Better tooling means better communication.
