# Product Update: Account Data Privacy and Deletion Controls

**Date:** June 6, 2026

## What Shipped

We've released new account privacy and data deletion tools directly in your account settings. These controls let you:

- **Review your data**: See exactly what Charging the Future stores about your account and activity.
- **Delete your information**: Remove your account data anytime, on your own terms.
- **Understand our practices**: Clear explanations of how we handle your information—no legal jargon.

The interface is mobile-responsive and works seamlessly on all devices. We also fixed timeout handling and added safeguards so deletions require confirmation.

## Why It Matters

Survivors deserve full control over their personal information. These tools reinforce your agency and autonomy—core values of the TI Skills Economy. Transparency builds trust. You're not locked into our platform; you own your data and can leave whenever you choose.

We're also shipping improved GDP estimate transparency across financial summaries so you understand exactly how our economic data is sourced and what it represents.

## Technical Notes

- Mobile-responsive design matches our broader platform parity standards
- Modal guards and timeout protections prevent accidental data loss
- React Fragment keys optimized for mobile list performance
- CodeRabbit review integrated to maintain code quality standards
