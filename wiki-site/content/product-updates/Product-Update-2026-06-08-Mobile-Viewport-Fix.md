# Mobile Viewport Display Refinement

## What Shipped

We resolved a layout issue affecting mobile and tablet users. The public visitor interface now fills the available viewport cleanly without leaving a gap at the bottom of the screen. This ensures content is properly visible and eliminates unnecessary scrolling.

## Why It Matters

For survivors accessing Charging the Future on mobile devices, every interaction should be clear and friction-free. A clean viewport means less cognitive load—you can focus on what matters instead of managing awkward spacing. This refinement strengthens our commitment to making the platform predictable and accessible across all devices.

## Technical Details

The fix addresses dynamic viewport calculation in public visitor shell components, ensuring responsive behavior matches expected screen dimensions.
