The Specterati made me destitute. Thanks to three TIs that I met on Quora, I was able to survive. I am paying it forward the way I know how. Starting today Service Credits Mutual Aid is available. Still no social or credit score. But a way for a TI with $0 to join and immediately receive goods and services with dignity and respect.

I run an open-source project for survivors — the TI Skills Economy. Hosted at [CTF Survivor Hub](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=959443&url=https://app.chargingthefuture.com), and I just added two things to our credit system, ServiceCredits.

First, a clear plan for how new credits are made, so the supply stays steady. Second, a way to pay called Mutual Credit. If you have skills but no credits yet, you can still take part. You pay another member now, go a little below zero up to a small limit, and pay it back as you earn.

This idea is old. Communities have run their own credit before — time banks, LETS groups, the Wörgl scrip in Austria, and business networks like Sardex in Italy and the WIR Bank in Switzerland. People traded using shared credit when regular money was short. The limit is small and the same for everyone. There is no credit score and no social score.
