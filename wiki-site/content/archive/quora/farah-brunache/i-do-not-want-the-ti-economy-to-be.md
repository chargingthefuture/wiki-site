I do not want the TI economy to be the creation of another Quora on a different platform.

This is not to disrespect trauma sharing. We can organically form friendships and speak of the trauma of human trafficking. However, the objective of these virtual and in-person meetings is to form a self-sustaining ecosystem where we live in dignity. We have housing, food, transportation, and work outside of a psy-op. And in turn can live fulfilling lives.

Please refrain from joining if it will be about sharing stories about the Specterati (gang stalkers). Several Quora spaces are the best place for that. Here, we are providing material value for a better life. It does not matter how much money you have; you can contribute. The Specterati, have bankrupted me beyond belief; everything is gone. I have 8 cents left. I get up every day to contribute to this space and other TIs. We can do this!
