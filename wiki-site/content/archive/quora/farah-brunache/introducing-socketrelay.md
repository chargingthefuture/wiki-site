Introducing SocketRelay! Request goods and services from your TI community!

You can now make a post within 140 characters requesting a good or service, and other TIs can fulfill that request.

Inspired by a TI who instantly mailed me a winter coat and jacket after reading about it in the TI group chat, after the Specterati had stolen my sole jacket.

Less than one minute to join:
- Request an invite code (DM me for one)
- Go to my Quora bio, and click the only link there to find the app and paste in the invite code at sign up
- Click SocketRelay on the left-hand side and get started!

For the love of humanity!
