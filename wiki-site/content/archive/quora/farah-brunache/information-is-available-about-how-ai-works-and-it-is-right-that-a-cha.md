Information is available about how AI works, and it is right that a chat bot reflects what it was trained on. Ask a commercial chat bot about organized stalking and you get the institutional line: deny, deflect, “call the authorities.” The Specterati operate within those companies’ reach, so that is the answer you will always get.

That is why I built my bot differently.

It is self-hosted, so your questions never touch a commercial AI company’s servers. Every answer it drafts is grounded in my own writing about the Specterati — hundreds of my answers, posts, and documents — not whatever is floating around the Internet. A human reviews every single answer before a TI sees it; nothing is auto-published. And it learns from TIs who use it, through corrections and helpful or not-helpful ratings from real Targets.

A chat bot built on their data gives their answers. One built on ours gives ours.
