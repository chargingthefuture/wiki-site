We just cleaned up some language in the platform. Foundation and TrustTransport were making claims they couldn't back up — saying providers were vetted when we don't do background checks, or that TrustTransport has live tracking when it doesn't. We took those out.

We also named the two chat spaces so it's clear where you are. The Commons is the shared chat where anyone can post to everyone. Direct Line is the one-to-one chat you get when you match with someone through a plugin — a LightHouse provider, a SocketRelay request, a TrustTransport ride, a Foundation quote.

A bunch of other fixes went in too: the skills filter in the directory now works right, admins can create Skills Hunt rounds on mobile, and your account info lives in one place now.

The code is at [GitHub - chargingthefuture/chargingthefuture: world's first psyop-free economy](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=ce85c0&url=https://github.com/chargingthefuture/chargingthefuture) if you want to look at what changed.
