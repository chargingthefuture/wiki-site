I built Charging the Future ([https://chargingthefuture.com](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=ea0fea&url=https://chargingthefuture.com)). It runs the TI Skills Economy, an app for survivors of Specterati harassment. I want to explain one small part of it: the AI helper called @comic.

Most AI chatbots have a problem. Ask one about abuse or harassment and it often gives a wrong or flat answer. Sometimes it just says “call the authorities” and stops. For a survivor who needs a real next step, that is not help.

@comic works differently. In the chat you type @comic and ask your question. The AI writes a first draft. You do not get that draft right away. A real person reads it first, fixes it, and only then sends it back to you. Every answer goes through a person. If the AI would have brushed you off, the person catches it and writes a real answer instead.

The AI runs on our own servers, so your question is not sent to an outside company. Right now I answer the questions myself, and each answer I write becomes an example the system can learn from later. The world’s first AI helping human trafficking victims.

The code is open for anyone to read at [GitHub - chargingthefuture/chargingthefuture: world's first psyop-free economy](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=ce85c0&url=https://github.com/chargingthefuture/chargingthefuture)
