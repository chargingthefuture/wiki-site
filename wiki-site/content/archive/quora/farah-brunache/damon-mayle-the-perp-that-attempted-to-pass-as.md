Damon Mayle, the perp that attempted to pass as a TI, proudly stated, that once I started following him on Quora he got more follows himself.

A friendly reminder that me following someone is not an endorsement. With the TI Skills Economy (TSE) app readily available, the vetting process in place is what signals who I believe is a TI. Because there, a person provides some level of social proof with the Quora URL, but it does not stop there.

There is a feature called Trust. Not a social or credit score. But shows us all how much material value a member provides. Example, how many times have they helped someone, how many rides they have given, how many real TIs they have recommended, etc. Material value is not money related rather if you are helpful.

This is what matters. Not who I follow.
