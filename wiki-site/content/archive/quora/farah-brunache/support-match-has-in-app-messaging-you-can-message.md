Support Match has in-app messaging

You can message your matches directly on the platform and coordinate how you would like to connect with each other. Remember the messages are securely stored, but can be seen by me. Meaning they are not end to end encrypted.

Just like how Uber and Airbnb can see the messages between you and the driver or host. This is done for safety reasons. I will not look at the messages unless you report and issue. This is why it is important to also pick an off platform solution, like Signal to share with people once you are comfortable.

Signal ([Signal.org](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=8a253e&url=http://Signal.org)) is end to end encrypted and you share a handle, you do not need to share a phone number or personal information to chat.
