Introducing WhatWorks!

[https://app.chargingthefuture.co...](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=0c2d0e&url=https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/whatworks)

What?

One shared, survivor-verified list of tools — organized by the exact problems survivors face. No ads, no affiliates.

Why?

Quora is where most real TIs can be found. And when a TI finds a product that works well at mitigating the Specterati's disturbances, they put it in a Quora post. But rediscovery of that post is difficult. WhatWorks is a running list of recommended products (no ads, no tracking) to make it easy to quickly find a product and solve a problem.

Comment with any feedback or questions!
