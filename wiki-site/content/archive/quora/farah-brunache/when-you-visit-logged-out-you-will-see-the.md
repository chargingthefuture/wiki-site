It is correct that the home page of the app is the group chat.

Two reasons. One so new TIs see this is real and not a perp managed app. Second, reduces harm from perps.

Perps will not provide material value. They will waste our time with trolling and hiding behind our stories posing as TIs. This is easy to do in writing. By limiting “story telling” early on one must provide material value first. This filters out not all but most perps instantly. Allowing you to build authentic relationships early and setting the foundation for you to be comfortable in having a pure relationship beyond “exchange.”
