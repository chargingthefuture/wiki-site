Accept that gang stalking won't end anytime soon. And begin networking with other TIs. TIs are some of the most skilled talent. Not only is it good to form friendships it can also lead to career opportunities and tips on how to cope with the stalking. Not going it alone is the best way. It is centering yourself vs gang stalkers and their harassment.
