I discovered it is not random. For example, if you befriend or become an acquaintance of a targeted person you get targeted. If only you are a person who also fits a certain profile. There are far more targeted people than we think and we are closer in proximity than we realize, everyone is just at different stages of targeting. And not realizing where they are in the spectrum of this program. Which is why you have people completely at a loss as to how this could have happened to them. It so happens it could be of a brief but positively impactful encounter with someone who is already a target. In which case, you must be neutralized from ever doing that again, as you do not fit the profile of being converted to a vigilante.
