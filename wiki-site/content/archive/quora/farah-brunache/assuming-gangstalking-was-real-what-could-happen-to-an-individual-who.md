It depends at what stage the TI is within the stalking. Once it becomes overt, you’ll likely get “lightly” targeted. They know you won’t turn on the TI so they’ll infiltrate the weak ones around you to mess with you in some way. From the looks of it, if you don’t fit a specific profile they likely won’t turn you into a target. They’ll just make it difficult for you to help the TI.
