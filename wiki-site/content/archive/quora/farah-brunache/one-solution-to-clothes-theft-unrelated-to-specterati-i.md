One solution to clothes theft

Unrelated to Specterati I am a minimalist. Though it was not until the overt stalking began that I learned about merino wool. If your stalking is like mine, the Specterati have stolen all your clothes including underwear! And at the time I had transitioned to buying more on clothes that last longer. After stealing everything in one go and loosing my clothes and 100’s of dollars I invested more time in researching material that order resist and high quality. After trial and error merino wool is the best fit.

With theft being a weekly experience I decided to go with two merino wool outfits. You do not need to wash them as much, saving you money as funds are limited for Targets, order resist increasing your comfort and with most of your items on you, their theft is reduced.

When it comes to merino wool and sliming down to two outfits there aren’t many fashionable choices. However as a minimalist this works extremely well.
