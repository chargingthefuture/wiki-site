Individuals are joining the app and Signal group, but their handles/names do not match anyone who has recently interacted with this Quora space.

So it is hard for me to do a quick check to see if you are possibly a TI. When you sign up for either, please let me know what your Quora profile is. By no means is it a foolproof way to only allow TIs in. But as a first filter, it works. After that, the community will verify if someone is real.

After joining Signal, post your Quora profile URL or handle. When it is the app, either join Signal and share your profile URL, and state that you are waiting to be approved in the app. Or leave a comment on any discussion thread in this Quora space.

The faster you provide the Quora profile URL/handle, the quicker you get approved.
