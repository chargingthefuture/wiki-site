Two quick updates landed in the home chat this week.

First, concierge replies now sort to the bottom of the conversation so you can read messages in order without confusion. The chat also scrolls automatically to the newest message—no more scrolling up and down to find what just came in.

Second, we swapped the Contributions icon from a heart to a gift. Keeps things clearer in the interface.

You can see the code and contribute at [GitHub - chargingthefuture/chargingthefuture: world's first psyop-free economy](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=ce85c0&url=https://github.com/chargingthefuture/chargingthefuture).
