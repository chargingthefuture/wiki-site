Did Nelson Mandela say it best?

“For to be free is not merely to cast off one’s chains, but to live in a way that respects and enhances the freedom of others.”

History has shown that oppressed people must take responsibility for their own liberation. And then it is up to us to maintain the standard. The government is not run by one person, but a collection of people. And it is not binary of good and evil.

Like it or not, TIs will be the ones who put an end to this. So the real question is, what will you or I do to prevent gang stalking?
