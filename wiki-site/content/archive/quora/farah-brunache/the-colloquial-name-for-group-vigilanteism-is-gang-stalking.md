The colloquial name for group vigilanteism is gang stalking. And I am personally tired of using it.

Recently, a TI mentioned that they had not joined TI Skills Network (TSN) as they were unsure if their physical location was included. Location is not an exclusionary criterion for TSN. Another TI mentioned that because of sleep deprivation by gang stalkers, it is not easy to follow everything that is going on with TSN.

And so I would like to clean up all my posts. By reviewing them for clarity, grammar, and consistency. To make it easy for Targets to digest what I am presenting. As a Target, I also suffer from sleep deprivation from gang stalkers, and this, along with all other attacks, affects my communication.

In this process of cleaning up my posts, I want to replace the colloquial name “gang stalking.”

What suggestions do you have?

I am leaning towards “predators.” When someone says “Targets,” it is distinctly known what is being referred to. I am not choosing stalkers, as that term is overly used. Another colloquial name option is banshees, but that seems too light-hearted or rather rolls off the tongue silly. I am searching for a single word (not two) and one that is not overly used in other contexts that could create confusion.

Any suggestions? If we get a few suggestions, I can create a public and anonymous poll to narrow it down.
