Welcome new followers to TI Skills Network! Every day, we get about 1–2 new followers.

I am not sure how long you have all been here. And so a friendly introduction.

I started this group around August 3, 2025. And it is mainly about TIs exchanging skills and services with each other. I state this because most other TI Quora spaces are more about sharing stories (which is perfectly fine).

Here, things are a bit different. I respectfully ask everyone who contributes to focus on actionable exchanges of information. What I envision is that all TIs can opt out of the Specterrealm (gang stalker ecosystem), so to speak, if they choose. And instead choose to be a part of our TI economy. For example, need an Airbnb, pay a TI, not a gstalker.  Our  own circular economy.

I am also updating over time all posts that reference gstalker to Speceterati. Many, including myself, do not care for the phrase gang stalker. Who says we have to use it? If you have alternative suggestions, let me know: [Changing the colloquial name for group vigilantism](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=4d3e34&url=https://chargingthefuture.discourse.group/t/changing-the-colloquial-name-for-group-vigilantism/103/2?u%3Dfarah).
