If I download a game. Shortly after a forced update will happen and the games story line trolls me. Or has trolling ads.

I have a fix to that: Farah’s Arcade!

[https://chargingthefuture.github...](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=c06599&url=https://chargingthefuture.github.io/arcade/)

Every game on it follows the same simple rules:

• No ads. Ever.

• No tracking. There are no accounts, and nothing watches you while you play.

• Offline-first. Once a game is on your device, it keeps working with the internet turned off. No surprise update can change it while you sleep.

• Open source. Anyone can read the code, so there are no hidden tricks.

• No harassment. The games are single-player. There is no chat and no strangers.

Right now you can play Glowline, Glowline 2, Chess, Cascade, and Redline. A new game called Specterrealm is on the way.

Farah’s Arcade is a side project that goes along with the TI Skills Economy, a safe space for survivors of human trafficking. Everyone deserves a quiet place to rest and have a little fun. This is mine to share.
