Services per person

Services total $210 billion. That equals $42,000 in services per person each year. The rest — $18,000 per person — comes from local goods and informal trade.

Modeling after succesfull service based economies such as Finland is important because as TIs we have been casted out of society and therefore limited in our ability for local/regional/international exchange beyond services. Informal local and regional trade is feasible.
