Updates
- Today will be the last day I go live on Twitch under the handle “the1690s”: [Twitch](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=c31b80&url=https://twitch.tv/the1690s) . I am in the process of finishing Chyme, a social audio app for TIs only. I will make an announcement when the bugs are resolved. I am looking for testers for the app. If interested, let me know.
- I am retiring the blog from this domain: [https://chargingthefuture.discourse.group](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=3a1f88&url=https://chargingthefuture.discourse.group/). Discourse is $20/month, and I personally cover it, while people do read posts there. It is more cost-effective to host the blog in the TI Skills Economy app itself. I will configure the blog so that you do not have to sign into the app to read the blog. I have exported all the posts from Discourse and migrated them to the app. I am working on the formatting and styling of the blog. Once this is complete, I will announce the relaunch of the blog.

If you have any questions or suggestions, let me know!
