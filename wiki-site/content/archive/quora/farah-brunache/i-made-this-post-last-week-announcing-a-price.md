I made this post last week, announcing a price increase:

[A Necessary Step to Keep Our Platform Running](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=881246&url=https://github.com/chargingthefuture/chargingthefuture/wiki/A-Necessary-Step-to-Keep-Our-Platform-Running)

But have thought of a new idea.

Today, the Specterati sent a letter denying me public assistance, stating I am actively receiving benefits in a different state and did not disclose which state I am allegedly receiving benefits in. In addition to this lie, they stated I visited their office this week in person, which did not happen, and also did not disclose who I allegedly met with and what topic was discussed.

This was yet another reminder I need to keep the contribution ask low, as difficult as it is for me. It is just as difficult for all other TIs. And so I am reversing the above announcement.

This is what will happen for the foreseeable future. When hosting costs are not covered, the app goes down. And when it does, there will be a landing page asking for TIs that have not signed up yet to join the waitlist. When the app is back up, they will receive a notice to sign up to start using services immediately.

Existing members, your account is not impacted. You are still a member when the app is offline. Once the app is back up, you will be able to log in normally and resume services.

This is a much better solution to include everyone and not miss the momentum in connecting the 5 million TIs globally. Let me know your thoughts.

Hang in there TIs!
