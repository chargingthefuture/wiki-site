This statement is incorrect. “Gang Stalkers are not out to kill you.” It is true most of the time they will relentless try to entrap you and make you you’re worst enemy. But they will attempt to kill you using different tactics if they cannot entrap you. The goal is so that your death cannot be traced to them.
