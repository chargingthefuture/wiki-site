I challenge us to make our own luck.

Being targeted by the Specterati is like winning the reverse cash lottery. 🙃

It is said if you win the lottery, cousins you never knew of pop up out of nowhere all looking to cash in.

The same happens when you are a Target though payout for the Specterati comes in the form of cash rewards derived from your exploitation, new jobs, news “friends,” new significant other, etc.

I find it best to not take it personal, move on and create our own luck.

I answered another TIs question on Quora weeks ago about how they were being contacted by people they came across in their past that they are not friends with and are known drug addicts. And they said these clear as day addicts are part of the Specterati are being overly friendly and persistent about forming a friendship.

Do not fall for it. It is the reverse lottery. They are only there for entrapment or to annoy you as part of their sick exploitation and perverted mind games all while getting a payout. There is zero reason why any stranger or someone from your past needs to be your new best friend urgently.

Creating your own luck is about being at the right place at the right time. There's is no magic involved in it. It is about doing and being around the right people. You cannot increase your chances of “luck” while be consumed by the Specterati.

I challenge all TIs to create their own luck. And I hope the TI economy can help you do this.
