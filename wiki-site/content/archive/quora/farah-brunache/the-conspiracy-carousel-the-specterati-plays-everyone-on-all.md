The Conspiracy Carousel

The Specterati plays everyone on all sides. One common game is falsely labeling a Target with mental health issues. There are many, but their favorite is that they have schizophrenia. After reading Matthew Cappadocia story on Quora, I was able to successfully avoid this entrapment scheme. And it was the sole inspiration for me to create this series of posts listing as many Specterati games so newbie TIs do not fall for the same games.

Here is Matthew’s recount:

Through my own personal experience, I can say that “They” play you on BOTH sides. That is, they have gang-stalking conspirators who plan and design actions to set you up. And they set you up to fail in front of people who are NOT part of the conspiracy plan. That is done so that people who have nothing to do with this cult of stalking, will AGREE with them, when they say you are “crazy”. The intended result is to blur the lines for your perception of “who is involved” and to further make you believe that “everyone is involved”. They are masters of creating chaos and confusion, so as to hide their crimes against you. HERE IS WHAT HAPPENED TO ME.

Someone from a “concerned citizens” group contacted my family and said I was SUFFERING FROM A MARIJUANA INDUCED PSYCHOSIS and was “going down the wrong path , and without intervention, I might end up on HARD drugs, homeless, in jail, or even dead”. They lied to my family and offered a proven solution of “behavior modification” to “save me from myself” [This is called EXTORTION].

When I went to see my sisters, at my mother’s house in Florida, my sisters were told to use a law called The Baker Act, to have me involuntarily committed to a mental hospital. When I rejected that idea, they called the police to enforce the Baker Act. The police officer who showed up, seemed to “know my whole story”, and wrote up a report based on complete lies.

When I arrived at the mental hospital, the intake Social Worker and Nurse also seemed to “know me”, and simply repeated the same lies told by the original “concerned citizens “, and the enforcement officer. But he said he “knew me”.

I exercised my right to have a second opinion, and a Court Hearing. So on Tuesday, I saw a Doctor I chose randomly. This doctor was not part of the script. And this doctor determined that I did mot have any mental health issues, or psychosis.

On Wednesday I had a video hearing with a Magistrate of the Florida Court. This person was also un-aware of the script and the conspiracy, and she order my release, based on my own testimony.

SUMMARY: 5 players were part of the gang-stalking operation. The “Concerned Citizens Group, the Baker Act enforcement officer, the hospital Social Worker and Nurse, and the hospital Psychologists. The victims are, myself and my family (we have not fully healed, but we are moving on). The unknowing participants were the 2nd Doctor and the Magistrate.
