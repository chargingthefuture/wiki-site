How many people do we need in the Directory?

In order to get a true representation of the skills distribution in the TI economy my guess is we need 384 Targeted Individuals listed in the Directory. That will give us rough idea about what skills 2.5 million of us have (5 million population size at 50% participation rate). Though the 15 sample size is a good start.

The math:
- ±5% margin of error / 95% confidence: 384 profiles
- ±3% MoE / 97%: 1,067 profiles
- ±2% MoE / 98%: 2,401 profiles
- ±1% MoE / 99%: 9,604 profiles

Now that I have announced this number. Perps may flood the space in an attempt to mess up the data. And so, I am adding to the goal that it be 384 verified Directory profiles.
