The Town Halls, held every Saturday, are streamed live on Twitch. However, some people mentioned issues logging in/creating an account on Twitch. Not surprisingly, it is Specterati interference.

When I do the Town Halls, it is only audio. As I face the camera away from me, I am only using Twitch, so there would be a way to do a livestream without a login.

However, without a login, you cannot interact with me directly, i.e., live chat. And with street theatre constantly around me, I am concerned about inadvertently triggering another TI. And so Twitch has outgrown its use. I have created an Android app, called Chyme, that has one feature – social audio to host the Town Halls. There will be no iOS app, as that requires Apple App Store approval, and it would be blocked by the Specerati. With Android, you can distribute apps outside of the Google Play Store. Hence, no need for Specerati approval.

However, others have told me some companies provide a free phone and free monthly recurring data/voice plans.
