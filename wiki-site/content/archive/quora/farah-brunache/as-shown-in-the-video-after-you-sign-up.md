As shown in the video after you sign up please go to your Profile and update your gender. The only matching criteria at this point is gender.

Choose woman and you will be matched only with women. Choose men and you will only be matched with men. Choose non-binary you will only be matched with non-binary. And choose prefer not to say, you will be matched with men and women, the equivalent of no preference.
