It is Saturday, March 7, 2026, and the Weekly State of the TI Skills Economy. An upgrade is underway for the app. Here will be most of the features:
- Chyme 🎙️: social audio
- LightHouse 🏘️: housing
- TrustTransport 📦🚕: people & package transport/delivery
- Directory 📇: people & skills directory
- Foundation 🪛: find tradespeople
- Peer Programming 🏘️: weekly global masterminds
- Gross Domestic Product 🗺️: quantify the progress of the TI Skills Economy
- Service Credits ⚙️: psyop-free utility tokens
- Workforce 💼: view skills distribution and fill in the gaps
- GentlePulse 💚: meditation
- Mood 😁: anonymous mood check-ins to improve the app
- SocketRelay 🔂: mutual aid

At this time the features are implemented but I am testing and making design changes to make the app as usable as possible. The app will be launched via web browser. And after that is working well, the Android app will be released. As mentioned previously there will be no iOS app as it would be interfered with by the Speceterati.
