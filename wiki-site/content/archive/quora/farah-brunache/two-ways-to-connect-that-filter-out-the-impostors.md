Two ways to connect that filter out the impostors

Quora and open forums are full of perps (perpetrators of organized harassment) posing as TIs (targeted individuals). This app is built to filter them out so real people can make authentic connections and live a meaningful life on their own terms. Every feature serves that goal. Here are two.

The app lets you make voice calls — one kind public, one kind private.

Chyme — [https://app.ChargingTheFuture.com/apps/chyme](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=fcd485&url=https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/chyme)

• Group voice call

• Public: anyone can listen; you must be signed in to speak

Foundation — [https://app.ChargingTheFuture.com/apps/foundation](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=d865f6&url=https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/foundation)

• Private 1:1 call

• You can reach anyone who has 1:1 calling turned on

• The person receiving the call sets a rate — 0 or higher, in ServiceCredits — for every 10 minutes of the call

(ServiceCredits are the app’s internal credits unit. They are not money and can’t be cashed out. You earn them by giving real value to a survivor.)

Why these two options?

• Group calls expose trolls. It’s harder for a perp to hide or troll at length when a group is listening.

• 1:1 calls raise the barrier. To reach a survivor one-on-one, a perp has to send ServiceCredits at the rate that survivor sets. Since credits are only earned by first giving real value to a survivor, that’s a genuine cost — and the survivor receives credits for their time regardless.
