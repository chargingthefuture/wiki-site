The Android app is becoming a Chyme app. The rest lives on the web.

I build this whole platform on my own. Every screen you see on the website also has to be rebuilt, as a separate Android app in a different language. That means every fix and every new feature has to be done twice. For one person (even with AI assistance), that is slow, and it pulls time away from the work that actually helps you.

So I am changing how Android works.

The website already runs on your phone — it is the same app, sized for a phone screen. From now on you can add it to your home screen like a normal app: it opens in its own window, with its own icon, no browser bar, and it can send you notifications. That covers everything: the Commons, the Directory, Foundation, housing, transport, all of it.

The one thing a phone does better as a real installed app is live audio. So the Android app you download is becoming a Chyme app — live audio rooms — and nothing else. If you want Chyme as its own app, install the Android APK from the releases page. Chyme also still works in the browser, so the download is optional.

Nothing is being deleted. If it makes sense later to bring more of the app back to Android, the work is still there and I can turn it back on. For now, this lets me move faster on what matters: getting real help to real people.

What to do: open the site on your phone, add it to your home screen, and you have the full app. Grab the Chyme APK from releases if you want live audio as its own app.
