As stated I used ChatGPT to suggest some names. Which by the way, says gang stalking does not exist. Come to find out the name is not that original.

While reading book one of the newly formed book club: [Book 1: Religion and the Rise of Capitalism](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=23ca22&url=https://chargingthefuture.discourse.group/t/book-1-religion-and-the-rise-of-capitalism/125?u%3Dfarah) . Friedman mentions Joseph Addison writing The Spectator which discussed the following volunteer private societies:

Starting in the 1690s, private Societies for the Reformation of Manners emerged, in London and other towns, to enforce moral discipline by informing on their neighbors’ misdeeds: sexual misbehavior, drunkenness, gambling, profanity, even merely failing to observe the Sabbath. These voluntary groups had limited success, however, and within a few decades the effort collapsed!" In the meanwhile, popular essayists like Joseph Addison, writing in The Spectator, repeatedly emphasized the same theme.

Why he says the “effort collapsed” rather morphed over the centuries is interesting. I do recognize that the society he mentions did not specifically focus on slander. However, the last one “failing to observe Sabbath,” leads me to believe that is not the case. Noting I do not stand behind the secret society gossip mill regardless.

I was going to go with the predator variations. Maybe I should use Specterati after all? Thoughts?
