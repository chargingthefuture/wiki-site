It is day two and we now have four people as part of the first ever TI economy app. I am honored to have you all join me!

At present there are four services:

SupportMatch - pair monthly with another TI for support

SocketRelay - request what you need. For example, I needed a jacket and a TI mailed me one

SleepStories - psyop-free mediation app

Lighthouse - find affordable housing with TI landlords or TI roommates

You do not have to participate in all services. But if you want to you will need to go to each one and complete the 10-second profile for each one. Each service is different and requires different information. Hence individual profiles. This also helps to limit exposing all your details to a possible perp or scammer.
