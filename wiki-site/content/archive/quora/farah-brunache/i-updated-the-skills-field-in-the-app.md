I updated the skills field in the app. It now has a variety of more options. This is part of the Directory feature. Where you create a simple profile.

First, a dedicated space for TIs to stay in contact regardless of what online platforms we are on. Second, find TIs with skills you are in need of. Like a mechanic, plumber, yoga instructor, etc. As close to utopia as we can get - psyop-free interactions.

You can choose up to 3 skills for your Directory profile.
![](images/qimg-884e80b5334d57a27cca923ff05cb972.png)

Once your profile is complete, you will have access to a list of everyone in the app that opted in to stay in touch:
![](images/qimg-0bbf55e797dfc94a34730772779e433b.png)

And if you choose to make your profile public, it will be here:
