Help plan a survivor-built community

[Andrea R](https://www.quora.com/profile/Andrea-R-453) I'm bringing together a group of survivors to plan, build, and run a gated community here in the US — and to write a runbook that any survivor group, anywhere, can follow to build their own.

The idea in one line: our platform already maps what each member can do —legal, construction, finance, health, food, security, and more. That map becomes the work plan.

People are grouped into teams by their real skills, each team plans its own part, and together we take it from land search to move-in. You don't need to know everything. You bring one skill; the group covers the rest.
