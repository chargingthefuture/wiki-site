Shortly after formation of this page my Quora account was deleted and with it several connections via the posts I created.

Thus I created a TI Skills Economy (TSE) app hosted at [CTF Survivor Hub](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=73022d&url=https://app.ChargingTheFuture.com). And I use this Quora page to keep the community updated.

I encourage every TI to sign up as a way for us all to keep in touch. I actively filter out perps and try to make connections so no TI is ever going it alone regardless of where they are in the world.
