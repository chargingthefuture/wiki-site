[The 12 Services of the TI Skills Economy](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=2792b7&url=https://github.com/chargingthefuture/mono/wiki/The-12-Services-of-the-TI-Skills-Economy)

The TI Skills Economy is designed to coexist with the lives of all TIs and to systematically replace the Specterati in our daily needs. For example, if you need a mechanic or a ride, log in and see if a TI in your area can provide that service. We’re in the early days, but the long-term goal is to connect an estimated 5 million TIs worldwide — roughly the size of a self-sufficient nation. Restoring autonomy to TIs is possible.
