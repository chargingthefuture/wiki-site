SocketRelay Update

In the forthcoming update of SocketRelay, Service Credits will be available! It is a Specterati tamper-proof utility token available only within the TI Skills Economy. Usage of Service Credits is not required to be part of the TI Skills Economy. However, for the TIs unemployed, you now have the opportunity to engage and receive services and goods with dignity and respect. Add value within TSE and earn Service Credits, and use Service Credits for goods and services.
