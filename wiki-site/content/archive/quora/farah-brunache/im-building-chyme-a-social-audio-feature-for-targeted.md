I’m building Chyme, a social audio feature for Targeted Individuals. It lets two or more people talk over a live audio call inside the app. I’ve gotten it most of the way there, but there’s one thing I can’t do on my own: test real calls. For that I need a second person on the other end.

So I’m looking for one volunteer to test it with me. Here’s what it involves:

• You make an account. It takes about two minutes.

• You open the Chyme app inside the product.

• We pick a few times when we’re both free and get on a call to see what works and what doesn’t.

During a test, please stick to the Chyme feature. The rest of the app works and you’re welcome to keep using it, but it isn’t fully ready for use yet, so Chyme is the part I need eyes on.

I’ll pay a flat rate for your time. This isn’t an hourly job. The way it goes: we test, I write down what’s broken, then I go fix it. I don’t know ahead of time how long the fixes take or how many rounds we’ll need, because I don’t yet know how many bugs are in there. So instead of paying by the hour, I pay one flat amount to test it with me over time until it’s ready for public use.

The time you’d actually spend is small — short sessions, spread out as I work through the fixes. We’ll agree on the rate up front.

The project is open source. The code lives at [GitHub - chargingthefuture/chargingthefuture: world's first psyop-free economy](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=ce85c0&url=https://github.com/chargingthefuture/chargingthefuture).

If you’d like to help, leave a comment or send me a message and we’ll set a time to speak further.
