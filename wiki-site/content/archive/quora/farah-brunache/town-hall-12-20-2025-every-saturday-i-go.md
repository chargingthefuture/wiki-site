Town Hall 12.20.2025

Every Saturday, I go live to do an interactive session where TIs can ask questions or listen to updates of the week about the TI Skills Economy.

This Saturday, today will be the last time I go live on Twitch under the handle “the1690s ([https://Twitch.tv/the1690s).”](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=84d16b&url=https://Twitch.tv/the1690s).%E2%80%9D) I will delete that account. I am not sure if Twitch recycles handles, and so moving forward, it is not the TI Skills Economy account or mine.

I was planning to use Chyme (

[https://chargingthefuture.discou...](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=ae69b6&url=https://chargingthefuture.discourse.group/t/social-audio-for-tis-talk-live-with-tis-around-the-world/206)

As mentioned in last week’s Town Hall, the weekly updates will focus less on the app and more on the TI Skills Economy itself.

This week, I focused on recruiting more TIs in our “workforce.” As of today, we have 23. And will continue adding more profiles to the Directory. Use the Directory ([psyop-free - Live, Work, Prevail](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=17eb31&url=https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory/public)) to browse for skills. For example, if you are looking for a hairdresser or a mechanic. And use [psyop-free - Live, Work, Prevail](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=f2bc51&url=https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/workforce-recruiter) to find where there are gaps in the TI economy. For example, you are being mobbed at work by the Speceterati, and you would like to either level up or offer your talents to other TIs. Workforce Recruiter enables us to create and sustain a circular economy.
