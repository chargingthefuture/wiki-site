This question makes no sense. Gang stalkers are "people," and outside of gang stalking, they experience typical human events. Gang stalking does NOT equal stalking; stalking is just a part of gang stalking. This question is like asking if a gang stalker ever had something stolen from them. Just because TIs have their things stolen during gang stalking doesn’t mean being a TI is the same as being a gang stalker. What is your real question? And why are you afraid to ask it directly?
