Use Workforce Recruiter to identify skills present within the global TI community and recognize gaps that need to be filled. This platform will help you level up your skills, connect with other TIs who share your expertise, and discover potential talent for hire or peer-to-peer exchanges.

To enhance the app's usefulness and strengthen our “workforce,” I am inviting TIs to meetups to discuss occupations they are interested in developing or engaging with.

You can find the list of meetups within the app. Below is a screenshot showing how you can RSVP for upcoming events. Once there are enough sign-ups, I will coordinate the meetups.

Additionally, this is a great opportunity to earn a “verified” badge within the TI Skills Economy. Each profile in the app displays either a Verified or Unverified badge. This allows TIs to quickly assess whether or not to transact with another individual on the platform. Verification indicates that the community believes the individual to be a genuine TI.
