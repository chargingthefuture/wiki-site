Overview of the Workforce Recruiter Tracker App

Since starting TI Skills Network, I have been thinking through how to coordinate as efficiently as possible. Workforce Recruiter, the new mini-app (found at [https://app.chargingthefuture.com](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=49216b&url=https://app.chargingthefuture.com/)), aims to monitor our skills network through the means of job recruitment and distribution within the TI community of 5 million people globally. It will help assess how healthy the TI economy is by keeping track of job roles, skill levels, and training options each year.

The United Nations stated at one point that 2 million people were identified as Targeted Individuals (TIs). However, the actual number could be as high as 4 million worldwide, as many related crimes go unreported at a rate of 50%. I have rounded up to 5 million, equivalent to a nation state.

A Focus on Countries with Similar Populations

There are several countries with populations of around 5 million, like Singapore, Finland, and Norway. Here are some examples of what makes these nations economically strong:
- Singapore: Offers a mix of financial services and technology.
- New Zealand: Focuses on agriculture and tourism.
- Norway: Benefits from natural resources.
- Finland: Invests in innovation and technology.

The TI Skills Economy is not intended to become a nation-state. Rather, enables us to live fulfilling lives as we are mobbed out of society, regardless of where we are in the world. These insights will help build a framework for the TI skills economy, focusing on key sectors, jobs, and skills that promote sustainable livelihoods.

Main Goal of the TI Skills Economy

The main goal is to develop a sustainable skills economy that helps victims of human trafficking (TIs) regain control over their lives. The Workforce mini-app will assess where the needed skills are located and plan how to share these skills globally to increase economic participation.

For example, on Quora, a TI stated they are 19 years old and unclear how to they can create a career for themselves. Here is our opportunity.
