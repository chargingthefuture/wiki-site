Studies suggest that about 40% to 60% of crimes are not reported. This means that if there are 1 million crime reports, the number of unreported crimes could range from 1.5 million to 2.5 million.

Conservatively speaking this could mean that at a rate of 50% of non-reports. There could be at least 2 million TIs.
