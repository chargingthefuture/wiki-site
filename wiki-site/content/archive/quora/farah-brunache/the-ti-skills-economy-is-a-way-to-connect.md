The TI Skills Economy is a way to connect the estimated 5 million TIs globally so they can get access to the resources they need and want. As we are mobbed out of communities and access.

I built an app to facilitate this: [https://app.chargingthefuture.com](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=959443&url=https://app.chargingthefuture.com). But this is just the beginning.

There are a few services in the app, like LightHouse, to find housing, and TrustTransport to receive reliable transportation. I consider these some of the foundational services needed in an economy.

Each week, on Town Hall Saturday, I post a screenshot of the app performance. This is mainly an internal tool for me to see if I am building something of value. Example:

[https://chargingthefuture.discou...](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=75dd04&url=https://chargingthefuture.discourse.group/t/weekly-state-of-the-ti-economy-town-halls/136/10?u%3Dfarah)

The above is checking on the health of the apps I built. Workforce Recruiter, however, is checking on the actual health of the TI economy itself. And I have made that tool directly accessible within the app. Please use it to discover where we are lacking and see if you can fill in the gaps.

I have said it many times, and I will say it again. There are at least 5 million TIs. While spread out globally. This is the size of a small nation-state. And nation-states of 5 million are thriving. Some are primarily service-based economies. It is unrealistic to think enough TIs will cohabitate with each other. However, at a minimum, we can exchange as many services and skills with each other remotely.

Give Workforce Recruiter a try and provide me with feedback to improve it. You only need two simple things to sign up for the app. An email and provide me with your Quora handle or Quora Profile URL.
