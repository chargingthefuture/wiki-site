Foundation has a new feature. Demo below.

Why Foundation exists. Find a TI on Quora who has a skill you need or want, but it is unclear if they are available to trade/barter/connect.

Foundation allows a TI to explicitly state which skill they will offer to another TI on a vetted platform. You can even do paid calls. For example, go to my profile and you can call and speak to me for 2 ServiceCredits per minute! Or message me for free. No sharing emails or phone numbers.

This app is at no cost. Offered by me, me paying it forward to all TIs, any TI in the world.
