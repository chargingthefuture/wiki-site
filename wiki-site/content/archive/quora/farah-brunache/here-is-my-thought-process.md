Here is my thought process.
- Crime is under-reported by 50%
- Supposedly, 2 million cumulative self-reports received by the United Nations
- Rounded, there are at least 5 million Targeted Individuals globally
- 5 million is the size of a nation-state

I do not propose that TIs form a nation-state. I do, however, suggest that we exchange goods and services in a peer-to-peer global marketplace, which I call the TI Skills Economy.

The TI Skills Economy is operated via the app: [psyop-free - Live, Work, Prevail](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=959443&url=https://app.chargingthefuture.com) and works on mobile or desktop.

The app has 12 mini-apps to help facilitate the TI Skills Economy. One is Workforce Recruiter, which lets you know based on self-reports where TIs are and what their skills are. Use it to trade or level up and start a new career.
