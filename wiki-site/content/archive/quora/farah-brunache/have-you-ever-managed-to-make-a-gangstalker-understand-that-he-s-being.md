Gang stalkers, the colloquial name for group vigilantes, are in the business of creating lies about you. And they work for (with the majority being paid from) organized crime to slander the Target, you. Therefore, they are 100% aware they are being told lies; they either enjoy or profit from your exploitation.

There might be individuals who proxy-stalk you, or someone who gossips about you that is not part of the human trafficking ring; however, those people would not be labeled as gang stalkers. For example, Jessica White mentions slandering Targets to neighbors, and more, unless those people, for example, join in on street theater or start getting paid to harass you, I would not consider them to be gang stalkers.
