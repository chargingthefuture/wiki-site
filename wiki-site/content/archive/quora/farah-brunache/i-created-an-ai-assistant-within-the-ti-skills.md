I created an AI assistant within the TI Skills Economy (TSE). The first of its kind, trained on our data and available for use now. Below is a demo of how to use it.

Why do we need an AI assistant? Go to any AI chatbot and ask questions about the Speceterati, and you will receive a response that they do not exist. Effectively saying that human trafficking is not real.

I am building this AI assistant from the ground up. I am paying for the infrastructure to host an LLM ([Large Language Model (LLM) - GeeksforGeeks](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=8e83a5&url=https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/artificial-intelligence/large-language-model-llm/)), and using the data from TSE to train it and provide responses.

For example, ask, “I need urgent housing, what options do I have?” And you will get a real response on what options are available within the global community. Specifically pointing you to LightHouse (the Airbnb for TIs). Another example, “Gang stalkers are constantly tampering with my car. What suggestions do you have?” You will get advice curated from several survivors on how they cope. Ask any of these types of questions in any chatbot for consumers today, and you will get ridiculous responses like “call the police,” or “you have schizophrenia,” or “gang stalking (aka human trafficking) isn’t real.”

TSE is here to provide real solutions to the real modern-day problems of survivors.
