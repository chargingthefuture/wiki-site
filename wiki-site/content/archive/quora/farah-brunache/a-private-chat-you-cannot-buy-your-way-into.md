A private chat you cannot buy your way into

The TI Skills Economy app is adding a private channel. Here is how you get in: you cannot apply, you cannot pay, and you cannot talk your way in. You earn it by helping people — posting, answering other members’ posts, completing real exchanges of skills and help inside the app.

Why build it this way? Two reasons.

First, the app has one public community channel and no direct messages, on purpose. No DMs means perps and time-wasters have nowhere to corner anyone. Everything happens in the open, where spam is easy to spot and good behavior is easy to see. That stays.

Second, the private channel is for members with a track record. Not a score, not a title — a record of actually helping. Members who earn access also earn a badge on their community profile, so others can see who has put in real help.

This is the same thing you already do on Quora, but with an actionable outcome. On Quora, one TI helped me get transportation out of a perp setup. Another TI helped me get a job — and mailed me a coat so I would not freeze living outside in winter. It took me two years on Quora to find real TIs who help.

The point of this app is to cut two years down to minutes.

The private channel is not open yet. But access will be based on what you do in the app, and that counts starting now. If you are a TI who wants to give help and get help, join, post, and answer someone.
