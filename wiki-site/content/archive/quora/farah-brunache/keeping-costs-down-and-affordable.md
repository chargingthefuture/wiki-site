Keeping costs down and affordable.

There are no email notifications in the app(s). For one I want to avoid any gstalker scamming by faking email communications.

Second, integrating email notifications would dramatically increase the cost to host and maintain the app(s). And I want to keep these apps affordable.

But this means that when you get a match, in Support Match app, you will not know. You would need to login every day and check.

I am a nerd so I am constantly logged in, looking at stats and thinking of ways to improve the app(s). As an option, if you would like to get a ping when you get a new match I can send you a boiler plate message via Signal. If you would like this option go to my Quora profile, click the “Farah | SleekBio” link and click the Support Match Signal group join link.
