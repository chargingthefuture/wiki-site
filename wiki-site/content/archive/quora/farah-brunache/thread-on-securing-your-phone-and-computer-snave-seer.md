Keep in mind the difference between a threat and a vulnerability.

A threat is the potential danger that can cause harm (e.g., hackers, natural disasters).

A Vulnerability is weakness in a system that can be exploited (e.g., software bugs, misconfigurations).

A vulnerability becomes a risk when a threat exists. If a software has a bug (vulnerability) and a hacker knows about it (threat), the risk of attack increases.

This thread will discuss mitigating vulnerabilities and does not imply gang stalkers will exploit it or have (a threat).
