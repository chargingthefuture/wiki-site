If you would like to participate in the discussion of economics and how it is implemented in the TSE app, here is a document to give you insight into my logic. Review of this document or participation at this level of discussion is not mandatory to be part of TSE.

What is notable about this post is that, rather than using Singapore, I will use Finland as the baseline.

I propose the baseline of Finland (national) and two high-quality city/regional datasets (Greater Helsinki and Tallinn metro areas) as the primary model. This combination more closely aligns with creating a baseline for a hybrid (virtual and in-person) circular economy of human trafficking victims: some gated communities of survivors globally. And some that interact in a dispersed manner throughout the globe.
