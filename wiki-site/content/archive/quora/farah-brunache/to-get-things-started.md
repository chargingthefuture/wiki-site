To get things started. List your top three skills or services you can provide. This way other TIs can know if they should contact you for a service or good. State if a good or service requires payment in advance. Be transparent!

Note that comments are not searchable on Quora. As this thread fills up it will be harder for others to discover. Ask to be a contributor if you would like to make a post rather than comment with your skills. Making your post keyword searchable on Quora. I only want contributors to be TIs. That is why you need to ask and I’ll add you right away.

Lastly, transact where you feel comfortable. The intention of this group is to efficiently find something you need without having to spend hours reading posts in other groups to figure out which TIs want to connect off platform.

Example:
- Mechanic (paid, parts and labor)
- Loan garden tools (free with refundable deposit)
- I sell home grown tomatoes (free or pay what you can)
