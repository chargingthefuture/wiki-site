This is fine. But you do not supplement with exercise (i.e. walking) or stretching? Those will make a difference. For example, at one point I was loosing a lot of mobility. 10 years doctors could not figure it out. Then I came across a statistic, if my memory is right, that 70% of Americans do not drink enough water. I began drinking 1 gallon of water a day and all my mobility came back. That and bringing up my vitamin D. 10 years and no doctor was able to figure that out. I humbly suggest natural avenues.
