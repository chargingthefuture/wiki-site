So you become your own worst enemy. “Making you feel crazy or defective” is a kill strategy and so if that does not work they try other methods, opting for the one that leaves the least amount of evidence. They continue to gang stalk you.

They have tried an abduction and multiple attempts to kill me. First find where you live and try to stage a suicide, if that does not work, staged car accidents (either chasing you and stopping short, sandwiching you between multiple of their cars or a barrier or throwing very large objects on the road to cause you to spin out or other cars to spin out onto you), I had a gun pulled on me twice (to my knowledge) one was real the other could have only been street theater.

Ultimately, they will continue to gang stalk you if they haven’t killed you. Also they will start staging physical altercations. They will get gang stalkers that are from your same ethnic group and have them provoke you into a physical fight (to make it appear as not a target). Yes it is about getting you in jail. As jail provides a better environment to torture you or fake your suicide. And would make it appear that “they were right all along.” When that did not work they caused my car to break down leaving me stranded to where a police officer gave me a ride home, a gang stalker, and they framed it as I was arrested, as they could not get me to do anything illegal.

What you think of yourself is not relevant to them, beyond how they can manipulate your feelings into an orchestrated slow death. And so they may know, that you know, you are not crazy. But that brings them just as much pleasure and doesn’t stop gang stalking. They are psychopaths, sociopaths and narcissists.

Rather than consume yourself with thinking that it will end if they know you know. It is better to accept that they are clinically insane and there is nothing you could possibly do to get them to stop or have empathy for you or anyone else.
