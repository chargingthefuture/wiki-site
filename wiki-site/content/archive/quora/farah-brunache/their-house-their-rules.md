The Specterati are successful in torturing us because we rely on their infrastructure. So stop relying on it!

When I ask a TI to join the TI Skills Economy, it can be as simple as following this Quora space and exchanging skills with a TI you discover.

However, if we want to rid our lives of the Specterati, joining the TI Skills Economy is about signing up for the app: [https://app.chargingthefuture.com](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=959443&url=https://app.chargingthefuture.com).

The app is a facilitator. It manages the economy and provides updates on its progress. No one person, including me, leads the economy. It is a circular economy where autonomous beings, TIs, log in and can match with other TIs for goods, services, mutual aid, and support — without a psyop.

The reason there is an app as the facilitator is that there are at least 5 million TIs globally. Having a centralized tool to connect us makes the economy sustainable and filters out perps to reduce harm.
