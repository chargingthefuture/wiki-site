I invited someone to this new space I created and they interpreted it as me charging TIs for information and trying to profit off our plight. This is NOT true.

If anyone has questions on my intentions with the space please ask. Let’s not jump to conclusions. Being a TI is hard enough. Anyone can comment on posts with questions.

The space is FREE to join and read. This is the first Quora Space I created and fairly new to Quora too. If it shows on your end you need to pay let me know so I can fix it.

The purpose of this Space is so no TI is going it alone. Many of us maybe the “only” TI in town. And so if you need a mechanic that won’t rip you off, where can you go?

There are several TI spaces. They are all valuable. But it is hard to connect directly with a person that has “X” skill or will extend themselves to help you in an actionable way. The purpose of this space is less about stories or advice about gang stalking. Again the other pages do this well. This space is to address the tangible needs of life with minimal exposure of getting played by a perp.

Again the space is free. Anyone can comment. Contributors will only be TIs. If one ends up being a troll or perp they’ll be promptly removed as a contributor.

No one has to charge for a good or service. However, it is fair to also allow TIs to receive money for fixing your car, for example. You can barter, exchange for payment or offer for free. All are equal here. Just everyone be upfront.

I am on the brink of starving off because of gstalking. Of all the spaces on Quora what TI could I reach out to? There’s no telling if one will respond at all, think you’re a perp, be hostile, or if they are okay being asked for help.

This page is to cut through that bullshit. Remove the second guessing. If a TI makes a post with what they have or are part of this group; you know there is a higher probability of them helping you. Or at the very least responding to you with respect. And won’t jump down your throat.

We are all TIs. If you’re like me you’re under attack 24/7. But even so, I will pick up a call or message another TI. Not all TIs can or will do that, and that’s okay. With this space you’ll know which TIs are willing to help or speak to you regardless.
