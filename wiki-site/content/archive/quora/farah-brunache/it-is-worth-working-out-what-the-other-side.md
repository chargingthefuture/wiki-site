It is worth working out what the other side actually needs, as opposed to what they say or what it feels like from the inside. It comes to two things. They are not the same thing, and neither one works without the other.

The first requirement is your income

In December 1961 Martin Luther King spoke to the AFL-CIO convention in Bal Harbour, Florida:

“The labor movement was the principal force that transformed misery and despair into hope and progress. Out of its bold struggles, economic and social reform gave birth to unemployment insurance, old-age pensions, government relief for the destitute and, above all, new wage levels that meant not mere survival but a tolerable life. The captains of industry did not lead this transformation; they resisted it until they were overcome.”

Read what he is actually claiming. Not that labor asked for those things and was granted them. That the transformation came out of struggle, that the people holding the money resisted it, and that it happened anyway because the people who needed it organized around the one thing they controlled — their own work.

That mechanism runs in both directions. If organizing labor is what moved people from misery to a tolerable life, then disorganizing someone’s labor is how you move them back.

Which is the first thing that has to be done to a Target, and it is done before anything dramatic. Mob them out of jobs. Keep them in work that pays too little to accumulate anything. Cycle them through positions that end every few months for reasons nobody will put in writing. A person with no income has no lawyer, no ability to move, no time to pursue anything, and no standing when they describe what is happening. Everything else gets easier once that is true, and a good deal of it stops being necessary at all.

The second requirement is that you know about it

A perp emailed me, unprompted, to tell me three things: that they gossip about me, that they enjoy doing it, and that they agree with what is being done to me.

Sit with how strange that is as an action. The gossip, if it was happening, was already happening. It needed nothing from me. Telling me about it did not increase it, did not extend its reach, did not add one listener. The email changed exactly one thing in the world: it moved the information from their side to mine.

So my knowing was the point. Not the gossip — the knowing. The email was not a side effect of anything. It was a delivery.

There is an internet version of an old question: if you post something and nobody likes it, did it happen. The older version is a tree falling in an empty forest. Both are asking whether an event with no witness is an event.

Here the answer is no, and the people doing this understand it perfectly. An insult nobody hears is not an insult. A rumor that never reaches the person is a conversation. The injury in being talked about lives entirely in the knowing, which is why the knowing has to be arranged, and why it gets arranged on purpose by people who will then tell you they were only talking.

The clearest evidence is what happens when the delivery stops working. Close the channel — stop reading, stop answering, stop being in the rooms where the talk is — and the method changes. It moves toward things that cannot be ignored (running me over with a car, punching me in the face, pushing me, etc), because the previous thing had stopped arriving. Nobody escalates a tactic that is working. Escalation is a report that the last one failed, and what failed was the delivery.

Why it takes both

Neither requirement is enough on its own.

Someone with no money and no awareness is not being got at. They are just poor, and poverty by itself does not produce the state this is aiming for.

Someone aware and solvent has options. They move. They hire somebody. They wait it out. They build something else and stop caring what is said.

The combination is the objective. No money, and constant notification. One removes the exit, the other makes sure you are staring at the door. That is what winning means here — not any single incident, but the condition of being unable to leave and unable to stop noticing.

Both requirements fail the same way

And the failure is not legal. It is not an investigation, a lawsuit, or an exposure. Both requirements run through other people, and that is the whole vulnerability.

The first one fails if your income does not come from anyone who can be reached. That is the entire premise of an economy among members: work, housing, rides, hiring, trade, moving among people who are in it. An employer who is a member is not a lever. A landlord who is a member is not a lever. A ride from a member is not a favor that somebody else can arrange to have withdrawn. This is not a theory about what ought to work — it is the same reasoning King was describing to that convention. People who needed something organized around their own labor instead of petitioning the people withholding it, and they were resisted, and it worked anyway.

The second one fails if the messages have nowhere to land. This part arrives as a byproduct rather than a feature, which is why nobody plans for it. Spend your time trading with people who are not doing this and the scripted conversation has no one to carry it. The insult needs a mutual acquaintance. The rumor needs a shared room. Change which rooms you are in and most of it stops reaching you — not because you developed a thicker skin, but because the delivery route was always other people, and you changed which people.

The friendships are not the goal. They are what happens after two years of hiring, renting, driving, and trading with people who are not in it.

What this is not

It is not a claim that the other side stops. It is a claim about what they need in order to succeed, and both things they need have to travel through your employer, your landlord, and your acquaintances.

An arrangement with that dependency can be beaten by changing your employer, your landlord, and your acquaintances. That is slow and unglamorous and it will not feel like justice. It is also entirely within your reach, which is more than can be said for waiting on an institution to move first.

Work, housing, rides, hiring, trade. Invite-only. It costs nothing to join, and you can use one part of it and ignore the rest.

EXIT THEIR ECONOMY, EXIT THE PSYOP
