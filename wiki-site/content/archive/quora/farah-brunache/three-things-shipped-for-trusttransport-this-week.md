Three things shipped for TrustTransport this week.

Providers can now log proof during a trip—a short reference for a photo, a confirmation code, or a note—and the app timestamps it automatically. When the trip ends, ServiceCredits settle automatically from requester to provider; you don't have to wait or ask. A new earnings surface shows what you've made and lets you request a payout whenever you want.

A few other fixes too: admin back buttons work the same way on desktop and mobile, the contributions page is now clickable, signed-out community posts show each author's color instead of generic purple, and the comic engine shows honest draft-failure reasons instead of misleading status badges.

The code lives at [GitHub - chargingthefuture/chargingthefuture: world's first psyop-free economy](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=ce85c0&url=https://github.com/chargingthefuture/chargingthefuture) if you want to see how it works or contribute.
