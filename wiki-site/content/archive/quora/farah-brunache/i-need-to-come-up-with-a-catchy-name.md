I need to come up with a catchy name. I am open to suggestions. I want to put together groups of people who share a resource.

For example, a group of TIs that share a gym membership. So we can shower. A group of TIs that are on a family plan for mobile service - so we can have a cheap rate. A group of TIs that share a mileage program, so we can travel at a discount.

It is about pooling our resources so the cost can be spread amongst us all as employment is difficult.

I asked ChatGPT and got these suggestions on naming this app. Do you like any of them? I would create the app, and people would sign up for the respective service. And I could easily manage membership across all the memberships.
- ShareSphere - A community-centric name that emphasizes sharing and connectedness.
- PoolTogether - Highlights the idea of coming together to pool resources effectively.
- ResourceBridge - Conveys the concept of bridging gaps through shared resources.
- GroupUp - A simple, inviting name that encourages forming groups for mutual benefit.
- CollectiveCost - Directly communicates the essence of collective sharing and cost savings.
- CrispCoop - A modern twist on “coop,” suggesting clarity and freshness in resource sharing.
- BuddyBundle - Suggests a friendly approach to bundling resources together.
- HuddleShare - A warm, community-focused name indicating gathering for sharing resources.
- CostCoop - A play on "co-op" to imply cooperative efforts in managing costs.
- SharedHaven - Implies safety and community in sharing resources.
