The Mail Mirage

One of the Specerati’s common tactics is to never deliver, delay, lose, tamper, open or damage a Target’s mail. For example, they “attempt” to deliver your mail and packages, specifically when you are not there. So they can say they missed delivery or the business is closed. And will do so on a Friday if it is a business or Saturday if it is a residence, so you cannot get the package until maybe next week, if they decide to deliver.

Today, I randomly caught on camera the post office worker as I was returning to the building. I thought it was odd when they got nervous when they saw me. Now, every Specerati does not want to get caught, and so if there is a camera their way, they panic. Not in the same way as when a person is being filmed without permission. For will call this being Caught Red-Faced. A play on “caught red-handed,” emphasizing both guilt and embarrassment when a member of the Specterati panics when caught committing a crime.

Well, turns out they stated that the package was undelivered because the business was closed. But there was someone at the front desk to deliver the mail to. In fact, they delivered everyone else’s mail in the building but mine.
