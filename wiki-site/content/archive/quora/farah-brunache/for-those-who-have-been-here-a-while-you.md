For those who have been here a while, you are aware that originally, I used Quora for *all* content. Then my account got deleted, after initially announcing the dedicated TI app, and I had to start over on Quora.

Because of that experience, I created a Discourse community, formerly at [http://chargingthefuture.discour...](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=0260c2&url=http://chargingthefuture.discourse.group) — but it required a fee, which is difficult to meet as a Target.

Therefore, I moved the blog (and Quora and Discourse backups) to GitHub Wiki ([Home](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=7f8974&url=https://github.com/chargingthefuture/chargingthefuture/wiki)). But I imagine it is difficult to navigate if you are not used to GitHub. And so I am announcing today that I have created a more user-friendly version:

[https://chargingthefuture.github...](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=75b28a&url=https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/)

This blog is *identical to the GitHub Wiki ([Home](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=7f8974&url=https://github.com/chargingthefuture/chargingthefuture/wiki))*, it simply has a better-looking interface. I will update the landing page: [World's First Psyop-Free TI Economy](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=67ad7a&url=http://chargingthefuture.com) with the link to the user-friendly blog interface.

If you have any questions or feedback, let me know.
