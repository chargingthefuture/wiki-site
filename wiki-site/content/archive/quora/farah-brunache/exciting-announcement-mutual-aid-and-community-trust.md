Exciting Announcement: Mutual Aid and Community Trust!

I’m thrilled to share some wonderful news today: I am launching Mutual Aid and Community Trust! This initiative draws inspiration from the incredible support systems found in Amish communities, where members uplift one another through unique practices of loans and lending.

This opportunity is specifically for TIs who have signed up for the app. To ensure we build a strong and committed community, I will not be fielding intake via Quora. This approach helps us prevent any unnecessary disruptions and allows us to focus on those truly dedicated to participating in the TI economy.

For all of you who have eagerly embraced the TI economy by joining the app, I’m excited to connect with you further! Please feel free to reach out to me via Signal. Together, we can make a positive impact and advance our collective goals!
