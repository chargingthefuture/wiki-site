), this week I added a “Verified” badge to the [Directory](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=89857a&url=https://chargingthefuture.discourse.group/t/weekly-state-of-the-ti-economy-townhall-meetings/136/9) profiles.
- Verified = several verified TIs within the community believe said person is a TI
- No Verified badge = unknown at the moment

The length of time someone is in the app without a verified badge varies. It is based on how efficiently the community can verify that someone is a TI. The intention is that everyone must be verified. And the badge is a simple way for someone else on the platform to decide to interact with someone. At any point, if someone is discovered to be a perp (member of the Specterati), their account will be deleted immediately. There is zero tolerance for human trafficking.

To become verified, one would need to provide material value and/or meet other TIs in person to confirm.
