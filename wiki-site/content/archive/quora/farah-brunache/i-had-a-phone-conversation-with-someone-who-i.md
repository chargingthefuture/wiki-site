I had a phone conversation with someone who I believe is a TI. And they were crying on the phone call the entire time as they were speaking. I intentionally did not speak much to allow them to vent. And they interpreted it as me being a perp. Which is bizarre however, I would like to learn more about the toolset mental health professions use when speaking to people who are in extreme distress.

I do not want to go back to school per say. However, I saw the following certificate program:

Certificate in Counseling Skills

Harding University offers a Certificate in Counseling Skills. This certification is an 18-hour program designed to equip [leadership](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=31dd87&url=https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/career-development/what-is-leadership) employees with the necessary tools to work with and help those struggling with marital issues, addictions, parenting issues and other mental health difficulties. Most individuals who take this course work as church ministers, missionaries or family ministers.

What is mentioned above would be a good option. Anyone have additional suggestions of programs? My goal is to deliver better products for TIs. While having better language to use when communicating with TIs that may not be in the best emotional state.

Note nothing I provide is religious or spiritual based. And only looking to be better prepared when speaking to people in distress.
