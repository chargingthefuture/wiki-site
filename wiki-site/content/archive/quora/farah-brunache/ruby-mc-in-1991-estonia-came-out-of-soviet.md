In 1991 Estonia came out of Soviet occupation with 1.3 million people, no money, and infrastructure that did not work. It built a functioning modern country almost entirely in software, because software was what it could afford.

The Skills Economy runs on the same arithmetic. Five million members is the size the model is built for — smaller than Finland, larger than Estonia. Skills are the only asset it requires.

Where we actually are: 89 people, covering 156 of the roughly 650 skills an economy needs. About a quarter. Posts are sitting open waiting for help.
