I made a conscious decision not to put notifications in the app, part of implementing trauma-informed design.

This past week, I implemented 1:1 instant calls. Say you are a mental health expert. You can make yourself available to other TIs for 1:1 phone call consultations. No sharing your phone number or email address — it is all done within the app. This is the only feature in the app with notifications.

If someone tries to reach you and the app isn't open, your phone will notify you anyway. You won't miss a ring.

You can enable or disable Foundation calls any time. The default is that it is off. Here is a screenshot of my profile on Foundation where I accept instant calls.

Foundation is like Thumbtack/UpWork for TIs. Sign up: [https://app.chargingthefuture.com](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=959443&url=https://app.chargingthefuture.com).
