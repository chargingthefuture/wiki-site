Someone asked me how they can help. And I do need help so I am sharing here:

In terms of planning a gated community (even if you cannot live in one you can help plan, some planning efforts are paid) there are a couple of options of how you or anyone can help. In no particular order.
- Find people you think are TIs and add them here, ServiceCredits are given as a reward: [CTF Survivor Hub](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=98277d&url=https://app.ChargingTheFuture.com/apps/skillshunt). How this helps: finding skilled TIs mean that we know who can help with what. We all will need a plumber or mechanic etc. By keeping a list we easily find people instantly. Quora has fragmented information for us to access and a lot of it is perp content. And have a circular economy that is psyop free. To not depend on abusers.
- Provide input in PeerProgramming. [CTF Survivor Hub](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=57a81e&url=https://app.ChargingTheFuture.com/apps/PeerProgramming). These are weekly global masterminds where we combine all of our expertise and resources to help each other. This week, help me plan how to start a TI community. Location ideas, supplies to build homes, for those who want to live there- what do you want to offer? Be security, farming, transportation for survivors there, etc
- Help fulfill mutual aid requests. Some are paid gigs: [CTF Survivor Hub](https://www.quora.com/_/redirect?sig=7ce256&url=https://app.ChargingTheFuture.com/apps/SocketRelay).

If you’re not sure how you can help. Send a message in the group chat (TI Skills Economy (TSE), not signal) with what your skills are or what you can offer. And I will respond and make suggestions. You can also ask the AI Assistant by adding “@comic” to the message.

The AI assistant conversations are not open to the general public. And the AI is also not public. I maintain the code within TI Skills Economy (TSE) on cloud resources I pay for to ensure there are no trolling responses. Like denying the Specterati exist.
