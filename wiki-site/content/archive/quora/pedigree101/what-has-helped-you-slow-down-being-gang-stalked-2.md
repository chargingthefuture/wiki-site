Curious how you define “slowed down.”

They are selectively choosing how much to reveal their level of harassment to you. And how much harassment they give you. This is not something you have control over. No one, including me, can answer this.

After I made the below post, I experienced a new DEW. I made the post outside. I walked into a public library and sat down and I felt like I was being boiled from the inside out. Prior to that moment the only DEW I experienced was vibration. They have all tools available to harass you at any moment.

[Pedigree101's post in TI Skills Network](https://tiskillsnetwork.quora.com/My-life-got-better-because-of-meeting-other-TIs-The-below-statement-regarding-experiencing-DEW-and-street-stalking-in)

TIs are electronically surveilled 100% of the time. Emails, texts, phone calls, visually tracked, etc. No one is 0% street stalked.

Amount of DEWs and street stalking varies per Target. You control none of those 3 things. You have been doxxed to EVERY gang stalker in the world. Regardless if you see them actively doing something to you or not.

A possible better framing is to continue living your life and mitigate their threats with for example body cameras. It does not “slow down” gstalking. They will adapt and find different ways to harass. A substitute. That is why the advice of ignoring is important. Someone’s strategy to “slow down” may not work as the torture is customized to you.

Another example, at one point they were purposely trying to hit me as pedestrian with their car. Oddly, it was around the same time I started my pet rock collection. And being that they made me carless and unhoused, I had my pets with me at all times. Only occasionally do they now try to hit me with their cars.

Do your best to center yourself and not them. They will do what they plan to do regardless of what you do or feel.
