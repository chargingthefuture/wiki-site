This is why I am advocating for our own community or multiple TIs living together. Why? When one of us is out, there is at least one or more person home.

Second, it will be harder to set us up for crimes as we now have our own witnesses to what they are doing.

It is not going to be easy and we will need to pair off with people that we live well together. But if we’re in our own community, you can have your own residence and break ins and set ups will be harder as there will always be someone in the neighborhood doing look out.
