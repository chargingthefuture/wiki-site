Here is a suggestion based on your ask. I am an engineer so I am a natural problem solver.

It is always best to try to build or offer things that are truly different than what exists.

With low effort I could put together a feature where we are matched with another TI for a week. And they are our accountability and support partner for the week. Then the following week another person.

This solves two problems that people mentioned.
- Wanting a database of TIs so we can keep track of each other better. Not stalking, lol. But for safety. There might be some TIs who might have boundary issues, in which case this can be mitigated. Like only having access to a list with your gender.
- Wanting to build meaningful connections. Avoiding flakey TIs. And avoiding perps: they cannot materially help or have consistent personas and are unreliable and thus would not thrive in this type of environment.

If we get 10 to 20 TIs to sign up for this, it may be worth it for me to build and maintain. If people like it, it can be permanent feature in the app.

Who is interested? Comment below.

By rotating weekly it will give a clean break from people who were not a good personality match without confrontation.

If there is a person you do not want to be matched with again, please let me know. Unless the reason is serious, do not tell me the reason. I do not want to get involved in any messiness :).
