The reason I discourage story telling in TI Skills Network, unless it is directly tied to an ask, is because there are a lot of spaces on Quora to vent, tell your story and ask questions about gang stalking. It would be redundant for me to do another space.

Second perps/gstalkers infiltrated most of the spaces and so the more legit TI content in concentrated areas will drown out the gaslighting and confusion perps create.

Third, it will make it easier to find actionable information within this space. As this is a get shit done type of place.

Lastly, it will limit proxy gang stalking as we try to build a vetted TI community. To my knowledge the gstalkers have mimicked three real TIs to me. I do not know why and kind of do not care. For example, they mimic [Matthew Cappadocia (aka The Wizard of Oz)](https://www.quora.com/profile/Matthew-Cappadocia-aka-The-Wizard-of-Oz) and [Mike "Katfish" Harris](https://www.quora.com/profile/Mike-Katfish-Harris-2). Maybe to make me think they are not real TIs? Maybe it is to trigger me as they cannot extort them to harass me? Have their existence annoy me if/when I meet them in person? I do not know. It also does not matter.

But what I do know is that every TI is at a different place when it comes to their gang stalking. Emotionally, financially, etc. By having a space where you are less triggered by mundane or normal human behavior or interaction is ideal. It will help us cut through the noise.
