Warbag

Let us do a running list. What are essential items? As the comments come in, I will add them here. Try to be as specific as possible. All items are in no particular order.

Inspired by [Cam Wallace](https://www.quora.com/profile/Cam-Wallace-16)’s post. I added 10 more items of my own.

Let’s go!
- Tinted yellow eye glasses
- Body camera
- Headphones; Airpods Pro (works with Android)
- Pet rock collection ;)
- Bottle of yellow gatorade ;)
- SD cards
- Portable battery bank
- Voice recorder, preferably different than phone so it does not accidentally turn off
- Sunglasses, “cause they movie watching”
- Playlist of the most annoying sounds
- Door stop alarm / door wedge
- Thermal camera
- Class 3 or 4 laser pointer
- Lighter
- Pocket knife
- Water
- Hand sanitizer
- Soap
- Fishing line & stakes (trip hazard)
