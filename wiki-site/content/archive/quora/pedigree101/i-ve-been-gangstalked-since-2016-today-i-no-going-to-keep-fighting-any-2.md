This question appears to be from two years ago. But relevant even now. As a TI it is important to attempt, cautiously, in making connections with other TIs. Quora appears to be the place to do so. It is easy to go at your own pace to determine if someone is a TI or not. Before meeting them in person. I was able to meet a TI from Quora and was able to change my mind set from “fighting” to working to thrive. I may not know your specifics but as a TI isolation is what the gstalkers work toward, so do the opposite of that.

And to the question is there a list. Well, (I could be wrong) that shows you want to be heard and seen. What better way than to be remembered by other TIs who can carry on your story. Note, I am not advocating for TIs to be become “lost.”
