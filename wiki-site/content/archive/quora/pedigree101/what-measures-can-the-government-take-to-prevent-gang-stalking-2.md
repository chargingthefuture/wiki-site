Did Nelson Mandela say it best?

"For to be free is not merely to cast off one’s chains, but to live in a way that respects and enhances the freedom of others."

History has shown oppressed people must take responsibility for their own liberation. And then it is up to us the maintain the standard. The government is not one person running the show. And it is not binary of either good or evil.

Like it or not it, TIs will be the ones that put an end to this. So the real question is what will you or I do to prevent gang stalking?
