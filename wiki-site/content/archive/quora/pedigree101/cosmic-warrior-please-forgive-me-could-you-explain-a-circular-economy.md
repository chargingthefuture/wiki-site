This system will create a trusted network of local and global providers of goods and services. It ensures that these individuals can access reliable support without unintentionally funding those who engage in harmful behaviors. This approach prioritizes safety and trust, fostering a sustainable environment that uplifts and supports those who have faced exploitation, ultimately transforming lives and strengthening our community.
