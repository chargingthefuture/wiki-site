> Off grid living on the go, tips thanks to Snave Seer   Canada you can camp on Crown Land for free for 21 days per site. Similar rules apply in some US states. People would have to consult maps and apps but there are plen&hellip;

<p>Off grid living on the go, tips thanks to <a href="https://www.quora.com/profile/Snave-Seer">Snave Seer</a></p> <ul> <li>Canada you can camp on Crown Land for free for 21 days per site. Similar rules apply in some US states.</li> <li>People would have to consult maps and apps but there are plenty of camping apps iOverlander is one I use to finding camping sites.</li> <li>Another app is called Offline Maps and it keeps / downloads your Google maps and terrains so that you can use them offline with your GPS which will continue to work anywhere in the world even when you don’t have cell.</li> <li>There are apps which provide / show electric outlets, sources of water, community centers where you can shower. It’s all a lot of work but half the battle is knowing these resources / apps exist.</li> <li>I have a solar panel and 2 deep cell storage batteries. On a sunny day I can run my laptop most of the day. I use the batteries to charge music players phones laptop and even the car battery when I have been stranded 150 km from civilization.</li> </ul> <p>Comment below with your tips.
