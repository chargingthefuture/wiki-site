> I originally created the circular economy amongst Targeted Individuals on Quora, via the TI Skills Network space. But then Quora banned my account (https://www.quora.com/profile/Pedigree101) and conveniently deleted all &hellip;

<p>I originally created the circular economy amongst Targeted Individuals on Quora, via the TI Skills Network space. But then Quora banned my account (<a href="https://www.quora.com/profile/Pedigree101">https://www.quora.com/profile/Pedigree101</a>) and conveniently deleted all the posts but one that never received an answer.</p> <p>And so instead of using Quora, this is the new place to exchange goods and services with other TIs. Use it as you would Craigslist. Reminder, that I do my best to only let in TIs. Even so, please use the same precautions you would on any public platform and during exchange.</p> <p>To use the “Craigslist” feature:</p> <ol> <li> <p>Go to: <a href="https://sleekbio.com/farah" class="inline-onebox">Farah | SleekBio</a> . And click the second link “Townsquare.” This will lead you to request an invite to this very forum. Why? Because I want to filter out perps the best I can. Once you request the invite, I will approve you. <em><strong>You can request to create multiple accounts. For example, have one anon and one real identity.</strong></em></p> </li> <li> <p>Create a post and use the category tag: Craigslist. Again, you can sign up for this forum as an anonymous user. And do just your Craigslist posts. And all other posts under a different account. It is all up to your comfort level.</p> </li> </ol> <p>Tip when doing exchanges:<br> There is Google Voice or MySudo: <a href="https://anonyome.com/individuals/mysudo/" class="inline-onebox">MySudo - All-in-one privacy app</a> . I prefer MySudo as you get emails, phone numbers, browsers and more. For when you are communicating with strangers.</p> <p>There is also <a href="http://Signal.org">Signal.org</a>, where you do not need to share your real phone number or a phone number at all. Lastly, directly on this forum, you can DM people instead of using different apps or phone numbers.</p>
