### Comment #2 (2025-09-22)

<p>Thanks for trying Discourse!</p>
<p>, here are some tips for launching a successful discussion community:</p>
<h3><a name="p-61-what-is-the-elevator-pitch-for-your-community-1" class="anchor" href="#p-61-what-is-the-elevator-pitch-for-your-community-1"></a>What is the “elevator pitch” for your community?</h3>
<p>The first thing people will ask: <strong>what is this place?</strong> How would you describe your community to someone you just met in a 60 second elevator ride? Make sure that’s in your welcome topic, pinned globally so it appears at the top of your home page.</p>
<h3><a name="p-61-build-some-interesting-discussions-to-launch-with-2" class="anchor" href="#p-61-build-some-interesting-discussions-to-launch-with-2"></a>Build some interesting discussions to launch with</h3>
<ul>
<li>
<p><img src="images/welcome-to-your-14-day-starter-hosting-trial-comment-2-bulb.png" title=":bulb:" class="emoji" alt=":bulb:" loading="lazy" width="20" height="20"> What comes up often in your internal emails? Are there common themes that tend to come up again and again? Try moving those discussions out of private email silos into your Discourse community.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><img src="images/welcome-to-your-14-day-starter-hosting-trial-comment-2-sunglasses.png" title=":sunglasses:" class="emoji" alt=":sunglasses:" loading="lazy" width="20" height="20"> If you find a cool link you want to discuss, quickly start a new topic by <strong>pasting a link into the topic title</strong>. Try it!</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><img src="images/welcome-to-your-14-day-starter-hosting-trial-comment-2-speech-balloon.png" title=":speech_balloon:" class="emoji" alt=":speech_balloon:" loading="lazy" width="20" height="20"> Have some open-ended getting to know you topics for people to share their opinions, experiences, stories, or pictures. An “introduce yourself” topic is always fun, and you should go first!</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><img src="images/welcome-to-your-14-day-starter-hosting-trial-comment-2-hugs.png" title=":hugs:" class="emoji" alt=":hugs:" loading="lazy" width="20" height="20"> What topics do you <em>want</em> your community to create? Imagine what a model member you would love to see on your site would do – and then try doing that yourself. Create multiple accounts if you need to; post example topics and reply to them so visitors can browse the existing conversations to discover what your community is about.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h3><a name="p-61-get-the-right-people-in-the-room-3" class="anchor" href="#p-61-get-the-right-people-in-the-room-3"></a>Get the right people in the room</h3>
<ul>
<li>
<p><img src="images/welcome-to-your-14-day-starter-hosting-trial-comment-2-email.png" title=":email:" class="emoji" alt=":email:" loading="lazy" width="20" height="20"> <a href="/my/invited">Send personal invitations</a> to your staff, power users, or friends. Ask them to help you by logging in and replying to your initial topics to generate activity. You can also create invite links and share them to many people at once.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><img src="images/welcome-to-your-14-day-starter-hosting-trial-comment-2-heart.png" title=":heart:" class="emoji" alt=":heart:" loading="lazy" width="20" height="20"> Generously <strong>like</strong> any and every post you enjoy! What type of content gets liked is a major part of your community’s culture. Set an example by frequently liking posts in the early days of your forum. Seeing liked posts also encourages people to reciprocate in kind, and come back for more.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><img src="images/welcome-to-your-14-day-starter-hosting-trial-comment-2-muscle.png" title=":muscle:" class="emoji" alt=":muscle:" loading="lazy" width="20" height="20"> Actively seek the help of power users and early adopters in your community. There’s a built in <a href="/c/site-feedback">feedback category</a> for discussing organization and governance. Let your most avid members have a say in what your community does, how the site works, and what your community becomes.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h3><a name="p-61-how-do-people-find-your-community-4" class="anchor" href="#p-61-how-do-people-find-your-community-4"></a>How do people find your community?</h3>
<ul>
<li>
<p><img src="images/welcome-to-your-14-day-starter-hosting-trial-comment-2-link.png" title=":link:" class="emoji" alt=":link:" loading="lazy" width="20" height="20"> Where can you place links to your community so that people will naturally discover it? In the header or footer of your website? Where else?</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><img src="images/welcome-to-your-14-day-starter-hosting-trial-comment-2-mega.png" title=":mega:" class="emoji" alt=":mega:" loading="lazy" width="20" height="20"> Promote your community. Add a note to your mailing lists or email newsletters, put up a notice on your website, or make a blog entry about your new community.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><img src="images/welcome-to-your-14-day-starter-hosting-trial-comment-2-trophy.png" title=":trophy:" class="emoji" alt=":trophy:" loading="lazy" width="20" height="20"> What rewards, perks, contests, or incentives can you give people for signing up, for posting, for replying? Check <a href="/u">your user directory</a> to see engagement statistics, and shower your best users with attention to encourage them.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>For additional advice, see our <a href="http://blog.discourse.org/2014/08/building-a-discourse-community/">blog post on how to build engaging Discourse communities</a>.</p>
