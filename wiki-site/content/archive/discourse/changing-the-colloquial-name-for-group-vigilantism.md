<p>The colloquial name for group vigilanteism is gang stalking. And I am personally tired of using it.</p> <p>Recently, a TI mentioned that they had not joined TI Skills Network (TSN) as they were unsure if their physical location was included. Location is not an exclusionary criterion for TSN. Another TI mentioned that because of sleep deprivation by gang stalkers, it is not easy to follow everything that is going on with TSN.</p> <p>And so I would like to clean up all my posts. By reviewing them for clarity, grammar, and consistency. To make it easy for Targets to digest what I am presenting. As a Target, I also suffer from sleep deprivation from gang stalkers, and this, along with all other attacks, affects my communication.</p> <p>In this process of cleaning up my posts, I want to replace the colloquial name “gang stalking.”</p> <p>What suggestions do you have?</p> <p>I am leaning toward “predators.” When someone says “Targets,” it is distinctly known what is being referred to. I am not choosing stalkers, as that term is overly used. Another colloquial name option is banshees, but that seems too light-hearted or rather rolls off the tongue silly. I am searching for a single word (not two) and one that is not overly used in other contexts that could create confusion.</p> <p>Any suggestions? If we get a few suggestions, I can create a public and anonymous poll to narrow it down.</p> <p>Post on Quora:</p> <p><a href="https://skillseconomy.quora.com/The-colloquial-name-for-group-vigilanteism-is-gang-stalking-And-I-am-personally-tired-of-using-it-Recently-a-TI-ment" class="onebox" target="_blank" rel="noopener">https://skillseconomy.quora.com/The-colloquial-name-for-group-vigilanteism-is-gang-stalking-And-I-am-personally-tired-of-using-it-Recently-a-TI-ment</a></p>

### Comment #2 (2025-10-08)

<p>Pushing things a bit further, why not invent a word? Thoughts on the following two: <strong>Predalite</strong> or <strong>Specterati</strong> as a replacement for “gang stalking” and “gang stalker.”</p> <p>Please provide feedback. My goal is to engage in <strong>Brand Language Development</strong> to refine my posts and TI products, utilizing consistent language for clearer communication. This does not need to be set in stone; if down the road, there’s a better word, we can adapt as necessary.</p> <p>With each of the options above. There could be additional words to then frame other concepts within our environment:</p> <p><strong>Choice A: Predalite -</strong> replacement for “gang stalking” and “gang stalker.”</p> <p>Indicates a group of individuals with predatory instincts or actions.</p> <p><strong>Predasphere</strong></p> <p>Suggests a pervasive environment where predatory behavior thrives.</p> <p><strong>Predacide</strong></p> <p>Implies a destructive force that seeks to eliminate individuality.</p> <p><strong>Predanox</strong></p> <p>Combines “preda” with “anox” (anaerobic), suggesting a suffocating presence.</p> <p><strong>Predaforce</strong></p> <p>Implies a dominating, forceful nature that imposes control.</p> <p><strong>Predarise</strong></p> <p>Suggests a rise in predatory behavior in society.</p> <p><strong>Choice B: Specterati -</strong> replacement for “gang stalking” and “gang stalker.”</p> <ul> <li> <p><strong>Specter</strong>: A ghostly apparition, symbolizing the unseen and insidious nature of their stalking and harassment.</p> </li> <li> <p><strong>-ati</strong>: A suffix often used in words to denote a group or collective, suggesting a network of these individuals working together.</p> </li> </ul> <p><strong>Specterwave</strong></p> <p>Suggests a pervasive influence that spreads fear and control.</p> <p><strong>Specterforce</strong></p> <p>Implies a powerful, unseen entity that enforces compliance.</p> <p><strong>Spectervox</strong></p> <p>Combines “specter” with “vox” (voice), hinting at silencing dissenting voices.</p> <p><strong>Specterbane</strong></p> <p>Indicates a destructive force that seeks to eliminate individuality and freedom.</p> <p><strong>Specterrealm</strong></p> <p>Conveys a domain ruled by fear and intimidation, highlighting their oppressive influence.</p> <p><strong>Specterrise</strong></p> <p>Suggests a rise in sinister activity and dominance in society.</p>
