<p>We have an odd number of sign ups on SupportMatch. Please join us, and be matched almost same day with another TI!</p> <p>Use SupportMatch to meet other TIs, form friendships or accountability partners.</p> <p>On the platform you can use in app messaging, report a match or exclude a match from happening again. You also get to choose if you are matched with the same gender.</p> <p>In an attempt to filter out gang stalkers. The app is invite only. Ask for an invite here or on Quora and you will get one same day.</p> <p><a href="https://supportmatch.chargingthefuture.com">https://supportmatch.chargingthefuture.com</a>.</p>
