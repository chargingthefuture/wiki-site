<p>TLDR;</p>
<p>Saturday TI Skills Economy Town Halls are moving to an Android social audio app called Chyme instead of Twitch. Find the latest Chyme app download here: <a href="https://github.com/chargingthefuture/mono/releases">https://github.com/chargingthefuture/mono/releases</a>. You will need an account at <a href="https://app.chargingthefuture.com">https://app.chargingthefuture.com</a> to use the Android app.</p>
<p>There is no iOS app.</p>
<p>–</p>
<p>The Town Halls, held every Saturday, are streamed live on <a href="https://twitch.tv/the1690s" class="inline-onebox">Twitch</a> . However, some people mentioned issues logging in/creating an account on Twitch. Not surprisingly, it is Specterati interference.</p>
<p>When I do the Town Halls, it is only audio. As I face the camera away from me, I am only using Twitch, so there would be a way to do a livestream without a login.</p>
<p>However, without a login, you cannot interact with me directly, i.e., live chat. And with street theatre constantly around me, I am concerned about inadvertently triggering another TI. And so Twitch has outgrown its use.</p>
<p>I have created an Android app, called Chyme, that has one feature – social audio to host the Town Halls. There will be no iOS app, as that requires Apple App Store approval, and it would be blocked by the Specterati. With Android, you can distribute apps outside of the Google Play Store. Hence, no need for Specterati approval.</p>
<p>If you do not have an Android device, consider signing up for a free government-funded phone. As a Target, you are being mobbed out of income, and you will likely qualify. Here is one company that provides such a phone: <a href="https://www.truconnect.com">https://www.truconnect.com</a>. To my knowledge, the phone is a one-time $50, but the monthly recurring data and phone plan is free. However, others have told me some companies provide a free phone and free monthly recurring data/voice plans.</p>
<p>Find the latest Chyme app download here: <a href="https://github.com/chargingthefuture/mono/releases">https://github.com/chargingthefuture/mono/releases</a>.</p>
<p>As a Target, downloading an app, especially one not in the App Store, is a risk. For your security, I have made all code of the TI Skills Economy open source. This means you can see what the app does.</p>
