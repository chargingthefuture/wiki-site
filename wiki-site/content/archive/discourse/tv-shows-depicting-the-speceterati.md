<p>I am re-watching the TV show The Good Place.</p>
<p>This space is exclusively for Targeted Individuals. However, if there is a random non-target/non-perp, the very first episode of The Good Place, and generally the whole series, shows what the Specterati (gang stalkers/group vigilantes) do to people.</p>
<p>Spoiler alert for the rest of this post.</p>
<p>Note, I am referring only to the torture tactics of the Speceterati. “Interesting” that the depiction of hell is literally what the Speceterati do 24/7 to innocent lives every day. Documented as early as 1690.</p>
<p>A very brief synopsis statement of the street theatre that takes place in episode one:</p>
<blockquote>
<p><em>The next morning, Eleanor wakes up to discover that many things that represent her crass comments about her life and her insults to Tahani at the party have taken on a physical form and are wreaking havoc on the neighborhood. Chidi tells her that presence in the Good Place is the cause of the disruptions. Eleanor begs Chidi to help her become a better person just as Michael knocks on the door to inform them of an emergency meeting.</em></p>
</blockquote>
<p><em>Source: <a href="https://thegoodplace.fandom.com/wiki/Chapter_1:_Everything_Is_Fine">https://thegoodplace.fandom.com/wiki/Chapter_1:_Everything_Is_Fine</a></em></p>
<p>Even “funnier” is that the series is inclusive of many ethnicities. This also depicts the Specterati accurately. EVERY class, “race,” and ethnicity is involved in group vigilantism in the form of human trafficking.</p>
<p>As a Targeted Individual, it is not remotely about mocking crass comments or insults made. Rather literally anything you do or say, the Speceterati display in physical form. And being added to their torture list has nothing to do with being a good person or not.</p>
