<p>To end gang stalking? No, I have not seen anything trustworthy. It is either trolling or naive/ignorant of who orchestrates targeting, and because of this, they cannot stop gang stalking.</p>\n<p>There are general resources you could use that are not necessarily for targets, like food banks. Someone correct me if I am wrong, but I think my space, TI Skills Network, is the only resource that focuses on mitigating the aftereffects of gang stalking, such as the loss of transportation, jobs, and housing. The resources we are excluded from if we are targets. Aside from my Quora space, it seems people are only doing emotional support as a resource. Not to say emotional support is not needed. But the only one that has fewer scams. As it is, TIs providing emotional support to other TIs.</p>\n<p>On Quora: <a href="https://qr.ae/pCrF7B">https://qr.ae/pCrF7B</a></p>
