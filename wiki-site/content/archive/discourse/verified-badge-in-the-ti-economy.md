> As announced yesterday at the Town Hall, this week I added a “Verified” badge to the Directory profiles. Verified = several verified TIs within the community believe said person is a TI No Verified badge = unknown &hellip;

<p><a href="https://chargingthefuture.discourse.group/t/weekly-state-of-the-ti-economy-townhall-meetings/136/9">As announced yesterday at the Town Hall</a>, this week I added a “Verified” badge to the Directory profiles.</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Verified = several verified TIs within the community believe said person is a TI</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>No Verified badge = unknown at the moment</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>The length of time someone is in the app without a verified badge varies. It is based on how efficiently the community can verify that someone is a TI. The intention is that everyone must be verified. And the badge is a simple way for someone else on the platform to decide to interact with someone. At any point, if someone is discovered to be a perp (member of the Specterati), their account will be deleted immediately. There is zero tolerance for human trafficking.</p>
<p>To become verified, one would need to provide material value and/or meet other TIs in person to confirm.</p>
