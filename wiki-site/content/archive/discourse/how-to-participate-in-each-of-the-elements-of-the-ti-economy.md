> The way the app works is that you get access to ALL psyop-free features. But you do not have to participate in all of them. In order to participate, you create a profile for each feature.  Notice in the screenshot of my &hellip;

<p>The way the app works is that you get access to ALL psyop-free features. But you do not have to participate in all of them. In order to participate, you create a profile for each feature.</p> <p>Notice in the screenshot of my original post, when you click LightHouse (“Airbnb for TIs”), you will be prompted to “Create Your Profile.” Without this, you cannot see the marketplace. Create your profile, which has only a few questions - one minute to complete- and you will see the marketplace!</p> <p>Here’s a screenshot of what the marketplace looks like.
