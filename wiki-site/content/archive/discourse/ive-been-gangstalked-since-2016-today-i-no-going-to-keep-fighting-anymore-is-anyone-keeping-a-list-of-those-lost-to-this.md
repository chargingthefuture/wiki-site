<p>Original post on Quora: Aug 16, 2025 09:17 AM PDT</p> <p>This question appears to be from two years ago. But relevant now. As a TI, it is important to attempt, cautiously, to make connections with other TIs. Quora appears to be the place to do so. It is easy to go at your own pace to determine if someone is a TI or not before meeting them in person or investing a significant amount of time. I was able to meet a TI from Quora and was able to change my mindset from “fighting” to working to thrive. I may not know your specifics, but as a TI, isolation is what the gstalkers work towards, so do the opposite of that.</p> <p>And to the question, is there a list? Well, (I could be wrong), that shows you want to be heard and seen. What better way than to be remembered by other TIs who can carry on your story. Note, I am not advocating for TIs to become “lost.”</p> <p>UPDATE on Quora Sept 21, 2025:</p> <p>I have started a list. Check the links in my bio (<a href="https://sleek.bio/farah" class="inline-onebox">Farah | SleekBio</a>) for more details, or comment below with questions.</p> <p>It has two features: Join and be listed in a database of vetted TIs. Second, and optional, be matched monthly with another TI so you may network and potentially build a friendship or business relationship. Use it for accountability support, emotional support, or to find clients for your business.</p> <p><a href="https://qr.ae/pCGMHx" class="onebox" target="_blank" rel="noopener">https://qr.ae/pCGMHx</a></p>
