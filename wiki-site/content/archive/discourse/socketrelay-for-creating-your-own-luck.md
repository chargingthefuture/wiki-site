<p>Do not forget about SocketRelay! Request anything from the TI Skills Network.</p> <p>In 140 characters, make your post, and any TI can fulfill the request. Inspired by my experience with <a href="https://www.quora.com/profile/Justin-Singer-34">Justin Singer</a>. I mentioned in the group TI chat that the Specterati stole all my clothes last year, including underwear. And this year, the Specterati yet again stole my coat, and winter was fast approaching. Within one week, Justin mailed me a coat and jacket at his expense. In this case, I did not ask but received.</p> <p>I would like to recreate this “luck” for other TIs. Hence, SocketRelay, create your own luck. Request services or goods in a peer-to-peer request marketplace. Here is my very first post in SocketRelay:</p><p><a href="https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/socketrelay/public/9fe8dc0c-47ce-45a6-b49a-fe68162dbe4e">https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/socketrelay/public/9fe8dc0c-47ce-45a6-b49a-fe68162dbe4e</a></p><p>And you can view any SocketRelay that is public here: <a href="https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/socketrelay/public">https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/socketrelay/public</a>.
