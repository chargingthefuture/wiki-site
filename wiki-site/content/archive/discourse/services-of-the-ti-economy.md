Operating like WeChat’s mini-app ecosystem, it provides a unified, secure environment where survivors can access multiple essential services through a single account. Each service operates as an independent mini-app while sharing core platform functionalities, ensuring seamless integration and consistent user experience.</p>\n<p><strong>Key Values:</strong></p>\n<ul>\n<li>\n<p><strong>Safety First</strong>: Invite-only access with robust security measures</p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p><strong>Privacy by Design</strong>: End-to-end encryption and data protection</p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p><strong>Trauma-Informed</strong>: WCAG AAA accessibility standards, no overwhelming animations</p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p><strong>Dignity &amp; Respect</strong>: User-controlled data, complete account deletion options</p>\n</li>\n<li>\n<p><strong>Community-Driven</strong>: Built by and for survivors</p>\n</li>\n</ul>\n<hr>\n<h2><a name="p-242-mini-apps-services-3" class="anchor" href="#p-242-mini-apps-services-3" aria-label="Heading link"></a>Mini-Apps &amp; Services</h2>\n<h3><a name="p-242-supportmatch-4" class="anchor" href="#p-242-supportmatch-4" aria-label="Heading link"></a>SupportMatch</h3>\n<p><strong>Accountability Partner Matching</strong></p>\n<p>A partner matching platform featuring monthly partnership cycles, real-time messaging, and robust safety features. Connect with accountability partners for mutual support and growth.</p>\n<h3><a name="p-242-lighthouse-5" class="anchor" href="#p-242-lighthouse-5" aria-label="Heading link"></a>LightHouse</h3>\n<p><strong>Safe Housing &amp; Accommodations</strong></p>\n<p>Dual-role system connecting housing seekers with hosts. Provides safe accommodations and support resources, guiding survivors towards healing and empowerment.</p>\n<h3><a name="p-242-trusttransport-6" class="anchor" href="#p-242-trusttransport-6" aria-label="Heading link"></a>TrustTransport</h3>\n<p><strong>Safe Transportation Services</strong> Request or provide safe transportation services. Connect with trusted drivers for rides to appointments, errands, or other essential travel needs.</p>\n<h3><a name="p-242-mechanicmatch-7" class="anchor" href="#p-242-mechanicmatch-7" aria-label="Heading link"></a>MechanicMatch</h3>\n<p><strong>Vehicle Repair Services</strong></p>\n<p>Connect with trusted mechanics for vehicle repair, remote diagnosis, or expert advice. Mechanics can build their profile and help car owners maintain safe, reliable transportation.</p>\n<h3><a name="p-242-socketrelay-8" class="anchor" href="#p-242-socketrelay-8" aria-label="Heading link"></a>SocketRelay</h3>\n<p><strong>Request &amp; Fulfillment Network</strong></p>\n<p>Find what you need or help others get the goods and services they request. A community-driven mutual aid system for sharing resources.</p>\n<h3><a name="p-242-directory-9" class="anchor" href="#p-242-directory-9" aria-label="Heading link"></a>Directory</h3>\n<p><strong>Skill-Sharing &amp; Collaboration</strong></p>\n<p>Find talented individuals to collaborate with. Build your professional profile and connect with others who share your skills and interests.</p>\n<h3><a name="p-242-workforce-recruiter-10" class="anchor" href="#p-242-workforce-recruiter-10" aria-label="Heading link"></a>Workforce Recruiter</h3>\n<p><strong>See distribution of skills across the Skills Economy</strong></p>\n<p>Track your professional profile against the needs of the community with comprehensive recruitment analytics.</p>\n<h3><a name="p-242-lostmail-11" class="anchor" href="#p-242-lostmail-11" aria-label="Heading link"></a>LostMail</h3>\n<p><strong>Mail Incident Reporting</strong> Report mail incidents (lost, damaged, tampered, delayed) and track your reports.
