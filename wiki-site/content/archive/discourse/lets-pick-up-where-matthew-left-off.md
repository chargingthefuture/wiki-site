<p>Where there is a will, there is a way.</p> <p>One of the most helpful TIs on Quora (<a href="https://www.quora.com/profile/Matthew-Cappadocia-aka-The-Wizard-of-Oz">https://www.quora.com/profile/Matthew-Cappadocia-aka-The-Wizard-of-Oz</a>) mentioned that Quora will not allow him to collect/post survey data. And they ban him every 14 days. Once one 14-day ban finishes, another begins. This limits his reach by limiting new posts and responding to comments. Who says we have to use Quora?</p> <p>Matthew asked each person to respond to a specific thread, answering survey questions and then manually entered the data into Google Maps. I prefer automation.</p> <p>If each TI has 2 minutes, we can collect the data we need. Here is what I suggest.</p> <ol> <li> <p>Create an account at <a href="http://app.chargingthefuture.com">app.chargingthefuture.com</a>. DM for an invite code</p> </li> <li> <p>Go to “SupportMatch” on the left-hand side and complete the optional profile.</p> </li> </ol> <p>With profiles completed, I can generate and release research data on TIs.
