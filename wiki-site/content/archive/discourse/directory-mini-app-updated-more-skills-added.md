Where you create a simple profile.</p> <p>First, a dedicated space for TIs to stay in contact regardless of what online platforms we are on. Second, find TIs with skills you are in need of. Like a mechanic, plumber, yoga instructor, etc. As close to utopia as we can get - psyop-free interactions.</p> <p>You can choose up to 3 skills for your Directory profile.
