> Point of this post is to say I am no different than any other TI and each day I think of all you. I have survived over 10 years of physical violence and stalking from the Specterati and 11 years of harassment from the po&hellip;

<p>Point of this post is to say I am no different than any other TI and each day I think of all you. I have survived over 10 years of physical violence and stalking from the Specterati and 11 years of harassment from the police.</p> <p>We are only on month 5 of TI Skills Network and I see good things happening. Hang in there.</p> <p>###</p> <p>I think it was last night or early morning, I was responding to a Quora post on the Specterati (also known as gang stalkers - colloquial name for group vigilantes). Someone asked why would a gang stalker have their child do street theater or any type of violence towards a target? Don’t they care about their children? I had responded something to the effect of, they feel protected by the gang stalking network. And will retaliate because they know they can do it against the target without any consequence.</p> <p>Today (approximately 4:15 pm EST), when I came to stand in line at a drop-in center, I placed my bag on a pair of steps to a building that’s close by as I wait for the line to die down. As soon as I come to the stairs, they have one of their stalker men walk up to me. I have my headphones on, so I don’t know what they were saying, but I guess what they were saying is that I needed to leave the stairs.</p> <p>However, the person is already starting a fight and angry and yelling. I had my backpack on, but I had a paper bag sitting on the stairs with food. The gang stalker grabs it and is about to throw it, so I grab it out of his hand. He then pushes me, slaps my phone out of my hand, punches me in the face, tasers me, and then walks away into the building.</p> <p>That’s an example again of them creating fake fights and trying to make everything you say come true if you write about. In this case the lack of accountability. As many stood by and laughed.</p> <p>Of course, no one did anything about the guy. He was at least twice my size.</p> <p>Shortly after another blatant stalker that was a “bystander” comes up to me, and with bunch of trolling stuff on. Color sensitization for example.</p> <p>This gang stalker then tries to race bait me by talking about how men of his race should not hit women. And then says how they are praying for me. And now have an excuse to move! Also stating it was wrong, saw the whole thing, did not call the police during it, that I should call the police so I can get them fired from their job.</p> <p>The person was covering up their face a little. I think they were about to laugh or maybe they were smirking so they wanted to hide that, but it was obvious. The whole thing was staged.</p> <p>This is similar to when the Specterati punched an innocent women in the face outside, while I was in my car idling, and tried to provoke me to fight the assailant. Pure evil.</p> <p>Aside from possibly trying to create something I mentioned in a post, the other thing is wanting me to leave the place I’m at now and go somewhere else, which is typically how they always do it. They always want the target hopping around different places, being displaced. In my case they are trying to provoke me to live with family members that are also part of the Specterati. In addition to displacement. In addition, they want to arrest me for assault or anything really. Weeks ago I was at a train station, after getting off the train, at a restaurant/food seating area and undercover cops (I am not joking) approach me and say that they have been watching me and I overstayed the seating limit. A limit by the way that is not posted or verbal announced anywhere. And they wanted to arrest me. I just stared at them. It was ludacris.</p> <p>I do not like telling many stories. Frankly, many TIs have similar versions. But this is not the first time I have been assaulted by the Specterati. And there are TIs that say they have it easier than me.</p> <p>None of us have it easy. And events like these are proof of how clinically insane these “people” are. They cannot even keep a straight face in the mist of violence.</p> <p>Post on Quora</p> <p><a href="https://skillseconomy.quora.com/Point-of-this-post-is-to-say-I-am-no-different-than-any-other-TI-and-each-day-I-think-of-all-you-I-have-survived-over-1" class="onebox" target="_blank" rel="noopener">https://skillseconomy.quora.com/Point-of-this-post-is-to-say-I-am-no-different-than-any-other-TI-and-each-day-I-think-of-all-you-I-have-survived-over-1</a></p>
