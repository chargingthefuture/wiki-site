<p><a href="https://www.quora.com/Could-a-targeted-individual-be-also-active-in-gang-stalking"><br> Could a targeted individual be also active in gang stalking?<br> </a></p> <p>No. Once a target, always a target. A gang stalker, for whatever reason they have joined, is in a separate category because they will never endure the same as a TI.</p> <p>There is proxy gang stalking. For example, if TIs interact with each other. They will purposely mimic the TIs to them. For example, if I search for a product online, they will push ads of that product to other TIs’ accounts. Then one day you see the TI with the product all of a sudden (just like the gstalkers you know are for sure in on it); gstalkers want you to panic and think the other TI is a gstalker. The same way they try to trick you into thinking “everyone” is in on it.</p> <p>There is also the targeting of your friends and family, but it appears temporary. Meaning they are only harassed until they either abandon you or become a gstalker and engage in your torture.
