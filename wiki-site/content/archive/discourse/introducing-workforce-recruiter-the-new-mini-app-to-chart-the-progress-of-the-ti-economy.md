<h1><a name="p-266-overview-of-the-workforce-recruiter-tracker-app-1" class="anchor" href="#p-266-overview-of-the-workforce-recruiter-tracker-app-1" aria-label="Heading link"></a>Overview of the Workforce Recruiter Tracker App</h1> <p>Since starting TI Skills Network, I have been thinking through how to coordinate as efficiently as possible. Workforce Recruiter, the new mini-app (found at <a href="https://app.chargingthefuture.com">https://app.chargingthefuture.com</a>), aims to monitor our skills network through the means of job recruitment and distribution within the TI community of 5 million people globally. It will help assess how healthy the TI economy is by keeping track of job roles, skill levels, and training options each year.</p> <p>The United Nations stated at one point that 2 million people were identified as Targeted Individuals (TIs). However, the actual number could be as high as 4 million worldwide, as many related crimes go unreported at a rate of 50%. I have rounded up to 5 million, equivalent to a nation state.</p> <p>A Focus on Countries with Similar Populations</p> <p>There are several countries with populations of around 5 million, like Singapore, Finland, and Norway. Here are some examples of what makes these nations economically strong:</p> <ul> <li>Singapore: Offers a mix of financial services and technology.</li> <li>New Zealand: Focuses on agriculture and tourism.</li> <li>Norway: Benefits from natural resources.</li> <li>Finland: Invests in innovation and technology.</li> </ul> <p>The TI Skills Economy is not intended to become a nation-state. Rather, enables us to live fulfilling lives as we are mobbed out of society, regardless of where we are in the world. These insights will help build a framework for the TI skills economy, focusing on key sectors, jobs, and skills that promote sustainable livelihoods.</p> <p>Main Goal of the TI Skills Economy</p> <p>The main goal is to develop a sustainable skills economy that helps victims of human trafficking (TIs) regain control over their lives. The Workforce mini-app will assess where the needed skills are located and plan how to share these skills globally to increase economic participation.</p> <p>For example, on Quora, a TI stated they are 19 years old and unclear how to they can create a career for themselves. Here is our opportunity.</p> <p>Post on Quora:</p> <p><a href="https://tiskillsnetwork.quora.com/Introducing-Workforce-Recruiter-the-new-mini-app-to-chart-the-progress-of-the-TI-economy" class="onebox" target="_blank" rel="noopener">https://tiskillsnetwork.quora.com/Introducing-Workforce-Recruiter-the-new-mini-app-to-chart-the-progress-of-the-TI-economy</a></p>
