Someone asked on Quora:

> How do you maintain regular employment while experiencing gang stalking?

Source: https://www.quora.com/How-do-you-maintain-regular-employment-while-experiencing-gang-stalking/answer/Farah-Brunache

Not possible in the Specterrealm. Another Targeted Individual taught me about healthy volunteer studies. It’s where pharma/clinical trial companies pay you to test medicine. It’s safe because they are Phase I studies.

Here’s an example: https://iconstudies.com/All-Clinical-Research-Studies. As you see, you can be paid up to $36,000 on some studies. The highest I have seen is $45,000 for one month of work. However, the average is $3-$5k/month.

I have done one study so far. Interestingly, the study I did did not involve medication. Rather I did an exercise, and at intervals they measured my vitals. Clinical trials are contract work. However, you can achieve the same results as a 9-to-5 job if you budget correctly. Reach out to me with questions.

And I did a more detailed write-up on clinical trials here: https://github.com/chargingthefuture/mono/wiki/Overview-of-clinical-trials
