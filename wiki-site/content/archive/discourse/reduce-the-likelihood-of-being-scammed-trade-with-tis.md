> “TI Craigslist” started on Quora, then migrated to here, our own Discourse server. But it is not being used, to be fair this townsquare is one month old. That being said maybe there is a simpler way for TIs to get what &hellip;

<p>“TI Craigslist” started on Quora, then migrated to here, our own Discourse server. But it is not being used, to be fair this townsquare is one month old.</p>
<p>That being said maybe there is a simpler way for TIs to get what they need. In the TI group chat I stated how winter is coming and the Specterati stole my one jacket. Last year they stole all my clothes including underwear, except what I had on.</p>
<p>And another TI immediately mailed me a jacket and winter coat. This is the energy I was looking to capture with the TI Craigslist. A simpler way might just be a TI making a post requesting an item. And someone fulfilling the request.</p>
<p>I will launch within the app such a feature. And since it is within the app there is a bit more privacy for those who prefer it when making requests.</p>
<p>For the love of humanity,</p>
<p>Farah</p>
