> There are many ways. One way was to compromise the Wi-Fi network I was connected to and block the use of VPNs. And using a firmware exploit to copy offline files, on a USB drive connected to my computer. They copied all &hellip;

<p>There are many ways. One way was to compromise the Wi-Fi network I was connected to and block the use of VPNs. And using a firmware exploit to copy offline files, on a USB drive connected to my computer. They copied all the files, and they remotely began wiping the USB drive. I was able to recover the files. It had exports of my emails, Notion, Obsidian, software code, and more. Some time later, Jack “Porsey” and other tech bros released my products and writings as their white papers, blog posts, etc.</p>\n<p>How to stop it? You are one person up against an international crime organization that appears to have mercenaries on the payroll. The best you can do is to try to mitigate being compromised in the future. <a href="https://www.quora.com/profile/Snave-Seer">Snave Seer</a> is working on a series of posts to teach TIs about securing our devices.
