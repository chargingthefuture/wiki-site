A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> What is the typical response from law enforcement agencies to victims who report gang stalking in their community Do they receive protection or are they ignored

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/What-is-the-typical-response-from-law-enforcement-agencies-to-victims-who-report-gang-stalking-in-their-community-Do-they-receive-protection-or-are-they-ignored. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

Where to report, and what a report can carry, is in [Who to report it to](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-to-report-it-to). Why a badge tells you nothing about the person wearing it is in [A badge is a job title](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/a-badge-is-a-job-title). This answer is about the call itself, and who wants it made.

## Neither word fits

Protected or ignored assumes the call goes to somebody outside the arrangement, who then decides whether to help. That is not what I have seen.

When a space on Quora polled which police departments were complicit, I answered on July 11, 2025: "All are." On October 2, 2025, asked whether the people doing this are protected by real investigators, I wrote that they protect each other, and that a police officer taking part is as protected as a cashier taking part. "Profession is not relevant. Rather, the level of access to information they can illegally get or leverage over the TI in their position is more valuable."

A call reaches a department, and a department is a place where information is kept. That is what makes a post there worth having.

## They asked me to call

For several years people taking part started physical fights with me in the street. One threatened me with a glass bottle. Another threw a full soda can at the back of my head. On May 20, 2025, I wrote on Quora that they had been asking me to call the police, "and that is likely also part of their game" ([You cannot argue a smear down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/you-cannot-argue-a-smear-down)).

A fight that is started for you, followed by a call you are pressed to make, is a sequence somebody planned. The response to the call is part of the plan.

## When police did come

In St. Louis, a woman approached me with scripted-sounding talk and would not leave. I backed away and told her to get away from me. She then told the bus company's staff I had assaulted her, and a second person confirmed it as a witness. There had been no contact of any kind. Police were called on that report, against me. They said they could not compel a private company to release its own camera footage, which would have shown nothing happened ([One trip, seven schemes](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/one-trip-seven-schemes)).

I have also called the police on individual incidents myself, and gone to a station to file a report. While I was there, an officer mocked a call I had made to a national hotline back to me, in person ([The Assistant Is Not a Clinician](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-assistant-is-not-a-clinician)).

## What that leaves

A report of one incident, described as the crime it is, can still be worth filing. What I would not do is make a call because somebody nearby keeps suggesting it, or treat the response as the measure of whether it happened.

What holds up regardless of who answers is a dated record kept as it happens, in your own hands. And the help that does not depend on who picks up comes from people: survivors who do not take part, listed by what they can do, findable without going through anybody.
