Two things have to be true for the targeting to work.

The first is that the people around you can be reached. You noticed how early that starts: high school kids who already know what to do. In what I have seen, it starts younger than that, often at home. [Who teaches them](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-teaches-them) is my answer to your question.

The second is that there is nobody else. That one is not a starting condition. It is what the first produces, given time.

Nothing I know of stops them reaching people. What answers it is having people to turn to anyway, doing the things a week runs on.

The listing carries one word, Advocacy, and it is a placeholder. Eli has not written about a trade anywhere I could find, so the listing holds the one thing that is on the record: what they wrote on Quora is about Targeted Individuals. It says nothing more than that, and neither does this post.

## What the list is for

The [Directory](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory) lists people and what they can do. It is how a ride, a room, or an hour of somebody else's trade finds the person who needs it. [Foundation](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/foundation) is where members offer and exchange that help.

Right now Eli's listing says only that they are here. Claimed, it can say what they can do, and that turns a name on a list into somebody other people can ask.

## How the listing came to be

I built it from what Eli asked in public and put it up under their name, which is how most of this list was made, one person at a time. They did not sign up, and nobody asked them first. That is why it is marked community-generated and carries my handle: so anybody reading it knows it is my account of somebody else, and knows who to come to about it.

## The invitation

Claiming the listing takes a free account and a few minutes, and after that it is theirs — what it says, whether the placeholder gets replaced with something they can actually do, and whether it stays up at all.

Nothing else is required. Not using the rest of the app, not posting anything, not agreeing with a word I have written. No is a complete answer, and the listing comes down on request with no account involved in that either.

## This does not run one way

Somebody who sees what Eli sees needs things too. A ride. A room. An hour of a trade they do not have. The same listing that makes them findable is what makes those people findable to them. That is the second of the two things, turned around: not nobody else, but somebody.

## Who is on the list

Everyone written about in this series is a self-identified Targeted Individual. That means they said so in public, and it is the only basis for the listing. I cannot verify it, and neither can a reader. Some people who say it are not survivors, and some are the people doing the harassing, so a post here is not a vouch for anybody and should not be read as one. I am not responsible for what anyone named on this list does or says, on the app, on Quora, or anywhere else. The closest anybody can get to finding a real Targeted Individual is through the app, which admits people one at a time through Unlock and has other mechanisms in place to filter actively. Even with those, there is no such thing as a hundred percent.
