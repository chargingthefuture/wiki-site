A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Why would a gang stalker want to date me

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Why-would-a-gang-stalker-want-to-date-me. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

I saved it two times.

## Wanting has nothing to do with it

The question assumes the person approaching you chose you. They did not. On Quora on July 27, 2026, I listed the approach beside the other arranged events: "This is why they stage altercations and various schemes around you. Why they send prostitutes to try and seduce you, etc."

Sent is the word that matters. On February 5, 2026, I wrote: "These people are prostitutes as well as stalkers," and that they take on whatever type is "directed by their handler." The person across the table is doing a job somebody else assigned.

In that July answer I named the one place attraction does come into it, and it is at the start, not in the approach: "If a predator is attracted to you and you do not date them or engage they will add you to the Target list." A refusal can put somebody on the list. What follows is not courtship.

## What it is for

On May 3, 2025, asked what a honeypot is, I answered in one line: "Extort you, fake crimes against them by you, smear/slander you to other people/the public."

On October 2, 2025, about an invitation into somebody's home, I wrote: "They will lure you into various 'acts' for extortion or for their slander campaign." Closeness is what makes each of these work. A claim about somebody carries further when the person making it can say they knew them. How a manufactured romance is run, and why I put it beside sexual assault, is in [A safe place to shower](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/a-safe-place-to-shower). Why answering a smear once it is out does not undo it is in [You cannot argue a smear down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/you-cannot-argue-a-smear-down).

A relationship can also be cover for a scheme further on. On July 26, 2025, I described a couple who "wanted me to know they were gstalkers but actively tried to hide it from the other TI," and guessed "they wanted to be covert so they could do some scheme against the other TI down the road."

## They give themselves away

Nobody has to catch them out. In that February answer I wrote: "all stalkers always reveal themselves as I have yet to run into a stalker that does not have erratic behavior. Or contradictory behavior. You will need to trust your instincts on it. You do not need to get them to do it, they just do."

My own answer to the approach is short. That same day I wrote: "I avoid honey pots." Declining needs no proof and no confrontation, and nothing about it has to be explained to anybody.
