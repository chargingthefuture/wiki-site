In what I have seen, where the parents take part, their children do too. I have not seen an exception.

It does not run the other way. Some children take part while one or both of their parents do not, and do not know.

## It starts long before high school

I have watched parents bring children into it as young as two. I was harassed by other children when I was five, and my targeting ran through school from there. [What about school?](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-about-school) is about that.

A child that age is not choosing anything. They are doing what the adults around them do and are rewarded for, the way a child learns anything else at home. What the adults are given for it is in [Count the recruits](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/count-the-recruits): favors, work, housing, rides.

## Why a teenager seems to know so much

A teenager who knows a lot has usually been taking part for years. What looks like knowledge at sixteen is a habit that started before they could have understood it.

## When it is not the parents

A child who takes part while a parent does not has learned it from somebody. I have not seen who, and I will not guess.

## What to do with it

The same as with anybody taking part. Write down what happens, with the date, as it happens. [ClickLog](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/click-log) takes about fifteen seconds an incident, and your notes are not shown to anyone else here.

Do not confront them, and a child least of all. The plainest method they have is provocation: an event built to get a reaction, and the reaction is then used as proof of whatever was already being said about you. A scene with a child in it is the one that reads worst for you afterward. [How I can help](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/how-i-can-help) has the rest of it.
