In what I have seen, both: parents, and strangers.

## Parents

Where the parents take part, their children do too. I have not seen an exception. I have watched parents bring children into it as young as two.

A child that age is not choosing anything. They are doing what the adults around them do and are rewarded for, the way a child learns anything else at home.

## Strangers

It does not run the other way. Some children take part while one or both of their parents do not, and do not know. Those children are taught by strangers.

The recruiting runs on slander. Somebody is told a story about the target, is swayed by it, and is offered something for acting on it: sex, drugs, a job. I wrote that list down in [My call to action](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/chargingthefuture/Call-to-Action) in January, and [Count the recruits](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/count-the-recruits) has more of what is handed out. The research on the Stasi, East Germany's secret police, found the same about the people who informed for it: some did it for free, some for the enjoyment, and some for favors.

## Adults first

My targeting started at five, and it was adults first. Then the children.

That is why it was obvious. An adult stranger "recognizes" a five-year-old, has a deep hatred for them, and does not know them. That is perverse. It is also obvious, and it looks like pedophilia.

The targeting ran through school from there. [What about school?](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-about-school) is about that.

## Why a teenager seems to know so much

A teenager who knows a lot has usually been taking part for years. What looks like knowledge at sixteen is a habit that started before they could have understood it.
