Quora runs a bot called Quora Prompt Generator. It writes questions that no person asked and posts them for people to answer. A question that exists is a page, and a page with answers is something the platform can show.

Some of those questions are about Targeted Individuals.

## Four of seventeen

For the last few weeks I have been answering, here, questions I once opened on Quora and never finished. Before writing each one, I check who asked it. Of the first seventeen, four were written by that bot:

> Is it possible for targeted individuals to stop being harassed by gang stalkers? If so, what methods have been successful in stopping the harassment?

> What is the longest period of time someone has been a target of gang stalking?

> How can I build a support network to help me manage the stress and fear associated with feeling gang stalked?

> How can someone identify if they are being targeted by "gang-stalkers"? What steps should they take if they suspect they are being followed or watched by strangers?

Those are the words the bot chose. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the vocabulary I use instead.

They are not bad questions. They are the questions survivors ask. The platform knows what we ask closely enough to write it for us.

## Fifty-six

Quora has erased my accounts fifty-six times. The standing record of every handle, when it opened and when it was banned, is [Old links, new links](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/old-links-new-links). Some lasted days. Some lasted minutes.

I do not advocate violence. What I write on Quora is what I write here: what is being done, what the people it is done to can do about it, and where to find each other.

So the platform generates questions about Targeted Individuals, addressed to Targeted Individuals, and removes the Targeted Individuals who answer them. The questions are wanted. The people who can answer them are not.

## What I do with the questions now

A question is answered here when a person asked it. A bot's question is answered only when people have been asking the same thing in their own words, which the first one had, so I answered it. The other three I leave alone.

The answers live on this blog now, at addresses that do not get erased. Quora gets a short version and a link back. The conversation under each post happens in [Fireside](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/fireside), in the app.
