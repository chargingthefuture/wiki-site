A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> What is the most difficult part about being targeted by gang stalking and or organized stalking activities

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/What-is-the-most-difficult-part-about-being-targeted-by-gang-stalking-and-or-organized-stalking-activities. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

I cannot rank the parts for anybody else. What is done is chosen person by person, and no two Targeted Individuals are handed the same list. Whether any of it ends is in [What can be stopped](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-can-be-stopped), and how long we hold is in [How long do we hold?](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/how-long-do-we-hold). This answer is about one difficulty that runs under the rest.

## It is built to take the center

Every piece of it asks to be noticed. A stranger who turns up again, a car that keeps needing repairs, a word from a private conversation repeated by somebody nearby. Each one is small, and each one asks the target to stop and work out what it meant. Together they are arranged so the targeting becomes what a life is organized around: what gets talked about, what gets planned for, what is still running through a person's head at night.

That is the hardest part to answer, because no single piece of it can be refused. The center is taken a little at a time. Nothing about this is a failing in the person it happens to. It is what the arrangement is for.

## What moves it back

On September 20, 2025, asked how we can get the most out of our lives, I wrote that it "won't end anytime soon," and then: "begin networking with other TIs. TIs are some of the most skilled talent." And: "Not going it alone is the best way." I called that centering yourself rather than them.

Two days earlier, on September 18, 2025, I wrote: "There are several TIs committed to 'starting over.'" I read that as refusing to accept what is done, "But at the same time letting go of the past and building a community among each other." I suggested relationships with other survivors where the talk covers more than the targeting, and wrote: "Rather than fixate on the past atrocities. We are coming together to figure out how to survive, focusing on the present."

The same day as the first answer, asked what helped me the most when I found out, I answered: "Meeting in person and building relationships with other TIs. I met a TI who taught me about healthy clinical trials." That was work, and I wrote it was the only job I had found where interference reached me less. Everywhere else a target ends up job-hopping, mobbed out of one workplace after another ([It happens at work](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/it-happens-at-work)).

A conversation with another survivor about clinical trials, a job or a skill is a conversation the targeting did not set. Each one is a piece of the center taken back.

## Where that happens now

The Directory lists survivors by what they can do, so a conversation can start from a skill rather than an incident. Being on it costs nobody an account. Commons is the group chat. The PeerProgramming goal board is where a member posts one goal with a finish line and other members take small tasks toward it.

None of this makes the targeting less. It changes what else is in the day.
