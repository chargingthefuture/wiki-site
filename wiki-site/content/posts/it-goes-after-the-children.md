A question on Quora, saved on November 1, 2025:

> Does gang stalking go after your kids?

The question is at https://www.quora.com/Does-gang-stalking-go-after-your-kids. While nobody has answered it, Quora serves it at https://www.quora.com/unanswered/Does-gang-stalking-go-after-your-kids instead. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

## Yes

I do not have children. So it went after the children of my friends. The ones who would not take part against me were bullied at school, and in the end every one of them gave in and went along. The people running it were disappointed I had no children of my own to bully or to bring in.

Children reach it two ways. Some are targets: mine started when I was five, and [Your children can have an option](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/your-children-can-have-an-option) was written for survivors raising children who are targeted too. Others are brought in to take part. Where the parents take part, their children do too, from as young as two, and where they do not, strangers do the recruiting ([Who teaches them](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-teaches-them)).

## School

School is where a child is away from the parent all day, in a place the parent does not control, among adults the parent did not choose. For a family that is being followed, that is a real exposure. Homeschooling needs an adult present through the day, and removing a parent's income and hours is the method. [What about school?](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-about-school) goes into both halves of that.

## A child sent after you

Sending a child to do this is abuse of the child, by the adult who sent them. If a child is sent after you or after your own children, write down what happened, with the date, as it happens. [Sending a child is abuse](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/sending-a-child-is-abuse) says more. [ClickLog](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/click-log) takes about fifteen seconds an entry, and your notes stay yours.

## What it does not decide

No five-year-old deserves to be trafficked. The work a working economy needs is mostly still open, and a child can grow into any of it. I did not get a choice about the neighborhood I was born into. In two generations, a child could.
