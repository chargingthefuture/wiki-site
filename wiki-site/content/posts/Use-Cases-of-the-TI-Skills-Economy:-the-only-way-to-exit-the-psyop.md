I, Farah, am the creator of the app. But I am not the "leader" of any kind. Every TI has the full autonomy to use the TI Skills Economy app as a marketplace to connect with TIs to exchange what they need and want. And I will accept feedback on how to improve this facilitation. I will not deny any TI use of the platform - barring egregious behavior. In fact, you are not required to exchange anything with me or interact with me. It is a peer-to-peer marketplace.

The following highlights three of the mini-apps/services available: Directory, Workforce Recruiter, and Socket Relay.

Directory - create a skills profile. List all your talents, skills, and work experience in a Directory listing. Why? Now the ~5 million TIs globally can search for other TIs to exchange or do business with. For example, say you are a vet. The Speceterati will poison and torture a TI's pet. They will need to find someone who is not part of the psyop. You list in your profile that you are a vet. And now other TIs can contact you directly and pay you or barter for pet care.

Workforce Recruiter - everyone can use this as it has multiple purposes.
To have a true economy and truly thrive as Targets, we need a population with a variety of skills - every population needs this. Think about it, the Specterati are successful at human trafficking because they have people at all points of the economy/class participating globally. From the homeless to billionaires. Anecdontally, it appears that all TIs are highly skilled. But let us not worry. For example, Singapore has a population of ~5 million people, and its economy is primarily service-based. That being said, Workforce Recruiter will inform us as to what gaps we need to fill to sustain true autonomy. And allow TIs to step up and fill the need. No more guessing or spreading misinformation, or having no hope about our circumstances. The data is there; either we take collective action, or we fold.

Workforce Recruiter Scenario one: "I am a 19-year-old TI. I am at a loss because the Specterati will not promote me or train me. How can I ever have a fulfilling career?" Workforce Recruiter will help you find a plethora of career options. It is broken by economic sector, job titles and skills. Giving a TI NO (lol) reason to say they cannot find anything or cannot contribute to the TI economy. If you have no idea what job you would be good at, filter by skills. And the app will list job titles and the economic sector they are linked to. The only work to on your end is to tell the app what you are either good at or skilled at. And the app will dump a list of suggestions of what you can pursue, and if there are enough TIs to do the work. AKA supply and demand.

Workforce Recruiter Scenario two: "I am a mechanic who owns my own shop. The Spectearti mobbed my business - I lost all my customers, they stole all my tools, and I had to lose the shop as I could no longer afford it." Use Workforce Recruiter to rebuild your business. Find where TIs need a mechanic and find apprentices or employees - people with skills that match up to mechanics and shop workers.

SocketRelay - The "Craigslist for TIs." It is a modern take on Craigslist for TIs. Keeping it high touch and transactional (to eliminate perps as fast as possible). Any TI can make a post requesting something or offering something.

SocketRelay Scenario two: The mechanic above needs tools to get their new mechanic shop up and running. This time around, the mechanic will do mobile car repair. They make a post on SocketRelay asking for tool donations, OR they will pay for some of the tools.
