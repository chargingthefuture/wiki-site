Some time ago I read a post on Quora by a self-identified Targeted Individual. I did not save the link, so this is my memory of it rather than their words: they wrote that you used to know who these people were — it was the feds — and now nobody has any idea who is doing it.

What stayed with me was not the theory. It was the shape of the situation. Here was somebody who had known about targeting, had known somebody else was being targeted, and had — I will not say decided — effectively ignored it. And then it came for them, and now they are in a panic, asking who these people are.

There is a well-known line about this shape. Martin Niemöller, a German pastor, said it after the Second World War, and the wording varies from telling to telling: first they came for one group, and I said nothing, because I was not one of them. Then they came for another. Then they came for me, and there was no one left to speak. I learned it at school when we studied the war, and it is the same thing.

## Thirty-four years of ignoring them

I am going to use myself as the example, on purpose. I do not want this to read as though ignoring is a failing that the person on Quora has and I do not.

I ignored them from age five to thirty-nine. That is a long stretch, and none of it changed anything.

During those years people would say things to me. I am proud of you for not reacting to them. I am proud of you for not becoming a terrorist. I am proud of you for not killing any of them. The people who say that are Specterati operators themselves — the [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the vocabulary — and I find it repulsive. They say it and I let it pass. In my opinion it is the wrong stance, and there is nothing to be had from arguing with it.

The first time one of them punched me in the face came after exactly that: years of no reaction, at the end of one of their cycles of extreme harassment. Their methods escalated to violence because the quieter ones were not producing the result. When you are a child the harassment runs around the clock too, but the impact is smaller and it is done less openly, because done to a child it starts to look like what it is, and the deniability goes. Doing it to an adult is still perversion.

So I was not reacting, they hit me anyway, and after that the compliments about my restraint started. Thirty-four years. It meant nothing to them. They went on to twenty-four hours a day regardless.

## Why ignoring is the natural response, and why I did it

It would be easy to read this as criticism of the person on Quora, or of anybody who has turned a blind eye. It is not. Ignoring is what a person does, and I did it for most of my life for reasons I still hold.

One: do your best not to become one of them. Do not internalize it, and do not turn into the thing that is being done to you. That is my personal strategy, and a lot of the ignoring was that.

Two: I have no desire to take part in a global human trafficking network. I am completely fine being rejected by their space. Ignoring them was a way of declining to enter it.

Both reasons still stand. What does not stand is the idea that ignoring is enough.

## What the person on Quora proves

Somebody knew about targeting, ignored it, and became a target anyway. That settles two things.

First, it finishes the lie about deserving it. The line that a Targeted Individual did something to merit this is said by agents of crime, and an agent of crime saying somebody should be targeted carries the weight of a car salesman saying the stranger who just walked in deserves a brand-new car, whether or not they can afford one. Rebecca L. Norrington — an acquaintance, never met, no relationship to speak of — wrote to me to say that I deserve to be trafficked and that she enjoys it. Of course she advocates the abuse of other people. She is one of the people doing it.

Second: they cannot be ignored. You do not get the choice. Not as a matter of principle — as a matter of what happens.

## What "cannot be ignored" means

Think of how people deal with a parasite or a disease. You try to stay away from it. But staying away is done one of two ways: coexistence or elimination. A vaccine, a cure, a way of surviving the encounter with the damage limited — or, sometimes, wiping the thing out. People generally choose coexistence. So do I.

I want to be exact here, because it is easy to misread. I am not advocating that Targeted Individuals go out and cause harm. And I am not saying that a Targeted Individual who harms a criminal in self-defense is a terrorist. I want that very clear. They ran me over with a car. They run up to me and punch me in the face. Self-defense is not what I take issue with; I believe in it. Every Targeted Individual's threat model is different, and some of them have to account for the fact that the operators already escalated to violence because everything else was not working.

Coexistence over conspiracy, then. I could spend my life on which organization it is. I would rather build a way to live alongside a threat that is not going away.

There is also a narrower thing I do not mean. When I say you cannot ignore them, I am not talking about the small disruptions in a day. We are outnumbered, it runs around the clock, it is in the digital space and the physical one, and a person cannot answer every one of them and still have a life. Those you recognize and let go. What cannot be ignored is the lifelong thing.

Some people had COVID with no symptoms. They got to ignore it. It still had to be dealt with, because it was landing on everybody else, and the same is true here. Human traffickers are no different from terrorists in the one sense that matters: a threat to life has to be planned against, and it cannot be waved away.

## What the Skills Economy is for

It is my belief that as Targeted Individuals we do not, one at a time, have the tools to not ignore them properly. I did not. So I built the thing that gives those tools to all of us at once.

My threat model is physical safety. For me, living among like-minded people, with a gate, is coexistence — it is my way of not ignoring them. A lot of people hear a closed community and think isolation. It is not isolation. It is survival, and it is living with people who understand. I do not believe in integrating with their economy. It is counterintuitive, because a barrier looks like looking away. It is the opposite. It is full recognition of the problem, followed by something done about it.

The other threat is destitution. They take your employment. They have decided your labor is worth nothing to them, and the harsh reality is that your skills are extremely valuable. The Skills Economy is how that threat gets mitigated: you trade with other people who need what you can do and can do what you need.

Two threats, two answers, and neither of them is ignoring anything.

## For the people who are doing well

I want to say something to Targeted Individuals who are doing well — in quotation marks, because that is whatever it means to you.

Stay connected to other Targeted Individuals. I have watched what happens otherwise. One person did not realize how badly they were outnumbered, because although operators knew they were targeted, not many of them bothered to do it to their face. A lot can be done from a distance. Disrupting somebody's employment does not require ever being in the same room as them. Norrington had to write to me to tell me what she had been doing, because I had no other way of knowing. So this person, seeing fewer people, concluded there were fewer people, and that their targeting was not that bad.

In one way that is good. Less stress, less anxiety. I deliberately do not point out everything, because it is not healthy to. The downside is that a person in that position can fall into destitution, and once they are there, there is nobody around to help. You do not know when a series of events will arrive at the extreme one. The person on Quora is the example: it fell to that point, and now they are asking how to recover. You cannot recover from being a Targeted Individual. Once your name is on the list it is never taken off.

So even if you are doing well, the Skills Economy thriving and available is still a benefit to you. Keep at least one leg in the door.

## And the next generation

I have written before about [children](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/your-children-can-have-an-option). At five years old I had no options. I could not run away — I knew nothing about the world, I would have been kidnapped, I would have been trafficked in the way most people picture the word. So I was stuck, with family and with the community, and there was no leaving it as a minor without severe consequences to my own safety.

The Skills Economy succeeding is what makes an actual option exist for the next child in that position.

They cannot be ignored. Not because I am telling you not to. Because it does not work, and I have thirty-four years that say so.
