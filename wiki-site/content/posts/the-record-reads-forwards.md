The blog has a new page: https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/record

It is called The Record, and it answers a question the feed cannot. The feed runs newest-first and answers what is being said now. The Record runs oldest-first and answers how long it has been said, how much of it there was, and how many places it reached.

## What is on it

The writing I scattered across Quora — not the posts to my own spaces, which are the blog's own archive material, but the writing that lived on other people's pages: answers under their questions, comments under their answers and posts, submissions to their spaces.

As I write this, the page counts 632 entries, under 149 different questions, across 48 spaces, spanning 22 months — from October 2024 to today. The page computes those numbers live from the files it reads, so by the time you look they may have grown.

That kind of writing never reads as a body of work while it is happening. Each piece sits alone under somebody else's question, and no one — including the writer — sees the whole. Put back in order, it is the whole: the better part of two years of showing up, answering, and building in public, day over day.

## Why the stats matter

Anyone can say they have been at something for two years. The Record does not say it. It shows it, entry by entry, each one dated, each one carrying the address it originally lived at.

Those original addresses print as plain text, never as links — every one of them is dead. Quora destroyed the two accounts that held this writing, and the page draws both takedowns across the timeline at the date they happened. What the timeline shows next is the point: the entries continue past each one. The dated running list of every erased account stays on [old links, new links](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/old-links-new-links); The Record deliberately carries no such count, because that number moves and this page is about the thing that does not — the span.

Consistency is the one credential nobody can fake backwards. A new account can be opened in an afternoon. Twenty-two months of dated writing under other people's questions cannot. When you are deciding how much weight to give what someone builds and says, how long they have kept at it — visibly, checkably — is worth more than any single claim they make. That is the stat this page exists to show, and it is why I keep every number on this blog checkable rather than asserted.

## How it fits

The pipeline is the same one the whole blog runs on now. The text on The Record is the raw export from Quora's own records, restored file by file; as each entry is copy-edited it is replaced in place, and the page shows the edited wording the moment that happens. Everything is in the public repository with its history, so the restoration itself is on the record too.

Read it forwards, from the first entry. That is the way it happened.
