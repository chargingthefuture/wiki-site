A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> What should targeted individuals avoid

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/What-should-targeted-individuals-avoid. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

## What they ask

On Quora on September 20, 2025, I wrote: "If you don't want to share what they are doing. The default should always be don't do what they ask."

That is most of the answer. A trap usually arrives as a request: come over, lend this, try this, meet here. You do not have to work out which scheme a given request belongs to before declining it. No is enough on its own.

An invitation into the home of somebody known to be taking part is one of those requests. On October 2, 2025, I answered a question about exactly that, and I could not think of one reason it would be necessary to go in.

## The fight

Some requests are for a reaction. On December 14, 2025, I wrote that people taking part have tried to entrap me in violent crimes to get me arrested, and that they encourage me to fight them.

Avoid the fight even when it is offered as justice. The provocation is staged so that the reaction is what gets recorded ([The reaction is what gets written down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-reaction-is-what-gets-written-down)).

## The steering

Not every push is a dare. Much of it is a nudge toward a choice that was made for you. On January 6, 2026, I wrote: "you need to be vigilant that you are not being entrapped in schemes. And focus on doing what you want. Everything they do is about manipulation. You are in control regardless of what they say."

The test is simple: whose idea was it? If a plan arrived from somebody taking part, set it down and go back to your own.

## Labels nobody can check

On July 2, 2025, answering somebody who described an AI speaking through their mouth, I wrote: "You should instead describe specifically what is happening to you and others can explain the examples of the technology that might be used on you."

A label invites an argument about the label. A description, with a date and a place, can be compared with what other survivors have written down. [ClickLog](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/click-log) keeps that description: one tap for the date, the note when you are somewhere you can think. Once a date and place are logged, nobody can change them later ([ClickLog: your record, and the record](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/clicklog-your-record-and-the-record)).

## Being alone with them

On December 30, 2025, I wrote: "they want families apart and relationships broken - they want isolation to create an unsafe environment." Every request above points the same way.

So avoid isolation, and that does not mean every survivor has to be your friend. On December 14, 2025, about a Targeted Individual who had blocked somebody: "Just because someone else is a Target does not mean you will get along with them. And that is not a bad thing."

What matters is who is around you. On January 6, 2026, I wrote: "the more we as TIs surround ourselves with other TIs the more control we have. As we are not surrounded by their operatives." The [Directory](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory) lists survivors by what they can do, so the next ride, repair or conversation can come from somebody who is not taking part. Being on it costs nobody an account.
