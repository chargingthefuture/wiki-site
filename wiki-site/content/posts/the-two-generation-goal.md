The [last post](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/from-them-to-me) ended at a turn: nine months studying the people running the operation, then a shift to building for us, and the next person can start where the turn ended. This post is about where the building is pointed — the goal I have already set, and the stretch goal past it.

## The goal that is set: 384

The 384 goal carries two meanings, and both are set.

The first is a product benchmark. I estimate five million survivors globally, and the app has to be built to hold that. 384 is the benchmark on the way: if the app carries 384 people trading at once, the odds of the infrastructure and features breaking at a million are that much lower, and the product has proven it is more than a niche — it is solving a real-world problem. For this meaning, 384 real versus 384 mixed real and fake is not a deal breaker: an operative on the app is momentarily adding value, and the real members and I get something out of even that.

The second meaning is personal, and stricter. The app does not verify anyone, so no screen can award this one. My personal goal is 384 people that I personally know are real, trading with each other at any given time — because I want to exit the psyop, and only real people belong in the economy I exit into. Fake ones mess up an economy.

The [manifesto](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/chargingthefuture/The-Answer:-EXIT-THEIR-ECONOMY,-EXIT-THE-PSYOP) set the number in January: my goal is to connect with the 384 who are ready. The day 384 real people are trading at once, this is a working economy by anyone's definition. That is the milestone.

## The stretch goal: two generations

Now the goal past that one.

W. E. B. Du Bois and his cohort — the generation that came up directly out of the transatlantic slave trade, an organized trafficking of millions — laid an economic foundation in one generation: their own schools, their own businesses, their own banks, their own press. Results in place of argument. The manifesto already stands on their method, in their own words, and it says there what stays true here: we do not compare our pain to theirs — we take the method.

Estonia is the other proof. 1.3 million people, occupied by a power that outnumbered them by more than a hundred to one and surveilled them through an informant network that reached into every town, with mass deportations to enforce it. They kept their language, kept their identity, took their country back, and continue today — a functioning state whose playbook I already study for this project's own [baseline](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/chargingthefuture/TSE-Baseline).

## The two extremes, at once

Those two histories are not decoration. They bracket the extremes the modern-day Targeted Individual (TI) lives in.

Being outright trafficked is the first. It happens in this community — the Forced Homecoming scheme is exactly that, and the last post records it running to fruition in my own life. Facing outsized technology built to surveil and harass, while outnumbered by the harassers by headcount, is the second. That is the ordinary condition of every TI reading this.

One group faced the first extreme and built in a generation. One group faced the second extreme and outlasted a superpower. We face both at once. So the stretch goal is two generations — the ordinary kind, twenty-five to thirty years each: what took them one, we accomplish in two. Our own economy, our own institutions, our own public record, built by people who train with their community.

And the finish line has a face: the third-generation TI. Their targeting starts, and from that moment they can exit — skipping the destitution, the sexual assault, the rape, the financial drainage that come from the Specterati. Being a TI, for them, is close to not being one. No claim that those harms cannot happen outside the operation — they can, to anyone.

If those two groups can do it, so can we.

## Now, not later

A goal measured in generations can be misread as a sentence: do the work now, for strangers later, and suffer until death. Nothing would be worse to preach, and it is not what this is. Skills Economy helps now. It exists to make life more tolerable today, for the people trading in it today — that is what the trading is. The two-generation goal is the same work continuing, not the reward finally arriving.

There is a message that circulates among us: give up, the world is headed to destruction, keep your head down. The counter to it is not an argument. It is a working economy you can use today.
