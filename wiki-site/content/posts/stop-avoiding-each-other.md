A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> How we can all put a stop to Gangstalking

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/How-we-can-all-put-a-stop-to-Gangstalking. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

Whether it can be stopped at all is in [What can be stopped](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-can-be-stopped), and why the people who know about it do nothing is in [Why nobody does anything](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/why-nobody-does-anything). This answer is about one word in the question: all.

## Who the all is

On September 20, 2025, asked what the government could do to prevent it, I answered: "History has shown that oppressed people must take responsibility for their own liberation. And then it is up to us to maintain the standard." And: "Like it or not, TIs will be the ones who put an end to this."

On December 13, 2025, asked where a frightened Targeted Individual in San Diego could find a group, I wrote that there are no direct services and peer groups appear not to exist, and then: "We as Targets have to liberate each other." On February 10, 2026: "It is not our fault, but it is our responsibility."

So the all in the question is us. Nobody outside is coming to do it, and the people inside the arrangement are paid to keep it running.

## The distance is worked on first

If the answer is survivors working with each other, then the first thing to break is survivors trusting each other. That is what happens.

On September 18, 2025, I described how it is done. When two Targeted Individuals are in contact, the people running it "will purposely mimic the TIs to them." I search for a product, the ads for it are pushed onto the other person's accounts, and one day that person turns up with it, exactly the way the people I know are taking part do. The aim, in the same answer, is that "you panic and think the other TI is" one of them, the same trick as convincing a person that everyone is in on it.

On October 11, 2025, I wrote about time spent with other Targeted Individuals. The people doing it would leave the person next to me alone and go at me in full, in that person's presence. Every Targeted Individual has been exposed to all of them, whether or not a given one acts on it. And then: "This is a prime example of why TIs should not avoid each other. It will not make your targeting less. You will just be more isolated for no reason."

Keeping away from another survivor protects nothing. The targeting stays where it was. The only thing that changes is that both people are more alone, and [isolation is the product](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/nearly-a-hundred-percent-isolated).

## Where it starts

The first step toward the all is the cheapest one: stop keeping the distance. A coincidence between two survivors is the one I would doubt last, because it is the one that is arranged. Judge another person by what they do, not by what turns up in their hands.

The Directory exists so survivors can find each other by what they can do. Being on it costs nobody an account; viewing it needs a sign-in. The Commons, the group chat, reads without signing in, so a person can listen to other survivors before saying a word. What to unite around once you are in the same room is in [Unite around what](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/unite-around-what).

None of this ends it on its own. Every other answer to the question needs it done first.
