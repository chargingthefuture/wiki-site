Nikki Martindale asked on Quora why nobody does anything when so many people know, and I answered in [Why nobody does anything](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/why-nobody-does-anything). The people doing something about it are mostly the people it is being done to.

A working economy needs people who can run things, as well as people who can do them. What survivors are building between themselves is no different.

## Nikki

Nikki Martindale (https://www.quora.com/profile/Nikki-Martindale-9), in Nebraska. The Directory profile is at https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory/profile/934fb894-bcdf-4b2a-96b4-1ff7b203a3bc.

The listing carries one specialization, business administration: keeping an organization running day to day. The schedule, the records, the paperwork, and knowing which of them is about to go wrong before it does.

## Why that matters here

The app keeps a catalog of 713 skills, the things a working economy of about five million people needs somebody to be able to do, grouped by sector. Read on October 2, the Directory's 171 people held 230 of them.

Most of what people offer on the list is a trade: a repair, a ride, a room, an hour of somebody's work. A trade done once is a favor. Done every week, for different people, it is a small business, and a small business needs somebody running it as well as somebody doing the work. That is the part that usually goes missing first, and when it does, good work stops reaching the people who need it.

## What this is not

This is not a request to run anything, for this project or for anybody on the list, and nothing here says Nikki will. Being on a list of what a person can do is not an offer of anything, and the Directory has never worked that way.

## How the listing came to be

I built it from what Nikki said about themselves in public and put it up under their name, which is how most of this list was made, one person at a time. They did not sign up, and nobody asked them first. That is why it is marked community-generated and carries my handle: so anybody reading it knows it is my account of somebody else's skills, and knows who to come to about it.

## The invitation

Claiming the listing takes a free account and a few minutes, and after that it is theirs — what it says, and whether it stays up at all.

Nothing else is required. Not using the rest of the app, not posting anything, not agreeing with a word I have written. No is a complete answer, and the listing comes down on request with no account involved in that either.

## This does not run one way

Nikki asked why nobody does anything. The people on this list are doing something, and the same listing that makes Nikki findable is what makes them findable to Nikki: a ride, a room, an hour of a trade they do not have, and a group chat of people who do not need convincing.

## Who is on the list

Everyone written about in this series is a self-identified Targeted Individual. That means they said so in public, and it is the only basis for the listing. I cannot verify it, and neither can a reader. Some people who say it are not survivors, and some are the people doing the harassing, so a post here is not a vouch for anybody and should not be read as one. I am not responsible for what anyone named on this list does or says, on the app, on Quora, or anywhere else. The closest anybody can get to finding a real Targeted Individual is through the app, which admits people one at a time through Unlock and has other mechanisms in place to filter actively. Even with those, there is no such thing as a hundred percent.
