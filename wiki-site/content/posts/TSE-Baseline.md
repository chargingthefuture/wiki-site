This is a working document as I update the TSE app's baseline: [chargingthefuture.com](https://chargingthefuture.com). Use it to learn my logic for what is implemented in the app. As well as an opportunity to provide feedback. I am only one person; multiple perspectives will make this economy better.

I am estimating 5 million TIs globally. The latest version of TSE uses Singapore as an example of where to start when it comes to facilitating an economy. I am now doing further research, and Finland might be a better option.

Examples of useful country targets with ~5M population (open data availability)
- Denmark (~5.9M) — excellent open stats (Statistics Denmark), strong circular policies.
- Finland (~5.5M) — high-quality datasets and open data portals.
- Norway (~5.4M) — rich public data (Statistics Norway).
- Slovenia (~2.1M), Latvia (~1.9M), Estonia (~1.3M) — smaller but very open data ecosystems and strong digital infrastructure (good for pilot scaling).
- Subnational regions: many cities/metro areas worldwide have populations of ~5M and publish detailed open data (transport, waste, land use).

Of the countries above or subnational regions, I propose the baseline of Finland (national) and two high-quality city/regional datasets (Greater Helsinki and Tallinn metro areas) as the primary model. This combination more closely aligns with creating a baseline for a hybrid (virtual and in-person) circular economy of human trafficking victims: some gated communities of survivors globally. And some that interact in a dispersed manner throughout the globe.

On March 29, 2026, I came across a writing about [Estonia](https://e-estonia.com/solutions) and its digital transformation. I will begin researching their implementation to see if there is anything we can integrate into TSE.

This document will be updated in the near future with more information.
