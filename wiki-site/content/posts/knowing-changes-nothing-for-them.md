A question I saved on Quora in November 2024 and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> What do gang stalkers do when they find out you know theyre gang stalking you

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/What-do-gang-stalkers-do-when-they-find-out-you-know-theyre-gang-stalking-you. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

The question expects a turning point: the moment they realize they have been seen, and something shifts. There is no such moment.

## Nothing changes for them

On Quora on December 15, 2024, I answered a question about what happens when the effort to make a Targeted Individual doubt themselves does not work. I wrote that if one method fails, "they try other methods, opting for the one that leaves the least amount of evidence." And: "What you think of yourself is not relevant to them."

The same answer went on: "they may know, that you know, you are not crazy." It does not stop anything. I ended it this way: "Rather than consume yourself with thinking that it will end if they know you know."

Your knowing was never what they were working against. Once somebody is on the list, the same schemes run in rotation regardless of who started it ([It Does Not Matter Who Put You on the List](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-put-you-on-the-list)). Nothing in that arrangement depends on whether the target has noticed.

## Telling them gains nothing

The next thought is usually to say it out loud, to a neighbor or to the person who keeps turning up. On October 2, 2025, asked how to explain to the people doing it why they were really sent, I answered: "You can outright tell them. I bet, however, they are not going to care."

Winning one over does not help either. In the same answer I wrote that if you did, "they will be replaced by 10 more" as soon as the person directing them finds out. The one in front of you is interchangeable, and the list is not theirs to take you off.

A confrontation also hands them something. Much of this is provocation: you are pushed until you react the way anybody would, and the reaction is what gets written down ([The reaction is what gets written down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-reaction-is-what-gets-written-down)). In the December 2024 answer I described the next step: "they will start staging physical altercations," with somebody sent to provoke a fight. "Yes it is about getting you in jail." A target who calls somebody out in public has given them the opening they were waiting for.

## Where your time goes

So the answer to what they do is: the same as before. The useful question is what you do.

In that October 2025 answer I wrote: "My suggestion is to invest zero time" in them. "You should invest time in yourself." And: "You need to stay focused."

Time spent arguing with a person who is paid or permitted to be there goes nowhere. Time spent with people who are not taking part does. The Directory lists survivors by what they can do, so somebody who needs a skill can find a person who has it without going through anybody else. Being on it costs nobody an account, and the people on it are doing rather than saying.
