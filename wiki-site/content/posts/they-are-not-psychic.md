A question on Quora, asked by Scott Rowell (https://www.quora.com/profile/Scott-Rowell-26) on November 24, 2024:

> Are gang stalkers psychic?

The question is at https://www.quora.com/Are-gang-stalkers-psychic. Its page carries 4 public followers and 721.9 thousand views, which is a lot of people arriving at the same question. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the vocabulary I use for the people doing this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

![The question on its Quora page, captured September 22, 2026. The heading reads: Are gang stalkers psychic? Below it, under Asked by, the name Scott Rowell. Under Question stats: 4 public followers, 721.9K views, last followed Jul 10.](images/scott-rowell-question-2026-09-22.jpg)

No.

That is the short answer, and the rest of this is about why it looks like something else, because the appearance is real and worth taking apart rather than waving off.

## They watch, around the clock, in both places

The surveillance runs all day and it runs in two places at once: on foot, and online. Neither half is exotic. A person on a street can be followed by people who take shifts. A person on a phone or a laptop can be read by anybody with access to the accounts, the network, or somebody who works where the data sits.

Do that for years and you do not need a sixth sense. You need notes. I have written about what that produces in [Pizza is not my favorite food](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/pizza-is-not-my-favorite-food): they watched what I bought when I had almost no money, saw the same cheap hot meal over and over, and concluded it was my favorite food. They were wrong, and the way they were wrong is the proof. Somebody reading a mind would have known it was a budget. Somebody reading behavior guesses the reason and gets it backwards.

That post also has the other half of how the accuracy gets built: people work at it full time, the same operatives run several targets at once, patterns learned from one person get applied to the next, and they watch each other too.

## They arrange the thing they appear to have foreseen

The second reason is the one that gets missed, and it is the larger of the two.

A prediction is impressive when the person making it has no hand in the outcome. Take that condition away and it is not a prediction at all. These people run named schemes with intended outcomes, and the list of them is public at [chargingthefuture.com/schemes](https://www.chargingthefuture.com/schemes). A canceled ticket, a job that evaporates, a landlord who suddenly will not renew, a delay that makes you miss the thing you were going to — those are arranged, and somebody who arranges an event knows how it ends.

[One bus trip of mine ran at least seven of those schemes in sequence](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/one-trip-seven-schemes), and one of them canceled my ticket while I was sitting on the bus it was for. Anybody with a hand in that could have told you in advance where I would be standing that night. It is not foresight. It is scheduling.

The plainest version of it is provocation. Somebody runs up and punches me in the face, and afterward they knew I would react. That is not a reading of me. It is an event built to produce the reaction it then claims to have foreseen.

And the reaction gets used twice, which is the part to watch for. Once to look prescient. Once as evidence for the story they were already telling about me: see, I told you she is violent, I told you she threatens people. The same staged moment does both jobs, and [you cannot argue a smear down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/you-cannot-argue-a-smear-down) once it is running.

Then they tell you about it, before or after, because the telling is the point. [They are not a secret society](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/they-are-not-a-secret-society) — they advertise themselves constantly, and an arranged event announced in advance is the cheapest way to look like they know everything.

## Poverty narrows a life, and so does isolation

The third reason is the one that compounds, and it is the one I would want a survivor to understand best.

Take away somebody's income and their choices go with it. One hot meal they can afford. One route they can walk. One person who will still rent to them. A life with the options stripped out is a life that can be called in advance by anybody looking, because there is only one thing left to do at each turn.

That is what the [Forced Homecoming](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/why-they-want-you-living-with-family) produces on purpose: a person destitute enough that the only door left leads back to relatives who can be reached, in a place where everybody around them is taking part. Surrounded by operators, with no money and nowhere else to be, a person becomes extremely easy to predict — and every scheme run on them afterward gets cheaper, because the operative can be told in advance where to stand.

Destitution is not the only way that narrowing happens. A self-identified Targeted Individual, in a comment under a different question on Quora, said in substance that they were fortunate: they had progressed far enough in their profession that holding a job, and being fulfilled in it, was achieved. That is my paraphrase rather than their words. The knock-on effect is mine to add rather than theirs — destitution at the traffickers' hands never arrived for them. Isolation does the same work by a different route. When the people around somebody have been turned, or were placed there to begin with, their week runs through a short list of known names — who drives them, who they eat with, who they tell things to. A person with an income and four people left in their life is not hard to call in advance either.

[Isolation is the aim rather than a side effect](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/nearly-a-hundred-percent-isolated), and being easy to predict is one of the things it buys them.

So the accuracy people take for mind reading is mostly this. Years of watching, plus events they staged themselves, plus a life narrowed by poverty or by isolation until it has one obvious next move.

## Why the psychic story suits them

It is worth asking who benefits from the other explanation.

If they are psychic then they are unbeatable, and there is nothing to do but endure it and try to interpret it. That is exactly the position all of this is designed to put a person in. The same holds for every version of the story that lifts them out of the human category — a bloodline, an ancient order, something with God behind it. Those are the most flattering accounts of themselves available, and they do not have to circulate them, because survivors do it for them.

What they actually have is money, technology, a network, and the willingness to commit crimes. Those are real advantages. None of them is insight, and calling them insight does part of their work for free.

## What to do with this

Two things, and the second is the one that changes anything.

Write down what happens, dated, as it happens. [ClickLog](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/click-log) takes about fifteen seconds an incident and your notes stay yours. A record is what lets you see the pattern afterward, including the times they got you wrong, which is the part that shows where the limit is.

And work on the options, because options are material rather than a mindset. Somewhere to cook. An hour of a trade you do not have. A ride from somebody who is not reporting on it. Money that does not come through anyone doing this to you. Every one of those is a move they cannot call in advance, and every one of them is what this economy is for.

That is not a defense against being watched. Nothing is. It is a way of being harder to predict, and worth less to watch.
