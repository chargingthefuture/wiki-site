## Distance is not an exit

The question asks how to escape, and I do not know of a way. On Quora on September 19, 2025 I wrote: "And so you will never escape them." The next line was the useful one: "Not possible, better to focus on how to mitigate their impact on your life."

Moving does not do it. I have crossed borders and it came with me; operators worked on me in other countries and traveled from other countries to do it ([Move toward somebody](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/move-toward-somebody)). On Quora on January 22, 2026 I answered whether moving away works, and wrote that "by the time you are aware you are being gang stalked, it is too late." Ignoring it did not end it either: I ignored it from age five to thirty-nine and it changed nothing ([They cannot be ignored](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/they-cannot-be-ignored)).

[What can be stopped](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-can-be-stopped) is the closest answer on this blog to this question, and it is worth reading first. This post takes the other half of the question: what they hold.

## A file is not a leash

The question treats a name, a number and a birth date as the thing that keeps a person caught. On Quora on December 2, 2024 I wrote: "There is no removing your name." What they know about you is already known, and getting out from under it is not an exit I can offer.

But being watched is not what gives someone power over you. Needing something from them is ([Estonia did not get to solve it either](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/estonia-did-not-get-to-solve-it-either)). A file does not hand anybody your week. What hands it over is the employer who can be reached, the landlord who can be reached, the relatives who can be bought.

## Change whose hands it runs through

That part can be changed, one arrangement at a time. Who you work for, who rents you a room, who drives you: each is a choice about whose hands your week runs through, and each one moved is leverage taken off the table. That is what exit their economy means.

The Directory lists survivors by what they can do. LightHouse lists rooms offered by survivors to survivors. TrustTransport is for a vetted ride. None of it changes what they know. It changes what they can do with it.

Poverty also makes a person predictable, and a person with options is harder to read ([Pizza is not my favorite food](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/pizza-is-not-my-favorite-food)). Options are material: somewhere to cook, money that does not come through anyone doing this to you.

## Keep a dated record of your own

They keep a picture of you. Keep a dated record of your own. It means your account of your own life stops depending on memory under stress, and it answers a smear in a way an argument never does ([You cannot argue a smear down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/you-cannot-argue-a-smear-down)).

ClickLog is that record: a date, a location if you choose one, about fifteen seconds an entry. Your notes are private and nobody but you sees them. It does not stop an incident. Start it before the next one, so the before exists.
