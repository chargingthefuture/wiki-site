A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Im a targeted individual by gang stalking Why wont anyone help me 10

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Im-a-targeted-individual-by-gang-stalking-Why-wont-anyone-help-me-10. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

I saved it five times. I answered a question in the same words on September 18, 2025, and the answer below starts from that one.

## It is not that nobody believes you

The usual guess is that people do not help because they do not believe it. So the plan becomes better proof: another recording, another log, another person to show it to.

That is rarely the gap. In the manifesto I wrote: "Very few people think you are crazy, they are turning a blind eye or are in on it. Because both have an economic gain to do so" ([Exit their economy, exit the psyop](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/mono/EXIT-THEIR-ECONOMY,-EXIT-THE-PSYOP)). A person who looks away keeps what looking away pays. Evidence does not change that arithmetic, because they were never unconvinced.

## Some did not know what they joined

On September 18, 2025 I answered that it is orchestrated by organized crime, and: "it appears that some initially didn't know they were joining a crime ring."

Some went along with it before they understood what it was part of. Once a person is on the list, every criminal group in reach joins in, and the people around that person are inside its reach ([It Does Not Matter Who Put You on the List](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-put-you-on-the-list)). Asking them for help means asking people the arrangement already holds.

## Where help comes from

The same answer went on: "When ready, start connecting with other TIs. It helps to focus on building or finding a new community that accepts you."

That is not a consolation. It is where help has come from. In two years on Quora, three Targeted Individuals changed my life: one helped me get a job and not freeze, one helped me escape the Forced Homecoming scheme, one gave moral support and found others to start a gated community ([Two years to find three people](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/two-years-to-find-three-people)). None of that passed through an app; the help happened between people ([Nobody Calls to Tell You Your Project Is Dead](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/nobody-calls-to-say-its-dead)). None of the three was part of the arrangement.

On September 20, 2025 I wrote: "Not going it alone is the best way." On March 4, 2025: "You are not alone. You have us other TIs. Doesn't matter that we cannot physically be in the same space."

What stands between a survivor and that help is mostly finding each other. The Directory lists survivors by what they can do, so a person who needs a ride, a repair or somebody to talk to can find one without asking anybody inside the arrangement first. Being on the list costs nobody an account, and anybody listed can claim their entry or have it taken down. Viewing it needs a sign-in.

The people who would not help are not going to be argued into it. The ones who will are already looking for each other.
