A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> As a targeted individual how can you find out who made you a target

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/As-a-targeted-individual-how-can-you-find-out-who-made-you-a-target. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

Why the answer stops mattering past a point is in [It Does Not Matter Who Put You on the List](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-put-you-on-the-list). This answer is about the finding out.

## The people within reach did not start it

Most of the people you can see were brought in after your name was on the list, and each was given a reason. On July 30, 2025, asked how Targeted Individuals found out what lies were told to get them followed, I wrote: "they will tell various lies catered to different people to see what will convince them to harass you. It likely won't be possible to know the exact of how many."

Many of them do not know much past their own story. On February 4, 2026 I wrote that even the ones at street level do not know what technology is being used, and that I once watched two of them argue about it until one was left baffled. Asking them who started it means asking people who were handed a story.

## What slips out is the lie

What does come out, sooner or later, is the lie. In that same answer I wrote that at least once, one of them "will slip up and reveal the lie they've been told." Mine: "One of them told me I deserve what is happening to me because I go around town threatening people." The reverse is what happens: I am followed around town and fights are provoked.

That tells you what is being said, and that it is said differently to each person. It does not give you a name.

The lies reach the target too. On August 4, 2025, to somebody who had seen something online and concluded they were on a government watchlist, I wrote: "You're not on a government watchlist. That's just one of their lies." The answer handed to a target is one more story, fitted to the person hearing it.

## A name does not change the next step

On June 29, 2025, somebody wrote that they knew who started it, an ex, and asked how to prove it and undo the hacking. I answered the part that could be acted on: "Each technology/app has a different process. Did they compromise your email, your phone, laptop. All three are very different."

Knowing the name did not change what had to be done that week. The phone still had to be dealt with as a phone, and the email as an email.

## What you can find out

Who is around you now, and what they do, can be found out. It shows in the schemes they run, and the same schemes run in rotation regardless of who started it. Memory under stress does not hold that. A dated record does.

[ClickLog](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/click-log) logs an incident with one tap and the date, and the note and scheme tags can be added later. Notes are not shown to other members, and the date and location cannot be changed afterward. Thirty entries read back show where, when, and which schemes repeat ([ClickLog: your record, and the record](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/clicklog-your-record-and-the-record)).

The other thing worth finding is who is not taking part. The Directory lists survivors by what they can do, and being on it costs nobody an account.
