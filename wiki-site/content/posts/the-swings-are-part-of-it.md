A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> What does being targeted by gang stalkers feel like What are some signs that someone is targeting you

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/What-does-being-targeted-by-gang-stalkers-feel-like-What-are-some-signs-that-someone-is-targeting-you. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

This answer is about the first half: what it feels like.

## It is meant to be felt

None of it is hidden from the person it is done to. On Quora on November 13, 2025, asked whether cars matching the target's own are used, I wrote: "Yes. In the same way, they will wear the same clothes as you and repeat parts of your private conversations or online searches. Please do your best to ignore it; it is intended to drive you crazy. They want you to know that they are stalking you 24/7."

So the feeling is not a side effect. It is what the effort is aimed at.

## It does not stay at one level

What it feels like most is a ride. On November 2, 2025, somebody wrote that they were doing much better and seeing less of it, and asked whether that meant it would stop. I answered: "No. They torture people differently. One reason is that they are regrouping. Pretending to be 'out of sight' aka not overtly harassing you is them laying low so you put your guard down. And once they think you have go back to overt harassment. To create an emotional rollercoaster."

The quiet stretch feels like relief, and then like a verdict: maybe it is over, maybe it was never there. Then it comes back. In that same answer I wrote that "you 'doing better' has nothing to do with whether or not they stalk you." The quiet stretches vary with no pattern I have been able to read ([Move toward somebody](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/move-toward-somebody)).

## Kindness has to be weighed too

Even a kind gesture arrives with a question attached. On October 6, 2025, asked how to tell whether money handed over while panhandling was genuine, I wrote: "If you are a TI, how would you decide if any act of kindness is genuine?" And about a $20 payment for a fake online gig: "Whatever, just take it, is how I feel." If it is meant to lower your guard, "don't. And still take the money."

That is the texture of it. Nothing arrives neutral, not the pressure, not the quiet, not the help.

## A mood they set

A feeling that rises when they go quiet and falls when they return is a feeling running on their schedule. Every provocation is also waiting on a reaction, and the reaction is what gets written down ([The reaction is what gets written down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-reaction-is-what-gets-written-down)).

The answer I gave in November was not to watch harder: "This is not encouragement from me to start paying closer attention." It closed with "still stay vigilant." Both at once: know it can return, and do not hand them your attention while it is quiet.

Doing better is still worth doing. It just cannot be measured by how much of them you see.
