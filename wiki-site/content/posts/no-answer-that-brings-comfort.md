A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> What is the truth about targeted individuals

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/What-is-the-truth-about-targeted-individuals. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

I saved it three times.

On Quora on February 10, 2026, answering a comment in a space for Targeted Individuals, I began: "I do not have any answers that will bring comfort." That is still where an honest answer starts. The truth is plainer than most of what is said about it, and none of it is reassuring.

## It is a crime, and people do it

The same answer went on: "It is organized crime/human trafficking." On September 17, 2025 I wrote that it is organized crime and psychological torture, and that the people doing it outnumber the people it is done to, which makes it easy for them to spread misconceptions.

They are not one kind of person. On January 4, 2026 I wrote: "They are human traffickers, with operatives from the homeless to billionaires." Organized crime has a working definition, and it describes people working together, not a force ([What organized crime means](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-organized-crime-means)).

## It is old

In that January answer I also wrote: "The earliest writings about them that I personally found are from the year 1690." It is older than every device it now gets blamed on. The methods are people, money, permission and a story, and none of those were invented recently.

## It is large, and it lasts

On February 10 I put the number at "maybe 5 million present day, not counting those that passed." That estimate is global rather than American, and there is no line on a map where the list ends ([Move toward somebody](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/move-toward-somebody)).

It also lasts. On January 4, answering somebody who had been told it would stop once they were enlightened, I wrote that "there are targets who have survived 30+ years of Targeting." I have been targeted for more than thirty years myself ([Unite around what](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/unite-around-what)). A promise that it ends after some test is passed is one more thing the people doing it say.

## It is not the target's fault

The February answer had one more line: "It is not our fault, but it is our responsibility." The fault sits with the people who do it, all of it. Nothing a target did earned it, and no target can talk their way out of it.

The responsibility is what survivors can build with each other, because nobody else is building it. In that same answer, the only thing I could offer was "to join our community to form our own economy," where Targeted Individuals "exchange goods and services with each other." The reason is economic: the people doing this are paid inside an economy that boxes targets out, and money that moves between survivors does not reach them ([Exit their economy, exit the psyop](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/mono/EXIT-THEIR-ECONOMY,-EXIT-THE-PSYOP)).

The Directory lists survivors by what they can do, so the next person who needs a skill can find one. Being on it costs nobody an account. Foundation is where members offer and trade help directly.
