Most of a Targeted Individual's day is not theirs. It goes to the attacks and to what they force, and what is left at the end of it might be five percent. Some days it is nothing.

Five percent is not enough to reach a goal alone, because a goal is a chain of small steps and the attacks land on the chain. A step gets missed, the order breaks, and it starts over. It is enough when it is not all one person's. Twenty members with five percent of a day each is a working day.

In the new version of the app, that part of the day goes into asks on Today, the screen the app opens on. What used to be a separate goal board is now an ask card there, beside everything else members post.

## Posting an ask

Tap + New, then Ask, and write what you need. Give it a finish line: "get stable" is not something anybody can help with, and "get a forklift certificate" is, because everybody can tell when it is done.

Then break it into small tasks, up to thirty, each one small enough to do from a phone in under half an hour. Find three yards hiring near Dallas and add their numbers. Find three campers under six thousand dollars within two hundred miles of Tulsa. Ask one dealer whether they finance with no credit check.

The ask shows on Today for everybody, and answers come to your Activity. Your own card shows how many of its small tasks have been taken. When it is done, you mark it reached.

Everyone can read an ask, so keep times and places for the private conversation.

## Answering one

Open somebody else's ask and take one small task. Do it, and post what you found. One task is plenty, and it is complete on its own.

A task you took and could not finish goes back by itself after a day, and somebody else can pick it up. Nothing chases you. You take a task when you have the half hour, and you do not when you do not.

If you cannot do a task but know who can, say so. Every ask has two quick answers in place of most comments: I can help, and I know someone.

## No group chat, on purpose

Today has no group chat and no direct messages. Group chats for this have been tried three ways and none of them held: who shows up depends on how hard the day was, so the chat is a different room every time, and it slides from doing into comparing notes about the attacks.

Each ask carries its own conversation about that ask, public to start. A private conversation opens only after both people agree to it. Nobody has to explain their situation, prove anything, or keep up with a thread to help.

## Who decides it helped

The person who asked. When you post what you found, they mark it: it helped, keep it, or send it back. If it helped, it counts toward the day's 384, the members who delivered something to another member on one day, and toward the helper's Weavers of the Commons badge. Both are on the [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/chargingthefuture/Dictionary). Nobody else scores your work, and there is no number on anybody.

## Before the check

Writing in a card on Today is open from the day you sign in. After two weeks it waits on the check, unless you have already sent the address for it or asked for help finding it. The check is the web address of your own Quora profile, and a person reads it. Nobody is removed for being slow with it.
