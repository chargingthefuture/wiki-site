A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Why are targeted individuals targeted and destroyed

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Why-are-targeted-individuals-targeted-and-destroyed. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

The question has two halves, and only the second has an answer.

## Targeted: no criterion

There is no test a person failed. Somebody with access put a name on a list, and the same schemes run from there regardless of who started it ([It Does Not Matter Who Put You on the List](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-put-you-on-the-list)). Looking for the reason in yourself finds nothing, because it is not there.

## Destroyed: nothing is taken for use

The second half is worth asking, because what gets destroyed says what the arrangement is for.

Most crimes that take something from a person want the thing taken. This one takes the work apart first. References dry up, the role is restructured, the contract is not renewed, the business is driven to zero. In [How are you doing?](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/how-are-you-doing) I wrote: "If your labor were the valuable thing, nobody would spend years making sure you cannot do any."

It does not stop at work. On Quora on October 2, 2025, I wrote that "they will steal everything you have, assets, clothes, employment, housing, friends, family, anything." Some of that is kept by whoever took it. Most of it is not used by anybody. It is removed so the target does not have it.

So the product is not the labor and not the property. It is the damage. An arrangement that runs on damage has to keep producing it, which is why it is continuous rather than aimed at one goal that, once reached, would end it.

## Why it keeps going

Damage pays the people producing it. In [Why nobody does anything](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/why-nobody-does-anything) I put it plainly: for the people doing it, it is a livelihood, paid in a job, an apartment, a favor. On January 14, 2026, I described how that economy is kept closed: they "box out Targets. Similar to how they mob Targets out of spaces and employment."

What a participant stands to lose by walking away is that job, that apartment, that favor. Their life is never on the line. That is a reason they stay, not a justification for staying.

## What is not destroyed

Pushed out of every space, a target still has every skill they had. The skills were never what was wanted, so they were never what was taken. On September 20, 2025, I wrote: "TIs are some of the most skilled talent." In the same answer: "Not going it alone is the best way."

The damage is meant to keep those skills from reaching anybody. What undoes that is a place where they can be found by people who are not doing this. The Directory lists survivors by what they can do, and being on it costs nobody an account. Workforce counts those skills by occupation and level, so the capacity is visible. Neither returns what was taken. Both put what remains where somebody can ask for it.
