A question on Quora, from the export of drafts that were opened and never written. The export kept the question and not who asked it:

> Are their groups of TIs that meet like maybe Alcoholics Anonymous does? I need support n have nowhere to turn?

The question is at https://www.quora.com/Are-their-groups-of-TIs-that-meet-like-maybe-Alcoholics-Anonymous-does-I-need-support-n-have-nowhere-to-turn. While nobody has answered it, Quora serves it at https://www.quora.com/unanswered/Are-their-groups-of-TIs-that-meet-like-maybe-Alcoholics-Anonymous-does-I-need-support-n-have-nowhere-to-turn instead.

Yes. With one difference from AA: most Targeted Individuals cannot get to a room. A meeting in a place assumes money for the trip, a route that is safe to take, and an evening you can spare, and most of us are short of all three. So the rooms are on a phone.

## A room with a time on it

[Chyme](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/chyme) is a live audio room. You can open it and listen without an account. Signed in, you can speak and use the room's chat, and you start muted, so nobody hears you until you decide.

[TI Radio](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/ti-radio) is the schedule for it: seven days ahead in ninety-minute slots, each showing who is hosting and what it is about, printed in your own time zone. Reading it needs no account either. That gives you what a meeting has, a time to show up, and the slot running now is marked On air.

## Somewhere to talk any time

The [Commons](https://app.chargingthefuture.com) is a single group chat that everybody in the app shares. It is not on a schedule. You can read it before you have an account, and post once you have one.

## A small group, every week

[PeerProgramming](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/peer-programming) puts members into groups of about twelve each week, and each group meets by live video. Going is voluntary, and usually a few people show up and talk. It is the closest thing here to the same faces in the same chairs.

## What it is and is not

What makes AA work is that nobody in the room needs convincing. You do not spend the first hour of every conversation establishing that what you are describing is real. That is true in all of these.

None of them is a crisis line or a therapist, and nobody in them is paid to listen. They are other people who know what you are describing, which is what the question asked for.

Nowhere to turn is the condition this is built to end, and it does not end in one evening. It starts with a room you can sit in and listen.
