A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Why are gang stalkers so nosey

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Why-are-gang-stalkers-so-nosey. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

Some of the prying is fishing for something new. This answer is about the other kind: the question about something that has already happened.

## They already know what they did

A person who arranges an event knows how it ends ([They are not psychic](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/they-are-not-psychic)). So when a stranger asks about something that was done to you, they are not asking what happened. They know that part. They are asking how it went.

On Quora on December 11, 2024, I wrote that the less obvious ones online are "asking questions about stuff they have done to you to see if you noticed and/or mock you."

Both halves are a check. If you noticed, the scheme registered. If you did not, it can be run again, louder. Either way the question is how they find out.

## The answer is the result

On December 13, 2025, somebody asked why the people around them kept wanting to know whether they had a fever. Agreeing with Matthew Cappadocia (aka The Wizard of Oz) (https://www.quora.com/profile/Matthew-Cappadocia-aka-The-Wizard-of-Oz), I added that "they are also asking because they did something and want to see if it caused you to have a fever."

In that same answer I described something placed in the air, more than once, and wrote: "I could tell they would up the dosage on different occasions to see if I would get sick." A dose is adjusted by its result. The question about a fever is how the result is collected.

That is why the curiosity looks out of proportion. A stranger has no reason to care about your temperature, your sleep or your week. Somebody waiting on a result does.

## Give them nothing to read

The same answer said what to do: "At all times deny them attention, answers, your time, etc." And: "If they are asking you if you have a fever, do not answer or say no and move on."

An unanswered question returns nothing. No confirmation that a scheme registered, no measure of what it cost you, nothing to adjust the next one by. A shrug carries less than a story does, and a no carries less than a shrug.

Not answering them is not the same as forgetting. What happened still matters, and it belongs in a record that is yours rather than in a reply that is theirs.
