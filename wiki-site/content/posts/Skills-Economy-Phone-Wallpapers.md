The Skills Economy logo — the three ascending bars, for skills built step by step — is now a phone wallpaper. The design keeps the top of the screen clear so your clock stays readable, and the background is dark so notifications stand out.

## Where to download

The wallpapers live on the same GitHub releases page as the Android app:

[github.com/chargingthefuture/chargingthefuture/releases?q=wallpapers](https://github.com/chargingthefuture/chargingthefuture/releases?q=wallpapers)

Open the newest `wallpapers-v` release and pick the file that matches your phone:

| File | Fits |
|---|---|
| `se-wallpaper-android-1080x2400.png` | Most current Android phones |
| `se-wallpaper-iphone-16e-1170x2532.png` | iPhone 16e (also fits iPhone 13 and 14) |

## How to set it

On Android: tap the file to download it, open it from your notifications or Files app, save it to your photos, then long-press your home screen → Wallpaper & style → choose the image from your photos.

On iPhone: tap the file to download it, open it and save it to Photos, then go to Settings → Wallpaper → Add New Wallpaper → Photos and pick it.

If your phone's screen is a different size, either file still works — your phone will scale it to fit. More sizes can be added over time; the design source is in the open-source app repository, so anyone can render a new size.
