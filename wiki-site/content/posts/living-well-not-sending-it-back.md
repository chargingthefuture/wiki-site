A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> How do I send the hits back to gang stalking

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/How-do-I-send-the-hits-back-to-gang-stalking. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

What blocks or shields against the hits themselves is a separate question, and I have said I do not have that answer ([What protects you from energy weapons? I am asking](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-protects-you-from-energy-weapons)). This answer is about the other half of the question: sending it back.

## I know of no way to send it back

I do not know of any device or method that returns an attack to the people behind it. Anything I wrote here would be a guess, and a guess on this subject can cost people money.

What I can say is what the wish to send it back leads to, because I have watched it being encouraged.

## The push to strike back is part of it

On Quora on December 14, 2025, I wrote: "I have perps trying to entrap me in violent crimes in an attempt to get me arrested. And do encourage me to fight perps."

Read that as a plan. A target who strikes back, at whoever they believe is responsible, has done the one thing that makes the rest of the arrangement easy. An arrest is a record. A record is the proof the smear was waiting for. And a fight in public produces exactly the footage it was staged for ([The best way to deal with it, in order](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/best-way-to-deal-with-it)).

On January 4, 2026, asked how to start going after the people who had been doing it for five years, I answered: "I believe that part of the psyop is seeking revenge. Make you bend to their level. Living well is a better alternative."

None of that makes anger wrong. Anger at what is being done is accurate. What the arrangement is waiting for is the act, because it gives everybody a reason to look at the target instead of at what was done to them.

## Where the attention goes instead

The same answer started from a different place: "Rather than focus on them, I take the stance of focusing on myself/TIs instead. They mobbed us out of society; therefore, we should form our own."

That is a choice about attention. Every hour spent tracking down who to hit back is an hour still organized around them. On January 6, 2026, asked how to stop them controlling a life, I wrote: "You are in control regardless of what they say. And the more we as TIs surround ourselves with other TIs the more control we have. As we are not surrounded by their operatives."

So the practical version is plain. Log what happens, with the date, under its own name, so the record holds and your memory does not have to ([The DEW List](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/dew-attack-types)). Put what has actually helped you on WhatWorks, so the next person spends less finding out. And find survivors who can do what you need, through the Directory, rather than through people nearby who may be taking part.

None of it sends anything back. It takes away what striking back was meant to deliver ([Exit their economy, exit the psyop](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/mono/EXIT-THEIR-ECONOMY,-EXIT-THE-PSYOP)).
