It is unfair. You are right about that, and you are right that a lot of people know.

## It is a livelihood

Some of the reasons I would only be guessing at, and some I know and will not share here. What I will say is that for the people doing it, it is a livelihood.

Think of the transatlantic slave trade. It was free labor, and it was control, and the entire world economy benefited from it.

The people doing this launder money through the global economy. Placing their own people in positions is about control and money. Most of the people who know were brought in and paid for it: a job, an apartment, friends, a favor. [An epidemic, not a secret society](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/an-epidemic-not-a-secret-society) describes how that payment works.

## Why organized crime exists

Asking why nobody stops it is like asking why organized crime exists. It exists for the same reason it always has, and nothing about that is new.

Stopping it would mean dismantling what feeds them and houses them. It is not happening to most people, and for most people that is all that matters. A few believe what they were told and think they are fighting crime. Those are few and far between.

Martin Luther King Jr. wrote about this kind of person in his Letter from Birmingham Jail in 1963: somebody "more devoted to 'order' than to justice."

## Fair

W. E. B. Du Bois did not get a fair ending. Neither did Estonia. Most of what King wanted never happened. It is unfair, yes.

But there have been times when people chose to build alongside it rather than spend themselves on the conspiracy. King did not; he wanted integration. Du Bois and his cohort built their own schools, businesses, banks and press within one generation, which [the two-generation goal](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-two-generation-goal) goes into. Estonia built in the direction of needing its neighbor less, which [Estonia did not get to solve it either](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/estonia-did-not-get-to-solve-it-either) goes into. They chose a different path, and they succeeded.
