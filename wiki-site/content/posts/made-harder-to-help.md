A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Assuming gangstalking was real what could happen to an individual who tried to help someone suffering from it

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Assuming-gangstalking-was-real-what-could-happen-to-an-individual-who-tried-to-help-someone-suffering-from-it. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

I answered a question in the same words on July 13, 2025, and this answer starts from that one.

## It depends on the stage

On July 13, 2025 I wrote: "It depends at what stage the TI is within the stalking. Once it becomes overt, you’ll likely get “lightly” targeted." And: "From the looks of it, if you don’t fit a specific profile they likely won’t turn you into a target. They’ll just make it difficult for you to help the TI."

So the usual outcome for a helper is not that they become the next Targeted Individual. It is friction, aimed at the help itself.

## The pressure goes around the helper

The same answer said how: "They know you won’t turn on the TI so they’ll infiltrate the weak ones around you to mess with you in some way."

A helper who will not be moved is not worked on directly. The people near them are: a coworker, a relative, a neighbor, whoever can be reached with a story or a reward. On October 11, 2025, asked whether a lawyer could be trusted with an unrelated case, I wrote: "It is not even about being a lawyer. [They] harass and sabotage anyone who is helping you do anything. Most people are cowards and will turn their backs on you and walk away from helping."

It reaches work too. On January 19, 2026 I wrote that they "mob the Target and anyone that does not want to harass you out of businesses, resources and communities." A helper can lose a client, a shift or a place they used to be welcome, and nothing about it will look connected to the person they helped.

## A helper is not a participant

The people taking part risk things if they stop: a job, an apartment, a reputation. Never their lives. Nobody answers for what is done to a target, and that cover extends to the people inside the arrangement, not to anybody outside it.

A helper is outside it. They declined to take part, and declining was never covered. The July answer describes what usually happens, and it is the difficulty above. It is not the only thing that has happened. Two people I knew would not take part, proved hard to turn into targets, and were killed ([The best description of it I have read](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/best-description-i-have-read)).

That is where the line runs. Somebody who helps a target is standing on the same side of it as the target.

## Help still happens

Three Targeted Individuals made a life-changing difference for me over two years on Quora: a job, not freezing, an escape from the Forced Homecoming scheme, and moral support ([Two years to find three people](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/two-years-to-find-three-people)). Each of them was a helper in exactly the sense this question means.

Help is harder to make difficult when it comes from somebody who is already a survivor, already off the list of people who can be recruited, and not living next to the person they help. The Directory lists survivors by what they can do, so that kind of help can be found. Being on it costs nobody an account, and anybody listed can claim their entry or have it taken down. Foundation is where members offer and exchange that help directly.

For somebody outside who wants to help: expect it to get harder, expect the people around you to be approached, and decide what you will do when they are.
