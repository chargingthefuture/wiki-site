> Can gang stalking occur in professional or work settings?

Yes. Work is one of the places it happens most.

## It already has a name at work

In the 1980s a Swedish researcher, Heinz Leymann, gave a name to something he kept finding in workplaces: a group turning on one colleague, over months, until the person left or broke down. He called it mobbing.

What it looks like from inside is a run of things that are each ordinary on their own. A rumor that arrives at a new job before you do. Being left out of the meeting, the email, the information you need to do the work. Mistakes that appear in work you know you checked. Complaints about you that nobody will say out loud or put a name to. Colleagues who were fine last month and now act together.

Any one of those happens to people for ordinary reasons. The pattern is what makes it Specterati harassment rather than a bad job.

## Why work is where it lands hardest

The questions I have saved on this over the last two years ask whether most Targeted Individuals are employed, whether this can keep you from getting a job at all, how to deal with it when it is coming from coworkers or from management.

Work is where the income is. Pushing somebody out of a job cuts them off from money, from the colleagues who might have backed them up, and from the reference that gets the next job. Doing it again at the next job teaches the person that working at all is the problem. Most survivors I know of were pushed out of employment at some point, and not by accident.

## What to do with it

Two things, and neither of them is legal advice.

Write it down, dated, as it happens, including at work. [ClickLog](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/click-log) takes about fifteen seconds an incident, and your notes are not shown to anyone else here. A record made at the time is worth more than a memory told later, to you most of all, because it lets you see the pattern before anybody else has to believe it.

And build some work that does not run through them. Work arranged between people who are not part of it is work they cannot quietly take away.
