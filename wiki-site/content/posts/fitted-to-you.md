A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> How do stalkers groom the person they target

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/How-do-stalkers-groom-the-person-they-target. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

## It arrives as friendliness

Grooming does not start with a threat. On Quora on September 20, 2025, I wrote: "one of the very first things they will do is have overly friendly strangers trying to get you to say or speak about controversial topics."

Friendliness is paired with its opposite. On July 2, 2025, asked whether the people doing this try to be your friend, I answered that they "will pretend to be your friend. They do good cop bad cop routines all the time. Everything is always a set up, never befriend them." The cruel person makes the kind one look like relief, and the kind one is the approach.

## It is fishing

What the friendly approach wants is information. In that same answer I wrote that it is "always about trying to get you to leak information about your self or manipulate you into doing something not beneficial, like spend money to make you go broke, entrapment in crimes, get you to do drugs and alcohol."

Even a question about your health can be the line. On December 13, 2025, answering somebody who was repeatedly asked whether they had a fever, I wrote: "It is all a fishing expedition to find your triggers, discover what will make you sick, or sensitize you to a skit they are planning later." Whatever is learned is used again.

## It is fitted to you

The closer the approach, the more closely it is fitted. On February 5, 2026, I wrote that "an obvious would be love bombing too soon," and that somebody being "exactly 'your type' in every way is a red flag. They will morph into any stereotype/type cast as directed by their handler. As the handler will assume to know what you like or know what type you like from the stalking."

That is the honeypot: a manufactured romance, arranged and run for as long as it is useful. What it is for, and why I think it belongs beside sexual assault, is in [A safe place to shower](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/a-safe-place-to-shower).

An invitation works the same way. On October 2, 2025, asked why somebody taking part would invite a Targeted Individual into their home, I answered: "Entrapment. They will try to get you to commit a crime, provoke you, or, if you do not do anything, if they have proof you were in the house, falsely accuse you of a crime."

## Where it is meant to lead

Every one of these points the same direction. On December 15, 2024, I wrote that they "isolate you with no friends, family or job." The friendly stranger, the romance and the invitation each pull a person toward somebody who is taking part and away from anybody who is not.

So the protection is not learning to tell which smile is real. It is going the other way. On September 21, 2025, I wrote that it is easy on Quora "to go at your own pace to determine if someone is a TI or not before meeting them in person or investing a significant amount of time."

Going at your own pace includes a test that does not depend on charm. On October 6, 2025, asked how to vet new members, I wrote that the people taking part "cannot materially help." Help that actually arrives says more than a friendly approach does.
