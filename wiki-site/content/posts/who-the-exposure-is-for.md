A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> How does a targeted individual take control of their situation and expose them or combat them

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/How-does-a-targeted-individual-take-control-of-their-situation-and-expose-them-or-combat-them. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

How uniting to expose it has gone over the years is in [Unite around what?](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/unite-around-what), and what can and cannot be stopped is in [What can be stopped](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-can-be-stopped). This answer is about the audience: who an exposure would be for.

## Expose it to who?

On January 6, 2026, asked how to expose one part of it, I answered with a question, and said I was not being snarky: "Expose it to who? The only people who do not know about it are people who do not know about targeting. And they would be no use as they are next up for targeting."

Sort the possible audience and it gets short quickly.

- The people taking part know. Telling them what they did tells them nothing.
- The people who have never heard of targeting would be no use, and many of them are next.
- The people who are comfortable, or who are paid out of it, will not help. In that same answer: "People living a cushioned life/live in a bubble or those profiting off your exploitation are not going to help you."

An exposure is aimed at somebody who will do something once they know. Run down that list and there is nobody on it.

## The people who already know

One group is left, and it needs no convincing: other targets. In the same answer I wrote: "Targets are going to have to solve this. History has shown that the oppressed must free themselves."

That changes what control means. Control is not a ruling from somebody outside who has finally been shown the evidence. It is who a target spends their days around, who they buy from, who fixes their car, and who they call. Each of those is a choice the target makes, and each can be made with somebody who does not take part.

On August 13, 2026, under a post calling for a march, I wrote: "I make no claim it stops targeting. There are fake TIs on Quora. I have met real ones and they have significantly help me. Connecting people who haven't 'given up' on living and resourceful works."

That is the claim, and it is a small one on purpose. It does not end the targeting. It changes who is around a target while it goes on.

## Finding them

The difficulty is finding each other. Some accounts are not what they say, and a target who has been isolated has few ways to tell.

The Directory lists survivors by what they can do: a mechanic, a cook, somebody with a spare room or a ride. Being on it costs nobody an account, and anybody listed can claim their entry or have it taken down. Reading it takes a sign-in, because a list like that gathered in one place is a privacy problem in the wrong hands.

An exposure asks somebody else to act. The Directory is for the people who already know, to find each other and do something about this week.
