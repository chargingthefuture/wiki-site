A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> What is the point of gang stalking someone if they don t even know why they are being gang stalked Why waste their time

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/What-is-the-point-of-gang-stalking-someone-if-they-don-t-even-know-why-they-are-being-gang-stalked-Why-waste-their-time. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

The question carries an assumption: that the point is for the target to understand something. A punishment needs the person to know what it is for. A lesson needs them to learn it. This is neither.

## No reason would make it right

On Quora on October 2, 2025, asked how to tell the people doing it the real reason they were sent, I answered: "You can outright tell them. I bet, however, they are not going to care." And: "there is a legal system for crimes. Therefore, no reason should get you on the list. And they know this!"

So a reason, if one were handed over, would settle nothing. It would be a story, and stories get fitted to the person who hears them. Who started it matters less than it seems, because once somebody is on the list the same schemes run regardless ([It Does Not Matter Who Put You on the List](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-put-you-on-the-list)).

## The point is what it costs

What it aims at is the target's life, not their understanding. In that same answer I wrote: "they will steal everything you have, assets, clothes, employment, housing, friends, family, anything."

None of that needs the target to know why. A job that evaporates evaporates whether or not the person understands it, and somebody who arranges an event knows how it ends ([They are not psychic](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/they-are-not-psychic)). The pace is arranged too. On November 2, 2025, asked whether things easing off meant it was over, I wrote that a quiet stretch is "laying low so you put your guard down," and then the harassment comes back, "To create an emotional rollercoaster."

The not knowing is not a gap in it. A person searching for a reason is a person whose attention is on the people doing it.

## Whose time is wasted

The second half of the question asks why anybody would spend their time on it. From their side, it is not spent at a loss. On January 19, 2026, asked how the people taking part are paid, I wrote that they are paid through "legitimate" work, and: "Think of how your new neighbors move in. They are given housing which they pay for with the jobs they were given." Some people do it unpaid; on November 2, 2025 I wrote that a "significant amount of people volunteer their time to harass you."

The time that is wasted is the target's. Hours spent trying to work out the reason, or trying to explain it to somebody who will not care, are hours that go nowhere. My suggestion in October 2025 was the same: "invest time in yourself," and "You need to stay focused."

Time put into a skill stays with the person who learned it.
