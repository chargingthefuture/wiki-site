A question saved as an unwritten answer, and one of the most common forms of the question people arrive with:

> What are some ways for a person to recover from being a victim of gang stalking and return to a normal life?

The vocabulary I use for the people doing this is in the [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary); the question keeps the words it was asked in.

I am going to answer it, and the answer starts by disagreeing with the middle of it.

## There is no returning

Once a name is on the list it is never taken off. [No method I know of stops this](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-can-be-stopped), mine included, and I have never met a survivor for whom anything ended it. A quiet stretch is not the end of it either; quiet usually means the people doing it are laying low, and it can stop being quiet the week after.

So there is no before to return to. Saying that is not despair and it is not a counsel to give up. It is the difference between a plan that works and a plan that cannot.

Measuring your life against a normal that is not coming back does real harm, and it is worth naming exactly what the harm is. A person who is working toward restoration reads every ordinary incident as a failure of the plan — proof that they are not recovering, that they did something wrong, that it is hopeless. It is not. It is Tuesday, and the conditions did not change. A plan that only succeeds if the harassment ends is a plan that will tell you that you failed, every week, forever.

## What does come back

Here is what I have watched come back, in my own life and other people's. It is a short list and every item on it is specific, which is what makes it usable.

Income. Not the career that was taken — that one usually does not come back the way it was, and people who wait for it lose years. What comes back is the ability to earn, which is not the same thing. You still know what you know. [Foundation](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/foundation) is where people list what they can do and reach each other directly, and [ServiceCredits](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/service-credits) settles an exchange in an internal unit rather than money, so somebody with nothing in a bank account is not out of the exchange.

Housing. [LightHouse](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/lighthouse) is rooms offered by survivors to survivors, which is the answer to the scheme that walks a destitute person back to [relatives who were reached first](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/why-they-want-you-living-with-family).

People who know you. This is the one that changes the most and the fastest. [Being on the Directory](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory) is what makes somebody findable, and finding two or three people who understand without being told is not a small recovery, it is most of one. [Commons](https://app.chargingthefuture.com) reads without an account, so it costs nothing to start there.

The ability to ask. This one is a skill rather than a circumstance, and it comes back with practice. Ask small, bring something, and do not measure it against equal — that is [what a working exchange looks like](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/mutual-not-equal) between two people who both need things.

Your own account of your life. [ClickLog](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/click-log) is fifteen seconds an incident and your notes stay yours. A person with a dated record is not relying on memory under stress, which is exactly where this puts it, and memory rewrites the earlier version to match the later one.

## What does not come back, and should not be promised

Proof. The methods are chosen to be deniable one act at a time, and no amount of recovery produces the evidence that was never going to be allowed.

The people who were turned. Some of them were approached before you knew anything was happening. Expecting those relationships back is a way of staying in the same week for years.

The time. That is a loss and it is not repaired by anything on this list. It also is not the most interesting fact about you.

## So what is recovery here

Not restoration. Rebuilding, under conditions that are still running.

The measure that works is whose hands your week runs through. Count them. How many of the things you need — the room, the ride, the work, the trade — come through somebody who is part of this, and how many do not? That number moves, it moves because of things you can do, and it does not require the harassment to stop first.

That is the version of the question I can answer. Not how to get back to normal, but how to get a week that is yours while this is still going on.
