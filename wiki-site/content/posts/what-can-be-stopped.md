I asked this one too, in different words, for most of my life. So here is the answer I would have wanted, which is not the one I was hoping for.

## No method I know of has stopped it

I have been targeted since I was five and I am forty-one. I have not found a way to end it, and I have not met a survivor who has. Ignoring it for thirty-four years [changed nothing](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/they-cannot-be-ignored); the first time one of them punched me in the face came after years of no reaction at all. There is no place in the United States I could move to that is free of it, and I have said so every time somebody asked about Texas. Whether another country is different is the next question in this queue, and it gets its own post.

Two things about the accounts of it stopping.

The first is that a quiet stretch is not the same as an ending. I was asked once whether doing better meant fewer operators were around. No. They change how they work. Laying low is regrouping, so that the guard comes down before the overt part resumes. A person who counts fewer of them in their face and concludes there are fewer has counted the wrong thing.

The second is who tells survivors it will stop. Specterati operators do. They will lean into any story a person accepts: that this is a watch list and it lifts, that it is spiritual and ends at enlightenment, that it is a test and a pass exists. I was told I am a truth teller and they are the Illuminati. The goal post moves every time, because the story is a method and not a promise. Somebody who reaches the finish line they were given finds another one drawn past it.

Could they stop? In theory. But a person's exploitation is worth something to the people doing it, and torturing one person is also how the rest of the operatives are kept in line. Nothing about that arithmetic changes because the person asked nicely, or moved, or went quiet.

## What can be stopped

So the honest answer to the first half is no. The second half has a better answer than it looks, once the question is asked about the right thing.

What they are after is not the harassment for its own sake. It is what the harassment costs: your account of your own life, your day, your work, your housing, and who you have to go through to get any of those. Every one of those costs can be cut, and cutting them is the only thing I have watched work.

Your record. A dated log you own means your account of your life stops depending on memory under stress. ClickLog is that: date, a location if you choose one, fifteen seconds. It does not stop an incident. It stops the incident from also taking your credibility with it, and it means you can put the noticing down instead of carrying it all day.

Your day. The theatre is built to be studied, and every hour spent mapping it is an hour they took. I spent nine months on that and [the turn is dated on the record](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/from-them-to-me). What replaces it is something that compounds, that is still there next month whatever they did this week.

Your footage. For years operators started fights with me in public so that new recruits could be shown a person scuffling in the street. Not supplying that is not composure. It is declining to make the picture, and it is the one thing in a staged incident that is yours to decide.

Your dependence. This is the largest one and the reason this project is an economy rather than an awareness campaign. [Being watched is not what gives someone power over you. Needing something from them is.](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/estonia-did-not-get-to-solve-it-either) Who fixes your car, who you work for, who rents you a room, who drives you: each of those is a choice about whose hands your week runs through, and each one moved is leverage taken off the table. Estonia did not get to choose its neighbor. It chose its arrangements, and thirty years of that is the difference between surviving and doing well.

## Coexistence, and why it is not giving up

I hold to [coexistence over conspiracy](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/how-are-you-doing). I could spend the rest of my life on which organization it is. My position is that it is a great many ordinary people in a distributed network of organized crime, so there is no single door to knock on and no petition that closes it.

That is not resignation. It is the same posture a person takes toward any hazard that is not going to leave: you stop organizing your life around its ending and start organizing it around your own footing. [The communities of 1903 did not get an ending either](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/how-few-people-it-takes), and they built trade schools and businesses anyway, one generation at a time. A hundred survivors trading with each other is not a support group with a spreadsheet. It is the part of the answer that was ever in reach.

And the part I would say to anybody who has been told it stopped: stay connected anyway. Once a name is on the list it is never taken off, and the quiet years are when people drift into destitution with nobody around. Keep one leg in the door.

## What none of this promises

It does not promise the harassment ends. I do not believe that, and I am not going to write it because it would sell.

It promises something smaller and real. The harassment stops being the only checkable thing about you. It stops setting your schedule. It stops deciding who you rent from and who you work for. And the people who matter, the ones who turn up with a ride or a room, are not deciding anything about you based on it.

The targeting is real. It is not the most interesting fact about you.
