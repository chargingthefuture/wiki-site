A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Why does everyone yell at the targeted individual going through harassment

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Why-does-everyone-yell-at-the-targeted-individual-going-through-harassment. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

What the yelling is for, a reaction that can be recorded, is in [The reaction is what gets written down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-reaction-is-what-gets-written-down). This answer is about the word "everyone": why it feels like every person in the room.

## The people who yell are picked

Nobody arrives at a scene by accident. On October 2, 2025, I described street theater as "staged altercations to provoke you for a slander campaign." Staged means cast.

The casting is deliberate. On December 15, 2024, I wrote that they bring in people "from your same ethnic group and have them provoke you into a physical fight (to make it appear as not a target)." The person chosen to shout is chosen so that a bystander reads it as a private dispute, not as one more person sent.

Sometimes the people chosen are the ones with the least to stand on. On October 6, 2025, I wrote that earlier that year, while I was traveling for work, about a hundred people, children among them, followed and provoked me in public. On January 19, 2026, I wrote that people who are not working are paid in "donated" goods and services instead of a wage.

## The rest were moved out first

That same January answer explains the room. "They mob the Target and anyone that does not want to harass you out of businesses, resources and communities. In turn it is only their operatives that remain."

So the store, the office and the building you walk into have been filtered before you arrive. The person who refused to join in is not behind that counter anymore. Some who refused paid far more than a job; I knew two people who would not take part, and they were killed ([The best description of it I have read](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/best-description-i-have-read)).

Of the people who are still there, not all of them are taking part. On January 5, 2025, I wrote: "the reality is, not everyone is in on it." On October 2, 2025, I wrote that outside other survivors, nobody will help, because they are either taking part or afraid of the people who are. Silence from the afraid and noise from the ones sent sound the same from where the target stands.

That is why it feels like everyone. It was built to.

## What to do with it

Do not yell back. The scene is ready for that and nothing else, and arguing with a picture does not take it down ([You cannot argue a smear down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/you-cannot-argue-a-smear-down)). Write down the date and place instead. ClickLog keeps that record, so a scene that was staged has a time on it that you set, not them.

Then go looking for the people who were sorted out. They exist, and many of them are survivors too. The Directory lists people by what they can do, so the next mechanic, cook or ride can come from somebody who was pushed out of the room rather than somebody who was left in it. Being on it costs nobody an account.
