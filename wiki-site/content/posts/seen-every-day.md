A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Why do gang stalkers have to see you every day

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Why-do-gang-stalkers-have-to-see-you-every-day. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

There are two reasons, and neither one is about you.

## Being seen is the message

Most surveillance tries not to be noticed. This kind wants to be. On Quora on July 28, 2025, asked why people follow somebody to the dumpster at their apartment complex, I answered: "Let you know you have no privacy and to scare you into overthinking everything and being afraid to live life." And: "see what will trigger you so they repeatedly trigger you."

A daily sighting is how that lesson is kept fresh. A trip to the dumpster, the mailbox, the corner store, each one with somebody there, until no errand feels like your own. What sets you off on Monday is what comes back on Thursday.

## Every sighting is a report

The second reason is that somebody has to write it down. On December 2, 2024, I wrote about one person who hung back and watched me: "each day a planned event occured he was tasked to notate my emotional state, and so they must have some sort of rating system and markers they use to determine the next phase of the program." Once I reached a certain point, he disappeared and the harassment intensified.

On November 2, 2025, I said the same about the people who stop being overt: "never think they are not around watching, stalking, reporting." And: "They are ops (informants) for organized crime and so they will report back to their handler/group."

So the daily look is not curiosity. It is a check-in, and the person doing it files it. What you look like that day goes up the chain, and what happens next week is planned from it ([They are not psychic](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/they-are-not-psychic)). Much of it is provocation, and the reaction is what gets written down ([The reaction is what gets written down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-reaction-is-what-gets-written-down)).

## A fixed address makes it daily

Every day takes logistics. On June 30, 2025, I wrote: "Either way staying in one consistent location is what they want. Makes it easier for daily harassment." A known address, a known routine, and the same people nearby every morning cost them almost nothing to keep up.

That is not a reason to keep moving. What follows you is a name on a list, not a neighborhood ([It followed you](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/it-followed-you)). In that November answer I put it this way: "you have been doxxed to a global network. Just because you are not on the front page news every day does not mean they do not recognize you."
