A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> What are some lesser known facts about Targeted Individuals that people should be aware of

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/What-are-some-lesser-known-facts-about-Targeted-Individuals-that-people-should-be-aware-of. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this.

There are many. This answer takes one that almost never comes up: how much of it is aimed at the ability to get around.

## The car

On Quora on March 10, 2025, asked whether targets are set up with housing and a car, I answered: "They do not want you having a car."

What I have written since bears that out. On April 21, 2026 I listed what had been done to me, and "stole my car" sits in the same sentence as every job interfered with and housing made hard to find. On September 20, 2025 I wrote that mechanics are paid to damage our cars, "So we are always at a repair shop, always wasting money and time."

A car that is gone, broken, or always in the shop does the same thing. It keeps a person where they are.

## The ride

Without a car, the next thing is a ride, and that is reached too. On October 11, 2025 I wrote that my car was disabled and "everyone else was told not to give me a ride." The one ride I got home was then retold as an arrest.

On December 15, 2024 I had written about it first: "they caused my car to break down leaving me stranded." In that answer I also listed staged car accidents among the attempts on my life: being chased and cut off short, boxed in between cars or against a barrier, objects thrown into the road.

The road itself is one of the named schemes. [Same car, same color](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/same-car-same-color) covers the cars that match yours. On October 11, 2025 I wrote about two men who followed my car for weeks, and that "They were trying to say I have road rage."

## The trip

Travel is reached the same way. On February 25, 2026 I wrote: "every where I go, they get me stranded for months at a time." [The Record Says I Called. I Was on the Bus.](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/one-trip-seven-schemes) is one of those trips, scheme by scheme.

## Why it matters

Each of these reads as bad luck when it happens. A breakdown, a ride that falls through, a delay. What is less known is that they point the same way. A person who cannot get around cannot get to work, cannot leave a bad apartment, and cannot reach the people who would help. Staying put is where the rest becomes easier to arrange, and it is where [Why they want you living with family](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/why-they-want-you-living-with-family) leads.

## What changes it

A ride from somebody who does not take part is a ride that arrives. On December 31, 2025 I wrote: "if you need a mechanic or an Uber, log in and see first if there is an available TI who can provide you with those services."

TrustTransport is that. It lists rides from drivers screened by the community. The Directory lists survivors by what they can do, mechanics among them, and being on it costs nobody an account.
