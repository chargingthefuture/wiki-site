When it comes to the Specterati, I do not care what they call themselves.

What did the Transatlantic slave traders fashion themselves to be? They had the entire globe participating, in one shape or form. What did Hitler consider himself to be? The label a criminal operation picks for itself has never been the useful fact about it. What it does, at what scale, and who takes part — that is the useful fact.

## Who started it matters to a point

Who put you on the hit list is relevant, up to a point. It explains how you got here. Past that point it stops being useful, because of what happens next: once you are on the list, every criminal group in reach joins in, and they run the same [schemes](https://www.chargingthefuture.com/schemes) in rotation regardless of who started it.

That is why the stories never match. One survivor is told it is a secret society. Another is told it is the government. Another, a jealous ex, a former employer, a neighbor. Each explanation is true enough to hold that person's attention and keep them working on the wrong question. Meanwhile the schemes are identical across all of us — same rotation, same playbook, different cities. That is not a claim you have to take on faith: the [named list is public](https://www.chargingthefuture.com/schemes), built from what survivors actually report.

## Build for the part that does not vary

If the identity of the instigator varies and the method does not, then build against the method.

That is the design of the Skills Economy. It never asks who put you on the list. It does not need a verdict, a name, or an agreed-upon theory before it can do anything for you. Work found through the Directory, help exchanged through Foundation, a vetted ride, a listing from a trauma-informed host — none of that depends on knowing who is behind your particular case.

And it works on the one thing none of them are exempt from. They can pick any name they like, tell every one of us a different story, and rotate through as many groups as they want. They still cannot opt out of economics. That is the ground they operate on, and it is the ground we can take back. [The last post](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/economics-oppress-save) makes that case at length.

Skills Economy. Exit their economy, exit the psyop.
