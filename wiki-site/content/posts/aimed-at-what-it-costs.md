A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> How can communities fight the harassment of targeted individuals

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/How-can-communities-fight-the-harassment-of-targeted-individuals. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this.

## Ending it is not on offer

On Quora on December 10, 2025 I wrote: "While we cannot end their harassment. We can lessen their impact by partnering together when it comes to housing, transportation, work and more."

I still put it that way. Whether the harassment goes on is decided by the people doing it, and no community of survivors can decide it for them. A fight aimed at stopping them is aimed at something out of reach.

What is within reach is the impact: where a person sleeps, how they get somewhere, whether they have income. That is where it lands, and that is where a community can stand.

## Start from the need

On January 23, 2026 I answered somebody this way: "What do you want to accomplish? Then people can figure out how to help." And then: "Do you want housing, transportation, a job?"

A question about them has no end. Who sent this person, what that car meant, what they will try next: each answer opens two more. A need has an end. A ride gets given. A room gets found. A job gets done and paid.

So the useful question for a community is not what is being done to somebody. It is what they are trying to get done, and who can help with that.

## Partner on each one

Each place the cost lands has its own kind of partnering.

- Transportation: a ride from a driver other survivors screened, instead of a stranger dispatched to you. That is [TrustTransport](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/trust-transport).
- Work: survivors finding each other by what they can do, through the [Directory](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory) and [Foundation](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/foundation). The jobs I know of past that are in [What jobs a Targeted Individual can get](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-jobs-a-targeted-individual-can-get).
- Housing: the longest of the three to build. Where planning for it stands is in [A gated community, and who could plan it](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/a-gated-community-and-who-could-plan-it).

None of these needs a large group to begin. On July 9, 2026 I wrote: "Pull our resources together. And then we each share the benefit." I said that under a question about survivors living together, pointing to the weekly masterminds in PeerProgramming, and added that the same space could plan one on water or transportation. Its goal board is where members take small tasks toward each other's goals.

## The other option

In the same answer from January 23, 2026 I wrote: "As once you are Target your old life cannot be returned to. Join other Targets or isolate."

Those are the two options I have seen. Isolation is what the targeting works toward ([Nearly a hundred percent isolated](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/nearly-a-hundred-percent-isolated)), and a person on their own carries every cost of it alone.

A community of survivors cannot end it. It can carry those costs together.
