Anybody can open [chargingthefuture.com](https://chargingthefuture.com), tap what they need, and see how many people on the list could help. No account and no name. When a need comes back with nobody, the page notes it so I know to look ([See who could help you, before you make an account](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/see-who-could-help-without-an-account)).

Two of the choices are health care and housing. First aid is among the skills behind the first, and home improvement among the skills behind the second. Alani is listed for both, so Alani is part of both counts.

## Alani

Alani (https://www.quora.com/profile/Alani-57), in the United States. The Directory profile is at https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory/profile/6493cda8-2cf3-4720-9fa8-de7934ca4c8b.

The listing carries three specializations:

- First Aid & CPR: looking after somebody who is hurt or taken ill until more help arrives, and keeping their heart and breathing going if they stop.
- Home Improvement: the repairs and upgrades that keep a house livable.
- Manufacturing: production work, making things on a line or in a shop.

## Why that matters here

The app keeps a catalog of 713 skills, the things a working economy of about five million people needs somebody to be able to do, grouped by sector. Read on October 14, the Directory's 321 people held 260 of them.

Where Alani's skills sit in the catalog:

- First aid and CPR is filed twice in Health, under community health workers and under EMS and paramedics. Health holds 53 of 96.
- Home improvement is filed under construction laborers in Housing and Construction, where 18 of 51 are held.
- Manufacturing is filed under production workers in Manufacturing and Industry, where 16 of 28 are held.

Somebody who picks both health care and housing counts Alani once. A count on the front page says how many people could help and never who they are. Names, and a way to reach anybody, stay behind a free account and the check.

## What this is not

This is not a request for anybody's first aid, medical help or repairs, and nothing here says Alani will give any of them. Being on a list of what a person can do is not an offer of anything, and the Directory has never worked that way.

## How the listing came to be

I built it from the skills Alani offered in public on Quora and put it up under their name, which is how most of this list was made, one person at a time. That is why it is marked community-generated and carries my handle: so anybody reading it knows it is my account of somebody else's skills, and knows who to come to about it.

Alani was the first Targeted Individual on the Directory beside me, and Member of the Day on October 31, 2025: [Member of the Day: Alani](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/chargingthefuture/member%20of%20the%20day/member-of-the-day-alani). They are also one of the earliest supporters named in [The People Who Backed This Publicly](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/honoring-the-earliest-supporters).

## The invitation

Claiming the listing takes a free account and a few minutes, and after that it is theirs: what it says, and whether it stays up at all.

Nothing else is required. Not using the rest of the app, not posting anything, not agreeing with a word I have written. No is a complete answer, and the listing comes down on request with no account involved in that either.

## This does not run one way

Somebody whose work is looking after other people and their homes needs things too. A ride. A room. An hour of a trade they do not have. The listing that makes them findable makes those people findable to them.
