A question on Quora:

> How do I overcome homelessness as a targeted individual? I currently live in my car and it's getting very cold out as I'm in Michigan.

It was at https://www.quora.com/How-do-I-overcome-homelessness-as-a-targeted-individual-I-currently-live-in-my-car-and-its-getting-very-cold-out-as-Im-in-Michigan, or at https://www.quora.com/unanswered/How-do-I-overcome-homelessness-as-a-targeted-individual-I-currently-live-in-my-car-and-its-getting-very-cold-out-as-Im-in-Michigan while nobody had answered it. Neither address loads any more, so the person who asked cannot be credited by name.

## The only place coordinating it

The Skills Economy is the only place trying to coordinate housing for all of us.

Until now, coordinating it has meant being on Quora every day, finding people, and asking each one whether they will host or join the Skills Economy. It took me two years to find three Targeted Individuals to help me.

The Skills Economy is trying to make that minutes, hours or days. [LightHouse](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/lighthouse) is where members list a place they have, see what other members are looking for, and request to stay.

## Keep going

Persistence. Stay motivated. Many of us have been through it, or are going through it now.

The [Directory](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory) and [Foundation](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/foundation) are where to look for emotional support too: people who list peer support and counseling among what they do.

## A worked sample

One of my own goals is a yard jockey job in Texas. Here it is placed on the Skills Taxonomy, with how many people on the skills map hold each skill it needs.

![A worked sample, details removed. The goal: A yard jockey job in Texas. Sector: Transport & Logistics. Job title: Truck Drivers. Vehicle operation and safety, 2 on the skills map. Load securement, 4 on the skills map. Cards anyone can take, from a phone, in under half an hour: Find three yards hiring near Dallas and add their numbers. Ask one yard whether they hire new Class A holders without OTR miles. Find one warehouse in Houston that trains its own yard drivers. Counts: people on the skills map who hold that skill, 2026-10-13. Nobody is named.](images/yard-jockey-texas-sample-2026-10-13.png)

The cards above are how a goal goes on the PeerProgramming Goals board, free in the Skills Economy, where anyone in the cohort can take one. [One Percent](https://farahbrunache.com) is the paid version of the same work, one-to-one: $7 for a first 30-minute session, after a short automated call, where the people behind these counts are matched by skill and introduced.
