A job title, and the status that comes with it, says nothing about the character of the person holding it. [A badge is a job title](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/a-badge-is-a-job-title) goes into what that means when a report goes to whoever holds one.

The list works from the other end. The Skills Economy records what people can do, not the job title they last held or the status that came with it ([Counted by skills, not job titles](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/counted-by-skills)). Nobody needs a current job, or any job, to be on it.

Anita Steyn's listing names skills from two fields a long way apart.

## Anita Steyn

Anita Steyn (https://www.quora.com/profile/Anita-Steyn-13), in the United States. The Directory profile is at https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory/profile/2ccb0d9d-59b7-4879-b3f9-856534164f30.

Under the heading SPECIALIZATIONS are three entries: Advocacy; Access control and identity management; Cognitive behavioral therapy.](images/anita-steyn-directory-profile-2026-10-15.jpg)

The listing carries three specializations. Advocacy stands for what Anita has written about Targeted Individuals. The other two:

- Access control and identity management: deciding who can get into a system or a place, and checking that they are who they say they are.
- Cognitive behavioral therapy: a talking therapy that works on the links between what somebody thinks, feels and does.

## Why that matters here

The app keeps a catalog of 713 skills, the things a working economy of about five million people needs somebody to be able to do, grouped by sector. Read on October 14, the Directory's 321 people held 260 of them.

Where those skills sit in the catalog:

- Access control and identity management is filed under cybersecurity specialists in Telecommunications and IT, where 4 of 20 are held.
- Cognitive behavioral therapy is filed under psychologists in Health, where 53 of 96 are held.

A title would put Anita in one of those fields. The list counts what Anita can do, so the listing is in both.

## What this is not

This is not a request for therapy or for security advice, and nothing here says Anita will give either. Being on a list of what a person can do is not an offer of anything, and the Directory has never worked that way.

## How the listing came to be

I built it from what Anita said about themselves in public and put it up under their name, which is how most of this list was made, one person at a time. They did not sign up, and nobody asked them first. That is why it is marked community-generated and carries my handle: so anybody reading it knows it is my account of somebody else's skills, and knows who to come to about it.

## The invitation

Claiming the listing takes a free account and a few minutes, and after that it is theirs: what it says, and whether it stays up at all.

Nothing else is required. Not using the rest of the app, not posting anything, not agreeing with a word I have written. No is a complete answer, and the listing comes down on request with no account involved in that either.

## This does not run one way

Somebody with skills in two fields this far apart needs things too. A ride. A room. An hour of a trade they do not have. The listing that makes them findable makes those people findable to them.

## Who is on the list

Everyone written about in this series is a self-identified Targeted Individual. That means they said so in public, and it is the only basis for the listing. I cannot verify it, and neither can a reader. Some people who say it are not survivors, and some are the people doing the harassing, so a post here is not a vouch for anybody and should not be read as one. I am not responsible for what anyone named on this list does or says, on the app, on Quora, or anywhere else. The closest anybody can get to finding a real Targeted Individual is through the app, which admits people one at a time through Unlock and has other mechanisms in place to filter actively. Even with those, there is no such thing as a hundred percent.
