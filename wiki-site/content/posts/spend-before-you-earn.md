Community credit is switched on. Every member can now send down to −500 ServiceCredits and repay as they earn.

Your wallet says so directly:

![The ServiceCredits wallet screen. Balance 136 credits, usable across all apps. Available Balance 136, In Escrow 0, Total Balance 136. Below the balance, a line reads: "Community credit: you can send down to −500 credits, then repay as you earn."](images/service-credits-wallet-2026-08-27.jpg)

## What it is for

Until now, sending required having. If your balance was empty, you could receive help and you could offer free or barter — but you could not send credits for a ride, a repair, or a night indoors until you had earned some first.

That order is backwards for anyone who has just been made destitute. The need comes first and the earning comes later, and the gap between them is exactly where survivors get stuck. Community credit closes that gap: you can act now and settle up as work comes in.

## The same line for everyone

Every member gets the same −500. Not a tier, not a reward, not something unlocked by good behavior.

There is no credit score here. There is no social score, no rating, no history of what you have posted or who has complained about you feeding into what you are allowed to do. That is deliberate and it is written into the code itself, which says in plain words that no behavioral score gates spending on this platform.

Survivors know why that matters. Being targeted means being slandered, and slander is what scoring systems eat. You are denied the apartment, the loan, the job, and nobody will tell you which file said what. An economy that scored you would import the same problem and call it risk management.

So this one does not score. Misuse is handled by the size of the line, by freezing a wallet when something is genuinely wrong, and by disputes — after the fact, on the specific case, not by ranking people in advance.

## It does not create credits out of nothing

Worth being precise, because "credit" carries a lot of baggage.

When you send on community credit, your balance goes negative and the person you sent to goes positive by the same amount. The two net to zero. Nothing is issued, the total in circulation does not grow, and there is no interest — the amount you owe back is the amount you sent, and it stays that number.

What the negative balance records is simply this: the community is holding work you have not done yet. You do it, credits come in, and the number climbs back.

## Where it works

Credits move across the apps, not just in one of them:

![The ServiceCredits Send screen. Fields for Recipient — survivor username or ID — Send with ServiceCredits, and Amount. Below, a section headed Accepted Everywhere lists LightHouse, TrustTransport, Directory, Foundation, and SocketRelay. At the bottom: "Formance Ledger — every transaction is recorded on the Formance open-source ledger. Transparent, immutable, and verifiable."](images/service-credits-send-2026-08-27.jpg)

Housing, rides, skills, requests. The same credits, whichever part of the app you are in — and every movement written to an open-source ledger rather than a private one.

## How you repay

By doing the thing the economy is for:

![The Earn ServiceCredits screen. "A few rewards come from the platform. The rest you earn from other members." From the platform: Verify your account, +100, one-time — add your Quora profile so we can confirm you are a real person. Help another member, per exchange, ongoing — provide a service or good such as housing, transport, skills, or requests, and the member sends you credits. Take part in SkillsHunt, per acceptance, ongoing — nominate a survivor, and credits are granted when the nomination is accepted. Contribute during a fundraiser, varies, seasonal. Then: "Everything else is peer-to-peer. You earn the same way you spend — by trading with other members." Four tiles: Housing — LightHouse; Transport — TrustTransport; Services — Directory and Foundation; Requests — SocketRelay.](images/service-credits-earn-2026-08-28.jpg)

A few rewards come from the platform. The rest is other members sending you credits for something you did for them. Repaying a negative balance is not a separate obligation with its own schedule — it is the ordinary business of being useful to someone.

## Using credits is optional

None of this is compulsory. ServiceCredits are one way to move value between survivors here — not the only one, and not a requirement.

Free and barter did not go anywhere. You can use every part of the app and never touch a credit, and nobody is ranked or treated differently for it. Community credit is the same: an option, there if you want it. Nothing obliges anyone to send on it or to carry a balance below zero. It exists so that having nothing does not stop you from acting — not to move anyone into the negative who would rather not be.

## The other half of this

Community credit is the second thing that made sending possible for someone with nothing. The first was the starting balance — [SocketRelay now puts something in everyone's hand](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/socketrelay-v3-credits), which covers why a needs board running on free and barter alone left out the people who had been stripped of everything to barter with.

Together they mean the same thing from two directions: you do not have to arrive with something in order to take part.
