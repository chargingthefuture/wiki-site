A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> How do gang stalkers manipulate and intimidate their targets What are the power dynamics of organized stalking

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/How-do-gang-stalkers-manipulate-and-intimidate-their-targets-What-are-the-power-dynamics-of-organized-stalking. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

The steps I would take are in [The best way to deal with it, in order](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/best-way-to-deal-with-it). This answer is about one part of the power: who explains the arrangement to the target.

## The rules are handed to you

A target is told how this works. Not in a meeting, but in remarks, comments and stories that arrive from people around them. Those rules are written by the people doing it.

On Quora on August 3, 2025, under a question asking whether they want to fight you, since they try to be intimidating, I answered in a comment: "There isn't one. It is one of the lies that are told to get you complacent with the abuse. Everything is plausible deniability for them. They'll touch you if they think they can get away with it." They had put their hands on me, after telling me the no-touch rule months earlier.

A rule like that does two jobs. It tells the target what not to prepare for, and it makes the target feel there are limits when there are none.

## The story is fitted to keep you still

On September 17, 2025, asked why this is so often called a spiritual battle, I wrote that it was the first story I was given: that it was spiritual warfare, that I chose this before I was born, and that I should accept it. Right after came the push "to give up because this is the way life is."

"They will say anything until something sticks with a TI," I wrote in that answer. The content changes from person to person. The aim does not: a target who accepts it stops looking for a way out.

The same move is aimed at a target's sympathy. On December 22, 2025, asked whether the people doing this think they are following criminals, I answered that most of them know they are not, and: "'Some say' is just another manipulation to coax you into feeling sorry for operatives/vigilantes for you to tolerate their abuse."

## Why their version travels

The dynamic underneath is numbers. In the September answer I wrote: "They outnumber TIs, making it easy to spread misconceptions, disinformation." Many people repeating one account to one person can make it sound like common knowledge. On October 11, 2025, I wrote: "It is all about manipulation on every side."

So the intimidation is not only a stare or a car. It is a target learning the terms of their own situation from the people setting them.

## Where the power changes hands

On January 6, 2026, asked how to stop them controlling a life, I wrote: "Everything they do is about manipulation. You are in control regardless of what they say. And the more we as TIs surround ourselves with other TIs the more control we have. As we are not surrounded by their operatives."

That is the practical answer to the power question. A rule heard from somebody taking part is a claim to check, not a fact. A rule compared across many survivors, each with their own dated account, is something else. [How to check me](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/how-to-check-me) is how I ask readers to treat what I say too.

Commons is where survivors talk to each other directly, and the Directory lists survivors by what they can do, so the people around you can be people who do not take part.
