A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> How do gang stalkers rob targeted individuals money

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/How-do-gang-stalkers-rob-targeted-individuals-money. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

I saved it three times.

How income is cut off at work is in [It happens at work](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/it-happens-at-work), and where destitution is meant to lead is in [Why they want you living with family](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/why-they-want-you-living-with-family). This answer is about the money itself: how it leaves.

## Some of it is taken

Outright theft happens. On Quora on October 2, 2025, I wrote that they stole "~90% of my clothes, car, mail (or delivered it opened), business ideas, and intellectual property." On July 15, 2025, asked about fundraisers being shut down, I answered: "I haven't done fundraisers, but yes to my financial accounts."

That is the visible part, and it is the smaller part.

## Most of it is spent

Every stolen thing has to be bought again. The clothes, the car, the service that went with the account. The target pays for that, with money they earned, at a store that was never told anything was wrong.

On September 20, 2025, I answered a question about financial sabotage: "They mob every income stream. And any money you have will be spent defending yourself or needlessly spent." And: "One way to bleed us dry is to pay off mechanics to damage our cars. So we are always at a repair shop, always wasting money and time."

Read that as a transaction. The mechanic is paid twice, once to do the damage and once by the target to fix it. Nothing about the second payment looks like a robbery. It is a receipt from a repair shop, and it is the target's own card on it.

That is how most of it goes. Income is mobbed until it stops, and the money that remains is drained through ordinary purchases arranged so the target keeps paying. On January 19, 2026, asked how the people doing it are paid, I wrote that they are paid "through 'legitimate' work," and that the people who refuse to take part are mobbed out of the businesses alongside the target. The ones left behind the counter are the ones who are in on it.

## Where the protection is

There is no lock for this. A drain that runs through every purchase cannot be stopped one purchase at a time, and I said so in that same answer: "Nothing at this point."

What can change is who gets paid. The same answer went on: "with 1 million plus of us we have mechanics among us. TIs exchanging goods and services with other TIs is the only way." A repair done by somebody who does not take part is a repair that gets done once. Money spent with other survivors does not come back around as the next bill ([Exit their economy, exit the psyop](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/mono/EXIT-THEIR-ECONOMY,-EXIT-THE-PSYOP)).

That is the reason the Directory exists. It lists survivors by what they can do, so the next person who needs a mechanic, a tailor or a ride can find one without going through anybody. Being on it costs nobody an account. Foundation is where members offer and trade that help directly.

None of this returns what was taken. It changes where the next payment goes.
