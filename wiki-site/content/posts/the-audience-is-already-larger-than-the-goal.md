My goal is 384 approved members working with each other at any given time. I have written about [where that number comes from](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/how-the-math-works); this post is about whether it is reachable.

I think it is, and I am going to show the evidence rather than assert it.

## 451

The Skills Economy space on Quora has been followed by 451 people, all time.

![The Skills Economy space's statistics screen on Quora, all-time view, with the space header reading "first-ever psyop-free economy. all posts survive here / chargingthefuture.com/feed." The tiles: Views 78,977 · Follows 451 · Upvotes 1,387 · Comments 1,439.](images/skills-economy-space-stats-2026-08-24.jpg)

![The rest of the same statistics screen, reached by scrolling the tile row sideways: Follows 451 · Upvotes 1,387 · Comments 1,439 · Content 938.](images/skills-economy-space-stats-content-2026-08-24.jpg)

The stats row scrolls sideways on Quora, so the two images together are the screen in its entirety.

That reading is from the evening of August 24, 2026. The figures move daily, so by the time you look they will read differently — the screen updates after 8:00 pm Eastern each day.

Let me be precise about what that number is, because precision is the whole point of this post.

A follow is not a member. It is not a sign-up, and it is not a person on the skills map. It is a person who saw this work and chose to keep seeing it. Evidence of reach, nothing more.

But look at what it means for the goal. 451 is more than 384. The people the goal needs are not hypothetical, not projected from a growth curve, not waiting to be discovered. An audience larger than the goal has already been reached once — by accounts that kept getting erased, which is the subject of the next section.

## Reached under fire

Every follower that space has was gained while the personal accounts posting into it kept being erased.

Quora has banned eight of my accounts. The full dated record is on [old links, new links](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/old-links-new-links), which is kept current so you can check the sequence yourself rather than take my word for it.

The pattern I observe is that the erasures track milestones. The clearest case is documented to the minute: on September 14, 2025, my first account posted the app's first release announcement, and seventeen minutes later the account was banned and everything it had ever posted was deleted. The launch date is independently on the public record — a Discourse post from five days later, [archived on this blog](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/chargingthefuture/discourse-migrate/support-match-waitlist), says the app "was released on September 14th, 2025."

I state plainly that I cannot prove the connection. A ban never arrives with its real reason attached, and that unprovability is part of how this works. What I can do is date everything and keep the record public, which is done.

Through all of it, the space stood. The accounts die; the 451 people who chose to follow the work are still there.

## The other number is my own work

The skills map currently holds 100 people, and that number is a different kind of claim.

Every person on it is someone I researched individually — skills stated in their own words, in public — and placed on the map with a community-generated Directory profile, labeled as such, claimable by them, removable on request. I do this daily.

So when I say 384 is reachable, I am not claiming I can persuade 384 strangers of anything. I am claiming I can keep doing work I have already done 100 times, aimed at an audience that already numbers 451. One claim is salesmanship. The other is arithmetic about my own hands.

## Why the first account mattered most

The account banned seventeen minutes after the launch had something none of the others did: reach outside the survivor community. Its readers did not arrive expecting a conspiracy account, and it was not read as one.

That is exactly the thing being prevented. An account that reads as a conspiracy account is dismissed without argument — no ban needed. An account that does not is a problem.

It is also why this project has a [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary). I abandoned the inherited vocabulary and built my own terms, because the inherited words mark the writer as dismissable before the first sentence is read. The Dictionary is not housekeeping. It is a countermeasure.

## What this adds up to

The goal is 384. The audience already reached is 451. The map already holds 100, added by one person working daily. The record of what it cost to get here is dated and public.

None of those numbers is a promise. Together they are why I think the goal arrives.
