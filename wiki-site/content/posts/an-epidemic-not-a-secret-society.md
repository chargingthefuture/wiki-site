People keep reaching for the Illuminati, or a secret society. A secret society would be easier. It has a top, a membership and a door, and all three could be found. [They are not a secret society](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/they-are-not-a-secret-society) goes into why the label is their own brag.

What I see is worse. Narcissists, and people worse than narcissists, in contact with each other across the world at a rate that has never been possible before, causing harm.

## Nothing in the kit is new

None of the methods is new, and all of them already have names.

- Performed kindness: kind words or acts delivered in a way that is not kind. The kindness is the cover, and the delivery is the content. [How are you doing?](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/how-are-you-doing) is where I first wrote it down.
- Weaponized kindness: a kind act used to deliver an insult. A door held open so something can be said on the way through.
- Covert contract: a kindness with an unspoken bill attached. The psychologist Robert Glover named it in No More Mr. Nice Guy, in 2000. The other person never agreed to the deal, and when the thanks does not come, the kindness turns to anger.
- The Nice Guy: the popular name for somebody who expects a reward for basic decency.
- Performative allyship: loudly supporting a group, then turning on its members the moment one of them disagrees.

A driver pulled up close to me at a curb today, asked me to move, and flipped out when I stood there without thanking them. Not hitting a pedestrian is the bare minimum of holding a license. It is not a favor, and nobody owes thanks for it. That was a covert contract.

These are tools in a kit, and narcissists and people like them use the kit to manipulate.

## How they are paid

This is also how they themselves are paid.

Somebody approaches a stranger with slander about a person the stranger may or may not know. Be nice to them here. Be mean to them there. The stranger receives something for it: sex, drugs, a job, friends. It starts at two years old and goes up from there. [Who teaches them](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-teaches-them) has more on how young.

We have an epidemic, not a secret society.
