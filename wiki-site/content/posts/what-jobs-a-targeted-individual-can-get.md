The only two jobs I know of are truck driving and healthy-volunteer studies.

## Truck driving

Trucking is a high-turnover field. Drivers leave all the time, so companies hire all the time, and a field that is always hiring is a better opportunity than one where every opening has a line of applicants.

## Healthy-volunteer studies

Another Targeted Individual taught me about these. They are phase 1 clinical trials, where a drug company pays healthy people to test an upcoming medicine. It is contract work, and it turns over constantly too, because every study needs a new group of people. [General Rundown](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/chargingthefuture/How-to-maintain-employment-as-a-TI.) covers how the studies work, how to find trial companies, and what the screening checks. Read it, and talk to me before you call a trial company.

## Past those two, the Skills Economy

Aside from those two, the answer is to join the Skills Economy.

[Workforce](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/workforce) tracks the skills a working economy of about five million people needs, sector by sector, and how many of them anybody in this community has. Read on October 2, the catalog held 713 skills, and the Directory's 171 people held 230 of them. 483 had nobody.

Some sectors are close to empty. Mining and extraction held 1 of its 12 skills. Emergency and reserve roles held 2 of 21. Energy and utilities held 3 of 20, water and sanitation 3 of 19, education 4 of 39.

None of that is a posted job. The number each sector needs is worked out from the size of a population, and nothing in it is advertised or applied for. It is a list of what has to get done and who can do it, and most of it is open.

## Being findable for it

The [Directory](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory) lists people and what they can do. [Foundation](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/foundation) is where members find somebody who offers a skill and arrange the work with them in a private chat. Being on the list is how somebody who needs what you do finds you, without anybody else standing in between.
