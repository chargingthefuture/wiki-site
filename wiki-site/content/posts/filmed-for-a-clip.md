A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Why do gangstalkers film you on their phones

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Why-do-gangstalkers-film-you-on-their-phones-5. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

What is being built with the footage, a standing picture of you as unstable or dangerous, is already in [The reaction is what gets written down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-reaction-is-what-gets-written-down). I will not repeat it. This answer is about the phone itself.

## The phone is waiting for one moment

Most of what is filmed is nothing: you walking, you buying groceries, you waiting for a bus. None of it is the point. The phone is out for the second you react.

On Quora on December 13, 2024 I wrote: "their goal is to provoke you to physical violence." People walked into me on purpose, in view of cameras, trying to get me to hit back so the footage would show a violent Black woman ([The Assistant Is Not a Clinician](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-assistant-is-not-a-clinician)). A fight in public produces the footage it was staged for ([The best way to deal with it, in order](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/best-way-to-deal-with-it)).

## Their footage is theirs to cut

A building's camera records what happened. A phone records what its holder decides to keep, starting where they start it and ending where they stop.

On Quora on October 11, 2025 I described two men who followed my car for weeks and drove at me to cause a crash. "They were trying to say I have road rage. So then they followed me to a charging station that had a sitting lobby, and they pretended on camera that I threatened them." In the same answer: "They release fake audio of me saying I hate Mexicans." A clip can be staged, and a recording can be made up.

The footage that would clear you is usually somewhere else. On one trip, a woman told bus staff I had assaulted her when there had been no contact of any kind, and the police said they could not compel a private company to release its own security camera footage ([The Record Says I Called. I Was on the Bus.](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/one-trip-seven-schemes)). Their phone was in their hand. The building's footage stayed with the company.

The rumors attached to a clip are mostly the same for every target, and arguing with them produces more ([You cannot argue a smear down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/you-cannot-argue-a-smear-down)).

## A record of your own

Two things are yours to decide. The first is whether there is a scene to film. Not reacting will not end it, and ignoring it for thirty-four years changed nothing for me, but a clip of a person walking away is not the clip anybody needed.

The second is a record that does not belong to them. The video I took of those two men was the one they did not know about, and it had their license plate on it. On Quora on October 11, 2025 I wrote that a better framing is "to continue living your life and mitigate their threats, for example, with body cameras."
