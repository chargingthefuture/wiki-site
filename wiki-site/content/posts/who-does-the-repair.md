A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Do gang stalkers scare away people who help you such as mechanics lawn workers etc

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Do-gang-stalkers-scare-away-people-who-help-you-such-as-mechanics-lawn-workers-etc. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

The short answer is yes, the people you hire are reached. The longer answer is that scaring them is rarely how it is done.

## They are told something fitted to them

The person who mows your lawn or fixes your car is one of the people around you, and the people around you are who the slander goes to. It is not one story told to everybody. On Quora on October 11, 2025 I wrote: "If someone works at Facebook, they will tell the Facebook employees, you hate Facebook employees, I am not kidding. It is that petty and pathetic."

A mechanic hears whatever a mechanic will believe. So does the person with the mower. None of it needs to be true, and you will rarely learn which version they were given. Getting hung up on the specific lie is a waste of the little time you have.

## Some are brought in

Most of the people who know were brought in and paid for it: a job, an apartment, friends, a favor ([Why nobody does anything](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/why-nobody-does-anything)). Nobody is asked outright. On Quora on July 28, 2026 I wrote: "They do not recruit by asking a person outright join organized crime it’s through slander and getting them to abuse the target. Thereby initiating them into organized crime."

So somebody you hired who stops answering, or never comes back, is not necessarily afraid of anything. They may have been given a reason, or something in return.

## And some were sent

The other half of the question is the people who seem to help. An approach that was arranged does not survive being asked for something real. A person who was sent to be close to you will not do your repair, take your shift, or drive you somewhere at a cost to themselves ([A Safe Place to Shower](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/a-safe-place-to-shower)). The friendly one who keeps saying they will get to it is answered the same way: by whether the work gets done.

## Moving the repair

Persuading the next hire is the expensive route. You cannot outtalk a lie you never heard, and every service that runs through somebody who can be reached is a place they can press.

The cheaper route is to move the service to somebody who does not take part. It took me [two years on Quora to find three people](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/two-years-to-find-three-people) who made a life-changing difference, and all three are listed in the Directory now.

Do not avoid other Targeted Individuals out of caution. On Quora on October 11, 2025 I wrote: "It will not make your targeting less. You will just be more isolated for no reason."

None of this ends the targeting ([What can be stopped](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-can-be-stopped)). It changes who your car, your yard and your week depend on.
