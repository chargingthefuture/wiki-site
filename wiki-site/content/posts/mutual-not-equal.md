The last post was about ignoring it — [thirty-four years of mine](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/they-cannot-be-ignored), and somebody on Quora who knew about targeting, looked away, and then became a target with nobody left to ask.

This is the other side of the same thing. What happens when you do ask.

## A comment I cannot link to

When I first announced the Skills Economy, a Targeted Individual left a comment under it. Their account is still there. The comment is not: when Quora banned the account I had posted from, the post went with it, and so did every comment anybody had left under it. So there is nothing to point at, and this is my memory rather than their words. It ran roughly: who cares about trading skills and goods, and who cares about their slander and their gossip — none of that bothers me. What bothers me are the directed energy weapons.

I was not offended by it and I did not take it personally. I had product experience before 2023, when the harassment went to around the clock, and a comment shaped like that is what arrives when something is working. Somebody sees value in what you are building and asks you to point it at the thing hurting them most. That is an ordinary thing for a person to do.

Two things in it are worth taking apart, and neither is about that person's character.

## The ask arrived with nothing in it

The first is that the ask carried no consideration of what the person receiving it needs.

That is how asking works before any of this starts. If you have had a few adult years without this happening to you, you came to every table with enough to offer that you never had to think about the offer — it was already in the room, and both people knew it. So the habit was never tested.

Then the targeting arrives. The work gets canceled, the doors close, and destitution and isolation are not side effects of it, they are what it is for. One day you are asking with nothing in your hands and hearing no far more often than yes. The habit is the same one you always had. The situation it was built for is gone.

I am probably an edge case here, because mine started when I was five, so it was always like this for me. Most of the Targeted Individuals I have met in person and read on Quora had a before.

## Picking one element and calling it the real one

The second is that it treats the directed energy weapons as a separate problem from the slander, the street harassment, the property theft, the violence.

They are not separate. It is the same people. The ones running the smear are the ones stepping in front of you on the street, and the ones on the street are the ones in the car, and the same network runs through to people being killed, and to people being pushed toward suicide or toward a mass shooting. Naming one element as the real one does not describe what is happening; it describes which part landed hardest on you. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the vocabulary for the rest of it.

I am not scolding anybody for that. They have information I do not — their own threat model, what their days actually look like — and I have information they do not. We each had a blind spot, and mine was not smaller. What I could see is that the elements are connected, and that this comes before the other. The Skills Economy can start on the directed energy weapons at a later date, depending on how far it gets. That is a separate conversation and I am not going to pretend otherwise.

What is worth saying plainly is that deciding only one element counts is the same move as looking away from somebody else's, one step over. The previous post is about where that ends up.

## Mutual is not equal

Here is the part I want a Targeted Individual reading this to take with them.

Every relationship a person has runs on an exchange. Not a transaction in the cold sense — an exchange. Two people in a partnership are each giving the other something of value, and it is almost never equal, and it does not need to be. It needs to be mutual: each side getting what they actually came for. That is what keeps a relationship standing.

Asking for help is the same. The reason it feels awkward after the targeting starts is that people measure the offer against what they used to be able to bring, decide it is not enough, and either ask badly or do not ask at all. Equal was never the bar. Value is subjective, and what you have is worth what it is worth to the person receiving it.

## The investor meeting

I used to be an entrepreneur and I raised funds for my company, so I have sat on the thin side of an asymmetric table many times.

You are a starving founder asking somebody who is not starving to put money into your product. You do not walk into that room asking what they can do for you. You position it as what both of you gain — here is what you are putting in, here is what it becomes. That is not a trick. It is understanding that having more than you does not make the exchange one-directional, and that the person across the table came to the meeting for a reason of their own.

Somebody who perceives you as having more than they do can come to you that way too, and should.

## Know who is receiving the ask

The other half of it is reading the person you are asking.

In my case, I am interested in exiting the psyop. That is what I am building toward and it is the lens I bring to every ask that reaches me. So if somebody approaches me and the exchange on offer would embed me further into a global trafficking network, I am going to decline to help with that. Not as a rule for anybody else — as a fact about me, stated up front so nobody has to guess.

That is not a gate on the Skills Economy. It is a peer-to-peer marketplace and a network, and you can use every part of it without a single interaction with me. I am the architect of the product, not the leader of anything.

## What the app does with this

The app is built to take the awkwardness out of that exchange, so the friction sits somewhere other than on a person who already has enough of it.

You post what you need on [SocketRelay](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/socket-relay) and whoever has one answers — nobody has to open with a speech. [Foundation](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/foundation) lists providers with what they offer, so the terms are on the table before the conversation. The [Directory](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory) lets you look up the skill instead of asking a room and hoping. [ServiceCredits](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/service-credits) settles what was exchanged in an internal unit rather than money, so somebody with nothing in a bank account is not out of the exchange. [Recurring Activity](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/recurring-activity) records an arrangement that has been running for months, which is often the help nobody wrote down. And [Trust](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/trust) builds credibility out of what somebody actually did, so being new is not the same as being unknown.

None of that makes an exchange equal. It makes it legible, which is the part that was missing.

It also does the filtering, which I have [written about before](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/transactional-on-purpose): a Specterati operator will not hand a Targeted Individual anything material. They talk, and they give you the runaround, and a place organized around actually passing each other something real leaves them standing outside it without anyone having to accuse anybody.

## Not everything here will fit your threat model

That is fine, and it is worth saying rather than arguing with.

You can look at the Skills Economy and find that half of it does not touch what is being done to you. Use the half that does. There is something in it at every stage and severity of this, and every person connected to the network makes it better at addressing all of it rather than one piece — including the piece that is hardest on you right now.

Come with something, know who you are asking, and stop measuring it against equal.
