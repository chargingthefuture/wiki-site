## In the spirit of GDP

A country's GDP adds up what its people make and do for each other. The Community Value Index does the same for this community, with two differences.

It never resets. It has counted since June 12, 2026, and every finished trade adds to it.

It is not money. Nothing in it can be cashed, priced, or swapped for a currency or a token. It is a measure of how much trading is happening, the way a thermometer reading is a measure of heat.

## What counts

A trade counts once it is finished: a ride, a training session whose work was checked, a call or a job somebody quoted for, a home matched, a request completed, a regular arrangement both people confirmed, or credits sent from one member to another.

A trade in the app's credits adds the credits that moved. A favor or a swap has no amount, so each one adds one point. A trade priced in a currency is converted with fixed weights, so different currencies can be added together; the weights are not exchange rates.

## What does not count

- A post nobody has taken up yet. Open posts go in a separate figure, Value waiting to happen, which measures interest rather than anything finished. A post is only ever in one of the two.
- Rewards and bonuses the app hands out. Those are not a trade between two people.
- Help that never touches the app. Most help between survivors happens directly, and the index counts only what was arranged through the app.

## The goal

300 billion comes from Finland. Survivors are estimated at five million worldwide, and Finland, at about five and a half million people, is the country the economic model is built against; its economy is about 300 billion a year by GDP. Singapore was added on top of that, for its public figure of what one worker's skills produce. [TSE Baseline Explained](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/mono/TSE-Baseline) sets out why Finland, and [How the math works](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/how-the-math-works) sets out the Singapore figure.

The goal borrows the size of that economy, not its currency. 300 billion is counted in the index's own points, the same points every finished trade adds, and none of them is a dollar.

The index has the goal beside it on the same screen, with how much of the way has been covered.
