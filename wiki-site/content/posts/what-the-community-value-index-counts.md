The Community Value Index is one number in the app. It adds up the help survivors trade with each other, such as a ride, a repair, a room or a lesson, and keeps a running count. Its goal is 300 billion.

## In the spirit of GDP

A country's GDP adds up what its people make and do for each other. The Community Value Index does the same for this community, with two differences.

It never resets. It has counted since June 12, 2026, and every finished trade adds to it.

It is not money. Nothing in it can be cashed, priced, or swapped for a currency or a token. It is a measure of how much trading is happening, the way a thermometer reading is a measure of heat.

## What counts

A trade counts once it is finished. These are the trades that add to it:

- A ride completed through TrustTransport.
- A trainer paid for checked work in SkillUp.
- A call or a quoted job completed through Foundation.
- ServiceCredits sent from one member to another.
- A home matched through LightHouse.
- A request completed through SocketRelay.
- A regular arrangement both people confirmed in Recurring Activity.

A trade priced in ServiceCredits adds its price. A favor or a swap has no price, so each one adds one point. A trade priced in a currency is converted with fixed weights, so different currencies can be added together; the weights are not exchange rates.

## What does not count

- A post nobody has taken up yet. Open posts go in a separate figure, Value waiting to happen, which measures interest rather than anything finished. A post is only ever in one of the two.
- Rewards and bonuses the app hands out. Those are not a trade between two people.
- Help that never touches the app. Most help between survivors happens directly, and the index counts only what was arranged through the app.

## The goal

300 billion is the goal set for an economy of five million survivors trading with each other. The index has the goal beside it on the same screen, with how much of the way has been covered.

[Peace Battle 2](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/peace-battle-2) shows one way there, a round at a time.
