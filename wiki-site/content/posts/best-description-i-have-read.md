The question that arrives most often, in one form or another, is what this actually is and what the methods are.

I am not going to answer it. Somebody answered it years ago and did it better than I would have, which is why the Quora space I ran pointed at her answer rather than mine. The description on that space carried the line for anybody arriving who did not know: if you do not know what this is, read Sherri Jenkins. I cited her twice more in posts there, in December 2025 and again in February 2026.

## Her answer

Sherri Jenkins (https://www.quora.com/profile/Sherri-Jenkins-12) wrote it in the space Real GangStalking Victims and Evidence. It stands at https://realgangstalkingvictimsandevidence.quora.com/What-are-gang-stalkers-Why-are-they-following-you-and-showing-up-wherever-you-are-11, with 8.1 thousand views and 72 upvotes, one of ten answers to that question.

Her words, not mine:

> Gang stalking is a systematic form of severe harassment performed by multiple people against a target. The target of such harassment is commonly referred to as a/the "targeted individual". Traditionally people think of a "stalker" as one perpetrator, whereas gang stalking involves multiple perpetrators using various techniques to severely bother or make a target conform to so-called rules of society. Common methods used by perpetrators include following the target on foot, by car and public transportation, crowding the target's space in a public place, murmuring insults under the breath so only the target can hear, sitting in the car outside the target's residence, starting "fights" in public with the target, doing "skits" on the street which involve certain information only the target should know but has been found out via surveillance of the target, stealing and vandalizing the target's possessions, creating and spreading false rumors about a target, slandering the target, taking pictures or videotaping the target, harming the target's pets, bugging the target's phone, and mail tampering. The target's family may also fall victim to gang stalking.

## Why the list is the part that matters

Read what she lists and notice the size of each item. Somebody walks behind you. Somebody stands too close in a queue. A car sits outside. A sentence you said in your kitchen turns up in a stranger's mouth on the sidewalk. Something breaks. A rumor arrives ahead of you at a job.

Every one of those is nothing, taken alone. Not a crime, not a threat, not even rude in a way you could describe to a third person without sounding like the problem. That is not an accident of the methods; it is the requirement they are built to meet. It is also why the reasonable next step — tell somebody — costs a survivor more than staying quiet, because the account of it sounds worse than the events.

Two other things in her list are worth pulling out.

The skits built from information only the target should know are the ones that make people think these people are reading minds. [They are not](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/they-are-not-psychic) — she says it in the same sentence, and says where it comes from: surveillance.

And the last line, that a target's family may fall victim too. That is the door into the scheme I argue against hardest: make a person destitute so the only place left is back among relatives, then [reach the relatives](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/why-they-want-you-living-with-family).

## What a description is not

It is not a remedy, and she was not offering one. [No method I know of stops this](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-can-be-stopped), mine included.

What a description is good for is smaller and real. It tells a person who has been told they are imagining it that the thing has a shape, a name, and other people who recognize it on sight. Every survivor I have met remembers the first accurate description they read.

After that, the thing to do is not to study it further. It is to move what you can out of reach: a ride, a room, an hour of a trade, arranged with somebody who is not part of it. That is what this economy is, and why it is an economy rather than a support group.
