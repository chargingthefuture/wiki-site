A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Why do people deny gang stalking so vehemently

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Why-do-people-deny-gang-stalking-so-vehemently. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

I saved it twice.

The people denying it are not one group, and the vehemence comes from different places.

## The method is deniability

Start with what is being denied. Asked on November 15, 2025 whether it is the end of a target's life, I answered yes, "through the means of plausible deniability." On July 26, 2025 I wrote: "What they do overall is plausible deniability and so it’s easy for them to continue regardless of who you’re around."

Every scheme is built so that each incident has an ordinary explanation. Denial is not a reaction to the method. It is part of it.

## Some are taking part

Asked on October 11, 2025 whether the people who deny it are the ones doing it, I answered in one word: "Yes."

For a person taking part, a firm denial is the job. One admission ends the deniability everybody else depends on. On October 6, 2025, writing about hidden cameras, I put it this way: "if you find them, then plausible deniability goes out the window." The same answer described the setup in reverse: "they told me they had cameras in my fire alarms. So I would tell people, and I would look crazy." The claim was planted so the target would repeat it, and the people hearing it would deny it on the target's behalf.

## Some were shown a setup

Not everybody denying it is in on it. On November 10, 2025 I quoted Matthew Cappadocia (aka The Wizard of Oz) (https://www.quora.com/profile/Matthew-Cappadocia-aka-The-Wizard-of-Oz), who describes people who "are purposefully kept unaware," and the ones on the ground who "set you up, and provoke you in front of people from the 1st group, so everyone will agree, you are 'crazy'."

A person who watched that scene believes they saw it with their own eyes. Their denial is strong because they think it is first-hand. And a target who is provoked until they react has handed them the scene ([The reaction is what gets written down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-reaction-is-what-gets-written-down)).

## Some see it and stay out

On January 5, 2025 I wrote: "Yes, there are some people that believe the slander. But the reality is, not everyone is in on it. And some see what is going on and they are not going to stick their necks out to stop it."

Denying it out loud is the cheapest way to stay out. A person who agrees it is happening has been asked to do something about it.

## What to do with that

None of the three is moved by an argument, and the people closest to you are often reached first ([Prove it to whom?](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/prove-it-to-whom)). In that same January answer I wrote: "I will not internalize their slander," and "I am not alone. As everyone is not in on it."

The time spent winning over a denier is better spent with people who need no convincing. The Directory lists survivors by what they can do, so the next person you need can be found without going through anybody who denies it. Being on it costs nobody an account. The Commons, the group chat in the app, is a room where the first hour is not spent on whether it is happening.
