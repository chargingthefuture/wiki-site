A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Does lack of sleep and general poor health make targeted individuals more susceptible to gang stalkers tactics

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Does-lack-of-sleep-and-general-poor-health-make-targeted-individuals-more-susceptible-to-gang-stalkers-tactics. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

I am not a doctor, and I said so on Quora on May 22, 2026: "I am not a mental health professional or a doctor." What follows is what I have written from my own experience, nothing more.

## Yes, and it runs the other way

A tired person reads a situation worse, reacts faster and has less left for anything else. Everybody knows that much. The question treats lack of sleep as a weakness the people doing this happen to find. In my experience it is something they produce.

On August 3, 2025, answering somebody who felt trembling under their house, I wrote: "It doesn’t matter where. It’s to disturb your peace, cause sleep deprivation, etc." I had it in an apartment complex, in my car, outside, and in an office. Sleep deprivation is on the [DEW list](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/dew-attack-types) as its own attack type: being kept from sleep or jolted awake.

So the susceptibility is not an accident of somebody's constitution. It is one of the things being worked toward.

## Health is a target

On January 17, 2026, I wrote: "as Targets we have to be on top of physical and mental fitness more as they work to deteriorate our health at a rapid pace."

Some of it is aimed at belief rather than the body. On May 22, 2026, answering somebody afraid their cancer would come back, I wrote: "They simulate conditions and symptoms and create false narratives. They have wanted me to believe I have high blood pressure, kidney disease and more." A person convinced they are ill spends time, money and attention on it, and that is spent whether the illness is there or not.

Most of a Targeted Individual's day already goes to the attacks and to what they force, sleeping badly or not at all among them ([Show up with your percent](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/show-up-with-your-percent)). Wearing health down shrinks what is left.

## What answers it

Nothing I have written stops it. What I have written is about upkeep.

The January answer went on: "Technically if we weren't targeted we would need to do this as we age." Exercise, sleep where it can be had, attention to brain health: ordinary maintenance, done more deliberately because somebody is working against it.

The May answer said what to do with the suggested symptoms: "by not letting it stop what you are doing and thinking its impact in you dwindles." Then: "Easier said then done, I know."

Two parts of the app are built for the practical side. [WhatWorks](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-works-knowledge-that-survives-a-ban) is one shared list of tools survivors bought, used and said helped, organized by problem. Its first problem is Sleep Deprivation, and the two tools under it are headphones. Anybody can add a tool. [ClickLog](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/click-log) keeps a dated record, so a night that was broken is a dated entry rather than a memory that blurs into the next one, and read back over weeks it shows when it happens and where.
