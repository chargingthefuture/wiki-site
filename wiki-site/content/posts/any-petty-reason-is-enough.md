A question I saved on Quora in October 2024 and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Why did I become a targeted individual for gang stalking

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Why-did-I-become-a-targeted-individual-for-gang-stalking. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

I saved it five times.

## There is no criterion

The question assumes a test that you failed. There is not one.

On Quora on June 26, 2025, somebody asked what the criteria were for choosing Targeted Individuals. I answered: "There is none. Any petty reason could have put you on their list." A few weeks later, on July 13, 2025, somebody who described themselves as a criminal said they did not think that played any role. I agreed: "What type of person you are has nothing to do with it."

On September 21, 2025, asked which psychological profiles are targeted, I wrote: "Anyone can be added to the list at any time and for any reason. It does not matter what profile you are. They will instead profile you and customize the torture program to you, the target, specifically." The profiling comes after the name is on the list, not before.

## What it takes

It takes one person with access. On October 11, 2025, to somebody asking whether a bigger purpose was behind it, I wrote: "Not saying 'Hi' to one of them could get you on the list." A big purpose is possible, a whistleblower for example, and if that were the reason you would surely know. On January 4, 2026 I put it in one line: "You crossed paths with a narc, who sold you."

What keeps it going afterward is not the reason it started. A person's exploitation is worth something to the people doing it, and torturing one person is how the rest of the operatives are kept in line ([What can be stopped](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-can-be-stopped)).

## The reasons you are told are fitted to you

The story changes to fit the listener. One survivor is told it is a secret society, another that it is God, another the government. I was told it is because I act like MLK ([The Economics That Oppress Us Can Save Us](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/economics-oppress-save)).

Each story is a lie fitted to the person hearing it, and each one keeps that person alone inside their own explanation. If you are asking why, it is worth checking where the answers you already have came from.

## You did not do anything to deserve it

Mine started when I was five, in a neighborhood I had just moved to, and the people there already knew me ([It goes after the children](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/it-goes-after-the-children)). A five-year-old has done nothing to anybody. There is no version of a five-year-old who deserves to be trafficked; trafficking someone is a choice, made by the person doing it ([Your children can have an option](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/your-children-can-have-an-option)).

The line that a Targeted Individual did something to merit this is said by agents of crime ([They cannot be ignored](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/they-cannot-be-ignored)). It is not a finding about you. It comes from the people doing it.
