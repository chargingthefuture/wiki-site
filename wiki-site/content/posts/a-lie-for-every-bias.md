A question I saved on Quora in October 2024 and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Are you a victim of gang stalking workplace mobbing or vigilantism Why are these forms of harassment and discrimination more interconnected than you may realize

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Are-you-a-victim-of-gang-stalking-workplace-mobbing-or-vigilantism-Why-are-these-forms-of-harassment-and-discrimination-more-interconnected-than-you-may-realize. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

## One arrangement, several names

They are connected because they are the same arrangement, named for where you meet it.

Mobbing is the name for it at work: a group turning on one colleague, over months, until the person leaves or breaks down. The purpose is that you quit, or get fired for reacting. [It happens at work](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/it-happens-at-work) goes into that. Sherri Jenkins (https://www.quora.com/profile/Sherri-Jenkins-12) lists mobbing, "organized stalking in the workplace", among the interchangeable names, alongside community stalking and street theater ([The best description of it I have read](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/best-description-i-have-read)).

Vigilantism is the name for it as the people taking part are told to see it. On Quora on September 21, 2025 I put it as an equation: group vigilantism is what the colloquial name describes. It hides behind ordinary groups, Neighborhood Watch and the Lions Club among them, and it makes criminals appear as though they are positively contributing to society.

## Discrimination is how it recruits

Bias is the way in. On Quora on December 22, 2025 I wrote: "The network will tell a harasser/vigilante a lie based on their bias. If they hate the government, they will tell them the Target is a government spy, if the person is a racist they will tell them a different lie related to racism."

So the story each person is told about the target is fitted to what that person already resents, and it does not have to be true. Most of the people taking part are aware they are not following a criminal.

The picture they build is often a bias too. People walked into me on purpose, in view of cameras, trying to get me to hit back so the footage would show a violent Black woman ([The Assistant Is Not a Clinician](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-assistant-is-not-a-clinician)). The footage was meant to confirm what its audience already believed.

## A reason is not a justification

Every vigilante has a reason. Having a reason is not the same as having ethics, and a story somebody was told about a stranger does not make following that stranger a public service. A line that asks you to feel sorry for the people doing it, because some of them believe what they were told, is asking you to tolerate it.

What answers a picture made of other people's biases is a dated record of your own ([You cannot argue a smear down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/you-cannot-argue-a-smear-down)).
