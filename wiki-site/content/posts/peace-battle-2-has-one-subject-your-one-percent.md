Peace Battle 2 has been running since Friday, September 18, 2026 at 7:00 PM Eastern. It is a distributed protest with no location, named for the line W. E. B. Du Bois wrote in 1903 about the most wonderful peace-battle of the 19th century. [Peace Battle 2](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/peace-battle-2) is where it was announced.

It now has one subject, your 1%, and three steps, in order.

## 1. See your 1% in Workforce

[Workforce](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/workforce?view=one-percent) runs the arithmetic from 50,000 people back to one person's trade, and shows what serving them would be worth in a year. That figure is your 1%. [What's Your 1%?](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/whats-your-one-percent) explains the arithmetic.

Workforce needs an approved account. Without one, a shorter version of the calculator runs with no sign-in at https://farahbrunache.com.

## 2. Start on it on the goal board

Nobody here gets a full day. Put the 1% that is left into the [PeerProgramming](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/peer-programming?tab=goals) goal board: one small card from somebody else's goal, and a goal of your own with cards somebody could do from a phone. For example:

- Goal: start selling repaired bicycles.
- Card: find three places that sell used bikes for parts, and list what each charges.
- Card: find out what two repair shops charge for a tune-up.

It is free and self-service, and it needs an approved account.

## 3. Take it further with One Percent

One Percent is the paid tier: a call of up to half an hour, in your browser, about the figure your trade produces and the first customer behind it. It costs $7 and needs no account. It is at https://farahbrunache.com.

[Start with socks](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/start-with-socks) shows the range, from the smallest version, which is a living, to the ambitious one, which is a company.

Steps 1 and 2 are complete without it, and they stay free.

The page to come back to is https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/pb2.
