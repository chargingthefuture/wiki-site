A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> What percent of your calls do you think are re routed to the gang stalker call center 2

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/What-percent-of-your-calls-do-you-think-are-re-routed-to-the-gang-stalker-call-center-2. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

## I do not have a number

I have never written a percentage for this, and I am not going to make one up here. From the handset, a rerouted call and an ordinary one sound the same, and nothing I can see from my end separates them.

What I have written is broader than routing. On Quora on October 11, 2025, I answered: "TIs are electronically surveilled 100% of the time. Emails, texts, phone calls, and visually tracked, etc." In the same answer: "You control none of those three things."

None of that takes special equipment. A person on a phone can be read by anybody with access to the accounts, the network, or somebody who works where the data sits ([They are not psychic](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/they-are-not-psychic)).

## Treat every call as heard

A percentage would not change what to do. If one call in ten is heard, you still cannot tell which one, so you plan as though it is every one. That ends the counting, and it frees the attention the counting was using.

So the working rule is plain. Say nothing on a call that you would not want repeated back to you by a stranger in a week. Keep plans, addresses and dates off the line where you can.

## Watch who keeps asking for a call

The more useful thing to notice is who wants the phone. A member I had already concluded was an operator kept asking me for calls that turned out to be about nothing. On the last one they told me the app was dead and asked for admin access. A call is a place with no record and no structure around it, and that is why the conversations kept wanting to be calls ([Nobody calls to tell you your project is dead](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/nobody-calls-to-say-its-dead)).

The same goes for people surfacing from the past. Another TI asked me whether to pick up calls and texts from high school acquaintances they remembered as agitators and petty thieves. I answered: do not pick up ([Your children can have an option](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/your-children-can-have-an-option)).

## Need the line for less

Being watched is not what gives somebody power over you. Needing something from them is ([Estonia did not get to solve it either](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/estonia-did-not-get-to-solve-it-either)). A phone line you cannot check is one more thing you depend on. The less of your life runs through it, the less any share of it being heard is worth to anybody.

In that October answer I also wrote: "Do your best to center yourself and not them. They will do what they plan to do regardless of what you do or feel."

Commons is the group chat inside the app, where people talk in writing among members who were let in after a check. Thirty dated entries say more than a guessed percentage.
