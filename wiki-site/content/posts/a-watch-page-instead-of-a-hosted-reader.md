In September I wrote about [an RSS feed reader I host](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/a-reader-nobody-can-close), at rss.lagatos.com, that you could sign into with your Skills Economy account. That post stays up as it was written. This one says what changed.

The hosted reader is closing. It cost a little over $7 last month, more than the month before, and I was the only person signed into it. That is a bill for one reader, and there are free ways to do the same thing.

## What does not change

The blog's feed address is the same, and it never depended on the hosted reader:

```
https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/feed.xml
```

Paste it into any reader app you like and every post turns up on its own. Anything else you read that publishes a feed works the same way in that app.

## Where the YouTube channels went

The part of the hosted reader I used most was the collected YouTube channels: each channel's upload list gathered once, and cut at a date so nothing after it ever arrives. Those were never inside the hosted reader. They are feeds published from their own repository, https://github.com/chargingthefuture/rss-feeds, and they are still there.

There is now a page for watching them that needs no reader at all:

```
https://chargingthefuture.github.io/rss-feeds/read/
```

Channels A to Z, 50 videos a page, newest or oldest first. Each video has a Watched button, and a channel reopens where you left it. You can save a video to come back to. Nothing loads from YouTube until you press play: no thumbnails, no recommendations, nothing from elsewhere on the site. The page lives on GitHub's free hosting for public pages, so nobody pays for it.

A channel being on that page says one person reads it, and nothing else. The repository has its own note on what a listing there does and does not mean.

## No sign-in, so keep a backup

The page has no sign-in. What you mark watched and the videos you save are kept in the browser you used, and only there. They do not reach your phone if you marked them on a laptop, and they are gone if that browser's data is cleared.

To keep them, use Back up or restore at the bottom of the page and download a backup now and then. To move your list to another device, or to get it back, restore that backup there. The page says this in a box at the top, so nobody finds out the hard way.

## Asking for a channel

If you want a channel collected, or cut at your own date, ask me in the [Skills Economy](https://app.lagatos.com) and give the channel address and the date. A channel I add shows up on the page for everybody.

The upload dates on a collected channel are worked out from labels like "2 years ago", so they are close rather than exact. Leave a month or two of margin either side of a cutoff that matters.
