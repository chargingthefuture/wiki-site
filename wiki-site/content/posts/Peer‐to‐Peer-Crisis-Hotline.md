Once my stalking got bad, I called a crisis hotline for the first time. The operator's answer, in short, was, "Join the club." And later, I saw many posts online from targeted individuals (TIs) on Quora asking which hotline can help them. There isn't one.

I believe I have created a way for us to get help.

Each week, more TIs join the TI Skills Economy. This helps us see we are not powerless. By working together, we can build support, earn income, and get the help we need.

If you need someone to talk to for mutual aid or emotional support, join Chyme or Signal. We now have a network of 21 TIs around the world committed to solidarity—our own crisis hotline.
