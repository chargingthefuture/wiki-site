## Platform and identity

Charging The Future — the organization and umbrella brand. Use the full name when referring to the company or mission.

Skills Economy (SE) — the product: the apps, community tools, and economic infrastructure built for survivors. Earlier writing says "TI Skills Economy" or "TSE"; those names are retired, and old posts keep them as historical record.

Survivors — the default word for the people here, in the app and in writing. Preferred over "victims" everywhere except legal or statutory references.

Targeted Individual (TI) — the term many survivors use for themselves and search by. Used as a bridge term; "survivors" is the default.

Psyop-Free Economy — the positioning phrase for the goal: economic participation outside Specterati influence.

Live, Work, and Prevail — the mission line.

## Counting people (this is not a typical app)

Signed up / approved members — people who created their own account and were approved. The only number the 384 goal measures.

384 — the personal, realistic goal: 384 people working with each other at any given time. Not the far number; the one a person can watch arrive.

Recruited — people researched individually — skills stated in their own words, in public — and placed on the skills map with a community-generated directory profile. The capacity math runs on this number.

Community-generated profile — a directory profile created by a member on a survivor's behalf, labeled that way in the app and naming who made it. Claimable by its subject at any time, deletable on request. Nobody has asked; some were outraged at the suggestion, because the profile is how other survivors find them.

Community Value Index — value actually exchanged and settled in the community, cumulative since June 12, 2026. A relative index in the spirit of GDP. Never money, a price, or an exchange or redemption value.

Value waiting to happen (GDP Projected) — what the open posts on the board would add if every one of them closed. Most posts never close: it measures interest, not achievement. Not part of the Community Value Index, and not money.

## The Specterati lexicon

Specterati — the replacement for the colloquial labels "gang stalking" and "gang stalker." Specter: a ghostly apparition, for the unseen and insidious nature of the harassment. -ati: a suffix for a group or collective, for a network working together.

Specterati operator — a person who carries out Specterati harassment. Used instead of "perp" in writing.

Specterati harassment — the organized harassment itself. Used instead of "gang stalking" in writing.

Specterwave — a pervasive influence that spreads fear and control.

Specterforce — a powerful, unseen entity that enforces compliance.

Spectervox — specter plus vox (voice): the suppression of dissenting voices.

Specterbane — a destructive force that seeks to eliminate individuality and freedom.

Specterrealm — a domain ruled by fear and intimidation.

Specterrise — a rise in sinister activity and dominance in society.

## Value and credits

ServiceCredits — the in-app credits unit members exchange with each other. A non-fiat internal unit: not money, not cash, not a currency, and never redeemable or withdrawable for fiat value. A credit movement is a send, a transfer, or an exchange — never a payment. Real fiat amounts elsewhere in the app (a LightHouse listing's actual rent, a confirmed donation) are real money and are described as such.

## ClickLog vocabulary

Problems — the published list of 50+ things done to survivors, used to tag incidents. The full list is public on the landing page.

Schemes — the named, canonical list of Specterati methods, used to tag incidents. Grown over time from documented reports; new schemes earn a name through member suggestions.

Weavers of the Commons — the badge whose holders can suggest a new scheme while logging an incident, and who get a private Commons chat and a private Chyme audio room.

The three ClickLog privacy rules — notes are always private; a private, untagged incident needs no location; tagging problems or schemes requires a location and trend sharing, because tags exist to feed the global map.

## Other terms

Caught Red-Faced — a play on "caught red-handed": the guilt and embarrassment when a Specterati operator panics upon being caught committing a crime.

DEW — Directed Energy Weapon.
