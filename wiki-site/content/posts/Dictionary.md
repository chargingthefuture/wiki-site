# Dictionary: TI Skills Economy Terms

Official terminology for the TI Skills Economy. This page is the authoritative reference for language used across the platform, wiki, and community communications.

---

## Platform and Identity Terms

**Charging the Future**
The organization and umbrella brand. When referring to the company or mission, use the full name.

**TI Skills Economy (TSE)**
The product name for the full ecosystem — apps, community tools, and economic infrastructure built for Targeted Individuals.

**TI**
Targeted Individual.

**Psyop-Free Economy**
A positioning phrase used in campaign and mission framing, describing the goal of economic participation outside Specterati influence.

**Survivors**
The default descriptor for platform users in UI and editorial content. Preferred over "victims" in all contexts except legal or statutory references.

**Live, Work, and Prevail**
The core mission line of Charging the Future.

## Specterati Lexicon

Specterati describes the organized harassment network using language grounded in their actual behavior: a dopamine-exploitation harassment economy funded through money laundering via the global economy. The terminology below replaces colloquial labels such as "gang stalking" and "gang stalker."

**Specterati**
Replacement for the colloquial labels "gang stalking" and "gang stalker."
- *Specter*: a ghostly apparition, symbolizing the unseen and insidious nature of stalking and harassment.
- *-ati*: a suffix denoting a group or collective, suggesting a network working together.

**Specterwave**
A pervasive influence that spreads fear and control.

**Specterforce**
A powerful, unseen entity that enforces compliance.

**Spectervox**
Combines *specter* with *vox* (voice), signaling the suppression of dissenting voices.

**Specterbane**
A destructive force that seeks to eliminate individuality and freedom.

**Specterrealm**
A domain ruled by fear and intimidation.

**Specterrise**
A rise in sinister activity and dominance in society.

## Other Terms

**Caught Red-Faced**
A play on "caught red-handed," emphasizing both guilt and embarrassment when a member of the Specterati panics upon being caught committing a crime.

**DEW**
