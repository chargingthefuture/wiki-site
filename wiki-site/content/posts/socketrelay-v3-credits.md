A while back I wrote about [what replaced the classifieds era, and what Targeted Individuals can use now](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/chargingthefuture/guides/scoketrelay/classifed-ads-for-tis). The purpose has not changed: a place to post what you need and offer what you have, without interference, among people who understand why the ordinary places stopped working for you.

What has changed is the app around it. That post described version two. We are on version three now — new interface, new address, and one addition that matters more than the redesign.

## The gap in the old version

In version two, SocketRelay ran on free and barter. Both are real and both still work. But barter assumes you have something to trade, and being made destitute is the standard outcome of being targeted, not the exception. Jobs go, savings go, belongings go. A survivor with nothing left to trade got the board's generosity and none of its exchange.

## What version three adds

Every member who joins receives 100 ServiceCredits on day one. That is stated plainly on the [landing page](https://chargingthefuture.com), and it applies to everyone, immediately, with nothing to earn first.

ServiceCredits are the community's internal credits unit — not money, not a currency, never redeemable or withdrawable for fiat value. What they are is a way to exchange inside the network: for food, for goods, for a service you need, for a want as well as a need. Free and barter did not go anywhere. Credits sit alongside them as a third way to move value between survivors.

The practical difference: nobody arrives empty-handed. On your first day here you can respond to someone's post with something real. And when another survivor does something for you, showing appreciation properly no longer depends on whether you happen to own something worth trading.

## Where it is

The board is at [app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/socket-relay](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/socket-relay). Post what you need. Offer what you have. Everything else about it works the way the original post described.

To sign up: https://chargingthefuture.com. It is free, invite-only, and you can use one part of it and ignore the rest.
