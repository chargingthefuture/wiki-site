A question I saved on Quora in October 2024 and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Do you regret being a targeted individual

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Do-you-regret-being-a-targeted-individual. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

## Regret needs a choice

No. Regret belongs to something a person decided, and being targeted was never mine to decide.

It started when I was five. A five-year-old does not pick the neighborhood they live in or the network running through it, and [trafficking someone is a choice made by the person doing it](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/your-children-can-have-an-option). The choice in this sits with them.

Nor did I do something to get on the list. There are no criteria, and any petty reason will do ([Any petty reason is enough](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/any-petty-reason-is-enough)). With no criteria, there is no earlier decision to look back on and wish undone. Somebody who knew about targeting and ignored it became a target anyway ([They cannot be ignored](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/they-cannot-be-ignored)).

## The story that you chose it

The question sits close to a story operators tell targets on purpose. On Quora on January 4, 2026 I wrote: "For me, they told me I am a “truth teller,” and they are the Illuminati. And I chose at the birth canal to be tortured on earth."

Others are told it is a watch list, or a spiritual test with a pass at the end ([What can be stopped](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-can-be-stopped)). The story is fitted to whoever is listening ([Peace Battle 2](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/peace-battle-2)), and many versions give the target a part in starting it. A target who believes they chose it has been handed the blame along with the harm.

## No trade to weigh

There is a usual answer to a question like this: that it was hard, but it gave something back. I do not give that answer. It treats the harm as a price paid for something, and nothing was bought with it.

Being targeted is a crime done to me. It is not a position I hold or a trait I have, and it is no more a thing to regret than being robbed is.

## The decisions that are mine

What I do decide is what I do with my own days. No method I know of stops the targeting. What can be cut is what it costs: your account of your own life, your work, your housing, and who you have to go through for any of them ([What can be stopped](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-can-be-stopped)).

Two of those are within reach today. A dated record in [ClickLog](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/click-log) takes seconds an entry, and nobody but you sees your notes. A listing in the [Directory](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory) puts survivors where others can find them by what they can do; being on it costs nobody an account, and anybody can claim theirs or have it taken down.
