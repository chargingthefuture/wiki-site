A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> What is a Targeted Individual and why would anyone want to be one

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/What-is-a-Targeted-Individual-and-why-would-anyone-want-to-be-one. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this.

I saved it three times.

The question runs two things together: being targeted, and calling yourself a Targeted Individual. Nobody wants the first. The second is a choice, and there is a reason people make it.

## What the word names

A Targeted Individual is a person something is being done to. The word describes the targeting, not a group anybody joined and not a trait of the person it is done to.

Nobody applies. Somebody else puts a name on a list, and once it is there the same groups and the same schemes reach it, whoever started it ([It Does Not Matter Who Put You on the List](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-put-you-on-the-list)). The person on the list was not asked. So the first half of the question has a short answer: nobody would want it, and nobody is offered the chance to decline.

## What people do take up is the word

The Dictionary defines Targeted Individual as "the term many survivors use for themselves and search by." Survivors is the word I default to. Targeted Individual is the bridge, and it is a bridge because of the second half of that definition: it is what people type when they are looking for each other.

That is why somebody would say it out loud. Not to be the thing, but to be findable by the others it is happening to. A person who never names it is alone with it. A person who does can be found by somebody who has seen the same thing and can say so.

On Quora on September 18, 2025, answering somebody who asked how to feel better, I wrote: "we are getting better at finding each other." And: "I suggest that you start building relationships with other TIs." On January 4, 2026, answering somebody who had been told it would end once they reached some kind of enlightenment, I wrote: "It is beneficial to speak with other TIs."

Neither answer offered a fix. Both pointed at the same thing: other people it happens to, who can compare what they have seen.

## More than the targeting

In that September answer I also asked for relationships that go past the subject, where people talk about more than the targeting. The word gets two people into the same conversation. What they do with it after that is theirs.

That is what the Directory was built on. It started in October 2025 as a running list of Targeted Individuals alongside their talents ([From them to me](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/from-them-to-me)). The name is what puts a person on the list. The skills are what make the entry worth finding: a repair, a ride, a remote consultation, somebody who does not take part.

Being listed costs nobody an account, and anybody can claim their entry or have it taken down. Reading it takes a sign-in.

The targeting is real. The word is how people it happens to stop being alone with it.
