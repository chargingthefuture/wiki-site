I just published [the page that shows the span](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-record-reads-forwards): two years of writing on Quora, entry by entry, dated and in order.

This post is about what those two years actually bought.

## Three people

In two years of daily activity in this community, three Targeted Individuals made a life-changing impact in helping me as a targeted person.

One helped me get a job. The same person helped me not freeze to death. Another helped me escape the Forced Homecoming scheme. Another gave moral support and helped find other Targeted Individuals to start building a gated community.

Direct help, between people — none of it ran through an app, and no figure anywhere captures it. All three are listed in the Directory, and that is not a coincidence: a findable list of people and what they can do is how people like them get found at all.

Three people, found the slow way, changed what my life is like.

## What the two years were

The two years were not writing. The writing is just the part that left a record. The two years were filtering, by hand, with no tools:

- Specterati operators posing as survivors — the most expensive kind of contact, because each one costs weeks before the pattern shows.
- Survivors who did not want to help. That is common, and after what people here have been through, it is theirs to decide.
- Survivors who wanted to help but could not — wrong skills, wrong place, nothing to spare.
- And survivors who wanted to and could. Three of them.

Every survivor who goes looking for help runs this same filter, alone, from scratch. Two years is what it cost me. Some people run it longer and find nobody, not because nobody exists but because the search is that bad.

## What Skills Economy is

Skills Economy is the culmination of that work and that generosity — the thing the three of them and those two years added up to.

It replaces the filter. Membership is vetted, one person at a time. The Directory lists who is here and what they can do, which handles the third category — you look up the skill instead of discovering by trial who holds it. And the people on the list are there because they chose to be findable, which is the fourth category self-selecting in.

Foundation is where the people who can offer help stand ready to give it: providers list what they offer, and you reach one directly and start a conversation or a call. The person on the other end is there because offering help is the thing they signed up to do.

The goal it is built around: you land, and within twenty-four hours you have assistance or guidance from people who understand what is happening to you — and no Specterati operators anywhere in the loop. That is the design target, stated as one. Two years, compressed to a day, because one person already spent the two years so the next one does not have to.

## A worked sample

One of my own goals is a yard jockey job in Texas. Here it is placed on the Skills Taxonomy, with how many people on the skills map hold each skill it needs.

The cards above are how a goal goes on the PeerProgramming Goals board, free in the Skills Economy, where anyone in the cohort can take one. [One Percent](https://farahbrunache.com) is the paid version of the same work, one-to-one: $7 for a first 30-minute session, after a short automated call, where the people behind these counts are matched by skill and introduced.
