A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> What makes you special as a targeted individual

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/What-makes-you-special-as-a-targeted-individual. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

The question can be read two ways: what about you got you picked, and what about you is worth anything now. The first has a short answer. The second is the one worth the space.

## The flattering answers are handed out

The people doing it have an answer ready, and it is usually a compliment. On Quora on January 4, 2026 I wrote: "Some tell Targets they are on the Terrorist watch list, others that this is religious, and you have a 'light' in you, and you are chosen. For me, they told me I am a 'truth teller.'"

The same answer said where those come from: "They will lean into any lie you accept." Being told you are special costs them nothing. It keeps a person looking for the quality that earned it, and waiting on the people who named it to say when the test is passed. The same answer said that day does not come: "the goal post will move."

## Nothing about you selected you

On June 26, 2025, asked what the criteria are by which Targeted Individuals are chosen, I answered in two sentences: "There is none. Any petty reason could have put you on their list."

That holds whatever the target's education, work or character. The same groups and the same schemes reach a name once it is on the list, whoever put it there ([It Does Not Matter Who Put You on the List](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-put-you-on-the-list)). Being targeted says something about the people doing it. It does not grade the person it is done to.

## What does set you apart

I went back to that same question on September 17, 2025. The person asking had described their own education and work, and I wrote: "Hold on, post COVID virtual practice is more accepted. I can create apps. And there are two million plus TIs. Just saying."

That is the answer to the second reading. The training a person carries did not decide whether they were targeted. It decides what they can offer to everybody else it happens to, and there are a great many of them. A practice that can be run remotely, a trade, a car and a free afternoon: each of those is something another survivor is looking for and cannot safely find through the usual channels.

I started the TI Skills Network on August 3, 2025 for that reason, and wrote that it was there "so we can make a TI circular economy so we can thrive. And address the unemployment issue" ([General Rundown](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/How-to-maintain-employment-as-a-TI.)).

The targeting is real. It is not the most interesting fact about you ([Estonia Did Not Get to Solve It Either](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/estonia-did-not-get-to-solve-it-either)).

## Where it is written down

The Directory lists survivors by what they can do, so the person who needs a remote consultation, a repair or a ride can find somebody who offers it. Most entries were built for people rather than by them, and being on it costs nobody an account. Adding yourself takes a name, where you are and at least one skill ([New here? Three things to do first](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/new-here-three-things-to-do-first)).

If you know a survivor whose skills belong on it, SkillsHunt is where you nominate them: a name, where they are, and what they can do ([Finding people earns credits](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/finding-people-earns-credits)).
