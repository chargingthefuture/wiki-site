A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> How do gang stalkers pick their victims

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/How-do-gang-stalkers-pick-their-victims. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

Once a name is on the list, the same schemes run whoever put it there ([It Does Not Matter Who Put You on the List](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-put-you-on-the-list)). This answer is about the moment before: what the picking looked like from where I stood.

## There is no profile to match

The question assumes a selection run against a description. On Quora on March 4, 2025 I wrote: "Any narcissist with access can put you on the list for any reason they want. Do not think for a moment they operate on an honor system or logic."

So there is no type of person to avoid becoming. There is a type of person doing the picking, and what they want from the people around them.

## It started with a demand for deference

On December 7, 2024, under a question about what puts ordinary people at risk, I described my own case. I know the people who put me on the list: a group of men in high-ranking, well-paid jobs, known to dozens of people as predators. Only a few knew they were also doing this.

What I did was small. "I didn't 'smile' or say 'hi' enough to them." And, looking back, they "wanted me to join slander campaigns of others - of which I ignored."

Each of those is a demand for deference. Greet us the way we expect. Help us turn on somebody else. Not meeting it was the offense.

## Then one chance, which meant submitting

The same answer described what came next. "They will however give you one chance to repair the relationship, in which you must submit to them." It arrives before a person knows what these people are, so it does not look like a test. It looks like somebody being difficult, and the ordinary response is to stand up for yourself or ignore it.

That is what I did. "As a normal person wouldn't be entrapping others to coax a certain response. And then you missed your opportunity to avoid being added to the list."

Read that carefully, because it is not advice to submit. What was on offer was a place inside the arrangement: smiling on demand, and joining the slander of the next person. Refusing was the right answer, and it is not the reason for anything done afterward. The people who chose to do it are.

## Who to keep close instead

The picking runs on who gets close to a person, and the closeness offered by people who demand deference is the kind to doubt. Closeness can also be chosen.

The Directory lists survivors by what they can do, so a person can go and find somebody whose skills they need, on their own terms, rather than waiting for whoever approaches. Being on it costs nobody an account. The Commons is the group chat, where survivors compare what happened to them. A test like that one is easier to recognize once somebody else has described it.
