Yesterday I wrote about [the two-generation goal](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-two-generation-goal) — what the Du Bois cohort did in one generation, and what this community could do in two. This one is for the parents. Specifically for Targeted Individuals (TIs) raising children who are targeted too.

## The part nobody has taken yet

The Skills Economy is barely tapped, and that is the opportunity.

A working economy this size needs about 650 different jobs and titles. Roughly a quarter of them have anyone in them at all. The rest of that list is empty — not competitive, empty. I go through [how that list was built and where the numbers come from](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/how-the-math-works) in its own post.

Now put a child in it. The population I model is five million people. One percent of that is 50,000 people served. That is not a side business anywhere on earth — it is a top-tier career, and the output rate the app calibrates against is Singapore's, one of the highest per worker in the world. Estonia is the other half of the comparison: 1.3 million people who came out of occupation with nothing but their own skills and built a functioning modern state, in about two generations.

A child growing up now, inside an economy their parents built, choosing from 650 roles with most of them unclaimed, serving people who need them and will not harm them. That is available. It is not a fantasy about a rescue that arrives from outside; it is arithmetic about a list that is mostly blank.

## The alternative, in their own words

The other option is the life I can describe from the inside, because it is mine. Circled around the clock by organized crime. And by people like these.

I am naming one person here. Rebecca L Norrington was an acquaintance from an online social networking app — not a friend, never met in person. In the interactions we had, she lost her temper on several occasions when asked general questions about her field, being a "happiness coach." About a year after my overt targeting began around the clock, I sent her a boilerplate email asking her to delete my account from her website.

Her reply said that I threaten people, and so I deserve to be trafficked. That she had been approached by organized crime to gossip about me, that she welcomed it, and that she enjoys it. She wrote that she is a former Department of Justice employee and can leverage the agency against me at will.

She is the only person I name in this post, and the reason is simple: she put it in writing and sent it to me. Everyone else described here said what they said out loud.

One of them is blood related to me, though we have never had a relationship. My only memory of this person is watching them steal, on drugs, arguing in the same breath, and being a hot head. They insinuated that I deserve to be trafficked, on the grounds that it would never happen to them. They are a seven-time felon who received a job and friends in exchange for trafficking. Their advice to me was to avoid speaking to any other TI, because who knows how dangerous those people are.

This past Monday I went to a convenience store, arriving around 7:55 in the morning. It opens at 8. The door handles on the main entrance were gone. Confused, I started to ask whether the store would be open or closed until the repairs were done. Before I could finish the sentence he was yelling, barely controlling his body, squared up as if preparing for a physical fight, accusing me of trying to get into the store before it opened.

There are many more conversations like these.

## Two things worth knowing

Another TI once asked me — on one of my deleted Quora accounts — whether they should pick up the calls and texts from high school acquaintances who kept phoning, wanting to talk and hang out. Their only memory of these people was that they were agitators, petty thieves, and drug users. I answered: do not pick up.

Two things, and the second one is why.

They accuse you of what they are. Every time.

And the people surfacing from your past — the estranged relative, the acquaintance you barely remember — are doing the same job as the stranger who follows you down the street. It is a setup. They are the criminals here, they are the ones slandering you, and they are assembling something they can point to later and say you deserved it. Which we all know is not a thing that exists. Nobody deserves that.

## Who is saying it

So when I read comments from TIs on Quora and see how distraught people are, I think about who they are hearing it from.

Only criminals believe crime should happen to people. That belief is not a judgment about you; it is the permission structure they need in order to do it at all. It is how they get through the day.

Which raises the question I would ask any survivor reading this: why seek their acceptance? Operatives are recruited out of blood relatives, so I understand the pull to take it inward when it comes from family. But everyone is somebody's family. Every Specterati operator out there is somebody's sister, brother, father, or mother, and it did not stop them.

Mine started when I was five. There is no version of a five-year-old who deserves to be trafficked. Trafficking someone is a choice, made by the person doing it. As a child, I had no choice about the neighborhood I was in or the network operating in it.

## The choice a child could have

That is the part that can change.

For the adults here now, the point is a more tolerable life — that is what the economy is for, today, not in some later generation. For those of you raising young children who are being targeted: your children can have an option that neither of us had. A list of work that is theirs to take. People to do it with who are not going to hurt them.

I could not opt out. In two generations, a child could.
