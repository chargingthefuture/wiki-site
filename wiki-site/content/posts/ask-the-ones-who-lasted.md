A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Are there any targeted individuals that made it out

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Are-there-any-targeted-individuals-that-made-it-out. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

Whether there is an escape is answered in [What they know is not the hold](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-they-know-is-not-the-hold), and whether anything ends it is in [What can be stopped](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-can-be-stopped). The short version of both is no, not that I know of. This answer is about the other half of the question: who to ask.

## Out is not where they are

I do not know of a Targeted Individual who got out, and I have not. On Quora on January 4, 2026, answering somebody who had been told it would stop, I wrote: "there are targets who have survived 30+ years of Targeting. If the average lifespan is 80 years. A lifetime of Targeting is 'never stopping.'"

Those people did not make it out. They are still here, and they have spent decades finding out what holds and what does not. Asking whether anybody made it out looks for them in the one place they are not.

## Who answers the question

A target on their own hears one account of how this ends. It comes from the people doing it, and it changes to fit whatever the target will accept. In the same answer I told the person asking: "It seems as though you have isolated yourself. It is beneficial to speak with other TIs —it will bring clarity and expose the lies of the Speceterati."

That is the practical reason to find the people who lasted. They can say what they were promised, and when, and what happened after. Set side by side, those accounts show which promises are made to everybody. No single person can see that from inside their own life.

On September 21, 2025, I wrote that isolation is what the people doing this work toward, "so do the opposite of that," and that meeting one survivor from Quora was what moved me from "fighting" to working to thrive. On January 6, 2026: "the more we as TIs surround ourselves with other TIs the more control we have. As we are not surrounded by their operatives."

## How to find them

Carefully, and at your own pace. In that September answer I said it is "easy to go at your own pace to determine if someone is a TI or not before meeting them in person or investing a significant amount of time." Being careful about who you meet is not the same as meeting nobody.

On September 20, 2025, asked how to get the most out of life while targeted, I told the person asking to begin networking with other TIs, and wrote: "Not going it alone is the best way."

The Directory lists survivors by what they can do, which gives a reason to reach out that is not the targeting: a skill to trade, a job, a question about a trade. Being on it costs nobody an account. Commons is the group chat, where members talk with each other directly. Neither one is an exit. Both are where the people who lasted can be found.
