A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Why would the government be involved in organized stalking How can we know who to trust

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Why-would-the-government-be-involved-in-organized-stalking-How-can-we-know-who-to-trust. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

It asks two things, and the answer to the second follows from the first.

## Not one thing, and not one side

On Quora on September 20, 2025, asked what the government can do to prevent this, I wrote: "The government is not run by one person, but a collection of people. And it is not binary of good and evil."

A government does not decide to take part. People inside one do, the same way people inside a repair shop or a landlord's office do. On January 5, 2025, writing about a threatening reply to a routine email, from somebody who said they had retired from an attorney general's office, I wrote that this individual "was likely a corrupt government employee." One employee, in one office.

The definition of organized crime lists corrupt public officials as one of the group's methods, next to violence and extortion: something done to a government, not something a government does with the group ([What organized crime means](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-organized-crime-means)).

None of that argues with a survivor who says the government is doing it to them. On December 2, 2024 I told one: "You are correct, it is the government as well." Governments have done this, and the documented cases are public. What happens to me is organized crime, and some of the people taking part hold public jobs.

## The word is also bait

The word gets used as a lever. On December 22, 2025 I wrote that the people taking part are each told a lie fitted to their bias: "If they hate the government, they will tell them the Target is a government spy." The target is handed a matching version. On January 7, 2026: "If you hate the government they will say you are on the terrorist watch list."

So "the government is behind it" can be true of somebody in an office and still be handed to you as bait. Either way, it says nothing about the person standing in front of you.

## Trust goes to conduct

If a government is a collection of people, so is every other group, survivors included. Nobody earns trust by the side they say they are on, and nobody loses it by holding a public job.

On December 20, 2025, asked who to turn to, I answered: "Your personal instincts. And real TIs." The same answer said that a lot of the people posing as TIs are taking part, and that finding the real ones "will take a significant amount of legwork."

On September 29, 2025, asked how I build trust where infiltration is constant, I answered in one line: "Be my authentic self and consistent." That is also the test to apply to somebody else. A claim can be made in a day. Consistency takes months to show, and it is hard to fake for that long.

## What to trust instead of a side

What a person does is easier to check than what they say they believe. The Directory lists survivors by what they can do, so the next person who needs a skill can find someone by it rather than by a stated position. Being on the list costs nobody an account, and the people on it are doing rather than saying.

That does not need the question of who runs it settled first. [Unite around what?](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/unite-around-what) is what I think to do that holds either way.
