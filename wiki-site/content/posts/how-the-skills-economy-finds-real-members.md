## By hand

When it launched in 2025, it was called the TI Skills Economy (TSE). I have since renamed it the Skills Economy (SE); the [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) keeps the names.

The TI Skills Economy verified people by hand. Every person who came through the door got a conversation with me.

That did not work, for three reasons:

- It does not scale. One person cannot hold a conversation with everybody who arrives.
- It was traumatizing for me. Most of the first people through the door were Specterati operators, there to troll, and I had to talk to every one of them to find that out.
- It carried my bias. Whoever got in, got in because of how a conversation with me went.

## Trust

The Skills Economy changed that with [Trust](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/trust). Trust shows what each member has actually done in the app, in plain lines other members can read: their exchanges, their connections with other members, the cohorts they finished. It highlights the people who provide material value. There is no score and no badge, and what other members see is set the same way for everyone, so it carries nobody's opinion of anybody, mine included.

A person still reads the Quora profile each new member sends in through [Unlock](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/plugin/unlock) before the app opens up. That is a look at a public page, not a conversation with me.

## Ourselves, not them

Rather than focus on the negative, we focus on the positive. Rather than focus on them, we focus on ourselves.

When that happens, authentic Targeted Individuals surface, because what surfaces is what people actually do for each other. And each person owns how and when they engage. True autonomy, for everyone.
