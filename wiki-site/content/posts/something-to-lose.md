I was listening to a stand-up set the other day, and one of the jokes was about mass shootings. I am not going to say which comedian, because this post is not about them and is not an insult to them. The joke, as I remember it: have you noticed that the people who commit mass shootings all live with their mother? They have nothing to lose. So forget the background check. The check should be a mortgage. Give a person a mortgage, and when somebody makes them angry they will stop and think about the payment due on the first.

At face value it is funny. What I want to write about is why it works, because the reason it works is the same reason a slander campaign works.

## The headline and what is under it

The joke lands on people who read the headline and stop. They lived with their mother. That is it, then. The question that never gets asked is why — why a grown adult was in that house in the first place — because the headline already feels like an answer.

That is what the network I write about counts on. Part of a slander campaign is handing out catchy lines for the people who do not read and do not examine, so that an entire story can travel in one phrase. Lives with their mother does that job. It sounds like a character, and it is a circumstance.

If you spend enough time looking at what is happening on the outside, you notice that the people described this way very often do fit the description. Then you notice that the description was produced. Correlation is being read as cause. The house did not make the person; something put the person in the house.

## The house is the scheme

I have written about this before, in [Why they want you living with family](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/why-they-want-you-living-with-family). The Forced Homecoming is on the [schemes list](https://www.chargingthefuture.com/schemes), and it is one of the things they run on everybody. Close off the income, interfere with the work until it ends, and leave one door open, which leads back to relatives who can then be bought. Put a grown adult back in a childhood bedroom, dependent on the people in it, with the harassment inside the place they sleep and no hour that is off.

In that post I said the survivors I have watched reach the worst endings — taking their own lives, killing someone in the house, walking out and doing violence to strangers — have very often been living exactly like this when it happened. I said then that this says nothing about who those people were, and I am saying it again.

They have spent more than a decade trying to get me into that arrangement. It is one of their top priorities, and I think part of the reason is optics: a person living with their mother is the headline the slander needs. Once you are in the house, the story about you writes itself for everyone who is not going to read past the first line.

My targeting started when I was five, so I learned early that I had to leave. By nine I knew I had to find some way not to return. Running away was not an option — a child with no understanding of the world is less safe on the street, not more — so I stayed until I could leave properly, and I did not go back.

## The gun

There is a part of this I have not written down before. For many years they tried to get me to buy a gun and use it on a crowd. Not once — as a campaign, across years, in the way everything they do runs: suggestion, provocation, the same idea placed by one stranger, then another, then another. I did not take the bait.

When the cycles did not produce that, they punched me in the face. I wrote yesterday about what came after, in [They cannot be ignored](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/they-cannot-be-ignored): Specterati operators — the [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the vocabulary — coming up to thank me for not becoming a terrorist, for not killing any of them. I find it repulsive, and I let it pass. But read it against the joke. The people congratulating me for not doing it are the people who spent years trying to get me to do it.

## Who is behind it

Several Targeted Individuals who write carefully on Quora have said that this distributed network is behind the mass shootings, at least in the United States. I did not save those posts, and I am not going to put words in anybody's mouth, so I will say it as my own position: I believe that too. Not every shooting, and I cannot prove any of it, and I would rather say what I think than keep it to myself. What I can say from my own life is that the attempt was made on me, over years, and that the arrangement the joke describes is one they build on purpose.

So the comments about the people who did it are ignorant, in the plain sense of the word: made without looking. And the shootings are orchestrated. Those are two different claims, and I hold both.

## Three endings, and a fourth route

Yesterday's post said that as Targeted Individuals we do not, one at a time, have the tools to not ignore them properly, and that this has consequences. Here are the consequences. Destitution. A mass shooting. Suicide. Those are the three places the road goes for a person isolated with this, and I have watched people arrive at each one.

The Skills Economy is an attempt at a fourth route, and I want to be exact about the claim. It is not a cure, and it does not stop the harassment; [What can be stopped](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-can-be-stopped) is about that. It is an alternative to the arrangement. Work that uses what you can do, traded with people who need it, so the income does not have to run through an employer they can reach. A room offered by a survivor rather than by a relative who has been bought. People who know what you are dealing with and do not need it explained. A person with those things has something to lose — which is what the joke was reaching for, and got wrong about how you get it.

I have said before that I am aiming at statistically zero suicides in this community and statistically zero mass shootings out of it. If this works, the effect does not stay inside the community. Fewer people arriving at either ending is a benefit to everyone around them, including the people who only ever read the headline.
