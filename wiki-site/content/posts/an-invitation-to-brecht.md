You do not have to agree with me.

Not about what this is, not about who is doing it, not about a word I have written. The list this invitation is about has never needed that from anybody on it. It needs people who can do things, and it needs them findable.

## Brecht

Brecht Corbeel (https://www.quora.com/profile/Brecht-Corbeel), in Antwerp, Belgium. The Directory profile is at https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory/profile/a972707e-c40c-4cc5-9d52-7673910d347b.

![Brecht Corbeel's Directory listing in the app, captured September 28, 2026. Under the heading SPECIALIZATIONS are five entries: Brand Management; Branding and identity systems; Visual design and interaction patterns; Visual concept development; Illustration and concept art.](images/brecht-corbeel-directory-profile-2026-09-28.jpg)

Brecht asked on Quora whether this happens in professional or work settings, and I answered it in [It happens at work](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/it-happens-at-work). Their Quora account has since been banned, so this may never reach them there. The blog is the address that stays.

Five specializations, all visual, and they run in order:

- Illustration and concept art is drawing the idea before it exists.
- Visual concept development is turning a drawing into a direction somebody can build.
- Visual design and interaction patterns is how a screen looks and how it behaves when you use it.
- Branding and identity systems, and brand management, is making something recognizable, and keeping it that way every time it appears.

## Why that matters here

Everything people build between themselves has to be seen before it is used. A sign on a shared house. A flyer for a skill somebody offers. The screen somebody opens when they need a ride and are deciding whether to trust it. Work that looks like what it is gets used, and work that does not gets passed over, however good it is.

## What this is not

This is not a request to work on this project, and nothing here says Brecht will. Being on a list of what a person can do is not an offer of anything, and the Directory has never worked that way.

It is also not a claim about anything Brecht believes or has written, beyond the question they asked and the skills on the listing.

## How the listing came to be

I built it from what Brecht said about themselves in public and put it up under their name, which is how most of this list was made, one person at a time. They did not sign up, and nobody asked them first. That is why it is marked community-generated and carries my handle: so anybody reading it knows it is my account of somebody else's skills, and knows who to come to about it.

## The invitation

Claiming the listing takes a free account and a few minutes, and after that it is theirs — what it says, and whether it stays up at all.

Nothing else is required. Not using the rest of the app, not posting anything, not agreeing with a word I have written. No is a complete answer, and the listing comes down on request with no account involved in that either.

## This does not run one way

Somebody whose work is making other people's things legible needs things too. A ride. A room. An hour of a trade they do not have. The same listing that makes them findable is what makes those people findable to them.
