A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Have there been any documented cases of individuals being targeted as targeted individuals without any valid reason 2

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Have-there-been-any-documented-cases-of-individuals-being-targeted-as-targeted-individuals-without-any-valid-reason-2. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

How a name gets on the list is in [Any petty reason is enough](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/any-petty-reason-is-enough). This answer is about the word valid.

## There is no other kind

The question asks for the cases without a valid reason, which leaves room for cases that had one. There are none.

On Quora on October 2, 2025, somebody asked how to tell the people targeting them that the real reason behind it was drama a handler had stirred up. I answered: "You can outright tell them. I bet, however, they are not going to care. These people think they are judge, jury, and executioner. A mentally stable person will recognize that as not a valid reason." And: "there is a legal system for crimes. Therefore, no reason should get you on the list. And they know this!"

If somebody did commit a crime, there are courts for it, with charges, evidence and a defense. A neighborhood that appoints itself to punish somebody, with no charge and no end date, is not a court with a lighter touch. It is a crime of its own, whatever the person was accused of. On October 11, 2025, I put it in one line: "There is a legal system for a reason."

So a reason, whatever it was, settles nothing.

## The cases on this blog

These are not court records. They are what I have written down, dated, on this blog and before it on Quora.

I was five when it started, in a neighborhood I had just moved to, where the people already knew me ([It goes after the children](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/it-goes-after-the-children)). A five-year-old has done nothing that a reason could attach to.

I have no children, so it went after my friends' children. The ones who would not take part against me were bullied at school until they gave in. Their reason for being targeted was that they would not join in.

Two people I knew would not take part, and proved hard to turn into targets. They were killed ([The best description I have read](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/best-description-i-have-read)). Declining to join is not a crime anywhere.

None of these began with something a court would hear. On March 4, 2025, I wrote: "Do not think for a moment they operate on an honor system or logic."
