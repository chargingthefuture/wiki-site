Holiday season is coming. Here are examples of things somebody would need help with. Each one can go up as an ask on Today, and somebody else picks it up and does it.

My own example: because of the targeting, I have no place to do laundry and no place to receive packages. I would rather pay a Targeted Individual for both than anybody else.

The list is grouped by kind. Take any line, change it to fit, or write one that is not here.

## Food

- Shop for groceries and deliver them
- Provide the ingredients for a holiday meal
- Cook and deliver ready-to-eat meals
- Put together a food box of things that are easy to cook
- Put together a holiday meal kit for a family
- Provide a holiday meal for somebody who lives alone
- Give a grocery gift card
- Host a free community meal

## Clothing and warmth

- Give warm coats, jackets and snow pants
- Give hats, gloves, scarves and warm socks
- Give blankets
- Give shoes for the weather
- Give pajamas or winter clothing for children

## Everyday needs

- Put together a personal-care kit
- Provide diapers and baby wipes
- Provide formula or baby food, when asked for
- Help with laundry, or cover the cost of a laundromat
- Receive packages for somebody with no safe address to send them to
- Offer a place to charge a phone, or a prepaid phone card
- Offer a haircut or a grooming appointment
- Help move or set up basic household items

## Rides and errands

- Give a transit or gas card
- Give somebody a ride to a food pantry
- Give somebody a ride to an appointment
- Give somebody a ride to a pharmacy or a grocery store
- Pick up prescriptions and deliver them
- Help carry groceries or donated items
- Keep somebody company while they run errands

## Paperwork and access

- Help replace a lost ID
- Help fill out forms or benefit applications
- Help call an agency or make an appointment
- Find local services
- Provide time on a computer with internet

## Company

- Make a friendly check-in call over the holidays
- Invite somebody to a low-pressure community gathering

## Children

- Give new toys that fit the child's age
- Give books, art supplies or games
- Give a gift card so a parent can choose what their children need
- Provide school supplies
- Help cover the cost of childcare
- Arrange trusted childcare for a few hours
- Organize a supervised activity during the school break
- Provide a ride to a children's activity or a community event
- Find a quiet place for children to do homework

## Coordinating help

- Make a local list of meals, shelters, warming centers, clinics and benefits
- Ask the people receiving help what they need before collecting or delivering anything
- Keep the help going after the holidays

## Posting one

On Today, tap + New, then Ask, and write what you need with a finish line somebody can see: "laundry done for the week" rather than "help with laundry". Break it into small tasks if it has more than one step, such as finding a laundromat near you and checking its hours.

Everyone can read an ask, so keep addresses and times for the private conversation that opens once both people agree to it.

To take one instead, open somebody else's ask and pick up one task. One is enough.

Asks are at [app.lagatos.com/today](https://app.lagatos.com/today).

To sign up: https://lagatos.com. It is free, everyone is let in one at a time after a check, and you can use one part of it and ignore the rest.
