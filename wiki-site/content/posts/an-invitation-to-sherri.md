Two things have to be true for the targeting to work.

The first is that the people around you can be reached, and so can the places. Who gets into a building. Who holds a key. Who is waved past a desk. Security is the work of deciding that on purpose rather than by habit.

The second is that there is nobody else. That one is not a starting condition. It is what the first produces, given time.

Nothing I know of stops them reaching people. The answer is to have people you can still turn to, including people who know how a door is kept.

## What this is not

This is not a request to secure anybody's home, and nothing here says Sherri will. Being on a list of what a person can do is not an offer of anything, and the Directory has never worked that way.

## How the listing came to be

I built it from what Sherri said about themselves in public and put it up under their name, which is how most of this list was made, one person at a time. They did not sign up, and nobody asked them first. That is why it is marked community-generated and carries my handle: so anybody reading it knows it is my account of somebody else's skills, and knows who to come to about it.

## The invitation

Claiming the listing takes a free account and a few minutes, and after that it is theirs — what it says, and whether it stays up at all.

Nothing else is required. Not using the rest of the app, not posting anything, not agreeing with a word I have written. No is a complete answer, and the listing comes down on request with no account involved in that either.

## This does not run one way

Somebody whose work is keeping other people safe needs things too. A ride. A room. An hour of a trade they do not have. The same listing that makes them findable is what makes those people findable to them. That is the second of the two things, turned around: not nobody else, but somebody.

## Who is on the list

Everyone written about in this series is a self-identified Targeted Individual. That means they said so in public, and it is the only basis for the listing. I cannot verify it, and neither can a reader. Some people who say it are not survivors, and some are the people doing the harassing, so a post here is not a vouch for anybody and should not be read as one. I am not responsible for what anyone named on this list does or says, on the app, on Quora, or anywhere else. The closest anybody can get to finding a real Targeted Individual is through the app, which admits people one at a time through Unlock and has other mechanisms in place to filter actively. Even with those, there is no such thing as a hundred percent.
