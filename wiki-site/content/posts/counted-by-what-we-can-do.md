A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Who are the most common targets of gang stalking or targeted individuals TI

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Who-are-the-most-common-targets-of-gang-stalking-or-targeted-individuals-TI. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

I saved it twice.

How a name gets onto the list is in [It Does Not Matter Who Put You on the List](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-put-you-on-the-list). This answer is about the count: who has been counted, and how.

## The question expects a profile

"Most common" asks for a census. An age range, a line of work, a kind of person who should be careful. Nobody holds that census, and I would not trust one that claimed to.

On Quora on September 21, 2025, asked what psychological profiles are targeted, I answered: "It appears not to be relevant." And: "It does not matter what profile you are. They will instead profile you and customize the torture program to you, the target, specifically."

So the profile runs the other way. It is not a description of who gets picked. It is something built about each person after they are picked.

## I tried to count

On July 13, 2025, I posted a survey and called it "my new approach at finding out who and where we are" ([From them to me](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/from-them-to-me)). The first question was age, asked in ranges so nobody had to give more than a letter. The next was where, and the answers went onto a map.

What came back did not sort into a type. It sorted into places, and into how much each person lived with. On October 11, 2025, answering the survey's question about how much of a day the harassment takes, I wrote that I had spent time with other Targeted Individuals (TIs) and seen it done fully to me and not at all to the TI next to me, in each other's presence. The difference was not the area or the people available. It was the target. The people doing it, I wrote, are "choosing which TIs to harass based on their bias/which slander they received."

That is the closest thing to a pattern the survey found. How hard somebody is hit follows what was said about them, and to whom. It does not follow a demographic.

The survey did not last on Quora. By October 25, 2025, I was pointing people who asked about it to the Skills Economy instead.

## The count that holds up

There is a number I can stand behind, and it answers a different question. On September 17, 2025, I wrote that there are "two million plus TIs."

What can be counted, one person at a time, is what each of us can do. The Directory lists survivors by their skills, not by why they were picked. The front page at chargingthefuture.com now tells anybody, without an account, how many people listed there could help with what they need ([See who could help you, before you make an account](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/see-who-could-help-without-an-account)). On October 13, 2026, it said 312 people were listed.

Who gets targeted has no tidy answer. Who is here, and what they can do, has one, and it grows most days.
