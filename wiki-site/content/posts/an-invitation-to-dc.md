The answer here was never one walled place, because the people it is for are in every country. [The lit country](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-lit-country) is a map of that: a hearth for each survivor, spread across a dozen scattered lands, each with a trade.

DC's is in the United Kingdom.

The listing carries three specializations, all from hotel work:

- Guest services and reservation systems: taking a booking, keeping it straight, and looking after somebody while they stay.
- Room preparation and cleanliness standards: getting a room ready to a standard, every time.
- Complaint handling: hearing out somebody who is unhappy and putting it right.

## Why that matters here

The app keeps a catalog of 713 skills, the things a working economy of about five million people needs somebody to be able to do, grouped by sector. Read on October 14, the Directory's 312 people held 260 of them.

All three of DC's skills are filed under hotel staff in Tourism and Hospitality, where 8 of 16 are held.

Survivors move, and often arrive somewhere new with nobody to call ([Move toward somebody](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/move-toward-somebody)). A room that is ready, and a person who knows how to receive somebody, is part of what makes arriving possible.

## What this is not

This is not a request to put anybody up or book anybody a room, and nothing here says DC will. Being on a list of what a person can do is not an offer of anything, and the Directory has never worked that way.

## How the listing came to be

I built it from what DC said about themselves in public and put it up under their name, which is how most of this list was made, one person at a time. They did not sign up, and nobody asked them first. That is why it is marked community-generated and carries my handle: so anybody reading it knows it is my account of somebody else's skills, and knows who to come to about it.

## The invitation

Claiming the listing takes a free account and a few minutes, and after that it is theirs: what it says, and whether it stays up at all.

Nothing else is required. Not using the rest of the app, not posting anything, not agreeing with a word I have written. No is a complete answer, and the listing comes down on request with no account involved in that either.

## This does not run one way

Somebody whose work is looking after guests needs things too. A ride. A room of their own. An hour of a trade they do not have. The listing that makes them findable makes those people findable to them, including the others on the list in the United Kingdom.
