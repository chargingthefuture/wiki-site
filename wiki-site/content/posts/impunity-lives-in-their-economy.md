A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Why are targeted individuals treated so badly 1

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Why-are-targeted-individuals-treated-so-badly-1. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

I saved it twice, in the TI Skills Network space. On August 3, 2025, under an answer to an earlier copy of the same question, all I wrote was "Please join" and the space's address.

## Nothing about you earns it

The question can be read as asking what a Targeted Individual did to be treated this way. Nothing. On Quora on July 27, 2026, asked how they would go after somebody with no flaws, I wrote: "That's not how they work. This has nothing to do with looks. And will target everything." And: "They will mock normal behavior."

A person with no flaws to shame is handled the same way. If they cannot shame somebody, the same answer went on, they "merely invent stories" for other people to act on. Which story gets told depends on who is hearing it, not on the target ([A lie for every bias](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/a-lie-for-every-bias)). That answer named it plainly: "It's pure trafficking."

## The cruelty is the method

Treating somebody badly, day after day, is not a side effect. On July 18, 2026, asked why the people harassing somebody for six years seem to find it funny, I wrote: "They are evil and do anything to annoy you. Your negative reaction is what they seek."

A reaction can be filmed, reported and retold. The provocation is arranged so the target's ordinary response is the part that gets written down ([The reaction is what gets written down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-reaction-is-what-gets-written-down)).

## It continues because nobody answers for it

The rest of the answer is impunity. On August 3, 2025, I wrote: "Everything is plausible deniability for them. They'll touch you if they think they can get away with it." They had put their hands on me, months after telling me there was a rule against touching.

They have punched me in the face twice, several years apart, in two scripted events that played out identically. The first time there were witnesses. The second time there were cameras and witnesses. Nothing followed either time ([Exit their economy, exit the psyop](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/mono/EXIT-THEIR-ECONOMY,-EXIT-THE-PSYOP)). People treat a target badly because they expect nothing to happen to them for it, and so far they have been right.

## Where that impunity stops

On May 26, 2026, I wrote: "Their impunity to destroy lives gives them the arrogance. But that impunity only lives in their economy."

It is not a law, and it is not universal. It holds where the people taking part are paid, employed, housed and served by each other. A mechanic, a landlord or a store that does not take part extends no such cover.

So the useful move is not explaining yourself to the people doing it. It is finding the people who are not. The Directory lists survivors by what they can do, so a Targeted Individual who needs a repair, a ride or a skill can find someone outside that arrangement. Being on it costs nobody an account.
