A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> What agency can you call to get gang stalking done with

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/What-agency-can-you-call-to-get-gang-stalking-done-with. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

What a police report can carry, and what it cannot, is in [Who to report it to](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-to-report-it-to). This answer is about the call a person makes when they want somebody to pick up.

## I do not know of that agency

I do not know of an office you can call that takes all of it and ends it. I would rather say so than send anybody to a number that does not answer.

The question is a fair one, and it is asked often. On January 3, 2026, I wrote that I kept seeing posts from Targeted Individuals on Quora asking which hotline can help them. "There isn't one."

## The calls I made

When my stalking got bad, I called a crisis hotline for the first time. The operator's answer, in short, was "Join the club." It conceded that this happens to a lot of people, told me to change my routine, and offered nothing else.

Later I went to a police station to file a report on an incident. While I was there, an officer mocked that hotline call back to me, in person. The call had not stayed where I made it ([The Assistant Is Not a Clinician](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-assistant-is-not-a-clinician)).

## The offices that do act

Some avenues do act on a call like this, and what they act on is the caller. Describe what has been happening and be told you are delusional, and everything said after that is heard through that frame.

That outcome is wanted. On Quora on November 17, 2024, I wrote that after decades, "my guess is that they are unable to coax me into doing any crimes or get committed and so now they are resorting to violence to get me to react." A call that ends with the caller written up as the problem is a result the people doing this were already working toward.

So the question I would put in place of "which agency" is who is on the other end of the line, and whether they have lived it.

## So we became the line

There was no hotline for this, so survivors became one. In that same January post I wrote: "We now have a network of 21 TIs around the world committed to solidarity—our own crisis hotline." It runs on people, not on an office, and nobody on it will tell you to join the club. They are already in it.

[Chyme](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/chyme) is live audio: rooms by topic that anybody signed in can walk into, listen, raise a hand and speak. In January I opened rooms there meant to stay open around the clock, one for each sector of the economy survivors are building, and a Town Hall for everything else. Every member can host a room of their own. Nothing is recorded by default.

The [Commons](https://app.chargingthefuture.com) is the group chat, for when talking out loud is not possible.

Neither ends the targeting. What they change is who answers when you call: somebody who believes you before you finish the sentence, at an hour an office is closed.
