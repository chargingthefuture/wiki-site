A working economy needs people who keep the books.

Not only for the taxman. A shared house, a cooperative, a small business, anything where more than one person puts something in, lasts as long as everybody in it can see where things went. When nobody can, the arrangement runs on trust alone, and trust is the first thing the people doing this go after.

Books kept so that anybody can check them are one of the few things that answer that without asking anyone to take anyone's word.

## King Unique

King Unique (https://www.quora.com/profile/king-unique-4), in Melbourne, Australia. The Directory profile is at https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory/profile/4b25fc47-5a34-4ae8-ac1a-a0abf2bb319c.

The listing carries eight specializations, and together they cover the work end to end:

- Financial reporting and bookkeeping: recording what came in and what went out.
- Auditing and internal controls: checking that the record is right, and setting things up so it stays right.
- Tax compliance: meeting what the law requires of the money.
- Financial planning and budgeting, and financial modeling and cash flow management: deciding ahead of time where money goes, and knowing when it arrives and leaves.
- Financial risk assessment: seeing what could go wrong before it does.
- Budgeting and personal finance education: teaching somebody else to do the same with their own money.
- Financial record-keeping and transparency: keeping a cooperative's books open to the people it belongs to.

## Why that matters here

The app keeps a catalog of 713 skills, the things a working economy of about five million people needs somebody to be able to do, grouped by sector. Read on October 11, the Directory's 311 people held 255 of them.

Finance and Public Administration is one of the thin sectors: 9 of its 34 skills are held by anybody. Two of King Unique's skills sit in Microfinance and SME Support, the sector for small businesses and cooperatives, where 5 of 24 are held.

Most of what people offer on the list is a trade. A trade done for different people every week is a small business, and every small business needs somebody who can tell it, in numbers, whether it is working.

## What this is not

This is not a request to do anybody's books or taxes, and nothing here says King Unique will. Being on a list of what a person can do is not an offer of anything, and the Directory has never worked that way.

## How the listing came to be

I built it from what King Unique said about themselves in public and put it up under their name, which is how most of this list was made, one person at a time. They did not sign up, and nobody asked them first. That is why it is marked community-generated and carries my handle: so anybody reading it knows it is my account of somebody else's skills, and knows who to come to about it.

## This does not run one way

Somebody whose work is other people's numbers needs things too. A ride. A room. An hour of a trade they do not have. The listing that makes them findable makes those people findable to them, including the others on the list in Australia.
