A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Are the families of targeted individuals forced by gang stalkers to harass the TI or do they do it voluntarily

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Are-the-families-of-targeted-individuals-forced-by-gang-stalkers-to-harass-the-TI-or-do-they-do-it-voluntarily. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

How a target is pushed back into a family home, and what relatives are given once they are there, is in [Why they want you living with family](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/why-they-want-you-living-with-family). This answer is about the relatives themselves: whether they had a say.

## They are pressured

The family is not left alone. On Quora on September 18, 2025, I wrote: "There is also the targeting of your friends and family, but it appears temporary. Meaning they are only harassed until they either abandon you or become a gstalker and engage in your torture. But I would not consider them to be TIs."

So there is pressure, and it is aimed at one of two outcomes. A relative who walks away has given them the first. A relative who joins in has given them the second. Either way the target ends up isolated.

## Pressure is not the same as no choice

On June 6, 2025, answering a question about what happens to people who do not comply, I wrote that the idea of "comply" or face consequences "is for non-TIs. Like the TIs family, friends and coworkers. Non-TIs have a choice to gstalk or not."

That is the difference between a relative and a target. The relative is offered terms. Join, leave, or say nothing, and the harassment aimed at them stops. The target is offered no terms at all. On the same day I wrote what the "comply" looks like for a target: suicide, prison or a mental institution.

Silence is one of the options, and plenty take it. On January 4, 2025, asked why family and friends of the people doing this do not speak up, I wrote: "staying quiet means they do not become a target." One of them told me directly: "they are not trying to kill you, so do not worry about it." That is the lie relatives are handed, and believing it makes the choice easier to make.

What a relative risks by refusing is real, and it is a reason. It does not turn the choice into somebody else's.

## Some come back

A family that took a side once can see it differently later. On July 8, 2025, asked whether anyone had gotten their family back after the character assassination, I answered that Matthew Cappadocia (aka The Wizard of Oz) (https://www.quora.com/profile/Matthew-Cappadocia-aka-The-Wizard-of-Oz) "mentioned it took 5 years for his family to realize what happened."

Five years is a long time to go without them. It is also not never.
