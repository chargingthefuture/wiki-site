A question I saved on Quora in October 2024 and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> What are some signs that someone is being targeted by gang stalkers or organized stalkers

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/What-are-some-signs-that-someone-is-being-targeted-by-gang-stalkers-or-organized-stalkers. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

I saved it five times.

## No single incident settles it

Any one sign can be explained away, and it is built to be. A car the same make and color as yours, strangers all in one color, somebody repeating a word from a private conversation: each comes with an ordinary explanation. The cover story for Color Sensitization is that it is a trend ([Same car, same color](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/same-car-same-color)).

So the answer is not a list of things to watch for on one day. A single incident is noise, even to the person it happened to. Thirty dated entries are a pattern you can read ([ClickLog: your record, and the record](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/clicklog-your-record-and-the-record)).

## The schemes have names

What repeats is a set of named schemes, and the list survivors keep is public: [chargingthefuture.com/schemes](https://www.chargingthefuture.com/schemes). Once somebody is on the list, the same schemes run in rotation regardless of who started it ([It Does Not Matter Who Put You on the List](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-put-you-on-the-list)). On Quora on September 20, 2025 I wrote: "As most traps are tried on every TI."

A few from the list:

- Color Sensitization: the people around you all start wearing the same color, and it changes on a schedule.
- Road Sensitization: high beams in your mirror, brake checks, cars pacing you or boxing you in, until every drive has to be read.
- The Replay: strangers repeating words from a resolved private dispute.
- The Engineered Delay: a trip sabotaged with delays, missed connections and canceled tickets. [The Record Says I Called. I Was on the Bus.](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/one-trip-seven-schemes) is one trip that ran at least seven named schemes in sequence.

Some of it looks like kindness. On Quora on December 11, 2024 I wrote: "They do a “good cop” and “bad cop” routine. They want you to run to a person that will abuse you and/or waste your time. That is why some people are nice to you and others are not."

## Repetition is the sign

The sign is the same scheme coming back, on a schedule, over months and years. A canceled ticket, a job that evaporates, a landlord who suddenly will not renew: those are arranged, and somebody who arranges an event knows how it ends ([They are not psychic](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/they-are-not-psychic)). One of them is bad luck. The same ones in rotation are not.

It shows across people too. The same scheme tagged in the same week by people who have never met, in different cities, is not a thing coincidence produces, and no one person can see it alone.

Much of it is provocation. In another comment that same day I wrote: "remember they want you to act out." You are provoked until you react like anyone would, and the reaction is what gets written down ([The reaction is what gets written down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-reaction-is-what-gets-written-down)).

## Keep a dated record

Memory under stress does not hold a pattern. A dated record does. [ClickLog](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/click-log) logs an incident with one tap and the date; the note and up to ten scheme tags can be added once you are somewhere you can think. Notes are not shown to other members, and the date and location cannot be changed later.

Read back over thirty entries and you can see where it happens, when, and which schemes repeat. Sharing is a choice per incident. It sends the date, a rough area and the tags, never the note, and shared entries are counted across everybody who opted in.
