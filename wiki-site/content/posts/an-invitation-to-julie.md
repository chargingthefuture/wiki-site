A working economy needs people who can teach the next one. Everything else on the list, the trades, the care, the rides and the rooms, has to be learned by somebody, and most of it is learned first as a child.

## Julie

Julie (https://www.quora.com/profile/Julie-6645), in Tennessee. The Directory profile is at https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory/profile/2a2ebe99-a6dd-4e65-83fe-0a14ceacfba9.

The listing carries one specialization, child development and classroom management. The first half is knowing how children grow and learn at each age. The second is keeping a room of them learning together, which is its own skill and a hard one.

## Why that matters here

The app keeps a catalog of 713 skills, the things a working economy of about five million people needs somebody to be able to do, grouped by sector. Read on October 1, the Directory's 168 people held 227 of them.

Education is one of the thinnest sectors on the list: 4 of its 39 skills are held by anybody.

Targeted Individuals raising children who are targeted too keep asking the same question, and [What about school?](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-about-school) and [Your children can have an option](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/your-children-can-have-an-option) are my answers so far. Every answer to it starts with somebody who knows how a classroom runs.

## What this is not

This is not a request to teach anybody's children, and nothing here says Julie will. Being on a list of what a person can do is not an offer of anything, and the Directory has never worked that way.

## How the listing came to be

I built it from what Julie said about themselves in public and put it up under their name, which is how most of this list was made, one person at a time. They did not sign up, and nobody asked them first. That is why it is marked community-generated and carries my handle: so anybody reading it knows it is my account of somebody else's skills, and knows who to come to about it.

## The invitation

Claiming the listing takes a free account and a few minutes, and after that it is theirs — what it says, and whether it stays up at all.

Nothing else is required. Not using the rest of the app, not posting anything, not agreeing with a word I have written. No is a complete answer, and the listing comes down on request with no account involved in that either.

## This does not run one way

Somebody whose work is other people's children needs things too. A ride. A room. An hour of a trade they do not have. The same listing that makes them findable is what makes those people findable to them.

## Who is on the list

Everyone written about in this series is a self-identified Targeted Individual. That means they said so in public, and it is the only basis for the listing. I cannot verify it, and neither can a reader. Some people who say it are not survivors, and some are the people doing the harassing, so a post here is not a vouch for anybody and should not be read as one. I am not responsible for what anyone named on this list does or says, on the app, on Quora, or anywhere else. The closest anybody can get to finding a real Targeted Individual is through the app, which admits people one at a time through Unlock and has other mechanisms in place to filter actively. Even with those, there is no such thing as a hundred percent.
