A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> What are the indicators of being a high level targeted individual

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/What-are-the-indicators-of-being-a-high-level-targeted-individual. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

## The ranks are on their side

The hierarchy I have written about is theirs. On Quora on September 21, 2025, asked whether the people doing this move up in rank, I answered: "Yes. It is organized crime. And all organizations have hierarchy."

On December 15, 2024, I described how that hierarchy shows up on a street. The handler, or somebody who outranks the person on the street, stays within visual range, and the instructions travel one way, to a phone that only receives: "there is always a higher up instructing from close distance what to do - all based on how you just reacted."

So a level exists, and it is measured on their side. The person on the street is low in it. The person giving instructions is higher. Somebody further up is paying for both.

## The signs point up their chain

What people usually mean by a high level case is reach: how far into ordinary life it goes. That is a fact about the people taking part, and it can be read.

Reach shows in who is in it. On December 7, 2024, I wrote that the people who put me on the list worked "in high ranking positions in well paying jobs." Most of the people taking part are paid in kind, a job, an apartment, a favor, and the money sits further up, laundered through the ordinary economy ([A badge is a job title](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/a-badge-is-a-job-title)).

Reach shows in which businesses are in it. On August 14, 2026, I gave the example of a networking app I was on. Money was invested in it to gain an ownership stake, some of the staff were placed there, and then the staff and the users started harassing me. Investor money kept the app alive while users were paid in perks and payments to take part. My summary of the method was: "Acquire or invest in legitimate businesses." A business is kept funded only while it takes part.

That is the sign worth reading. When the people involved hold titles, and the businesses you depend on turn at once, the reach is long. It tells you about their chain. It does not rank you.

## What a level does not change

Once somebody is on the list, every group in reach joins, and they run the same schemes in rotation regardless of who started it ([It Does Not Matter Who Put You on the List](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-put-you-on-the-list)). Knowing how high their side goes does not change what helps.

What helps is the part of daily life that does not run through people who can be reached. If reach travels through jobs, landlords and businesses that can be bought, then the useful question is who among those you deal with is not taking part. The Directory lists survivors by what they can do, so a person who needs a mechanic, a ride or a place to stay can find one directly. Being on it costs nobody an account. Foundation is where members offer and trade that help.
