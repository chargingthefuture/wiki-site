The listing carries one word, Advocacy, and it is a placeholder. Jerrod has not written about a trade anywhere I could find, so the listing holds the one thing that is on the record: what they wrote on Quora is about Targeted Individuals. It says nothing more than that, and neither does this post.

## The question

Jerrod asked on Quora:

> How can you help me? I'm a victim of gang stalking and I know some of who are involved.

I answered it in [How I can help](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/how-i-can-help). The short version: nobody honest can promise to make it stop. What helps is a private, dated record of what the people involved do, not confronting them, people who believe you without an argument first, and help with the week from people who are not part of it.

## Somebody to talk to

Jerrod has asked for that on Quora more than once, in their own words:

> Does anybody want to chat now?

> Can I get a hold of you? This is happening to me.

The [Commons](https://app.chargingthefuture.com) is a group chat of other Targeted Individuals, in the app. It is where that question gets an answer, from people who will not spend the first hour on whether it is happening.

## Where the listing comes in

That last part is what the [Directory](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory) is for. It lists people and what they can do, and it is how a ride, a room, or an hour of somebody else's trade finds the person who needs it. [Foundation](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/foundation) is where members offer and exchange that help.

Being on the list is also how other Targeted Individuals find you. Right now Jerrod's listing says only that they are here. Claimed, it can say what they can do, and that turns a name on a list into somebody other people can ask.

## How the listing came to be

Jerrod did not sign up, and nobody asked them first. That is why the listing is marked community-generated and carries my handle: so anybody reading it knows it is my account of somebody else, and knows who to come to about it.

## The invitation

Claiming the listing takes a free account and a few minutes, and after that it is theirs — what it says, whether the placeholder gets replaced with something they can actually do, and whether it stays up at all.

Nothing else is required. Not using the rest of the app, not posting anything, not agreeing with a word I have written. No is a complete answer, and the listing comes down on request with no account involved in that either.

## Who is on the list

Everyone written about in this series is a self-identified Targeted Individual. That means they said so in public, and it is the only basis for the listing. I cannot verify it, and neither can a reader. Some people who say it are not survivors, and some are the people doing the harassing, so a post here is not a vouch for anybody and should not be read as one. I am not responsible for what anyone named on this list does or says, on the app, on Quora, or anywhere else. The closest anybody can get to finding a real Targeted Individual is through the app, which admits people one at a time through Unlock and has other mechanisms in place to filter actively. Even with those, there is no such thing as a hundred percent.
