A question I saved on Quora in October 2024 and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Can gang stalkers actually be out to harm or even kill their targeted individuals

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Can-gang-stalkers-actually-be-out-to-harm-or-even-kill-their-targeted-individuals. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

## Yes, and more than once

Yes. When somebody on Quora asked whether anyone had had a real attempt on their life, I answered on September 21, 2025: "Yes, multiple times."

On December 15, 2024 I had written out what they were. An attempted abduction. Attempts to find where I lived and stage a suicide there. Staged car accidents: chasing me and stopping short, boxing me in between their cars or against a barrier, throwing large objects onto the road so that I, or the cars around me, would spin out. A gun pulled on me twice, to my knowledge. One was real; the other could only have been street theater.

It is not only me. Two people I knew would not take part, and proved hard to turn into targets. They were killed ([The best description of it I have read](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/best-description-i-have-read)).

## The method that leaves the least evidence

In that same answer I wrote that when one approach fails they try others, "opting for the one that leaves the least amount of evidence." There are deaths, and they are arranged to look like something else ([What's Your Hangup?](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/whats-your-hangup)). A staged suicide reads as a suicide, and a staged crash reads as a crash. The list of endings they want, the car crash among them, is in [The reaction is what gets written down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-reaction-is-what-gets-written-down).

Jail is on that list too, and I said why in 2024: "jail provides a better environment to torture you or fake your suicide." So they stage physical altercations and send somebody to provoke a fight. When that did not work on me, they caused my car to break down and leave me stranded, a police officer who was one of them gave me a ride home, and it was framed as an arrest.

## Most of it is slower

Most of the harm does not happen in one day. In the same answer I called making you feel crazy or defective "a kill strategy". The isolation around it, with no friends, family or job, works the same way, toward what I called "an orchestrated slow death."

The pushing can be direct. On September 17, 2025 I wrote that they told me a lie about why I was targeted, "and immediately after tried to convince me to commit suicide." For many years they also tried to get me to buy a gun and use it on a crowd. When that did not work, one of them punched me in the face ([Something to lose](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/something-to-lose)).

## Only the target's life is on the line

Across these networks there is an understanding that a target can be robbed, injured or killed and nobody will answer for it, and that the people who did it will be rewarded instead. No such cover extends to the people taking part. What they risk is property, a job, a reputation, and never their life ([Why people leave, and what it costs them](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/why-people-leave-and-what-it-costs)).
