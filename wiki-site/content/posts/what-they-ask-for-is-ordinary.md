A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> What are the motivations of individuals who claim to be Targeted Individuals

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/What-are-the-motivations-of-individuals-who-claim-to-be-Targeted-Individuals. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this.

## A description, not a position

The word claim places the motive on the person speaking, as though saying it were a move made for some gain. Saying that something is being done to you is a description of what is being done. It says nothing about how the person carries it, and it is not a stance anybody takes up.

So I will not guess at motives. What I can do is point at what people ask for once they have said it, because that is written down.

## Read what they ask for

The questions survivors put to each other on Quora are mostly practical.

On December 4, 2025, somebody asked whether it can keep a person from getting a job. I answered: "Yes." Then I passed on what another survivor had taught me, healthy volunteer studies, "the only job I can get." How income is cut off at work is in [It happens at work](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/it-happens-at-work).

On October 11, 2025, somebody asked for the best way to live with it, "with work, relationships, and moving. A lot seems impossible now." I answered that the first thing is to live as normally as possible, and then: "when you can, connect with other TIs." And: "real friends will make things better."

On January 15, 2026, somebody in Perth, Western Australia asked how to be added to the list of Targeted Individuals. They had recently found others nearby. I pointed them to a map of people who have identified themselves, and to the part of the app where survivors can find a place to live with each other.

On February 25, 2026, somebody asked where people go for refuge. I answered that there is no place out of reach, and that it is "better to focus on how to mitigate their impact on your life."

None of those asks for attention. They ask for a job, a place to live, a way to keep relationships through a move, and other people it is happening to. Those are what anybody wants. The difference is that a survivor has to ask for them out loud, from strangers, because the ordinary routes to them are the ones being closed.

## What is built from the asking

The Directory started in October 2025 as a running list of Targeted Individuals alongside their talents ([From them to me](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/from-them-to-me)). It is the answer to the Perth question: a findable list of people and what they can do. Being listed costs nobody an account, and anybody can claim their entry or have it taken down. Reading it takes a sign-in.

Lighthouse is the answer to the housing question. In that January answer I called it "the Airbnb for TIs," for anybody who wants to move into a house, an apartment or a community with another survivor.

Both were built from what people asked for. Neither one needed a theory of why they asked.
