A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Why is there a lack of awareness about targeted individuals and gangstalking

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Why-is-there-a-lack-of-awareness-about-targeted-individuals-and-gangstalking. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

I saved it twice.

Why people who are told about it deny it is in [Denial is part of the method](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/denial-is-part-of-the-method). This answer is about what reaches people in the first place.

## What stays up

On Quora on September 18, 2025, I wrote: "If someone is on here saying spiritual warfare and the illuminati their account is up. Otherwise we are getting banned. As soon as I started a Quora space I got a 14 day ban. Then I created a TI app and within one minute of me announcing it, they permanently banned my account."

That was not the last one. The running count of erased accounts, and the one address that does not change, are kept on [Old links, new links](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/old-links-new-links). When an account goes, every answer and comment on it goes too.

So the public does not meet a random sample of what survivors say. It meets what was left standing, and what was left standing is the version easiest to dismiss.

## The version that reads as crazy

On October 11, 2025, asked what the people doing this call themselves, I answered that it is various groups collaborating, and that "there are two legal terms for it already - human trafficking and group vigilantism." In the same answer I warned that repeating their own words to people who do not know about it "will label you as crazy, which is one of their missions."

That is the mechanism. A plain description in ordinary legal terms is a claim somebody could check. A description in terms of the supernatural is one most readers stop reading. If the first kind comes down and the second stays up, the lack of awareness is not an accident of attention. It is what was left after the removals.

## Most of it is never reported

The United Nations' own reports count about 2 million people describing this, and that is only the people who reported. I am not in that number, because I have never reported mine to anyone ([How the math works](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/how-the-math-works)). Awareness can only be built from what was recorded somewhere, and most of this never was.

## Keep it where it cannot be deleted

On September 20, 2025, I wrote under a post that had been removed: "Quora deleted it. We need to screenshot stuff if it is good."

That is the practical answer. Write it in plain terms, and keep it somewhere that does not depend on a platform's decision. Everything I write lives on this blog, in full, with a timestamped history nobody can quietly empty. What survivors learn about what works, from having tried it, goes into [WhatWorks](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-works-knowledge-that-survives-a-ban), where an account ban elsewhere does not take it down with it.

Awareness does not need a better story. It needs the plain one to still be there the next time somebody looks.
