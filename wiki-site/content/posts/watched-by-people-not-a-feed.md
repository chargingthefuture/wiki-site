A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Are targeted individuals put on a live stream for all stalkers to see

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Are-targeted-individuals-put-on-a-live-stream-for-all-stalkers-to-see. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

Why people film a target on their phones is in [Filmed for a clip](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/filmed-for-a-clip). This answer is about the feed people picture behind it.

## Some of them believe there is one

I have not seen a live stream. I have seen people taking part who assume there is one.

On Quora on October 6, 2025, asked about hidden cameras, I wrote that some of the people taking part "do not know either if there are cameras or not. And rather assumed there are cameras recording Targets 24/7."

In the same answer I described one of them getting angry that I had photographed her. She brought in a second person and asked, in front of me, to see "the stalking footage they have been collecting on me." The second one said "there are no hidden cameras inside the building, and therefore, there is no proof I took the picture."

So the footage she believed in was not there, and the person she asked for it said so.

## Where cameras might be

That answer drew a line. In a target's own home, maybe: "If it is your apartment, they might have cameras. Because you will predictably be there." In public and private facilities, "they likely do not." And: "if they tell you there are, there isn't." They told me there were cameras in my fire alarms, so that I would repeat it and look unreliable ([Denial is part of the method](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/denial-is-part-of-the-method)).

Tracking does not need a broadcast either. On July 26, 2026 I wrote: "nearly everyone in the world has mobile phones. That is an easier way to track people."

## The watching is done by people

Most of what feels like a stream is a person nearby. On December 2, 2024 I wrote that "one specific individual hung back and watched me to determine my 'breaking point,'" noting my emotional state each day a planned event took place. Once I hit a certain point, he disappeared and the harassment intensified. That was when they "began to make the effort in showing me they are watching."

That watching was done by a person standing back, not a feed.

## The camera is not the reach

On January 21, 2026, one of them walked up while I stood under a camera and said, "you know there is a camera?" I wrote that day: "big picture the camera does not matter — their economy does" ([My call to action](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Call-to-Action)).

Being watched is not what gives somebody power over you. Needing something from them is ([Estonia did not get to solve it either](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/estonia-did-not-get-to-solve-it-either)). Who fixes your car, who rents you a room, who drives you: each one moved to a survivor is reach taken away, feed or no feed.
