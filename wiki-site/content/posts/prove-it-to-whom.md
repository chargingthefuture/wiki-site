A question on Quora, saved on September 18, 2025:

> How do prove I'm being gangstalked?

The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this instead.

[Proving it, and taking your accounts back](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/proving-it-and-taking-your-accounts-back) answered the how. One incident can be denied, and a pattern is much harder to deny, so write down what happens, with the date, as it happens: what, when, where, and who if you know. That record is the proof in every case below.

What it proves depends on who it is for.

## To yourself

This comes first, and it is the one the record does best. Over months, a dated record shows a pattern you could not hold in your head, and it answers the doubt that months of being told otherwise put there. Nobody else has to read it for that to work.

## To somebody who can act

A police report, a complaint, a lawyer's file: each carries one incident. Your own record carries the pattern behind it, and it is what you bring when you go. [Who to report it to](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-to-report-it-to) is my answer to where. None of this is legal advice.

## To the people around you

This is where most people spend their proof first, and where it moves least. Somebody who has decided is rarely moved by evidence. And the people closest to you are often the ones reached first: [the best description of it I have read](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/best-description-i-have-read) lays out how relatives are recruited before the person being targeted knows anything is happening.

That is not a reason to stop talking to them. It is a reason not to make the next year depend on convincing them.

## To other survivors

They do not need convincing, which leaves the record free to do something else. In [ClickLog](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/click-log), an entry you choose to share joins a trend view of what members report, and that view shows what no private log can: the same experience, filed by different people in different places, and which named schemes keep arriving with it. [The record has started](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/clicklog-the-record-has-started) shows what the first entries in it look like. Your notes are not shown to anyone else, and names belong in your own record, not on a shared page.

And the [Commons](https://app.chargingthefuture.com), the group chat in the app, is a room of people who do not need the first hour spent on whether it is happening.
