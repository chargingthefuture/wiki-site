Two questions I saved on Quora in October 2024, as their titles were recovered from the addresses they were saved at:

> What is a gang stalker and how do they operate

> What is gang stalking

The addresses they were saved at are https://www.quora.com/What-is-a-gang-stalker-and-how-do-they-operate and https://www.quora.com/What-is-gang-stalking. I saved the second one 12 times. I answered it once, on December 15, 2024, with one thing I had seen rather than a definition; that answer is kept on the [Record](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/record). The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the questions keep the words they were asked in.

The fullest description on this blog is not mine. [The best description of it I have read](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/best-description-i-have-read) carries Sherri Jenkins's answer to nearly the first question, with her list of the methods, and it is the post to read for that list. What follows is the short version in my own words.

## What it is

It is organized crime. The [manifesto](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/chargingthefuture/The-Answer:-EXIT-THEIR-ECONOMY,-EXIT-THE-PSYOP) describes a coordinated network that profits from surveilling, isolating and exploiting people, and says it is not a mystery and not spiritual warfare: it is human trafficking, and it is old. On Quora on October 11, 2025 I wrote that "there are two legal terms for it already - human trafficking and group vigilantism." [What organized crime means](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-organized-crime-means) has the narrower definition of that phrase.

The Dictionary has the plainest version: a control mechanism run by traffickers, with the harassment as its enforcement arm. Specterati harassment is my word for the harassment itself, and a Specterati operator is a person who carries it out.

## Who does it

Not a secret society. [That label is their own brag](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/they-are-not-a-secret-society): a secret society hides who belongs to it and what it is for, and these people announce themselves to their targets all day. The signaling is the harassment.

It is a great many ordinary people in a distributed network. On October 11, 2025 I wrote that "it is various groups collaborating," and that "To them, it is business as usual, no need for naming it." [An epidemic, not a secret society](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/an-epidemic-not-a-secret-society) covers how they are paid: somebody brings a stranger slander about a person, and the stranger gets sex, drugs, a job or friends for acting on it. Members are handed a name and do not know the person.

## How it operates

Most of it is not ordered. There is a standing permission: anybody who wants to have a go at a target may, and nothing happens to them for it. Some of it is planned and coordinated, and that part runs on instructions. The one time I saw how, by accident, it was an app on an operative's phone. On December 15, 2024 I wrote: "The chat app is one way communication only." The person in front of me looked as though they were messaging. "They are only reading instructions."

The methods are small on purpose: following, crowding, a car outside, a rumor that reaches a job before you do. Each one is nothing taken alone, which is what makes it so hard to report. What they are after is what that costs you: your account of your own life, your work, your housing, and who you have to go through to get any of them.

## What a definition does not do

It does not stop anything. [No method I know of has ended it](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-can-be-stopped), mine included. What it does is show a person who was told they were imagining it that the thing has a shape and a name.
