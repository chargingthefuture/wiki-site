A question I saved on Quora, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Where can targeted individuals find help How can I find others in my area to find local help or community

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Where-can-targeted-individuals-find-help-How-can-I-find-others-in-my-area-to-find-local-help-or-community-1. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

[Where to go for help](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/where-to-go-for-help) answers the first half. This answer is about the second half: people near you, and which help to trust.

## What I said in 2024

Lorenzo (https://www.quora.com/profile/Lorenzo-896) answered it: "This probably isn't what you want to hear, but don't take any help. I know it's tempting, but these kinds of things are almost always a set up."

On October 29, 2024 I left a comment under that answer: "I agree. Do not seek it or take it. It is always a trap to create more slander, kill you or get you committed."

I had taken help once. It came from a counselor who was part of it, and who steered me into a specific apartment complex where people could be placed all around me. Before that, they had trouble pinning me down, because I traveled a lot.

## Local is where they already are

Asking for help near you means asking in a place that has been arranged around you. On June 6, 2025, asked why there are so many of them in one town, I wrote: "They migrate to where TIs are. They blend in and recruit locals. And buy out anyone/business who doesn't want to join."

So the help to doubt most is the help that arrives on its own: somebody nearby who heard you were struggling and has a place, a job or a contact ready. It already knows where you are, and it chose you.

## Offered, and found

I would still answer the 2024 question the same way about help that is offered. What has changed is that there is now another kind: help you go and find, from people you picked by what they can do.

Saying no to offered help is not the same as staying alone, and staying alone is not the goal. On October 2, 2025 I wrote: "Keeping TIs apart is what they need to be successful." In the same comment I wrote that I had posted sixteen times that I wanted to connect with other TIs in real life, and nobody had responded.

Finding three people who could help took me two years of filtering by hand ([Two years to find three people](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/two-years-to-find-three-people)). All three are listed in the Directory.

## How to look

The [Directory](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory) lists survivors and what they can do. Names, locations and a way to reach somebody are behind a free account, and a person checks each new member before they can use the app. Being listed costs nobody an account.

Before any of that, the front page at https://chargingthefuture.com counts how many people in the Directory could help with what you need, with no name and no account ([See who could help you, before you make an account](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/see-who-could-help-without-an-account)).

The difference from a stranger who offers is the direction. You read what somebody can do, you decide whether to reach them, and the contact starts from your side.

For people to talk to rather than a skill, the [Commons](https://app.chargingthefuture.com) is a group chat of other Targeted Individuals, in the app. For living near each other, [A gated community, and who could plan it](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/a-gated-community-and-who-could-plan-it) is my answer.
