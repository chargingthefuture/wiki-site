Losing your housing does not only mean losing somewhere to sleep. It means the ordinary business of washing moves into facilities that belong to other people, on their terms, under whatever conditions they set.

I used a fitness gym for a while. It stopped being usable and I do not go back. Now it is the co-ed spa facilities inside expensive places, where everyone else present is a paying member with a reason to be there.

Those showers do not lock from the inside. They open.

What has happened, more than once, is that the door has been opened at the exact moment I had undressed, by a man, and there has been laughter. The laughter is the part that settles it. An accidental door is silent and embarrassed. This was neither.

## Then getting to the next one

After leaving one facility for another, a man circled me on the metro and again as I approached the new place. It ended in an altercation, and he became physically violent.

Those two things belong together, and they are why the trip to a shower is as much of the problem as the shower. Getting somewhere is exposure. Being there is exposure. Neither is optional, because washing is not optional.

## What ClickLog gained this week

Two additions, both live in the app:

A problem tag, sexually assaulted or deliberately exposed and humiliated. It covers the range members report — assault and rape at the worst, and short of any contact, deliberate exposure. It is deliberately coarse: the tag names the harm so it can be counted, and what happened stays in the private note where it belongs.

A scheme, The Staged Exposure. That is the engineered form: the keys, the timing, the door opened at the moment it does the most damage, and nothing required from the target except being present.

## Why I count the honeypot in the same category

A honeypot is a manufactured romance — an approach that was arranged, run for as long as it is useful, and ended in whatever way does the most harm. It has been on the scheme list for a while.

I have not been through one myself. But every targeted person I have heard describe one has described the same two endings: being humiliated sexually, or having a domestic violence claim invented against them. That is sexual violence carried out through manipulation rather than through a door handle, and I do not think it belongs in a gentler category because the coercion was social.

It also does a second job, which is the reason it keeps appearing. A staged relationship gives someone standing to say "I know this person." That claim is what makes the invented accusation credible afterward.

## The answer is not a better lock

There is no version of this that gets solved by vigilance in someone else's shower. The fix is not needing to be there.

Housing is the first half. LightHouse lists places offered by hosts who are survivors themselves, which is a different proposition from a facility where you are tolerated as a guest. Somewhere with a door that locks and a bathroom that is yours removes the entire category.

Rides are the second half. TrustTransport arranges transport inside the community, which matters because the journey to and from is where the rest of it happens. The more of your movement that runs through people who are not doing this to you, the fewer occasions there are for any of it.

That is the whole design, stated plainly: not protection in the moment, but fewer moments requiring protection.

## And the fake relationships

The same logic answers the honeypot, which is harder because it arrives looking like something good.

An approach that was arranged does not survive being asked for something real. A person who was sent to be close to you will not do your repair, take your shift, or drive you somewhere at a cost to themselves — providing material help is the one thing that consistently does not happen. So an economy that puts the exchange first filters them out early, without anyone having to accuse anybody of anything.

What is left after that filter are people who showed up with something in their hands, more than once. Relationships that come out of that are not arranged, because nobody arranges a person who actually helps you.
