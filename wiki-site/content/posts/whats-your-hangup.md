If something about the Skills Economy stops you, I want to know what it is.

Not as a challenge. I am asking because I cannot answer an objection nobody says out loud, and because some of them are going to be right.

You can write it under this post. Reading takes nothing; writing takes a free account: [app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/fireside](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/fireside).

Here is the one I hear most, and the one I want to answer properly.

## "It is not a real job"

Underneath that is usually something like: I had a career, I was good at it, and this looks like a step down.

I understand it. I want to put the record next to it.

## Destitution comes after the proof, not before

Look at when it happens.

A person gets educated. Gets hired. Gets good. Gets promoted. They are a productive participant in the global economy, by any measure that economy uses.

Then it starts. The references dry up. The role is restructured. The contract is not renewed. The next interview goes strangely. It compounds until there is nothing coming in.

The destitution arrives *after* the proof of capability, not instead of it. That order matters, and it is the same order for almost everybody this happens to.

If what they wanted was your work, they would have let you keep the job.

## So your participation is not what they are after

This is the part worth sitting with.

Whatever is being extracted here, it is not your labor and it is not your output. They removed those on purpose, from people who were demonstrably good at producing them.

Five million people leaving the global economy would not dent it. Not a little — not at all. And they already push survivors out of it, which tells you they do not want that participation either.

## This is the opposite of what was asked for before

Dr. King's last campaign was about money. Not symbolism, not seats — wages, jobs, and a union for sanitation workers in Memphis, and a Poor People's Campaign aimed at economic demands. The ask was to be let into the economy on fair terms.

He was killed in the middle of that work.

What happens to Targeted Individuals now is not that. There are deaths, and they are arranged to look like something else. But the public killing of somebody for making an economic demand is largely not the method anymore.

I can think of two reasons. One is that killing somebody in the open is no longer something a person can do and walk away from. The other is that what is wanted out of us changed — that the daily suffering of somebody living in destitution is now the thing being harvested, and a dead person stops producing it.

I do not know which. I am not sure it matters, because both land in the same place: they do not want you in that economy in any sense.

## Their whole method runs on humiliation

Every scheme, when you strip it down, is built to make a person feel small in front of others. Shamed. Discredited. Not believed.

Knowing that changes the shape of the question.

If the method is humiliation, and the removal from work is part of the method, then staying loyal to an economy that already removed you is not principle. It is the last part of the humiliation still doing its job.

So what exactly is the hangup?

## What working among them actually looks like from here

I want to be straight about the alternative rather than strawman it.

Until there is justice, there is no reliable path to working among them except the one they allow. And what gets allowed is narrow: roles where somebody else sets your hours, watches your day, and decides your pay.

That is not a judgment about the work or the people doing it. Every one of those jobs is real work and somebody has to do it. The point is which jobs stay available to a person who is being targeted, and why those particular ones do.

They are the ones with the most control in them.

## The Skills Economy is built the other way round

You set the level of engagement. One ride a month, or nothing for six months, or every day.

Nobody schedules you. Nobody approves your rate. Nobody can remove you from it, because there is no gate for anybody to stand at — there is no second tier and no way to buy influence over another account.

And if you are ambitious, the ceiling is not set by whoever is willing to hire you. [What's your 1%?](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/workforce?view=one-percent) runs that arithmetic for your own trade: one percent of five million survivors is fifty thousand people, and it works backwards from there to what serving them would be worth in a year.

That figure is not a promise. It is what the arithmetic says is available to somebody who decides to go after it.

## Back to the question

I would rather hear the objection than have you close the tab holding it.

If it is "I do not believe the numbers", say that. If it is "I do not trust an app", say that. If it is "I tried and nobody answered", say that one especially, because that is a thing I can fix.

Say it under this post. Nobody has to agree with me for this to be worth writing down.
