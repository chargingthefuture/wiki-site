Under the Peace Battle 2 post on the Skills Economy space on Quora, Oksana (https://www.quora.com/profile/Oksana-387) asked:

> How long we are suppose to hold?

![Oksana's comment on Quora, captured October 4, 2026, under the Peace Battle 2 post in the Skills Economy space. It reads: How long we are suppose to hold?](images/oksana-2026-10-04.jpg)

It is the right question, and no post here had answered it. The [Peace Battle 2](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/peace-battle-2) announcement said it runs for years and left it there. There are two answers, and by the end they are the same one.

## For our lifetimes

The first answer is the one nobody wants: there is no date. I wrote in [Estonia did not get to solve it either](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/estonia-did-not-get-to-solve-it-either) about a country of 1.3 million people next to a neighbor a hundred times its size. The pressure on it did not end in 1991 and there is no version of the future where its geography changes. Estonia did not get an ending. It built a working country anyway, by needing that neighbor for less every year.

If holding means waiting for the people doing this to stop, the honest answer is that most of us will be holding for the rest of our lives. That is not advice. It is the condition the rest of this post starts from.

## Or it was never a hold

The second answer is that Peace Battle 2 is not a hold at all, and the word is doing the damage.

A typical protest stands in a physical place and demands to be recognized. It faces outward, toward whoever it wants an answer from, and it lasts until that answer comes or the people go home. There is nothing wrong with that. It is just not what this is.

Peace Battle 2 borrows an idea from the years after the United States Civil War, the one W. E. B. Du Bois named in 1903 when he wrote about the peace-battle of that century: a group of people who were not going to be given anything, taking a stand by combining what they already had among themselves. It faced inward.

So does this. I am not protesting to the people doing this. We are protesting to Targeted Individuals. Everything we need and want, we already have collectively: the skills are on the [Directory](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory), the arithmetic is in Workforce, and the goal board is where a task changes hands. Peace Battle 2 is a challenge to every Targeted Individual to turn to another Targeted Individual for something of material value. A ride, a repair, a room, an hour of somebody's trade. It is a conscious choice, made again each day, not to fund and not to take part in the economy that is run on us.

A protest that faces outward has a length, because it is waiting. This one is not waiting on anybody's answer, so there is nothing for it to run out of.

## Both at once

Maybe the two answers are one answer. We hold for our lifetimes, and the holding is not endurance. It is where we turn when we need something. Coexistence over conspiracy: the people doing this do not go away, and they stop being the subject.

So, how long? As long as you need things and another Targeted Individual can supply them. A day at a time, which is the only way anybody has ever done it.
