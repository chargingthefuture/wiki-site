## Not one or the other

Both, and a good deal more. When somebody asked what they call themselves, I answered on Quora on October 11, 2025 that they probably do not call themselves anything, "as it is various groups collaborating, i.e., Lions Club, gangs (i.e., crips), private security firms, Neighborhood Watch, fire departments, police, etc."

My position is that it is a great many ordinary people in a distributed network of organized crime ([What can be stopped](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-can-be-stopped)). Police officer is a job title, and so is the person who runs the watch group on your street. A title says nothing about the character of the person holding it, and it is not evidence that they are outside it. [A badge is a job title](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/a-badge-is-a-job-title) goes into that.

## The case

Yes, though what is built is mostly not a criminal case. It is a picture: the standing verdict that you are mentally ill or dangerous, the one that follows you into a doctor's office, a housing application, a family conversation, a courtroom ([Why They Want You Living With Family](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/why-they-want-you-living-with-family)).

The method is provocation. You are provoked until you react like anyone would, and the reaction is what gets written down. Street theater is staged altercations to provoke you for a slander campaign. People walked into me on purpose, in view of cameras, trying to get me to hit back so the footage would show a violent Black woman ([The Assistant Is Not a Clinician](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-assistant-is-not-a-clinician)).

Two versions go further than a picture. One is a false diagnosis: working with the police and the mental health community to get you committed under a false schizophrenia diagnosis. The other is a false accusation, such as the fabricated domestic violence claim that an arranged romance makes credible ([A Safe Place to Shower](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/a-safe-place-to-shower)).

A picture is not answered by arguing with it ([You cannot argue a smear down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/you-cannot-argue-a-smear-down)). A person with no witnesses and no record is easy to describe as unstable; a person with a dated log is not. [ClickLog](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/click-log) is that log: a date, a location if you choose one, a few seconds an entry, and your notes stay yours.

None of this is legal advice.

## The main goal

On Quora on October 2, 2025, I put it this way: "Ultimately, they want any of the following: to bankrupt you, for you to commit suicide, to kill you in a car crash, or to have you in prison." A mental institution belongs on the same list.

Under those endings sits the economy. The harassment is not the point; the economy behind it is ([the manifesto](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/chargingthefuture/The-Answer:-EXIT-THEIR-ECONOMY,-EXIT-THE-PSYOP)). Pushing somebody out of a job cuts them off from money, from the colleagues who might have backed them up, and from the reference that gets the next job ([It happens at work](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/it-happens-at-work)). Once a person is nearly alone, every ride, every room, every job runs through hands that can be reached ([Nearly a hundred percent isolated](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/nearly-a-hundred-percent-isolated)).

So the case and the goal are one thing. A person without income, without witnesses and without a record of their own is easy to describe however the picture needs.
