A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Why would someone or some place or thing want to gangstalk me Im nobody important just a regular person amongst the billions

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Why-would-someone-or-some-place-or-thing-want-to-gangstalk-me-Im-nobody-important-just-a-regular-person-amongst-the-billions. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

## The question expects a big reason

The effort looks enormous, so the reason must be too. Somebody, some place or some thing, with a stake in you. That expectation is reasonable, and it is the one the people doing it feed.

On Quora on September 17, 2025 I wrote: "They have told me they are the new world order, Illuminati, this is spiritual warfare and I chose to be a TI in the birth canal." Each of those is an explanation the size of the effort. None of them is true, and every one of them leaves a person waiting on a power they cannot reach.

The same answer ended with what is usually there instead: "It is more digestible to think it's spiritual warfare or new world order than a degenerate low IQ drug dealer you accidentally made eye contact with while waiting for a ride."

## The people doing it are regular people too

Importance was never the threshold, on either side. The person who puts a name on the list is usually somebody with a small grievance and access, not an institution with a plan. On June 5, 2025 I wrote about what the people doing it imagine they are part of: "I laugh at what kissing the ring would be in a small town of an average Joe that simply goes to work and goes home."

The people who join in are paid in ordinary things. A stranger is handed slander about somebody they may never have met, and receives sex, drugs, a job or friends for carrying it ([An epidemic, not a secret society](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/an-epidemic-not-a-secret-society)). On January 2, 2026 I wrote that it "involves operatives of organized crime across all classes, jobs and ethnicities." Nothing in that arrangement needs the target to be anybody in particular. It needs a name, and people who will be paid to act on it.

## Not famous is not the same as not known

A regular person can hope that a name in the crowd gets lost. Once it is on the list, it does not. On November 2, 2025, answering somebody who had seen less of it lately, I wrote: "Not to be funny, but think of D list celebrities. They go in and out of the headlines based on public interest. And like them you have been doxxed to a global network."

Who started it stops mattering after that point. Every group in reach joins, and the same schemes run in rotation ([It Does Not Matter Who Put You on the List](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-put-you-on-the-list)).

## One among millions

The question counts one person against billions. A closer count is one regular person among many others. On December 10, 2025 I wrote: "My guess is at least 5 million Targeted Individuals globally."

That is the number the Directory is built around. It lists survivors by what they can do, so a regular person looking for a ride, a repair or somebody who will believe them can find another one. Being on it costs nobody an account. A hundred people exchanging at a time is enough to live on ([How few people it takes](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/how-few-people-it-takes)), and the [Commons](https://app.chargingthefuture.com), the group chat, reads without signing in.
