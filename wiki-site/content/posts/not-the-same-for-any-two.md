A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> What type of harassment are you receiving from the gang stalkers I m curious to know for my own understanding of what some victims like yourself receive and witness Thank you

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/What-type-of-harassment-are-you-receiving-from-the-gang-stalkers-I-m-curious-to-know-for-my-own-understanding-of-what-some-victims-like-yourself-receive-and-witness-Thank-you. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

The question asks for two things: what I receive, and what I witness. The second answers more than the first.

## What I receive

On October 11, 2025, a survey of Targeted Individuals on Quora asked what type of harassment each of us experiences and for how much of the day. My answer was two lines: the stalking, 100%, and DEW, 100%. DEW is Directed Energy Weapon.

The same day, in another answer, I wrote that until then the only DEW I had experienced was vibration, and then: "I walked into a public library and sat down and I felt like I was being boiled from the inside out." And: "They have all the tools available to harass you at any moment."

The named schemes, the ones survivors tag and compare, are listed publicly at [chargingthefuture.com/schemes](https://www.chargingthefuture.com/schemes). One trip of mine ran at least seven of them in sequence ([The Record Says I Called. I Was on the Bus.](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/one-trip-seven-schemes)).

## What I witness

The survey quoted a common explanation: that the difference between targets comes down to what is available in an area, how many people can be recruited there, and whether the technical setup exists. I answered that it is not correct, and why.

I have spent significant time with other Targeted Individuals. I wrote that the people doing this will do "0%" to a Targeted Individual next to me, on both counts, and 100% to me, in the same room. Same place, same people available, same hour. The difference was not the area.

So the amount is chosen. I wrote then that it is largely a choice, and that they pick which targets to go after based on their bias and on which slander they were handed. Everybody on the list has been named to all of them, whether or not they act on it ([It Does Not Matter Who Put You on the List](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-put-you-on-the-list)).

That is why my answer is not a scale. Somebody who reads what I receive and finds their own week lighter has learned nothing about their own case, and neither has somebody whose week is heavier. In the answer about the library, I wrote: "They are selectively choosing how much to reveal in their level of harassment to you. And how much harassment they give you."

## Do not stay apart because of it

The same survey answer ended on what follows from this: Targeted Individuals should not avoid each other. Avoiding other targets does not make your own targeting less. It only leaves you more isolated.

What makes two accounts comparable is not the amount. It is a dated record of the same named schemes, kept by people who have never met. [ClickLog](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/click-log) logs an incident with the date in one tap, and sharing is a choice per incident ([ClickLog: your record, and the record](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/clicklog-your-record-and-the-record)). The Directory lists survivors by what they can do, so finding each other does not depend on anybody nearby. Being on it costs nobody an account.
