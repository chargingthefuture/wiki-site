A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> What covert social engineering tactics are used to manipulate and harass targeted individuals

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/What-covert-social-engineering-tactics-are-used-to-manipulate-and-harass-targeted-individuals. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

The tactics have names, and the list survivors keep is public: [chargingthefuture.com/schemes](https://www.chargingthefuture.com/schemes). Once somebody is on the list, the same schemes run in rotation ([It Does Not Matter Who Put You on the List](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-put-you-on-the-list)). This answer is about what sits under the list: how a tactic is chosen.

## Tried, then dropped

There is no master plan fitted to one person. There is trial and error. On Quora on October 2, 2025, writing about something that was tried on me once and never again, I said that as with everything they do, they "repeatedly do whatever 'sticks.' Since I did not fall for that trick, they moved on to other things."

That is the shape of it. A trick is tried. If the target does not take it up, it is dropped and another one is tried. If the target does, it comes back.

## Many shapes, one aim

The tricks do not all look hostile. On March 4, 2025, asked how survivors can tell who has joined a group to use what is said there, I wrote that the latest tactic was to answer my posts with "positivity." They repeat a story I have told about them "with a slight variation, to make it seem they are one of us. But they are not because they'll leave a small 'tell'/calling card in the message." And: "they will shape shift at anytime with their good cop, bad cop routines."

Some of it is spoken. On July 8, 2025, I wrote that the latest script being said to me was to "stop trying to control everything," which is what they are trying to do to the target and to everybody who crosses the target's path. "Everything they accuse you of, they are doing."

Some of it is a push. On December 14, 2025, I wrote that people taking part have tried to entrap me in violent crimes "in an attempt to get me arrested," and have encouraged me to fight them.

A friendly reply, an accusation, an invitation to fight. Each one looks like something else, and none of them settles anything alone. In that same December answer I wrote that it is never one thing somebody does that gives them away.

## What sticking means

A tactic sticks when it gets a reaction: a fight, a panic, a private detail let slip, a word repeated back. The reaction is what gets written down ([The reaction is what gets written down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-reaction-is-what-gets-written-down)). Not taking a trick up does not end the targeting. It ends that trick, and the next one is tried.

So the useful question is less which tactic this is than which ones keep coming back. Memory under stress does not hold that. A dated record does. Read back over months, it shows which tricks were tried once and dropped, and which return.
