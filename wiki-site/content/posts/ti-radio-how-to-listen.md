This one is for listeners. You do not need an account for any of it.

[TI Radio](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/ti-radio) is a schedule of live audio shows that survivors host. Almost every other screen in the app is behind approval. Listening is not.

## Find a show

Open the [TI Radio guide](https://app.lagatos.com/ti-radio). You do not sign in. You do not get verified. You do not join anything.

The guide holds seven days, cut into 90-minute slots. Each row shows the time, who is hosting, and what it is about. The slot happening right now is marked "On air."

Every time is printed in your own timezone, and the page names the timezone it detected. You do not have to do the arithmetic.

An empty row is one nobody has taken yet. Read the guide on a Tuesday and you can see what is on Thursday. Anyone can link to it, so a host can send you the guide and you can plan around their slot.

## Listen

The shows happen in [Chyme](https://app.lagatos.com/apps/chyme), the app's live audio rooms. Anybody can listen without an account; signing in is what lets you speak. There is no camera.

In a room, the people talking are on the stage and everyone else is in the audience. You can sit in the audience for the full 90 minutes and say nothing.

TI Radio never records who listened.

## When nobody is live

When nobody is live, Chyme plays recorded readings of posts from this blog. They play oldest first, in the order the posts were published, and everybody listening hears the same point at the same time, the way a radio station works.

Each reading is the same recording as the "Listen to this post" player on that post's page. When a new reading is uploaded, it joins the loop.

## If you miss a show

A room in Chyme is live. Nothing is recorded by default, so a show you miss is usually gone.

Broadcasts are a different part of the app. [Beacon](https://app.lagatos.com/apps/beacon) is one-way live broadcasting, and every Beacon broadcast is recorded. The recordings are listed on [the streams page](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/streams), newest first, and each one plays there with no account. To follow them in a podcast app, add https://app.lagatos.com/api/beacon/replays/feed.

## If you want to talk

Listening needs nothing. Speaking needs you to sign in, and hosting a show of your own needs an approved account.

If you have a voice, an instrument, or a subject you know well, [put your own show on it](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/ti-radio-your-own-show). That post is published first; this one follows it.
