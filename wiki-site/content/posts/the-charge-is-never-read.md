A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Do individuals on the targeted individual list know the reason for being placed on it

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Do-individuals-on-the-targeted-individual-list-know-the-reason-for-being-placed-on-it. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

How the reasons handed to the people taking part are fitted to each of them is in [A lie for every bias](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/a-lie-for-every-bias). This answer is about the person on the list, and what they are told.

## Nobody reads them a charge

Mostly, they are not told. That is not an oversight.

A legal accusation comes with a charge. The person accused hears what they are said to have done, and gets a hearing and a chance to answer it. The list comes with none of that. Nobody is told what they are accused of, by whom, or when it will end, and so there is nothing to answer.

## What reaches the target arrives sideways

What a target does learn comes from other people, as accusations that were made to them and never put to the target.

On October 11, 2025, asked on Quora what is said about Targeted Individuals that is not true, I wrote: "Everything, I only know of some." What I knew of: "To my job, they said I am on drugs, to others that I 'threaten' people." And: "They lied and said I was arrested. When really they disabled my car that left me stranded."

Each of those was said to somebody else. My employer heard one, other people another. None of them came with a charge I could answer, because none of them was ever said to me as a charge. In the same answer I listed what is said about every target in general, from drug addict to thief to stalker. The accusation is chosen for its audience, not for anything the target did.

## Some learn a reason, and it settles nothing

Some do find out. On December 3, 2024, under an answer about why people are targeted without having done anything wrong, I wrote: "I also got put on the list for revenge."

Knowing did not change what followed. A reason is not a verdict, and the list was never a court. On October 2, 2025 I wrote: "there is a legal system for crimes. Therefore, no reason should get you on the list. And they know this!"

That is the reason the charge is never read. A charge can be answered, and an answer is the one thing the arrangement has no use for. The same October 11 answer said not to get hung up on which specific lie was used to convince somebody. The lie is replaceable. The target's time is not.

## One list you can read

There is a list a person can see their own entry on. On December 13, 2025, Cheryl J WILLIAMS (https://www.quora.com/profile/Cheryl-J-WILLIAMS-3) asked on Quora: "Have I made the TI list yet?" I answered: "yes. I created your Directory profile," and that I had attached it to their account: "You can edit it anytime!"

The Directory lists survivors by what they can do. Nothing on it is an accusation, and every entry can be read, corrected, claimed or taken down by the person it describes. Being on it costs nobody an account.
