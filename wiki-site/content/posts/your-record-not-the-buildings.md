A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> How do I get evidence of harassment when living in a community building It is by the workers as well 1

The address it was saved at is https://zersetzung.quora.com/How-do-I-get-evidence-of-harassment-when-living-in-a-community-building-It-is-by-the-workers-as-well-1. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

Why the people doing this want a target out of their housing is in [Why they want you living with family](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/why-they-want-you-living-with-family). This answer is about the evidence, in a building where the staff take part.

## The building's record is theirs

When the workers take part, everything the building keeps is held by them: the cameras, the visitor log, the complaint form, the maintenance tickets. Asking the building for evidence means asking the people doing it to hand over proof against themselves.

I lived through a version of this. On October 29, 2024, I wrote that a counselor "tricked me into moving into a specific apartment complex so that they could plant people all around me to ruin me." On March 10, 2025, asked whether housing gets filled with them, I answered: "They will try to make you loose housing, one way is to have the street stalkers move in and take over the neighborhood/apartment complex to surround you."

So the evidence has to be yours.

## Record quietly, write it down

On September 20, 2025, asked what to do when it happens at work, I answered: "If possible secretly record at the very least audio." And: "Keep written documentation as much as you can." A building where the staff take part is a workplace turned around: you live where they work. The same advice holds. A recording made on your own phone and a note written the same day are records nobody at the front desk can lose for you.

Write what happened, where, when, and who was on shift. A dated entry made that day holds up better than a memory reached for a month later. ClickLog keeps that record privately, with notes that are not shown to anyone else here, and lets you read it back over time ([ClickLog: your record, and the record](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/clicklog-your-record-and-the-record)).

## Do not claim what you cannot show

On October 6, 2025, I wrote that they told me there were cameras in my fire alarms, "So I would tell people, and I would look crazy." And: "if they tell you there are, there isn't." A claim nobody can check is what they want repeated. Record what you saw and heard yourself, and leave out what you were told.

## What the building is for

On September 20, 2025, I wrote about work: "The purpose of the mobbing is so you quit or get fired from reacting to them." In a building, the equivalent is losing the housing. On July 28, 2025, asked why they follow a target to the dumpster at an apartment complex, I answered that it is to "see what will trigger you so they repeatedly trigger you."

The record is kept so that the reaction they are waiting for is not the only thing on file. Recording quietly, and not confronting anybody on shift, protects the record and the housing alike.
