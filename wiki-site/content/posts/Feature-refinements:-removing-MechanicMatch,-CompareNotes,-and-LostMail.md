I have been reviewing the app's features and have decided to remove the apps MechanicMatch, CompareNotes, and LostMail as they are not being used.

* MechanicMatch: The Directory app can assist in finding mechanics. It seems most TIs are working on their own cars. If that is not the case and you need a psyop-free mechanic, use the [Directory](https://the-comic.com/apps/directory/public) app to keyword search for Mechanics and then message the individual for a quote/services.

* CompareNotes and LostMail: I have decided on a better way to reach their objectives. An announcement about how these issues will be addressed in the near future. CompareNotes was an app that intended to have legitimate resources and information surface above misinformation and disinformation, an alternative to Quora, where legitimate Targeted posts are marked as conspiracy theories or are deleted. And LostMail as a starting point to find a solution to mail fraud against Targets.

In the next few days, the above apps will be removed. Leaving the following apps:

* Chyme: social audio for TIs
* Directory: find talented TIs to exchange skills, goods, and services
* GentlePulse: meditations
* LightHouse: Airbnb for TIs
* SocketRelay: Craigslist for TIs
* SupportMatch: Pair with psyop-free people for friendship or collaboration
* TrustTransport: Uber for TIs
* Workforce Recruiter: Find work amongst the 5 million TIs globally

If there is no uptake in Chyme, that might be the next app to be deleted.

Lastly, a major and exciting economy update will be released late this year. I am excited to present the next iteration of the world's first psyop-free economy! The above refinements will make space for an upgrade that will assist in TIs exiting the psyop.
