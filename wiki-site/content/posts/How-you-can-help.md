For the past year, I've been juggling a job (amongst a staff of Specterati members) while dealing with harassment outside of work from the Speceterati, which led to my termination without cause last month. This has made it challenging for me to cover the hosting costs for the TI Skills Economy app, which is a crucial tool for our community.

This means the TI Skills Economy is desired.

This app has the potential to foster self-sufficiency and employment within our community. However, hosting services are not free, and I've been covering these costs myself.

I believe that if we all contribute in some way, we can make this app sustainable and beneficial for everyone. Some of us may be able to contribute financially, while others might offer their support through upvoting and commenting on Quora posts. I am personally donating plasma to generate additional funds, but I can't do this alone.

If you're able to contribute financially, please consider doing so. If not, please upvote and comment on Quora posts. For those who genuinely cannot afford to contribute, please continue to use the app, and I will cover your costs by donating plasma.

Remember, our collective effort can make a significant difference in our community. Let's work together to make the TI Skills Economy app a valuable resource for all Targeted Individuals.

The [subscription for the TI Skills Economy](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/payments) is $1/month or $12/year, with several payment options available: https://app.chargingthefuture.com/payments.
