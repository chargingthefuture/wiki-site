A question I saved on Quora in November 2024 and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Is there a such thing as a targeted individual and is it real

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Is-there-a-such-thing-as-a-targeted-individual-and-is-it-real. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this.

Yes, and yes. What follows is about why the question gets asked at all.

## Two choices, and a third

The question offers two answers: real, or imagined. There is a third, and it is the one that causes most of the doubt. Some of what a target sees is staged.

On October 2, 2025, asked how to tell real threats apart from feelings of being targeted, I wrote that the threats "are real threats," with one exception: street theater, "staged altercations to provoke you for a slander campaign."

That exception is where the confusion lives. A scene put on in front of a target, a fight that is not really a fight, an argument nobody in it means: a target who sees through one can start to wonder what else was not what it looked like. That wondering is the point of it.

## A performance needs performers

Staged is not the same as imagined. A fake fight still has people in it, who were told where to stand and when to start. On December 13, 2025, I described one: "they staged a fake fight to get me outside at a specific entrance at a specific time," with everybody taking part cleared out of the area beforehand. The fight was fake. Getting a person to one door at one time was not.

So the question of whether it is real has a plain answer. A performance is something people do, and people doing something is real in exactly the sense the question asks about. Who gets cast, and who was pushed out of the room first, is its own subject ([The room has been sorted](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-room-has-been-sorted)).

What it is called also already exists. On October 11, 2025, I wrote that there are "two legal terms for it already - human trafficking and group vigilantism." Targeted Individual is the name for the person it is done to. It describes what was done, not what kind of person they are.

## Doubt is one of the methods

On December 15, 2024, I wrote: "“Making you feel crazy or defective” is a kill strategy." And in the same answer: "they may know, that you know, you are not crazy. But that brings them just as much pleasure and doesn’t stop" it.

That is why convincing them settles nothing. They already know. The doubt is aimed at the target and at anybody the target might tell, and on October 2, 2025, I named where it can lead: "One of their setups is to collaborate with the police and the mental health community to get you committed under a false schizophrenia diagnosis" ([It tries to make the crime](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/it-tries-to-make-the-crime)).
