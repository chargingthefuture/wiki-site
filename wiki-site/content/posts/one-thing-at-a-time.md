A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> How can I get help with someone hacking my accounts and gang stalking me

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/How-can-I-get-help-with-someone-hacking-my-accounts-and-gang-stalking-me. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

Whether to report it, and to whom, is in [Who to report it to](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-to-report-it-to). Keeping a record of it is in [Proving it, when you know who started it](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/proving-it-and-taking-your-accounts-back). This answer is about the help itself: what to ask for, and who to ask.

## "My accounts" cannot be answered

On June 29, 2025, somebody asked how to undo the hacking. After asking which device it was, I wrote: "for each thing they compromise you will have to tell us and you’ll get advice for each thing separately."

That is still the answer. An email account, a phone, a laptop and a home network are reached in different ways and closed in different ways. A question about all of them at once gets general advice, and general advice changes nothing on any one device. A question about one of them can be answered by somebody who knows it.

So before asking anybody, write down what you have actually seen, one thing per line. The email that was opened before you read it. The password that stopped working. The phone that behaves differently on one network. Each line is a separate question.

## Not every symptom is the device

On October 5, 2025, asked how strangers change what appears on a person's screen, I listed the ways a feed gets steered by accounts you follow and content you upvoted, none of which needs your phone. Then the other case: "if they compromised your network, you are experiencing a man-in-the-middle attack." That one is fixed at the network, not on the phone, and replacing the phone would not touch it.

The leak can also be on the other side of a conversation. On October 12, 2024, I asked a question of my own on Quora: "I cannot tell if every company has a back door and part of this or if everyone I speak to is simply hacked and exposing my communications." A message is held at both ends. Securing your own device does nothing about the copy on a device somebody else carries.

Naming which of these it is comes before fixing it, and it is most of what a person who knows the subject will ask you first.

## Who to ask

Ask people who know the specific thing and who are not part of what is happening to you. The [Commons](https://app.chargingthefuture.com) is a group chat of other Targeted Individuals, where a question about one device can be asked without spending the first hour on whether any of it is real. The [Directory](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory) lists survivors by what they can do, so somebody who knows phones or networks can be found by that skill. Being on it costs nobody an account.

One thing at a time, to somebody who knows that thing.
