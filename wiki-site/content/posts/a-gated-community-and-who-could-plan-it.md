A gated community for Targeted Individuals is one of the most common things people ask me for. It comes up on Quora more often than almost anything else.

My answer is that it is possible. Not by me alone, and not as a single place somewhere — but possible.

## What the question is actually asking for

People asking usually picture the finished thing: a gate, a road, houses, neighbors who believe them.

What comes before any of that is a document. Who does what, in what order, on what money, on what land, under what rules for who gets in. Every community that ever got built had one, whether or not anyone called it that.

That document is the real work, and it is not one person's job. It needs someone who can form a legal entity and write membership criteria. Someone who can read a title and a zoning map. Someone who can cost out water, power, and waste. Someone who can plan a care model and a quiet room. Someone who can plan a gate.

And it needs someone to write all of it down in a form a different group can pick up and use somewhere else entirely. That last part is the point. One runbook, reusable anywhere in the world, is worth more than one compound in one state.

## The Community tab

The Workforce screen in the app has a tab called Community. It sorts the people already on the skills map into the ten teams that document needs.

Nobody signs up for it. It is not a second roster and it is not a second application. Everyone in Community is already in the Directory — it is the Directory, filtered on the categories a gated community would need.

The screen says so itself: "Each team draws from the Workforce sectors it maps to; the roster is every member who already matches those sectors. This recomputes live from the Directory — it updates itself as members and skills change."

So the rosters are not proposals anyone signed off on. They are what the skills already on the map add up to when you sort them by this particular question.

Ten teams, each with the sectors it draws from and the part of the document it owns:

- Legal & Governance — 12 members
- Finance — 14 members
- Land & Site — 9 members
- Build & Infrastructure — 9 members
- Food & Agriculture — 7 members
- Health & Wellbeing — 19 members
- Safety & Security — 1 member
- Technology — 6 members
- Communications & Documentation — 36 members
- Operations & Maintenance — 13 members

A person can appear on more than one team. The sectors overlap — someone in housing and construction is relevant to both the land search and the build order — and the app counts each roster on its own.

Every placement carries its reason. "Skill match via Site safety practices" means that person listed site safety practices, in their own public words, and that is the skill the occupation needed. "Sector match" is the weaker one — the person works in that sector but the specific skill is not listed. Nothing here is my opinion about who belongs where, and anyone can check any row of it.

Communications & Documentation is the largest, and that team is the runbook itself: editing every other team's section into one document somebody else can use. Which is what makes it the team that decides whether any of this leaves the app.

## The question everyone asks second

Who gets in.

It is not a theoretical question and I have already had to answer it. In November 2025 a person posing as a Targeted Individual came to me wanting a spot in exactly this kind of community — one that had been organized entirely through Quora. He spent weeks in the messages first. I wrote the whole thing up at the time: [Damon Mayle, posing as a TI](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/chargingthefuture/discourse-migrate/damon-mayle-posing-as-a-ti).

My answer then is my answer now, and it is in that post:

> "Vetting" TIs is what I signed up for in the TI economy. And by vetting I mean meeting in person. That is the only way I can tell if someone is a TI or not. Most Quora posts, DMs, calls, video chats (which I will never do) are mostly meaningless. Easy to fake being a TI. Anyone can copy and paste real TI posts and mannerisms and use it as their own. You cannot fake authenticity in person.

That is the work sitting behind the Safety & Security line: gate and perimeter model, emergency plans, drills, and an incident process that respects member privacy. The screen lists those as its job, and one person is mapped to it.

The 2025 attempt is also why I do not think a community like this can be organized on a platform. A platform is where the people trying to get inside it already are.

## Where the numbers come from

The Workforce screen reads 100 recruited and 24 percent skills coverage — 159 of the 650 things a working economy this size needs.

Where 650 comes from, where 5 million comes from, and how a person ends up on the map at all, is in [How the math works](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/how-the-math-works). It is the same map underneath. Community is one view of it, cut to one question.

The summary card on that first screenshot also states the skills coverage as a dollar figure, and it states its own limits in the same breath: the figures are speculative rather than actuals, that card is the only place in the app where anything is expressed in US dollars, and the Skills Economy has no intention of forming a nation state. It is a baseline for reasoning about an economy of this size, nothing more. I am repeating those limits here because the screenshot is in this post and the caveats belong with it.
