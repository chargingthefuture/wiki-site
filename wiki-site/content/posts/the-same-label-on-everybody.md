A question on Quora, saved on August 14, 2026:

> What is a person called that accuses innocent strangers of pedophilia in every one of their comments on here, are they a danger and should they be banned?

The question is at https://www.quora.com/What-is-a-person-called-that-accuses-innocent-strangers-of-pedophilia-in-every-one-of-their-comments-on-here-are-they-a-danger-and-should-they-be-banned. While nobody has answered it, Quora serves it with /unanswered/ in front of the same address. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

## What it is

It is a smear, and pedophile is one of the stock words in it. I have listed before the lies told about every target: pedophile, racist, sexist, snitch, threatening, aggressive, hates dogs, drug addict, mentally ill, thief, liar. The label changes from one year to the next, terrorist, addict, dangerous, and the sameness of it is then offered as the evidence ([Pizza Is Not My Favorite Food](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/pizza-is-not-my-favorite-food)).

So an accusation that turns up under every comment says nothing about the person it lands on. It would land on anybody.

## Is it a danger

The danger is less the comment than what it is for. A smear is not offered to be examined. It is offered to be repeated ([You cannot argue a smear down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/you-cannot-argue-a-smear-down)). And the recruiting runs on slander: somebody is told a story about the target, is swayed by it, and is offered something for acting on it ([Who teaches them](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-teaches-them)).

There is a difference between hearing slander and believing it, and hearing it, believing it, and going after the person it names. The second is the harm.

## Should they be banned

Whether an account comes down is Quora's decision, and Quora makes it on its own terms. It has erased my own accounts again and again, and [Old links, new links](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/old-links-new-links) keeps the record of which are left.

What is yours is not answering it. A reply says the accusation again in your own voice, puts you in the frame as the person it is about, and takes the day. What a smear cannot survive is something anybody can check without taking your word: a dated record of what happened, work that is visible, and people who know you because you helped them.
