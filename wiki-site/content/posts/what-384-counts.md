384 is a count of members exchanging with each other on one day.

## A day at a time

It is counted one day at a time, and the same 384 people do not have to come back tomorrow. Who is in it rotates. Somebody who gave a ride on Monday and nothing else that week was in Monday's count.

## What counts

Exchanges that delivered something:

- a ride given
- a repair done
- a skill taught
- credits sent for work that happened
- a small task done toward somebody's ask, once the person who asked says it helped

Talk does not count, and neither does signing up. An account that only takes, or only posts, does not move the number.

## Why 384

The estimate is five million survivors worldwide. 384 is how many people a reading has to cover before it holds for a population that size. So a day the app supports 384 members exchanging with each other is a day that shows it could, in theory, support all five million.

## When it is reached

One day at 384 is not the goal reached. A day is a day, and an economy is what holds. The goal is reached when the typical day across a year sits at or above 384.

## A different number from sign-ups

People who created an account and were approved are counted separately, and that count is not what 384 measures. A member can be approved and in no day's count, and the count is made of members doing something for each other.
