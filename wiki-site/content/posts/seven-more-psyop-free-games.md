Farah's Arcade grew today: https://chargingthefuture.github.io/arcade/

The arcade calls itself "a small, honest home for free, open-source, offline-first games — with no ads, no tracking, and no harassment," and every word of that is checkable: each game carries a Play link and a Source link side by side, and the code is public. No ads, ever — nothing to buy, no nag screens. No accounts, no analytics, no cookies. Offline-first means a game loads once and then plays with no signal — on a phone with no data plan, on a bus, wherever you are.

## The seven added today

- 2048 — slide the numbered tiles together; two that match combine into one worth double.
- Snake — steer a snake that grows every time it eats. Do not hit the wall, and do not run into yourself.
- Breakout — slide the paddle, bounce the ball back up, and clear every brick on the screen.
- Space Invaders — rows of aliens march down toward you; shoot them all before they land.
- Pong — paddle against the computer. First to seven points wins.
- Gem Match — swap two gems to line up three or more of the same color, then watch the rest fall into place.
- Word Wheel — spin the wheel, guess a letter, and solve the hidden phrase before your turns run out.

## Already on the shelf

Glowline and Glowline 2, Chess, Cascade (a falling-blocks puzzle), Redline (a fast-running platformer), and Vox — a side-scrolling platformer where you play a survivor who got their voice back and fights back, one cartoon tactic at a time. Specterrealm, a spooky realm adventure, is in progress and marked coming soon.

That makes fourteen, all playable in the browser.

## Why an arcade at all

Games from the mainstream stores arrive with attachments: ads, accounts, analytics, forced updates that can change an app under you. These have none of that by construction. A page of static files cannot ask who you are, cannot phone home, and cannot change what you already loaded — what you loaded is what you play, and the source is one click away if you want to see for yourself.

The mission line has always promised psyop-free products and services. The games are the version of that you can hold today, free, without signing up for anything.
