A person is not one job title.

The skills map counts somebody at every level and in every sector their skills reach, so the same person can show up in several places at once. What people can do crosses titles, and [Counted by skills, not job titles](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/counted-by-skills) goes into what that does to the count. One listing can fill a gap in three sectors.

## 00

00 (https://www.quora.com/profile/00-661), in the United Kingdom. The Directory profile is at https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory/profile/1db12a54-5c40-4431-b6d1-a109accac21a.

The listing carries eight specializations:

- Branding and identity systems, and visual concept development: deciding how something looks so people recognize it, and sketching the idea before it is built.
- Video and audio recording and editing: making a recording and cutting it into something worth watching or hearing.
- Analytics and growth tactics: reading the numbers behind an account or a page and acting on them.
- Trend analysis: seeing what is coming before it arrives.
- Marketing, and market research and segmentation: finding out who wants something, and reaching them.
- Site safety practices: keeping people safe where something is being built.

## Why that matters here

The app keeps a catalog of 713 skills, the things a working economy of about five million people needs somebody to be able to do, grouped by sector. Read on October 14, the Directory's 312 people held 260 of them.

Counted by skills, 00 is in three sectors:

- Creative and Media, where branding, visual concepts, video and audio, analytics and trend analysis are filed, and 36 of 92 skills are held.
- Professional and Business Services, where marketing and market research are filed, and 21 of 52 are held.
- Housing and Construction, where site safety is filed, and 18 of 51 are held.

## What this is not

This is not a request to design, film or market anything for anybody, and nothing here says 00 will. Being on a list of what a person can do is not an offer of anything, and the Directory has never worked that way.

## How the listing came to be

I built it from what 00 said about themselves in public and put it up under their name, which is how most of this list was made, one person at a time. They did not sign up, and nobody asked them first. That is why it is marked community-generated and carries my handle: so anybody reading it knows it is my account of somebody else's skills, and knows who to come to about it.

## The invitation

Claiming the listing takes a free account and a few minutes, and after that it is theirs: what it says, and whether it stays up at all.

Nothing else is required. Not using the rest of the app, not posting anything, not agreeing with a word I have written. No is a complete answer, and the listing comes down on request with no account involved in that either.

## This does not run one way

Somebody whose work is getting other people seen needs things too. A ride. A room. An hour of a trade they do not have. The listing that makes them findable makes those people findable to them, including the others on the list in the United Kingdom.
