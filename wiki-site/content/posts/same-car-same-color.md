## The same car as yours

Yes. I answered this on Quora in November 2025, before that account was erased, and the answer has not changed. In the same way, they will wear the same clothes as you and repeat parts of your private conversations or your online searches. It is meant to show you that you are being watched all the time.

Two of the schemes on the [list survivors keep](https://chargingthefuture.com/schemes) describe it. Color Sensitization: the people around you all start wearing the same color, and it changes on a schedule. Road Sensitization: high beams flashed in your mirror, brake checks, cars pacing you or boxing you in, repeated until every drive is something you have to read and second-guess.

## White cars

I cannot tell you they drive white sedans or white construction vehicles. Nothing I have recorded shows it, and I will not guess.

What I can tell you is what has happened to me. Cars have followed mine for weeks. Cars have tried to hit me on foot, and one ran me over ([They cannot be ignored](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/they-cannot-be-ignored)).

A color is the hardest thing for one person to judge. The cover story for Color Sensitization is that it is a trend, and what gives it away is that survivors in different cities notice the same color in the same week. No one person can see that. It shows only when enough people write down what they saw, with the date.

## Writing it down

[ClickLog](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/click-log) has entries for this: cars parked outside your home, and cars that park right next to yours and sit. It has the named schemes too, Color Sensitization and Road Sensitization among them. An entry takes about fifteen seconds, and your notes stay yours.

What members choose to share is counted across everybody. The first report already paired Color Sensitization with cars parked outside a member's home three times ([The Record Has Started](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/clicklog-the-record-has-started)). If white cars are a pattern, that is where it will show, and it will show for everybody at once rather than for one person trying to read every car on the road.

Once it is written down, you can stop reading the road for it.
