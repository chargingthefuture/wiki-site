SupportMatch is an opportunity to form real bonds with other TIs with no psyop or hidden agenda.

SupportMatch randomly pairs you with others looking for a monthly accountability partner. Every 30 days—if enough people have opted in—you may receive a new match.

As matches, you mutually decide how to use your time: friendship, business collaboration, skill exchange, etc. The app includes in-app messaging and reporting features to protect your privacy. Share as much or as little as you like with your match. You are in control.

You can opt out of being matched with someone again at any time; no judgment and no reason required. If you suspect someone of abusive or predatory behavior or a member of the Specterati, please report them. We all share responsibility for removing such users.
