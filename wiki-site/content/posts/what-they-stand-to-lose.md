A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> What do gang stalkers fear

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/What-do-gang-stalkers-fear. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

Three things, from what I have written over the years, and none of them is their life.

## Being noticed

On Quora on August 26, 2025, answering whether the number of Targeted Individuals would one day grow large enough to bring the country down, I wrote that they target on purpose only "a percentage that is enough to go unnoticed" by the people who do not know about this.

The size is kept down so the people who do not know stay that way. Being seen by them is the first fear. On October 11, 2025, in a survey answer, I wrote that the people doing it know who the Targeted Individuals around them are, and that one of them panicked when I pointed them out to another Targeted Individual.

It also explains the quiet. A quiet stretch is usually the people doing it laying low, not stopping ([What can be stopped](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-can-be-stopped)).

## The people directing them

On June 6, 2025, answering somebody who had been called a slave, I wrote that the people doing the street theater are the ones afraid not to obey whoever tells them to perform it. On November 10, 2025 I put the people taking part into two groups: "cowards and sadists." The cowards are the ones who are extorted and will not stand up to it. The sadists enjoy it.

The sadists fear little. The rest fear the people above them.

## What they were given

What holds the arrangement up is an economy. Very few people around a target think the target is crazy. They are turning a blind eye or they are in on it, and both have something to gain from it ([Exit their economy, exit the psyop](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/mono/EXIT-THEIR-ECONOMY,-EXIT-THE-PSYOP)).

So the third fear is losing what they got for taking part: a job, an apartment, a favor, a reputation. That is all a participant risks. Ordinary crime still applies to somebody who is not a target, and no part of the arrangement puts a participant's life on the line. Only the target's is. A reason to stay is not a justification for staying, and losing things is not the same as losing your life.

The opposite case is not a participant. Two people I knew would not take part, proved hard to turn into targets, and were killed ([The best description of it I have read](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/best-description-i-have-read)).

## What a target can do with this

None of it is a way to frighten them, and I do not offer one. What it shows is where they depend on things staying as they are.

They depend on not being noticed. A person with no witnesses and no record is easy to describe however the story needs, and a person with a dated log is not ([The reaction is what gets written down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-reaction-is-what-gets-written-down)). ClickLog is that log: a date, a location if you choose one, a few seconds an entry, and your notes stay yours.

They depend on what they are given, and part of it comes through the target: the job, the room, the ride. Each one moved to somebody who does not take part is one place they can no longer press. The Directory lists survivors by what they can do, and being on it costs nobody an account.
