A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> What exactly do gang stalkers want from me

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/What-exactly-do-gang-stalkers-want-from-me. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

I saved it four times.

What the people doing this are after, as an end point, is in [The reaction is what gets written down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-reaction-is-what-gets-written-down). This answer is about the words "from me," and what they assume.

## It reads like a demand

Asked that way, the question sounds like a negotiation. Find out what they want, hand it over, and it stops. That is a reasonable thing to hope, and it does not match anything I have seen.

On June 6, 2025, answering a question about the consequences of not complying, I wrote: "There's no such thing for TIs." Compliance is a deal offered to the people around a target, their family, friends and coworkers ([A choice under pressure is still a choice](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/a-choice-under-pressure)). Nothing a target can give is accepted as payment.

## Every request is a step

What does arrive are requests. Each one looks small, and each one points the same direction. On July 2, 2025, I listed what the friendly ones are after: "to get you to leak information about your self or manipulate you into doing something not beneficial, like spend money to make you go broke, entrapment in crimes, get you to do drugs and alcohol, anything to downward spiral your life."

None of those settle anything. A secret told is something to use later. Money spent is money gone. A drink accepted is a pattern somebody can describe later. The request is not the price of being left alone; it is the next step toward the outcome.

The same goes for reactions. On December 11, 2024, I wrote: "remember they want you to act out." A reaction looks like something you give up under pressure, and it works the same way. It is collected, not traded.

So the answer to "what do they want from me" is never something to hand over. On September 20, 2025, I wrote the rule I still use: "The default should always be don't do what they ask."

## Turn the question around

The question that leads somewhere is the opposite one. What do you want, and who can you get it from without going through them?

I answered that on August 3, 2025, in a question of my own: "I would like to create a TI circular economy. There's likely a TI a mechanic, we can provide for each other." The same day, under somebody else's answer to this very question, I left two words and an address: "Please join," and a link to the TI Skills Network. That was my answer then, and it is still my answer.

What that space set out to be, a list of survivors by what they can do, is what the Directory is now. It lists survivors by what they can do, so a person who needs a mechanic, a ride or a second opinion can find somebody who does not take part. Being on it costs nobody an account. It does not answer what they want. It moves what you need to people outside the arrangement, where asking for it does not become the next step.
