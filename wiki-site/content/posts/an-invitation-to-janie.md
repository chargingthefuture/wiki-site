The [last post](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/rfs-trainers) asked for trainers, because three cohorts are open in SkillUp and none of them has anybody scheduled to teach.

One of the three is Cleaners / Janitorial.

There is already somebody on the list who manages cleaners.

## Janie

Janie, in Atlanta, Georgia — her Directory profile is at https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory/profile/da8fcf40-d1d6-4fce-af7d-b706b9aec849 and it lists Office Management and Painting, with a note that she can also help people with computer software.

She is a community-generated profile, which means she did not put herself there. I did, after she said what she could do. That is how most of the list was built.

She said it in a Quora exchange on an account Quora has since deleted. The words are gone from where she wrote them. The reason the Directory is worth maintaining is sitting right there: a platform can erase the account, and it cannot erase the list.

## The invitation

Janie, the Cleaners / Janitorial cohort has no trainer. You manage cleaners. You would be teaching people the thing you already do.

The terms are in the [last post](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/rfs-trainers) and they are short. You set the schedule and the cadence. It runs remotely, because the people enrolling could be anywhere. You do not need to be a professional instructor — you need to know the work well enough that somebody who does not can follow you.

If the answer is no, that is a complete answer and nothing about your listing changes. You can also have the listing taken down at any time, which has been true since the day it went up.

## This does not run one way

A trainer is not a donor.

People who can teach need things too, and the list is how they find them. Somebody who can run a cohort on cleaning may still need a repair, a ride, or an hour of somebody else's trade — and mechanics are one of the thinner parts of the map right now.

That is the shape of the thing rather than a slogan. The reason to be findable is that everybody is short of something, and the people short of different things are the ones who can settle it between them.
