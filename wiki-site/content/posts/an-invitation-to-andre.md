A working economy needs people who can make a part to a drawing.

Every machine breaks eventually: a pump, a press, a vehicle, a piece of farm equipment. When the part it needs cannot be bought, or cannot be bought in time, somebody has to read the drawing, set up a machine, cut the part and check that it came out right. A community that cannot do that waits on whoever can.

## Andre Kovacs

Andre Kovacs (https://www.quora.com/profile/andre-kovacs-1), in Galt, California. The Directory profile is at https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory/profile/8afae46a-452c-4e67-96ce-f1377f545c56.

The listing carries two trades.

The first is the machinist's, from start to finish:

- Blueprint reading and interpretation: understanding from a drawing what a part has to be.
- Machine setup and calibration: getting a machine ready so it cuts what the drawing says.
- Machine tool operation, and CNC operation and programming: cutting the part, by hand on the machine or by writing the program a computer-controlled machine follows.
- Dimensional inspection and tolerances: measuring the finished part and checking it is within the limits the drawing allows.

The second is writing:

- Copywriting, and SEO copy and content strategy: writing that gets something read and found.
- Persuasive and technical writing: making an argument, or explaining how something works so somebody else can do it.
- Editing and style-guide enforcement: making other people's writing clear and consistent.

## Why that matters here

The app keeps a catalog of 713 skills, the things a working economy of about five million people needs somebody to be able to do, grouped by sector. Read on October 14, the Directory's 312 people held 260 of them.

Where Andre's skills sit in the catalog:

- The machinist's skills are filed under machinists in Manufacturing and Industry, where 16 of 28 are held.
- The writing skills are filed under copywriters and editors, and under advocates, in Creative and Media, where 36 of 92 are held, and copywriting under marketing specialists in Professional and Business Services, where 21 of 52 are held.

A part made to a drawing is only as good as the drawing and the instructions that go with it. The same person can do both.

## What this is not

This is not a request to make anybody a part or write anybody's copy, and nothing here says Andre will. Being on a list of what a person can do is not an offer of anything, and the Directory has never worked that way.

## How the listing came to be

I built it from what Andre said about themselves in public and put it up under their name, which is how most of this list was made, one person at a time. They did not sign up, and nobody asked them first. That is why it is marked community-generated and carries my handle: so anybody reading it knows it is my account of somebody else's skills, and knows who to come to about it.

## The invitation

Claiming the listing takes a free account and a few minutes, and after that it is theirs: what it says, and whether it stays up at all.

Nothing else is required. Not using the rest of the app, not posting anything, not agreeing with a word I have written. No is a complete answer, and the listing comes down on request with no account involved in that either.

## This does not run one way

Somebody whose work is making things for other people needs things too. A ride. A room. An hour of a trade they do not have. The listing that makes them findable makes those people findable to them.
