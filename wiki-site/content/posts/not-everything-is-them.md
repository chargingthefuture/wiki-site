A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> What is the general perception of targeted individuals who claim to be victims of organized gang stalking Are they considered delusional or paranoid

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/What-is-the-general-perception-of-targeted-individuals-who-claim-to-be-victims-of-organized-gang-stalking-Are-they-considered-delusional-or-paranoid. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

I saved it three times.

How the verdict of mentally ill or dangerous gets built out of provoked reactions is in [The reaction is what gets written down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-reaction-is-what-gets-written-down). This answer is about the label itself, and what it claims.

## There is no single perception

It depends on who is asked.

Some avenues answer delusional. Describe what has been happening and the account becomes the symptom, and everything said after that is heard through it, including the parts anybody could go and check ([The Assistant Is Not a Clinician](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-assistant-is-not-a-clinician)).

A national hotline answered differently. When I called one, what came back was, in substance, join the club: this happens to a lot of people. The people who take the calls did not dispute it. They said it was common, and offered nothing.

And the UN Special Rapporteur on Torture's 2020 report to the Human Rights Council (A/HRC/43/49) addressed coordinated harassment as a category and said the volume of allegations warrants investigation rather than dismissal ([ClickLog: your record, and the record](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/clicklog-your-record-and-the-record)).

Three answers, and only one of them is delusional.

## Paranoid would mean it is everything

The paranoid label claims something specific: that the person puts every strange thing down to the people doing it. Read what survivors actually tell each other and that is not what you find.

On Quora on January 6, 2026, somebody asked why they would put something in a person's left eye. I answered that it would be very risky from a success rate standpoint and would leave physical evidence, and then: "There is likely a different reason you are having sensory issues." The same answer warned against taking their word for it: "If so, everything they say is a lie or duplicity. Believe nothing."

On May 22, 2026, answering somebody afraid their cancer would come back, I opened with: "I am not a mental health professional or a doctor."

That is sorting. Some things are arranged, and the named schemes repeat. Some things have an ordinary cause, and an answer between survivors says so. A label that calls all of it delusion skips the sorting and decides the matter without looking.

## A label cannot be checked

Being called delusional is a verdict about a person. It cannot be tested, because it arrives before anything is looked at.

A record can be. [ClickLog](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/click-log) logs an incident with the date, and the date and location cannot be changed later. Thirty entries show which schemes repeat and which things happened once and were nothing. That sorting is yours to do, on paper, rather than somebody else's to decide in advance.

The same goes for what I write. Every post here is a file with a public history and a Wayback Machine capture ([How to check me](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/how-to-check-me)).

The [Knowledge Library](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/knowledge) answers from what Targeted Individuals have written, and does not treat a description of organized harassment as evidence that the person describing it is unwell. It is not a clinician, and neither am I.
