A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Can a person be targeted by secret societies or organizations without their knowledge

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Can-a-person-be-targeted-by-secret-societies-or-organizations-without-their-knowledge. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

Why "secret society" is the wrong name is in [They Are Not a Secret Society](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/they-are-not-a-secret-society). This answer takes the other half of the question: without their knowledge.

## Yes, and for a long time

A person can be well along before they notice anything. On Quora on December 25, 2024, I wrote: "There are far more targeted people than we think and we are closer in proximity than we realize, everyone is just at different stages of targeting. And not realizing where they are in the spectrum of this program. Which is why you have people completely at a loss as to how this could have happened to them."

Being at a loss is the usual way in. Nobody is told it has started.

## The first part happens where you are not

Most of the early work is talk, and it is said to other people. On October 11, 2025, asked what is said about targeted people that is not true, I answered: "Everything, I only know of some. To my job, they said I am on drugs, to others that I "threaten" people."

The person it is said about is not in the room. What reaches them is the result: an employer who turns cold, a friend who stops calling, a door that closes without a reason given. Each of those has an ordinary explanation on its own, and the target is left to find one ([It happens at work](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/it-happens-at-work)).

## It is not one organization

The question pictures a single body with a membership a person could be kept unaware of. That is not what I have seen. The same day, asked what names the people doing this use for themselves, I wrote: "They probably do not call themselves anything, as it is various groups collaborating." The groups I named in that answer included a civic club, private security firms, Neighborhood Watch, fire departments and police. "To them, it is business as usual, no need for naming it."

There is no roster to find and no meeting to walk in on. There are ordinary people in ordinary roles, each doing a small part, and nothing about any one part looks like membership in anything.

## It can start with kindness

How somebody ends up on the list is not always something they did wrong. In the same December 2024 comment I wrote: "I discovered it is not random. For example, if you befriend or become an acquaintance of a targeted person you get targeted." And: "It so happens it could be of a brief but positively impactful encounter with someone who is already a target. In which case, you must be neutralized from ever doing that again, as you do not fit the profile of being converted to a vigilante."

So a person can be targeted for having helped someone, and never connect the two. Once a name is on the list, the schemes run the same way regardless of who put it there ([It Does Not Matter Who Put You on the List](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-put-you-on-the-list)).

## Ask somebody further along

If everybody is at a different stage, somebody else has already been through the stage you are in. A person completely at a loss is often describing something another survivor recognized years ago.
