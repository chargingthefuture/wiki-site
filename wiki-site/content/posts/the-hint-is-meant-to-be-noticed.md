A question I saved on Quora in October 2024 and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> How do gang stalkers use subtle hints to control targeted individuals and what impact does this have on the victims lives

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/How-do-gang-stalkers-use-subtle-hints-to-control-targeted-individuals-and-what-impact-does-this-have-on-the-victims-lives. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

## The hint is the harassment

A hint only works if it is noticed, and it is built to be. Operatives announce themselves to their targets constantly: the crowding, the repeated phrases, the run-ins, the things said just loudly enough. The signaling is not a slip in the operation. It is the operation ([They Are Not a Secret Society](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/they-are-not-a-secret-society)).

On Quora on November 13, 2025 I wrote that they "will wear the same clothes as you and repeat parts of your private conversations or online searches," and that "They want you to know that they are stalking you 24/7."

On one bus trip, a pair of strangers in St. Louis repeated a phrase back at me from an old employment dispute of mine that had already been resolved. Private history, performed on a sidewalk by people I had never met. That scheme has a name now, The Replay ([The Record Says I Called. I Was on the Bus.](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/one-trip-seven-schemes)).

## How a hint controls

Mostly through what it costs to read. A hint hands you a puzzle: who knew, how, what comes next. Every hour spent on it is an hour of your day spent on them. I spent nine months studying the people running it before I stopped ([You cannot argue a smear down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/you-cannot-argue-a-smear-down)).

The Good Day, Bad Day is the clearest case. A particular weekday is made reliably bad, and the verdict comes back in phrases no bystander could object to, like "have a good day", delivered pointedly right after a day they arranged to be bad. You learn the shape and start dreading the day before anything has happened on it. The anticipation does the work, and it is cheaper for them than the day itself. When a bad day simply happens, as bad days do to anybody, they claim it anyway.

"How are you doing?" from that direction works the same way. It is not concern. It is a meter reading ([How are you doing?](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/how-are-you-doing)).

## What it does to a life

On Quora on July 28, 2025 I wrote that it is meant to "scare you into overthinking everything and being afraid to live life," to "Sensitize you to the most minor things to drive you crazy," and to "see what will trigger you so they repeatedly trigger you."

So the first cost is attention, and the second is credibility. A reaction to something small, seen by somebody who missed the hint, looks out of proportion. On that same bus trip the line I was handed was "You are overreacting, she just asked you a question." That one is named too: The Sensitization Skit.

## Write it down, then put it down

You cannot decide to stop noticing. Noticing is often accurate, and switching it off is neither possible nor safe. It does not have to be carried.

[ClickLog](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/click-log) takes about fifteen seconds an incident, and your notes stay yours. The schemes on the public list at [chargingthefuture.com/schemes](https://www.chargingthefuture.com/schemes), The Replay and The Good Day, Bad Day among them, are tags any member can log with. One incident can be denied; a pattern written down over months is much harder to deny ([The best way to deal with it, in order](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/best-way-to-deal-with-it)). Once it is written, put it down, and spend the rest of the day on something that is still there next month.
