A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> What are some hotel survival tricks for TIs 2

The address it was saved at is https://gangstalkingquestionsandanswers.quora.com/What-are-some-hotel-survival-tricks-for-TIs-2. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

I have not written about hotels before. Most of what I would say about them I have already said about other places, and it carries over.

## The room is not yours

A hotel room has a door that locks, and other people hold the key. That is the same arrangement I described at the day facility where I shower: on August 20, 2026 an employee keyed me into a shower, and once I had undressed, an employee opened the door and exposed me to a man standing there, who laughed ([A Safe Place to Shower](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/a-safe-place-to-shower)). A lock protects you from the people who do not have the key. It does nothing about the ones who do.

So treat the room as a place to sleep, not a place to keep things. On Quora on October 2, 2025, answering somebody facing eviction, I wrote: "get rid of/sell as much as possible. As insane as it sounds, get down to one bag so you can travel." One bag can go everywhere you go. Whatever stays behind in a room is in a room other people can open.

## Expect noise

Sleep Deprivation is on the survivor-verified tools list in What Works, described as loud noises meant to disrupt your sleep ([What Works: knowledge that survives a ban](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-works-knowledge-that-survives-a-ban)). A hotel room shares its walls with strangers, and you do not choose who is on the other side.

On Quora on October 6, 2025 I wrote: "AirPods Pro earbuds are the best. They also work with Android. Get a second device and play in the background “the most annoying" sounds playlist from YouTube while you use your Pro’s on another device." On December 19, 2024 I wrote that they "will block 100% of anything they do. But you need to adjust the filter settings to achieve that." And: "At no point do I ever know what they are doing. Making me desensitized to everything."

## Give them nothing to work with

Some of it is questions with no reason behind them. On Quora on December 13, 2025 I wrote: "At all times deny them attention, answers, your time, etc. It is all a fishing expedition to find your triggers, discover what will make you sick, or sensitize you to a skit they are planning later." A question you do not answer gives them nothing to build the next one on.

## Travel itself is a target

Getting to the hotel is part of it. On Quora on February 25, 2026 I wrote: "every where I go, they get me stranded for months at a time, with interference and causing me to loose money so I cannot travel." One bus trip of mine ran at least seven named schemes before it ended ([The Record Says I Called. I Was on the Bus.](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/one-trip-seven-schemes)).

None of this stops it. On October 11, 2025 I wrote that a better framing is "to continue living your life and mitigate their threats, for example, with body cameras." A recording made on your own device does not depend on anybody's key.

Then write it down. [ClickLog](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/click-log) logs an incident with one tap, and its date and location cannot be changed later. A night in a room you will leave in the morning is easy to lose track of. A dated entry is not ([ClickLog: your record, and the record](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/clicklog-your-record-and-the-record)).
