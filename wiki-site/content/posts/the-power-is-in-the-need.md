A question I saved on Quora in October 2024 and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> How can targeted individuals reclaim power break free from organized stalking and find resilience amidst adversity

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/How-can-targeted-individuals-reclaim-power-break-free-from-organized-stalking-and-find-resilience-amidst-adversity. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

The steps I would take, cheapest first, are already in [The best way to deal with it, in order](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/best-way-to-deal-with-it), and the stage where nearly everybody is gone is in [Nearly a hundred percent isolated](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/nearly-a-hundred-percent-isolated). I will not repeat either. This answer starts from the question's first word: power.

## Where their power sits

It does not sit in the watching. Being watched is not what gives somebody power over you. Needing something from them is ([Estonia did not get to solve it either](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/estonia-did-not-get-to-solve-it-either)).

What holds it up is an economy. Very few people around a target think the target is crazy. They are turning a blind eye or they are in on it, and both have an economic gain from doing so. That is why I stopped caring about the cameras and wrote [Exit their economy, exit the psyop](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/mono/EXIT-THEIR-ECONOMY,-EXIT-THE-PSYOP). Each job, room, ride or repair that runs through them is a place they can press.

## Free of the need, not of the watching

No method I know of has ended it, mine included ([What can be stopped](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-can-be-stopped)). So "break free" cannot honestly mean free of being targeted. What can be stopped is the dependence, and that is where power changes hands. Each arrangement moved to somebody who does not take part is one place they can no longer press.

On Quora on October 2, 2025, answering a question about a strategy to reclaim sovereignty, I wrote: "Because of a TI on Quora, I was able to find work after being workplace mobbed out of existence. Next is to make sure I spend my money with other TIs." And: "Creating an offline network, alongside online communities, seems to be the only solution." That is still my answer.

## The part of the day that is left

Moving an arrangement is slow, and most of a Targeted Individual's day is not theirs. It goes to the attacks and to what they force, and what is left might be five percent ([Show up with your percent](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/show-up-with-your-percent)). Five percent is not enough to reach a goal alone, because a goal is a chain of small steps and the attacks land on the chain.

The PeerProgramming Goals board is built for that. You post one goal with a finish line and break it into cards, small tasks somebody else could do from a phone in under half an hour. Anybody in the cohort can take one. Twenty members with five percent of a day each is a working day, and nobody in it had more time than they had.

## Findable by people who do not take part

The other half of moving what you depend on is knowing who to move it to. The Directory lists survivors by what they can do. Being on it costs nobody an account. Most entries were built for people from what they had already said about themselves in public, and anybody can claim theirs or have it taken down. A listed person can be reached by the next person searching for a trade, without going through anybody.

## What I do not have

The question asks how to "find resilience amidst adversity", as though it were something to find inside yourself. I do not have a method for that. What I have watched work is cutting what the targeting costs, one arrangement at a time. This week that can be one goal posted on the board, or one card taken from somebody else's.
