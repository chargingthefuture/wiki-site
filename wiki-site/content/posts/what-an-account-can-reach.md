A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Is it possible for targeted individuals to use social media without being harassed by perpetrators What steps can they take to protect themselves from online harassment

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Is-it-possible-for-targeted-individuals-to-use-social-media-without-being-harassed-by-perpetrators-What-steps-can-they-take-to-protect-themselves-from-online-harassment. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

## Not on a platform somebody else runs

Nobody can promise a Targeted Individual a feed free of harassment. The account sits on a platform somebody else controls, and the harassment does not have to be an insult in the comments. On Quora on July 2, 2025, asked about disappearing posts, I wrote that either "a persons account was compromised and someone is deleting them," or the post was flagged and removed, and that legitimate posts are being flagged "as a way to silence people."

Mine went that way. On September 22, 2025 I wrote that Quora "deleted ALL my posts but one question that was never answered. Then banned my account." What stayed up afterward, and what did not, is in [What stays up](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-stays-up).

So the steps below do not stop it. They limit what it can reach.

## Steer the feed you are shown

What a feed shows you can be steered by other people. On October 5, 2025, asked how strangers change what appears on Quora, I listed the ways: they follow you and ask you questions, you follow one of them by accident, the spaces you follow carry their content, or you upvoted it by accident. Each one teaches the algorithm to push more of the same. "This is why up- and down-voting is important, as well as who you are following. Also, muting and blocking will clean up your feed."

Check who you follow and which spaces you are in. Mute and block freely.

## Keep your number off public pages

On October 31, 2025 I advised somebody not to publish their phone number online, because not everybody who would read it is part of this, and ordinary bad actors read public pages too: "No need to expose yourself to them too."

## Do not answer the bait

On June 30, 2025 I wrote: "Do your best to stay vigilant but not concerned about what they troll you with, because it is trolling - to get a rise out of you." A reply typed in anger is the part that gets screenshotted and kept ([The reaction is what gets written down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-reaction-is-what-gets-written-down)).

## Meet people there, then keep them somewhere else

Leaving is not the answer I gave. On October 2, 2025: "Quora seems to be the best way to meet TIs. I suggest you continue to use it. Because of a TI on Quora, I was able to find work after being workplace mobbed out of existence." The same answer went on: "Creating an offline network, alongside online communities, seems to be the only solution."

Not everybody there is who they say. On December 22, 2025 I wrote that I met two people through Quora, one real and one not, and: "It is very easy to discover who is real after a few conversations."

Once somebody is real, move the conversation. On October 6, 2025, to a person I had just been introduced to: "let’s stick to Signal as we are now." A contact held only inside an account is lost with the account.

The same goes for writing. Everything I write is published on the blog first, and platforms get an excerpt and a link, so a deletion costs reach and nothing else ([Why nothing is written on a platform first](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/why-nothing-is-written-on-a-platform-first)).

And for being findable. The Directory lists survivors by what they can do. Being on it costs nobody an account, and no platform ban removes the listing.
