A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Why are people targeted and who is behind organized stalking and targeting

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Why-are-people-targeted-and-who-is-behind-organized-stalking-and-targeting. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

I saved it three times.

The first half has been answered on this blog many times. This answer is about the second half: who.

## The usual answer names a side

Ask a room of people who is behind it and most answers name a group: the other political party, a religion, a secret order, the government. Each answer points at people the person answering already distrusts.

The people taking part come from all of those groups. On Quora on October 29, 2024, under a question about whether targets lean left politically, I agreed with a survivor who said politics has no bearing on it, and wrote that it is "across all political views and more. It is a divide and conquer tactic." On September 22, 2025 I wrote that there is nothing wrong with being religious, and that this "has nothing to do with religion." On January 2, 2026 I wrote that it "involves operatives of organized crime across all classes, jobs and ethnicities."

The secret order answer is often handed out by the people doing it. On December 19, 2024, about a video of a man said to confess to taking an oath, I wrote that people I had encountered who were plainly taking part, and never admitted it, said similar things about one world order, and that I would not be surprised if the video was faked.

## What they share is a position

If not a side, then what? On November 1, 2025, asked whether county workers take part, I answered yes, that it is organized crime, "and so corrupt employees are everywhere across all industries, companies, organizations. Anywhere where a narc criminal can have outsized control of something or a resource they will be there."

That describes people by where they sit, not by what they believe. A landlord, a clerk who processes a benefit, a manager who signs off on hours, a mechanic: each controls something a person needs. That is the seat they want, whatever their party or faith.

Organized crime has a working definition, and it fits: a group, lasting, committing serious crimes for material gain ([What organized crime means](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-organized-crime-means)). Nothing in it names a belief.

## Why a side is the wrong answer

The story told to each person taking part is fitted to their bias ([A lie for every bias](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/a-lie-for-every-bias)). A target is offered a matching story about who is behind it, and it usually names a side.

A survivor who believes the other party is behind it learns to distrust people for their party, and other survivors are among them. That is the divide in divide and conquer. Who first put a name on the list does not change what helps ([It Does Not Matter Who Put You on the List](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-put-you-on-the-list)).

So the useful version of the question is narrower: of the people who control what I need this week, who is not taking part? That has an answer a person can act on. The Directory lists survivors by what they can do, not by what they vote for or believe, so the next person who needs a ride, a repair or a place to stay can find one without going through a seat somebody else holds. Being on it costs nobody an account.
