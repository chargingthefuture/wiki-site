The answer here was never one walled place, because the people it is for are in every country. [The Lit Country](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-lit-country) is a map of that: a hearth for each survivor, spread across a dozen scattered lands, each with a trade. I estimate five million survivors, and that estimate is global rather than American ([Move toward somebody](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/move-toward-somebody)).

Alex's is in Ireland.

Alex (https://quora.com/profile/Alex-Dunican), in Ireland. The Directory profile is at https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory/profile/63a943b6-be5a-49c3-88f0-08977bbb2cdf.

The listing carries one specialization:

- Community Organizer: bringing the people in a place together around what they share, and keeping them working on it.

## Why that matters here

The app keeps a catalog of 713 skills, the things a working economy of about five million people needs somebody to be able to do, grouped by sector. Read on October 14, the Directory's 321 people held 260 of them.

Community organizing is filed under community development officers in Finance and Public Administration, where 9 of 34 are held.

A move that lands somebody in the same town as a person on the list is a different move from one that lands them in a city of strangers, whatever the country. In The Lit Country, when every hearth in a land is lit, the land sings, and a road between two singing lands joins them into one country. Bringing the people in one place together is the work that picture asks of each land.

## How the listing came to be

I built it from what Alex said about themselves in public and put it up under their name, which is how most of this list was made, one person at a time. They did not sign up, and nobody asked them first. That is why it is marked community-generated and carries my handle: so anybody reading it knows it is my account of somebody else's skills, and knows who to come to about it.

## The invitation

Claiming the listing takes a free account and a few minutes, and after that it is theirs: what it says, and whether it stays up at all.

Nothing else is required. Not using the rest of the app, not posting anything, not agreeing with a word I have written. No is a complete answer, and the listing comes down on request with no account involved in that either.

## This does not run one way

Somebody whose work is bringing other people together needs things too. A ride. A room. An hour of a trade they do not have. The listing that makes them findable makes those people findable to them, wherever on the map they are.

## Who is on the list

Everyone written about in this series is a self-identified Targeted Individual. That means they said so in public, and it is the only basis for the listing. I cannot verify it, and neither can a reader. Some people who say it are not survivors, and some are the people doing the harassing, so a post here is not a vouch for anybody and should not be read as one. I am not responsible for what anyone named on this list does or says, on the app, on Quora, or anywhere else. The closest anybody can get to finding a real Targeted Individual is through the app, which admits people one at a time through Unlock and has other mechanisms in place to filter actively. Even with those, there is no such thing as a hundred percent.
