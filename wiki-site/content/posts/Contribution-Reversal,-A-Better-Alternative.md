I made this post last week, announcing a price increase:
[A Necessary Step to Keep Our Platform Running](https://github.com/chargingthefuture/chargingthefuture/wiki/A-Necessary-Step-to-Keep-Our-Platform-Running).

But I have thought of a new idea.

Today, the Specterati sent a letter denying me public assistance, stating that I am actively receiving benefits in a different state and did not disclose which state I am allegedly receiving benefits from. In addition to this lie, they stated I visited their office this week in person, which did not happen, and also did not disclose who I allegedly met with and what topic was discussed.

This was yet another reminder I need to keep the contribution ask low, as difficult as it is for me. It is just as difficult for all other TIs. And so I am reversing the above announcement.

This is what will happen for the foreseeable future. When hosting costs are not covered, the app goes down. And when it does, there will be a landing page [https://chargingthefuture.com](https://chargingthefuture.com) asking for TIs that have not signed up yet to join the waitlist. When the app is back up, they will receive a notice to sign up to start using services immediately.

_Existing members, your account is not impacted. You are still a member when the app is offline. Once the app is back up, you will be able to log in normally and resume services._

This is a much better solution to include everyone and not miss on any momentum in connecting the 5 million TIs globally. Let me know your thoughts.

Hang in there, TIs!
