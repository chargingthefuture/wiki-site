## What it does

You tap what you need. A job, food, housing, someone to talk to, legal help, a ride, or a skill of your own if it is not on the list. The page counts how many people listed in the Directory hold the skills behind those needs, and shows you the number. Somebody who can help with two of the things you picked is counted once.

That is all it asks of you. No account, no name, no email address, no phone number. Nobody sees who you are, and you do not see who they are.

The counts are live. They come from the Directory as it stands at the moment you look, and I add people most days.

## If you want to come back

You can save what you picked as a 10-character code. Come back later, enter it, and the page shows how many people match you now, so you can see who has been added since.

The code holds the needs you picked and the count on the day you saved it. It does not hold your name, your contact details or where you are. Keep it somewhere safe: nobody asked who you are, so nobody can look it up for you.

## If nobody matches

When a need comes back with nobody, the page notes it. The note is the need and nothing about you. I read those notes and go looking for people with those skills to join.

## To reach the people who match

Names, locations and a way to reach somebody are behind a free Skills Economy account. A person checks each new member before they can use the app, and that can take a few days.

The check is there because the Directory is a list of trafficking survivors and what they can do. Everything in it came from public sources, but gathered and sorted in one place it becomes something else, so it is not left in the open. The counts show you whether there is somebody to meet without showing anybody who.

Once you are in, you can find people in the Directory yourself, or post what you need on the PeerProgramming Goals board, where any member can take a small part of it.

The other way is [One Percent](https://farahbrunache.com), the paid tier. For $7 I look at your match and introduce you by name to the people who fit.
