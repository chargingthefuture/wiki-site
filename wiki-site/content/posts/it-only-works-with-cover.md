A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Why are gang stalkers so cowardly

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Why-are-gang-stalkers-so-cowardly. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

I saved it two times.

## Every move is made with cover

Nothing in it is done one on one and in the open. On Quora on July 2, 2025 I wrote that there are more of them than there are targets. On September 18, 2025 I wrote that we are outnumbered. That is by design. The aim is that everybody ends up on one side or the other, and that the people taking part outnumber the people they target ([The best description I have read](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/best-description-i-have-read)).

So it is many against one, and every act arrives with an ordinary explanation ready. On March 4, 2025, writing about their good cop, bad cop routines, I called them cowards who "will do anything to manipulate."

That is what the question is noticing. Somebody who acts only in a crowd, and only where nothing can come back on them, has arranged never to need courage.

## Staying in is the easy choice

Some did not know at first what they were joining. The test comes when they find out. On July 4, 2025 I wrote about the ones who learn that what they are doing is wrong: "rather than admit it they just keep doing it. Absolute cowards." And of that faction: "Who rather pray, cry and not admit they are wrong."

On November 10, 2025 I described them as "People who are extorted and will not stand up to oppression."

Their lives were never on the line. What staying protects is what they were given: a job, an apartment, a standing with the people above them. Leaving would cost them things. That is a reason they stay, and it is not a justification for staying.

## The nerve is on the other side

The same July 4 answer records people who did the harder thing. A coworker would not take part, and their lunch was stolen. Another person had been taking part, realized it was not merited, and stopped; something of theirs was stolen too, and they moved on with their life with a clear conscience. A stranger was sent alerts in real time to join in and ignored them. As I put it in that answer: "It's a choice."

Declining is not always that cheap. Two people I knew would not take part, proved hard to turn into targets, and were killed ([The best description I have read](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/best-description-i-have-read)). The cover the arrangement gives extends to the people inside it, never to the people who stay out.

That is the difference the question is circling. The people taking part act with numbers behind them. The people who say no do it alone.

## Do not wait on their nerve

On October 11, 2025, asked whether a lawyer would help, I answered: "Most people are cowards and will turn their backs on you and walk away from helping." Nobody should stake their week on that changing.

What I suggested in the September 18, 2025 answer still stands: start connecting with other TIs, and "focus on building or finding a new community that accepts you." The Directory lists survivors by what they can do, so the next person can find a mechanic, a ride or a skill without going through anybody. Being on it costs nobody an account.
