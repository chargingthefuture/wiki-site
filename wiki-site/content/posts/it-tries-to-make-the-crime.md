A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> What are the signs you re under investigation

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/What-are-the-signs-you-re-under-investigation. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

I saved it two times.

I cannot list the signs of a real investigation. I can describe what gets mistaken for one, and how it differs.

## An investigation ends somewhere

An investigation looks for a crime that already happened, and it leads to a legal system: a charge, a hearing, a chance to answer. On Quora on October 11, 2025 I wrote: "There is a legal system for a reason."

Targeting skips all of it. On October 2, 2025, about the people doing it, I wrote: "These people think they are judge, jury, and executioner." No charge is filed and nothing can be answered, because there is no proceeding to answer it in.

## The watch list is a story

Some targets conclude they are on a government watch list. On August 4, 2025, to one of them, I wrote: "You're not on a government watchlist. That's just one of their lies." A watch list that lifts one day is one of the stories survivors are handed, and its finish line moves every time somebody reaches it ([What can be stopped](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-can-be-stopped)).

The claim of authority shows up in person too. On January 5, 2025 I posted an email I received after asking somebody I had done brief contract work for to delete an unused account. It said they had retired from a Department of Justice office, and it carried the message I summarized as "I know what you've done, but I am not going to tell you what I have been told that you did and who told me" and "I know where you are at all times."

That is the sound of an investigation with none of its parts. No charge, no source, nothing to answer.

## Job titles do not decide it

Some of the people taking part do work in law enforcement or investigation. On October 2, 2025, asked whether real investigators protect the people doing this, I wrote that investigators are as protected as anybody else taking part, and: "Profession is not relevant. Rather, the level of access to information they can illegally get or leverage over the TI in their position is more valuable."

A badge does not make what they do an investigation. It makes their access worth more.

## It tries to produce a crime

This is the clearest difference. An investigation looks for a crime. Targeting tries to make one happen.

On December 14, 2025 I wrote that people were "trying to entrap me in violent crimes in an attempt to get me arrested." On December 15, 2024: "Yes it is about getting you in jail." And on October 2, 2025: "One of their setups is to collaborate with the police and the mental health community to get you committed under a false schizophrenia diagnosis."

Provocation works the same way. You are pushed until you react like anyone would, and the reaction is what gets written down ([The reaction is what gets written down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-reaction-is-what-gets-written-down)).
