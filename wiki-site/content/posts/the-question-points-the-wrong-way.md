A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Does being targeted for TIs targeted individuals indicate that someone was involved in the occult prior to the targeting

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Does-being-targeted-for-TIs-targeted-individuals-indicate-that-someone-was-involved-in-the-occult-prior-to-the-targeting. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

No. The question searches the Targeted Individual's past for a cause, and the cause is not there.

## The spiritual story is handed out

The occult explanation does not come from what a target did before. It comes from the people doing the targeting, as one of the stories they offer.

On Quora on September 17, 2025, asked why this gets associated with spiritual battles, I answered that the people taking part spread it, and that it was "the first lie I was given." Then: "They told me that lie, and immediately after tried to convince me to commit suicide. To give up because this is the way life is."

A spiritual cause makes the targeting sound fixed, owed, or deserved. That is its use. A person who believes it has been told there is nothing to report and nobody to name.

The story is also chosen to fit. On January 7, 2026 I wrote: "If religious, you are chosen, if a racist, a lie about race. The list goes on; they cycle through each bias until they find one you latch on to." Somebody who has never touched the occult can be told it is spiritual, and somebody who has can be told it is the reason. Neither tells you anything about how the targeting began ([It does not matter who put you on the list](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-put-you-on-the-list)).

## Where the groups actually sit

Religious groups and cults do show up in this. They show up on the other side.

On October 22, 2025, answering whether it is demonic, I described "a human trafficking ring organized by the likes of stalkers, thieves, drug dealers, cults, gangs, religious groups, vigilantes and more working together." Calling it demonic, I added, is unnecessary. It is group vigilantism, and organized crime has a working definition ([What organized crime means](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-organized-crime-means)).

So if a group with occult beliefs is part of somebody's targeting, the membership that matters is the membership of the people taking part. A belief is one more reason somebody gives themselves to join. It is not a mark found on the person they chose.

## Why the question gets asked at all

A story about a target's past is useful to the arrangement whether or not the target believes it, because other people hear it too. On January 5, 2025 I wrote that ordinary people are approached "with slander and getting people worked up for false reasons," and: "If you have a prejudice, you'll harass people for free." The occult is an easy rumor to plant. It invites people to look at the target and wonder what they did.

Being targeted indicates that somebody decided to do it and others agreed to join. That is what can be written down.
