In September 2025, Indiko posted on Quora trying to connect with other Australians who had been dealing with this. I answered under it that I had built an app where Targeted Individuals could find each other. That answer is gone now, along with the account that wrote it.

A year on, the Directory lists four people in Australia. Indiko is one of them.

## Indiko

Indiko (https://www.quora.com/profile/Indiko-1), in Australia.

The two specializations on the listing are one piece of work:

- Business-plan development and market analysis is finding out who would pay for a thing, how many of them there are, and what they pay now.
- Business modeling and financial case building is turning that into numbers: what it costs to start, what it brings in, and when it stops losing money.

## Why that matters here

The app keeps a catalog of 713 skills, the things a working economy of about five million people needs somebody to be able to do, grouped by sector. Read on October 7, the Directory's 175 people held 230 of them.

Microfinance and SME Support, the sector for starting and running small businesses, is one of the thin ones: 4 of its 24 skills are held by anybody.

[Workforce shows the gap](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/workforce-shows-the-gap) describes how a gap becomes a living: read the gap, pick an outcome, fill it yourself or team up with somebody who can. Most people here have the trade. Fewer have the plan that says whether the trade will pay, and that is the part that decides whether a week of work turns into a second week.

## What this is not

This is not a request to write anybody's business plan, and nothing here says Indiko will. Being on a list of what a person can do is not an offer of anything, and the Directory has never worked that way.

## How the listing came to be

I built it from what Indiko said about themselves in public and put it up under their name, which is how most of this list was made, one person at a time. They did not sign up, and nobody asked them first. That is why it is marked community-generated and carries my handle: so anybody reading it knows it is my account of somebody else's skills, and knows who to come to about it.

## The invitation

Claiming the listing takes a free account and a few minutes, and after that it is theirs: what it says, and whether it stays up at all.

Nothing else is required. Not using the rest of the app, not posting anything, not agreeing with a word I have written. No is a complete answer, and the listing comes down on request with no account involved in that either.

## This does not run one way

Somebody whose work is other people's plans needs things too, and the post that started this was Indiko looking for people nearby. The three other listings in Australia are findable the same way Indiko's is.

## Who is on the list

Everyone written about in this series is a self-identified Targeted Individual. That means they said so in public, and it is the only basis for the listing. I cannot verify it, and neither can a reader. Some people who say it are not survivors, and some are the people doing the harassing, so a post here is not a vouch for anybody and should not be read as one. I am not responsible for what anyone named on this list does or says, on the app, on Quora, or anywhere else. The closest anybody can get to finding a real Targeted Individual is through the app, which admits people one at a time through Unlock and has other mechanisms in place to filter actively. Even with those, there is no such thing as a hundred percent.
