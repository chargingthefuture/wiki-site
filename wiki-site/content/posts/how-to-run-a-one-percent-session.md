In the [last post](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/my-turn) I said what I can offer, beyond building the app: sitting with Targeted Individuals over their own figures.

There is an obvious problem with that offer. There is one of me.

So here is the whole method, written down. Run it on yourself. Run it for somebody else. You do not need me in the room and you do not need anything from the app to do it — the app makes one step faster, and that is all.

## What it is

Eight questions, in order. They turn a list of what you can do into a figure, and then turn the figure into the first thing to do about it.

It takes about an hour. Most of that is the last three questions; the first five go quicker than you expect.

## Before you start

If you are doing this for somebody else, read their listing or ask them what they can do, and nothing else. Do not go researching a person before you sit down with them. Whatever turns up shapes how you hear them and they did not offer it.

If you are doing it alone, write your answers down as you go. This does not work in your head. The figure in particular has to be seen.

## Two things to say first, if it is for somebody else

Both before anything else, because both change what a person feels able to say.

**Nothing about their standing changes.** Not because of what they say, not because they end it early, not because they say no to the whole thing. If you have any power over them at all — you run the group, you made the list, you know people — say this out loud rather than assuming it is understood.

**Notes are not publication.** If you are writing anything down, say so, and say that writing it down is not permission to repeat it. That is a separate question, asked separately, later, and never in the same conversation as arranging help.

## The eight

### 1. What do you need?

Not how are you doing. [I have written about why](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/how-are-you-doing) — the short version is that one of those questions has an answer somebody can act on and the other collects something.

Take the answer as given. Do not fix it yet.

### 2. Is the list of what you can do right?

Read their skills back to them, or read your own. What is missing, what is wrong, what would come off.

Most people have never checked. If the list was built for somebody rather than by them, this is often the first time anybody has read it to them, and the corrections are usually substantial.

### 3. What would you charge one person, once?

Their rate. Not a market rate they read somewhere, not what they think they are allowed to ask, and not an aspiration.

What would they actually ask a real person for the thing they actually do. If they have never charged for it, ask what would make it worth the trouble.

This question stalls people. Let it. The stall is information.

### 4. Run the arithmetic in front of them

One percent of five million survivors is fifty thousand people. Multiply.

Then say what the number is and what it is not. It is a target with their own rate underneath it. It is not a forecast, it is not income, and nobody is owed it.

The comparison worth giving: the app's [Workforce](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/workforce) screen splits a whole economy evenly and lands near $142,500 a person. No real outcome is an even split. Doing well comes out of three parts — talent, luck, and how much work goes in — and none of the three is evenly distributed. The average has all of it averaged away, which is why nobody lands on it.

If they have an account, the What's your 1%? tab does this multiplication for them off their listing. If they do not, a calculator does the same job.

### 5. Who is the first one?

Not the fifty thousandth. The first person who could pay that rate for that thing.

A name if they have one. A kind of person if they do not.

### 6. What stands between you and that one?

Let it be concrete. The answer is almost always material: no transport, no address, no tools, no room to work in, no way to be found by anybody looking.

Write the specific obstacle, not the category. "Transport" is a category. "No way to get to a client across town on a Tuesday morning" is an obstacle, and an obstacle has things you can do about it.

### 7. What can be arranged about that specific thing?

This is where a tool is a tool. The [Directory](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory) makes somebody findable. [Foundation](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/foundation) is where an exchange gets offered or asked for. [SkillsHunt](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/skills-hunt) finds a person with the trade that is missing. [SkillUp](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/skill-up) teaches one or learns one.

If none of that touches the obstacle, say so plainly. Reaching for a tool that does not fit is worse than admitting the gap, and the gap is worth knowing.

### 8. One next action, with a name against it

One. Not five.

Who does it, and roughly when. A session that ends in five good ideas ends in nothing, and everybody involved knows it by the following week.

## Afterwards

Write it down the same day if you are helping somebody. Memory rewrites a conversation inside twenty-four hours and the figures go first.

Then do the thing you said you would do. The conversation was not the work.

## What this is not

**Not therapy.** It is not a place to take a detailed account of what has been done to somebody. If that is what is needed, it is a different conversation with a different person, and this one should say so rather than absorbing it.

**Not a sales call.** Nobody has to join anything, use the app, or agree with a word I have written for the hour to be worth their time. Every question above works with a pen and a calculator.

**Not a promise.** The figure is arithmetic on an estimate. Saying that is part of the session, not a disclaimer at the end of it.

## Why it is written down rather than offered

Because one person cannot run five hundred thousand sessions, and a method that only exists inside somebody's head reaches exactly as far as that person's calendar.

It is also the honest answer to anybody who suspects this is a funnel. Here is the entire thing.
