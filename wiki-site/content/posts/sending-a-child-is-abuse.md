Yes, they know.

## It is abuse

Commanding a dog to attack a person who poses no threat is abuse of the dog. Nobody argues otherwise.

Getting a child to do the same thing is abuse of the child.

In my view it comes close to prostituting them. Being told how to dress, what scene to act out, who to sleep with: that is what is asked of children brought into this, and it is perverse. [Who teaches them](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-teaches-them) goes into how young it starts and who does the teaching.

## The other risk is the lesser one

There is also the chance that the person a child is sent to follow and harass strikes back. That risk is real.

It is not the main harm. The main harm is done before the child ever reaches anybody: it is done by the adult who sends them.
