Yes, they know. The question is what they are doing to the child when they send them.

## It is abuse

Commanding a dog to attack a person who poses no threat is abuse of the dog. Nobody argues otherwise.

Sending a child to do the same thing is abuse of the child.

The children brought into this are told how to dress, what scene to play out, and who to get close to. An adult who arranges a child's clothes, conduct and company that way, for the adult's own ends, has already done the harm, whatever word is put on it. [Who teaches them](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-teaches-them) goes into how young it starts and who does the teaching.

## The other risk is the lesser one

There is also the chance that the person a child is sent to follow and harass strikes back. That risk is real, and the person asking is right to name it.

It is not the main harm. The main harm is done before the child ever reaches anybody, by the adult who sends them. A child who is never sent faces neither.

## What to do with it

If a child is sent after you, write down what happened, with the date, as it happens: what the child did, who was with them, and what the adult did. [ClickLog](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/click-log) takes about fifteen seconds an incident, and your notes are not shown to anyone else here. A record kept over months is what shows the pattern, and the pattern is what an adult sending a child counts on nobody keeping.
