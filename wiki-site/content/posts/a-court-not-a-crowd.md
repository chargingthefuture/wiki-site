A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> When gang stalking is exposed how should those involved be treated What should we do when the tables turn What would feel like justice for you as a targeted individual

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/When-gang-stalking-is-exposed-how-should-those-involved-be-treated-What-should-we-do-when-the-tables-turn-What-would-feel-like-justice-for-you-as-a-targeted-individual. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

## Turning the tables keeps the table

The question pictures a day when it is out in the open and the roles reverse. The people who followed a target are now the ones followed, and the people who were mobbed get to decide what happens to them.

That is the arrangement already in place, with the names swapped. On Quora on October 2, 2025, I wrote about the people doing it: "These people think they are judge, jury, and executioner." And in the same answer: "there is a legal system for crimes."

A crowd that decides somebody is guilty, with no charge, no evidence anybody can answer and no end date, is what a Targeted Individual lives under. Pointing the same crowd the other way does not make it a court. It makes another target.

## How the people involved should be treated

The way anybody accused of a crime is treated. A charge, the evidence, a defense, and a decision by people whose job it is to decide. Every part of that is what was withheld from the target, and it is the part worth insisting on for everybody, including the people who withheld it.

Taking part is a choice. On October 11, 2025, I wrote: "As I suspected, it is a choice. And they can quit anytime as well." A choice is something a person can be held to account for, one person at a time, for what that person did. Not for belonging to a group, and not for what the group did on a day they were not there.

A case needs a record, and a record cannot be rebuilt afterward. The record is the start of justice: whatever path anybody ever chooses, it begins with dated, consistent entries kept as things happen ([ClickLog: your record, and the record](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/clicklog-your-record-and-the-record)). Seeking justice and building a life do not wait on each other ([From them to me](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/from-them-to-me)).

## What would feel like justice

What I have written toward is not somebody else's punishment. It is narrower and more practical: work, housing and a day that do not run through the people doing this.

Until there is justice, there is no reliable path to working among them except the one they allow, and what gets allowed is narrow ([What's Your Hangup?](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/whats-your-hangup)). So the part I can start on does not wait for anything to be exposed. I said as much in [How are you doing?](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/how-are-you-doing): "Not integration. Not exposure as a strategy. Coexistence — with each other."

On August 13, 2026, I wrote: "I make no claim it stops targeting." And: "Connecting people who haven’t “given up” on living and resourceful works." In the same comment I wrote that I have met real survivors, and that they have helped me significantly.

The case, if it comes, is decided in a court. The life is built now, with other survivors, and it is not on hold until then.
