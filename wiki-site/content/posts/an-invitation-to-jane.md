Jane Doe asked on Quora how to prove it, now that they know who started it, and how to take their accounts back. I answered in [Proving it, and taking your accounts back](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/proving-it-and-taking-your-accounts-back).

They do not have to agree with me about any of it: not the words I use, not who is doing it, not how far it reaches. The list this invitation is about has never asked that of anybody on it. It asks what a person can do, and makes them findable for it.

## Jane

Jane Doe (https://www.quora.com/profile/Jane-Doe-11966), in the United States. The Directory profile is at https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory/profile/f2078144-1ce1-49a9-95c4-69ddefd5f8a6.

Three specializations, all legal:

- Case preparation and negotiation is getting a matter ready, and settling terms between two sides before anybody needs a courtroom.
- Contract and statutory interpretation is reading what an agreement or a law actually says, rather than what somebody says it says.
- Legal advocacy and advice is standing for somebody, and telling them plainly where they stand.

## Why that matters here

The app keeps a catalog of 713 skills, the things a working economy of about five million people needs somebody to be able to do, grouped by sector. Read on October 1, the Directory's 168 people held 227 of them.

Lawyers sit in Public Safety & Justice, and on that read 1 of its 22 skills was held by anybody. Jane's listing went up after it.

Everything people arrange between themselves rests on terms somebody can read later. A room let to another member. Help exchanged in [Foundation](https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/foundation). When the terms are written down and read correctly, an argument about what was agreed has an answer. And a survivor deciding whether to report something needs to know what the law in front of them actually says.

## What this is not

This is not a request for legal work, paid or unpaid, and nothing here says Jane will give anybody legal advice. Being on a list of what a person can do is not an offer of anything, and the Directory has never worked that way.

It is also not a claim about anything Jane believes, beyond the question they asked.

## How the listing came to be

I built it from what Jane said about themselves in public and put it up under their name, which is how most of this list was made, one person at a time. They did not sign up, and nobody asked them first. That is why it is marked community-generated and carries my handle: so anybody reading it knows it is my account of somebody else's skills, and knows who to come to about it.

## The invitation

Claiming the listing takes a free account and a few minutes, and after that it is theirs — what it says, and whether it stays up at all.

Nothing else is required. Not using the rest of the app, not posting anything, not agreeing with a word I have written. No is a complete answer, and the listing comes down on request with no account involved in that either.

## This does not run one way

Somebody whose work is other people's disputes needs things too. Jane asked how to take their accounts back, and an hour from somebody who secures accounts for a living is the kind of help the list exists to find. A ride. A room. An hour of a trade they do not have. The same listing that makes them findable is what makes those people findable to them.

## Who is on the list

Everyone written about in this series is a self-identified Targeted Individual. That means they said so in public, and it is the only basis for the listing. I cannot verify it, and neither can a reader. Some people who say it are not survivors, and some are the people doing the harassing, so a post here is not a vouch for anybody and should not be read as one. I am not responsible for what anyone named on this list does or says, on the app, on Quora, or anywhere else. The closest anybody can get to finding a real Targeted Individual is through the app, which admits people one at a time through Unlock and has other mechanisms in place to filter actively. Even with those, there is no such thing as a hundred percent.
