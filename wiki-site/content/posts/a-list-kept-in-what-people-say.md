A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> What are the signs that someone has been placed on a gang stalking watch list

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/What-are-the-signs-that-someone-has-been-placed-on-a-gang-stalking-watch-list. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

## No register to check

The question pictures a list the way an office keeps one: a name entered on a date, a file somebody could pull. What I have seen has no such file. Nobody is notified, nothing is signed, and there is no clerk who could confirm an entry or strike one out.

The idea of an official watch list is itself one of the stories survivors are handed, and its promise that the list lifts one day moves every time somebody reaches it ([What can be stopped](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-can-be-stopped)). Once a name is on the list, the same schemes run whoever put it there ([It Does Not Matter Who Put You on the List](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/who-put-you-on-the-list)).

So the list is not a document. It is held in what people have been told.

## The list travels as a story

On Quora on October 11, 2025, I wrote that the people doing this "will tell any and every lie to any sucker to convince them to harass and stalk a Target." The same answer went on: "If someone is triggered by racism. They will say you are a racist. If someone works at Facebook, they will tell the Facebook employees, you hate Facebook employees, I am not kidding. It is that petty and pathetic."

That is how a name gets passed along. Each new person is handed a version fitted to them, so the story about one target is many stories at once ([Peace Battle 2](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/peace-battle-2)).

A story needs scenes to point at. The same answer says they "stage provocations" and have others "lie and say you did something to them that is negative and slander you to no end." On May 20, 2025 I described fights started with me on the street, "Obviously to slander me to new recruits." The reaction to a provocation is what gets retold ([The reaction is what gets written down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-reaction-is-what-gets-written-down)).

## What it looks like from where you stand

There is no moment of placement to spot. What shows is afterward, and it shows in other people.

- Strangers treat you according to a reputation you never earned, in places you have not been before.
- What they accuse you of changes with who is doing the accusing.
- Altercations start around you that you did not start, and they seem to be performed for somebody watching.
- The same schemes come back, whoever the people involved are.

Any one of those can be ordinary. The sign is that the conduct of people you have never met fits a story about you, and not anything you did.

The October 11 answer ended with advice I still give: "I would not get hung up on what specific lie they used to convince someone." The lie is chosen for its listener. Tracing it to its source does not take anybody off the list.

## A list you add yourself to

There is another list, and it is kept the opposite way. The Directory started in October 2025 as a running list of Targeted Individuals alongside their talents ([From them to me](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/from-them-to-me)). Nobody is put on it by a story. An entry says what a person can do: a repair, a ride, a room, a trade.

Being on it costs nobody an account, and anybody can claim their entry or have it taken down. Viewing it takes a sign-in. The people on it are findable to each other for what they do.
