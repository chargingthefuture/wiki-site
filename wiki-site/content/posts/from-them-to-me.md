This is the third post in a row reading [the Record](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/record). The [first showed the span](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-record-reads-forwards) — two years, entry by entry. The [second showed what the span bought](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/two-years-to-find-three-people) — three people who changed my life. This one reads it for direction, because the entries divide into a before and an after at a single point — the day the writing turned from them to me — and that dividing point is the most useful thing on the page.

## The first nine months

The first entry is dated October 12, 2024. For about nine months after it, almost everything I wrote studied the people running the operation. What their texts install. How they are recruited and what they are told. Whether they enjoy it. What they want from me. Who is above them. Why they pick who they pick.

I consider myself an independent thinker, and I fell for it anyway — because it is not an intelligence test. It is the design. An operation this theatrical is built to be studied. It rewards attention with new puzzles, and every hour spent mapping them is an hour not spent on me. The operation runs on stolen attention, and for nine months it had mine.

The Record shows how long the design can hold a person when nothing interrupts it. The questions I answered came from people six years in, thirteen years in, one targeted since 2002 — most of them deep in the same questions I spent my first nine months on. That is not a verdict on any of them. It is a measure of the trap.

## The turn, dated

On July 13, 2025, I posted a survey and called it "my new approach at finding out who and where we are." The lens moved off of them and onto us — who we are, where we are, what we live with.

Three weeks later, on August 3, 2025, I wrote the sentence everything since has come from:

> Do you want to join a network to list what each TI is skilled in? I am being literally starved. I would like to create a TI circular economy. I was an engineer before this. There's likely a TI a mechanic, we can provide for each other.

I wrote that while being starved, and the sentence does not stay there. It moves, in the same breath, to what each of us can do. That is the turn, in one line, on one day, on the public record.

What followed is a straight line: the surveys and the map that summer, the network the same day as that sentence, the first version of the app on September 14, 2025 — six weeks from that sentence to running software — the Directory that October — a running list of Targeted Individuals (TIs) alongside their talents — the manifesto in January, the full economy of version 3 in June, this blog in August. Everything that exists today was built after the turn.

## No car, no home, no job

One thing that line of dates hides. A reader could assume it was built from a stable base — a car, a home, a job. I had none of the three.

The targeting began when I was five and has cycled between covert and overt my whole life. On January 1, 2024, it restarted as 24/7 overt, and it made me destitute very fast. I spent these two years living outside. My car was stolen. Every employment opportunity was blocked. I was punched in the face, back-handed, pushed to the ground and, the same day, exposed nude. Before this cycle, I had been punched in the face and run over with a car. The Forced Homecoming scheme was run all the way to fruition, and one TI — one of the three the last post is about — helped me escape it.

I list this for accuracy, not for rank. I have met TIs who endure the torture differently, and this is not me saying I have it worse. It is here so the two years are read as they happened: the survey, the network, the Directory, the app, and the Skills Economy were built outside, while this was going on.

## The luck was manufactured

People say you cannot meet a real TI, in person or at all. The Record says otherwise, in both directions. Three real ones changed my life. I have also met an operative posing as one of us in person — the Record holds that meeting too.

The difference is what you connect to do. Connecting to talk is the easier trap: talk is open-ended, and an operative can hold a conversation for as long as it takes. Connecting to organize is harder to fake, and it is how the three found me — I was findable, daily, in public, with concrete asks and concrete offers. The skills map and the Directory exist to make that findability the default instead of a two-year project. What looks like luck was surface area.

The app retraced the same arc, version by version. Version 1 launched with a single feature, SupportMatch — peer-to-peer counseling, connection to talk. Version 2 introduced the Directory — being findable. Version 3 is a full economy — connection to organize. Each version moved the same direction the whole two years moved.

## Coordination runs in parallel

I also used to think coordination was the only solution. I no longer think it is the only one. It runs in parallel with seeking justice — the report, the paper trail, the case. Neither waits on the other, and neither substitutes for the other. Building does not pause while a report is made, and making one does not pause the building.

## The nine months are optional

Two years of this reads as advanced. It was one turn, held daily, from outside. Nothing in it required anything another survivor does not have — no credentials, no money, no permission, no address. And the next person does not have to buy the turn at the price I paid: the nine months are on the public record to be skipped, and the tools the turn produced are running now. You do not have to start where I started. You can start where this ends.
