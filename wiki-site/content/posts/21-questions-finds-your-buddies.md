Finding another Targeted Individual who wants the same things you do is hard to do by asking around. 21 Questions does it a question at a time.

## One question a day

Each day a new question shows on Today, the screen the app opens on. It is the same question for everyone, with four answers to pick from as buttons on the card. Which skill would make you most independent? What do you want from people here first? What would you rather help a newer member with?

The questions are about what you want from life after the targeting: the work and income you want, what you want to learn, who you want to meet, what you want to give back. None of them asks about home or family, where you live, who you live with, or how you grew up.

Tap an answer and it is saved. Your first answer stands. The card stays for the rest of the day, marked done, showing what you picked.

If you missed a day, that question gets its own card beside today's so you can catch up. If a question is not for you, tap Skip this question under the answers. A skip is not an answer, and the next question you have not answered takes its place.

## Your buddies

When you and another member picked the same answer on three or more of the same questions, they show up on Today as your buddy: "You and @handle answered 4 of 6 the same", with what you both picked. Today shows up to five at a time, the ones you have the most in common with. Your buddies together are your buddy network.

Tap Talk here on a buddy's card and the conversation opens inside it. Messages are short, up to 500 characters, and your buddy sees a new one in Activity. Reaching out is optional, and so is replying.

A member either of you has blocked never shows up as a buddy. Mute on the card's menu hides one you would rather not see.

## Turning it off

Answering is how you opt in. Once you have answered one question, a switch shows under the Matches filter on Today and on Me: Find me buddies who answer like me. Turn it off and you leave every other member's buddy network and see no buddies yourself, from the next time Today loads. Your answers are kept, and you can keep answering. Turn it back on and your buddies come back. Answering never turns it back on; only the switch does.

## Before the check

21 Questions is for approved members, after the check. The check is the web address of your own Quora profile, and a person reads it. Nobody is removed for being slow with it.

Today's question is at [app.lagatos.com/today](https://app.lagatos.com/today).

To sign up: https://lagatos.com. It is free, everyone is let in one at a time after a check, and you can use one part of it and ignore the rest.
