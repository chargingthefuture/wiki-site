A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Why do some people attribute organized stalking to aliens or supernatural forces

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Why-do-some-people-attribute-organized-stalking-to-aliens-or-supernatural-forces. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

## Each piece looks too small to explain the rest

Every act in this is built to look ordinary on its own. A car needs repairs, a job ends, a neighbor stares. Added up, the pattern feels like more than people could arrange, and a cause bigger than people is easy to reach for: something watching from above, something unseen.

It does not take that. It takes people, working together. On October 22, 2025 I described it as "a human trafficking ring organized by the likes of stalkers, thieves, drug dealers, cults, gangs, religious groups, vigilantes and more working together." Nothing in that list needs a power humans do not have.

The same goes for the technology version. Asked on January 8, 2026 whether it always needs a chip or implant, I answered that it was unlikely: "It would leave physical evidence and the Specterati want your demise to fall under plausible deniability." Something a doctor could find is a risk to them. Something only the target can describe is not.

## Some of it is offered

Not every one of these stories starts with the target. On June 29, 2025, a question in a Quora space asked whether Targeted Individuals were targeted over alien abduction or implants. I answered that it read as if somebody taking part had written it, and: "They are pure evil is all." I meant the people, not a force.

An explanation like that serves the arrangement whether or not anybody believes it. A target who repeats it is easier to dismiss, and a target who is busy with it is not looking at who gains.

## Look at the business

In [An addition, not an alternative](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/an-addition-not-an-alternative) I wrote that traffickers dressing themselves up as the illuminati is nonsense, and that "the costume is the silly part, and arguing about the costume is exactly what it is for." Aliens and the supernatural are more costume. Underneath is the same thing: "There is no mystery in it and nothing supernatural about it. It is a business, run on recruitment and disposal."

A business has an economy, and that part can be seen. Asked on January 14, 2026 about a target being set up to look unwell, I answered: "Money laundering. They run their own economy where they only employee each other and box out Targets." Organized crime has a working definition ([What organized crime means](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/what-organized-crime-means)), and it describes people, not forces.

A force from outside leaves a target nothing to do. People who are paid can stop being paid. That is the reason for building an economy among survivors instead ([Exit their economy, exit the psyop](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/mono/EXIT-THEIR-ECONOMY,-EXIT-THE-PSYOP)). Foundation is where members offer and trade help directly, and the Directory lists survivors by what they can do. Being on it costs nobody an account.
