A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Do you really feel alone because everybody in your off line life refuses to accept that your gang stalking is even real

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Do-you-really-feel-alone-because-everybody-in-your-off-line-life-refuses-to-accept-that-your-gang-stalking-is-even-real-4. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

What to do once you are nearly cut off is in [Nearly a hundred percent isolated](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/nearly-a-hundred-percent-isolated). This answer is about the "because" in the question: whether the loneliness comes from not being believed.

## Two different things

Being disbelieved is one thing. Being alone is another. The question joins them, and it is easy to see why: the people around you are the ones you would turn to, and they will not hear it.

But the two do not have to travel together, and only one of them is the aim. On Quora on September 21, 2025, I wrote that for a Targeted Individual, isolation is what the people doing this work toward, "so do the opposite of that."

If the loneliness is waiting on the people nearby to accept it, the remedy sits in their hands. Some of them may never accept it. Company does not need their verdict.

## You have likely met others

On July 2, 2025, somebody wrote that they had not met one other person going through anything close to it, "Not one person in real life." I answered: "You likely have. But what you described every Target experiences so you likely didn't notice because of the bombardment you are under."

Under that much pressure, the people going through the same thing are hard to see, even standing nearby. That does not mean they are absent. It means the conditions make them hard to recognize.

## Company from people who already know

The people who do not need convincing are other survivors. In that September 21 answer I described how it went for me: "I was able to meet a TI from Quora and was able to change my mindset from 'fighting' to working to thrive." I also wrote that it is easy to go at your own pace to decide whether somebody is a TI "before meeting them in person or investing a significant amount of time."

That pace matters. Nobody has to be trusted on day one, and nothing has to be proved to anybody.

On September 18, 2025, asked how to feel better, I wrote: "My words of encouragement are you are not alone. And I suggest that you start building relationships with other TIs." In the same answer I said those relationships should reach past the targeting itself, so the conversation is about more than what is being done to you. Two days later I added that networking with other survivors "can also lead to career opportunities," because they are "some of the most skilled talent."

Company like that does not replace the people in your offline life, and it does not ask them to change their minds. It sits alongside them.
