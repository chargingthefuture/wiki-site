A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> How does it feel to be targeted What can be done to prevent being targeted by individuals or agencies

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/How-does-it-feel-to-be-targeted-What-can-be-done-to-prevent-being-targeted-by-individuals-or-agencies. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this.

This answer is about the second half: prevention.

## No precaution does it alone

The question asks for a measure, something an individual or an agency can put in place. I know of none that keeps a person from being chosen.

On Quora on October 2, 2025, asked whether there is a way to stop files being taken from a computer, I answered: "You are one person up against an international crime organization that appears to have mercenaries on the payroll. The best you can do is to try to mitigate being compromised in the future."

Securing a phone and a laptop is worth doing. It narrows one route. It does not change who is doing this, or how many of them there are.

## An agency is people

On September 20, 2025, asked what measures the government can take, I wrote: "The government is not run by one person, but a collection of people. And it is not binary of good and evil." And: "Like it or not, TIs will be the ones who put an end to this."

An agency does not prevent anything by itself. People inside it do, or do not. That holds for every institution a target deals with: the office, the clinic, the repair shop, the building.

## It runs on who surrounds the target

On January 19, 2026, asked how the people doing this are paid, I wrote: "They mob the Target and anyone that does not want to harass you out of businesses, resources and communities. In turn it is only their operatives that remain."

That is the condition it depends on. A target ends up surrounded, at work, at home and at every counter, by people who are taking part.

On December 30, 2025, I wrote that "they want families apart and relationships broken - they want isolation to create an unsafe environment." And that without survivors "being surrounded by their operatives," there is less opportunity to torture them and "no one to extort."

So the prevention that exists works on that condition. It is less a precaution than a change in company: the neighbor, the mechanic, the coworker and the person you call who are not part of it. Where survivors find each other and do business with each other, there are fewer openings and fewer people to send ([Nearly a hundred percent isolated](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/nearly-a-hundred-percent-isolated)).

## Individuals decide too

The individuals in the question are not only targets. Everybody who is asked to take part is an individual, and each one who declines is a person the arrangement cannot use.

That costs them something. The January answer says so: people who will not harass a target are pushed out of businesses and communities alongside them. It is still the only measure that works on the people doing it, and it is one only they can take.

For a target, the measure within reach is the first one: not being surrounded. The longer version, a place where survivors live together, is in [A gated community, and who could plan it](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/a-gated-community-and-who-could-plan-it).

The Directory lists survivors by what they can do, so the next mechanic, ride or coworker can be found among people who are not taking part. Being on it costs nobody an account. Foundation is where members offer and trade that help directly.
