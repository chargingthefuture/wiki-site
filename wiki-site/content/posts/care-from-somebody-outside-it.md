A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Do gang stalkers poison

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Do-gang-stalkers-poison. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

I have answered versions of it before, and the answers point two ways. Both hold.

## Sometimes, yes

On Quora on December 13, 2025, I wrote that "on several occasions, they placed some poison or allergen in the air." The people taking part cleared out of the area first so they would not get sick, and I got sick. That is my own account of what happened to me, and I have not written more about what it was than that.

It is not only aimed at people. On December 28, 2025, writing about who the Directory is for, I wrote: "The Specterati will poison and torture a TI's pet."

## And sometimes it is a story

On March 4, 2025, somebody wrote that they were being told they had been poisoned by an ex. My answer began: "Maybe they are poisoning maybe they are not."

That is not a dodge. Being told you were poisoned is a separate thing from being poisoned, and the telling does its own work whether or not anything was done. On May 22, 2026, I wrote: "They simulate conditions and symptoms and create false narratives. They have wanted me to believe I have high blood pressure, kidney disease and more."

So the answer to the question as asked is yes, and the answer to any one claim handed to you is that a claim alone does not settle it.

## Protect yourself without living in it

The same March 4 answer ended: "Do what you can to protect yourself without becoming paranoid about what they say and do to you."

Suspecting every meal, every glass and every room costs a person their days, and that is a result they want either way. A person who cannot eat in peace has been reached without anything being put in anything.

What protects is narrower and more practical: who handles what goes into your body, your home and your animals. A meal cooked by somebody who does not take part, a vet who is not in on it, a repair done once by a person with no reason to do it badly.

## Find the people outside it

That is why the Directory exists. In the same December 28 post I wrote that a TI whose pet has been harmed "will need to find someone who is not part of the psyop," and that a vet who lists the skill can be contacted directly, paid or bartered with for pet care.

The same goes for a cook, a nurse, a mechanic or a cleaner. The Directory lists survivors by what they can do, and being on it costs nobody an account. Foundation is where members offer and trade that help directly ([Use cases of the TI Skills Economy](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Use-Cases-of-the-TI-Skills-Economy:-the-only-way-to-exit-the-psyop)).

None of this tells you what was in the air on a given day. It changes who is standing near your food, your medicine and your animals the next time.
