A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Are gang stalkers aware of what theyre doing

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Are-gang-stalkers-aware-of-what-theyre-doing. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

I saved it three times.

## Most of them do

The short answer is yes. On Quora on December 14, 2025, asked whether anybody could be made to see they were being told lies, I wrote: "they are 100% aware they are being told lies; they either enjoy or profit from your exploitation."

Each person taking part is handed a story about the target, fitted to what they already resent ([A lie for every bias](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/a-lie-for-every-bias)). The story is not what keeps them there. On December 22, 2025 I wrote: "Most of their operatives are aware they are not following criminals. Otherwise they would not be following them or having their 2 year olds harass an adult." Nobody who believed they were tracking a dangerous person would bring a toddler along.

Some were raised into it. On April 16, 2025 I wrote: "Many are groomed to do this as early as 5 years old. And so in my case, many are 100% aware they are harassing innocent people."

They also know it is against the law. On December 11, 2024 I wrote that some of them "know what they are doing is illegal," and that they still coordinate over commercial apps, which is sloppy for people with something to hide.

## Not everybody nearby is taking part

There is a line, and it matters for who the answer covers. A neighbor who hears a rumor and repeats it is not the same as a person who joins in. In that December 14 answer I wrote that somebody who only gossips is not counted with the people doing this, unless they join in on street theater or start getting paid to harass. On October 22, 2025 I put it in one sentence: "There is a difference between receiving slander and believing it vs receiving it believing it and stalking and harassing people."

Then there are the people who see it and say nothing. On November 17, 2024 I wrote that I have crossed many people who are fully aware, "but keep quiet so they don’t end up on the list." They know too.

## It is a choice, and so is quitting

On September 20, 2025, asked how to tell whether somebody does it out of desperation or malice, I wrote that it is a choice: "That's like asking if a drug dealer is dealing out of desperation or has malicious intent. What difference is their intent? It's still selling crack." On October 11, 2025: "As I suspected, it is a choice. And they can quit anytime as well."

A question about whether they really know often comes with an invitation to feel sorry for them. Every one of them has a reason. A reason is not a justification.

## What follows from that

If they know, explaining yourself to them changes nothing. They were not misinformed, so there is nothing to correct, and the scene a correction turns into is what gets recorded ([The reaction is what gets written down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/the-reaction-is-what-gets-written-down)).
