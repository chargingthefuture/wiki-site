The vocabulary I use for the people running this is in the [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary); the question keeps the words it was asked in.

Better to defeat them than to expose them. That is fighting by the method rather than by the argument, and it is the ethos of [Peace Battle 2](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/peace-battle-2): a battle fought with teaching and with work. It also picks up where [You cannot argue a smear down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/you-cannot-argue-a-smear-down) left off. A smear is built to be answered. Exposure is built to be attempted.

## They are prepared for it

The method is woven into the global economy. Money, people and time have been put into it over centuries, and the earliest writing I have found on it dates to 1609. It is not a group that will be surprised by somebody describing it.

The schemes say the same thing about scale. The list of them is public at [lagatos.com/schemes](https://lagatos.com/schemes), and each one takes people placed in position, funding, and patience. On me alone, the [Forced Homecoming](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/why-they-want-you-living-with-family) has run for more than ten years. An operation that will spend a decade on one scheme against one person has planned for the day that person writes down what happened. The post naming it is the expected next step, and the answer to it was ready before it was written.

Kim's last line says what that answer usually is: "they are attempting to make you look crazy." Every hour spent trying to expose it produces more material for that picture, the same way the [staged fights](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/you-cannot-argue-a-smear-down) produced footage.

## Nobody nearby needs it exposed

Anybody who spends a day around a Targeted Individual sees what is happening. People circle. The same strangers turn up. Drama follows the person from place to place. None of it is hidden from the people standing there.

What they see is arranged to sit where the law has nothing to charge. The plausible deniability is not built for the people watching. It is built for a courtroom. They adjust their behavior to get around the law, not around public opinion, so a public that has been convinced changes nothing they have to adjust to.

Exposure answers a question nobody nearby is asking. The question still open is a legal one: laws arrive late, the way they arrived late for social media and for data privacy, and the people running this keep changing shape while the statutes stay still.

## What defeating it looks like

Every scheme needs a dependence to work on. The Forced Homecoming needs a person with nowhere to go but back. A canceled job needs a person with one income. Isolation needs a person who cannot find anybody else.

Defeat is that dependence not being there to use. A person who can find somebody with the skill they need, get a ride or a place to stay, and put a small part of their day toward somebody else's goal is a harder person to walk anywhere. None of that has to be explained to anybody first, and none of it waits for a statute.
