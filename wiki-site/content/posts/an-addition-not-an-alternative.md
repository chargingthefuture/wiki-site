A popular account on Quora says the Skills Economy is nonsense. Their position, repeated at volume: keep quiet, keep your head down, all is lost.

I am not going to name the account, because this is not about a person. It is about the two positions, and the disagreement is worth answering once, plainly, because right now these are the only two positions on the board.

## First, the credit they are owed

That same account teaches things that are genuinely worth knowing. How not to get falsely committed. How not to fall for the traps that get set for people in our situation.

Keep using those tips. I mean that without irony. Defense is necessary, and someone who teaches it well is doing real work.

## Where we part ways

Defense is necessary. Defense is not a plan.

Keep your head down describes today. It says nothing about next year, or the year after, or the ten after that. A person can dodge every trap and still end up alone, broke, and worn down — because dodging traps was the whole strategy, and nothing was ever built underneath it.

I will say the ending plainly, because pretending it is not there helps nobody. What comes after years of keeping your head down, alone and with no income, is despair. Suicide is what despair does quietly. Mass shootings are what it does loudly. Both are what the keep-quiet strategy leads to when it is the only strategy, and I refuse to stop there.

So I am not offering an alternative to defense. Keep every tip. I am offering an addition: income that does not run through people who can be reached. Housing. Rides. People who trade with you instead of watching you.

Defense keeps you alive today. The addition gives you something to be alive for.

## "Nonsense" is free

Notice what it costs to call something nonsense: nothing. No risk, no work, no skin. Anyone can say it about anything, which is why it proves nothing.

Trying to solve the problem costs something. Building costs money, hours, and the chance of failing in public. That asymmetry is worth keeping in view whenever you weigh who to listen to — the person who built something checkable, or the person who spent one word on it.

And the Skills Economy is checkable. It is not a mood or a promise. It is math and economics: Finland's catalog of what jobs an economy needs, Singapore's public number for what one worker's skills produce, and a live count — 97 people, 158 of 650 skills, $13.8 million in listed capacity — that updates in the app as people join. I walked through every step of it in the last post: https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/how-the-math-works

You do not have to believe me. That is the entire point of using public numbers.

## What is actually nonsense

Human traffickers calling themselves the illuminati — that is nonsense. The costume is the silly part, and arguing about the costume is exactly what it is for.

Strip the costume and what is underneath is old and ugly: children get sorted young. The ones with skills and integrity get marked and trafficked. The ones who submit get folded into the crime. There is no mystery in it and nothing supernatural about it. It is a business, run on recruitment and disposal.

I do not argue with the costume anymore. I look at the business.

## They already use this economics

Here is the part that should make you optimistic, strange as that sounds.

The Specterati run on economics. Favors, jobs, housing, rides — handed out to recruit, withheld to punish. Dependency is the whole mechanism. That is an economy, and it works, which is exactly why they use it.

So when someone calls the Skills Economy nonsense, they are calling the other side's own operating system nonsense. The same laws of economics that let them organize people against us let us organize people for each other. The tool is proven. The only question is which direction it points.

Every one of those leaves you alone, and none of them builds anything. As of today, the Skills Economy is the only position on the board that is about solving the problem — and it is the one position that gets called nonsense at volume.
