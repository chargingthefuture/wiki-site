A question I saved on Quora and never answered, as its title was recovered from the address it was saved at:

> Are gang stalkers psychic

The address it was saved at is https://www.quora.com/Are-gang-stalkers-psychic. The [Dictionary](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/Dictionary) carries the words I use for this; the question keeps the words it was asked in.

I saved it two times.

The short answer is no, and [They are not psychic](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/they-are-not-psychic) explains where the accuracy comes from: watching, arranging events, and narrowing a life until the next move is obvious. This answer is about the other side of it: what they do not know, and what they do with the gap.

## They are guessing

On Quora on July 8, 2025, asked how the people following a Targeted Individual read their mind, I wrote: "They are not. They are coordinated peeping Toms with a lot of money. Without stalking every minute of your life they would know nothing. They are just “best guessing” based on your reactions and behavioral patterns."

A guess built from behavior gets the what right and the why wrong. They watched what I bought when I had almost no money and decided it was my favorite food ([Pizza is not my favorite food](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/pizza-is-not-my-favorite-food)). It was a budget. Anybody reading a mind would have known that.

## The speed is what feels like mind reading

What makes a guess feel like a thought being read is how fast it comes back. On December 11, 2024, I wrote that I suspect the mimicking of movement, behavior and conversation is "a code system to instantly share information. As they like to mock you as soon as possible. Or at the very least start plotting a scheme."

Many people, each in a role, passing along what they saw within minutes. A thing you did in one place is echoed by a stranger in the next. That is a relay, and a relay is fast without being clever. The same answer noted that a code system "likely creates additional layer of plausible deniability."

## Wrong or right, the guess comes back as a story

The same July 8 answer went on: "Time and time again they create false narratives based on half truths, some on purpose for the slander campaign, narc abuse, or because they are simple not intelligent and guessed wrong."

Some of those narratives are aimed at other people, and [you cannot argue a smear down](https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/article/wiki-site/you-cannot-argue-a-smear-down). Some are aimed at you. On May 22, 2026, I wrote: "They simulate conditions and symptoms and create false narratives. They have wanted me to believe I have high blood pressure, kidney disease and more." On March 4, 2025, answering somebody who was being told they were poisoned, I wrote that the tactics are "filled with lies, half truths, and trying to make you paranoid and consumed with their antics 24/7."

A story handed to you is not information about you. It is a guess, sometimes a wrong one, delivered so you will carry it.

## Keep your own record

Their guess and your life can be checked against each other, and only one side keeps notes you can read. Read back over months, a record shows the misses as well as the hits. The misses are where the limit is.
