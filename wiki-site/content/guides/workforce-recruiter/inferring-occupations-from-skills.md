# Inferring occupations from skills

One of the features of Workforce Recruiter is that a survivor can list their skills, and the built-in algorithm will infer what occupations match those skills.

This is because there are Targeted Individuals who may have had their careers stunted because of the Speceterati and are therefore unsure where they could potentially excel. At the same time, there are individuals who have skills that can be used across multiple sectors in an economy. And without organized harassment would not have known this. And have an opportunity to choose a new career path.

As seen in the screenshot below. Everyone who has a profile in the Directory (https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/directory/public), public or not, has their skills automatically piped to Workforce Recuriter and is shown how their skills align with the Targeted community as a whole.

Screenshot of Workforce Recruiter is taken from this URL: https://app.chargingthefuture.com/apps/workforce-recruiter/skill-level/Intermediate

And it is a filtered view of TIs that demonstrate "intermediatory" skill level. In respect to commonly used phrasing in economics, when looking to understand if there is an even distribution of skills to sustain an economy.
