# Chyme is a Clubhouse-style social audio Android app.

A live, drop-in audio platform where people join topic-based rooms to listen, raise hands, and speak—moderators control the stage while the audience casually discovers and hops between conversations in real time.

Social audio platforms connect people in live, drop-in audio “rooms” where conversations happen in real time.

## Core concepts

- Rooms: Temporary audio spaces for a topic or event. Anyone can join as an audience (listener) or participate.
- Stage / Speakers: The visible area showing current speakers and moderators. Speakers broadcast audio; moderators control the conversation.
- Audience / Listeners: Join rooms to listen, raise hands to speak, and react without speaking.
- Moderation controls: Mute/unmute, invite-to-stage, remove from room, and hand-raising queue.
- Clubs / Profiles: Persistent groups or user identities that follow interests and schedule recurring events.

## Typical interactions

1. User opens app → sees list of live rooms and upcoming events.
2. User taps a room → joins as listener; sees stage and audience list.
3. Listener taps “raise hand” → moderator sees request and can promote to speaker.
4. Moderators manage conversation (mute speakers, demote to listener, remove users).
5. Room ends or user leaves; no persistent recording (real-time ephemeral audio).
