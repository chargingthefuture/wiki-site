# Quora inspiration queue

534 things saved or circulated on Quora, kept as prompts for new writing. This is a checkout
list, not a reference document: tick a line when it has been used in a post or looked at and set
aside, the same way the question queue works.

None of it is archive or Record material — there is no writing here, only what was saved.

## What is on the list, and what was dropped

The exports carry 812 saved or circulated items: 750 bookmarks, 42 posts shared into other
people's spaces, and 20 questions submitted to a space. Two cuts get from there to 534.

- 122 have no recoverable title. Their addresses are numeric post ids, or slugs that were nothing
  but a flattened link to somewhere else. A dead address with no title cannot prompt anything.
- The rest are repeats of a line already on the list, including the ones where a share's title was
  cut short by the address it came from and the same piece also appears in full. The count is kept
  where something was saved more than once, since saving the same piece twice says it mattered
  twice.

There is no upvote or like data anywhere in the exports. Quora's export does not include it, so
nothing here is drawn from what was upvoted.

## Every address here is dead

The accounts that held these links were erased and a Quora address does not outlive that. The
title on each line is recovered from the address itself, which is the part that still carries
meaning. Treat it as a subject, not as somewhere to go and read.

## How this runs

- Tick a line when a post has used it, or when it has been read and set aside. Both are done.
- The queue is worked alongside `QUORA_QUESTION_QUEUE.md`, not instead of it. A question there is
  a post to write; a line here is raw material for one.
- 534 lines is a long list and it does not need to reach zero. It is a place to look when
  starting a post, and ticking is how it stops being looked at twice.

## The queue

Grouped by the month it was saved. A space name follows a line that was saved inside somebody's
space; the rest were saved from Quora at large. Where a line was saved more than once, the count
follows it.
