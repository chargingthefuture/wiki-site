Paste the summary and the 'Full post:' line together. The label keeps the URL
inside a sentence; a bare URL alone on its own line is what Quora's editor turns
into a preview card instead of leaving the link as written.

This file mirrors the blog feed: one flat list, newest first. The number is the
entry's place in publication order, so No. 1 is the oldest page on the blog and
No. 333 is the newest. A number identifies one entry and never repeats. The blog's
collection tags are not used here — they are filters for readers, and several are
retired.

The UPDATE PASTES section at the end is different. Those are extra pastes tied to
a change on a living page rather than to the page itself, for when the change is
the thing worth posting about. A living page keeps its one standing summary in
the list above; an update paste sits alongside it and does not replace it. They
carry no number, because they are not one-per-page.

UPDATE · 2026-08-21 · Old links, new links — fifth account erased, and why the reason does not hold

Quora has now erased five of my accounts. The standing record of which handle is current and which are dead has been updated, and so has the part that matters more than the list.

Each of those accounts ran a different content strategy, on purpose. One posted at volume. One posted twice in its entire life — two short summaries with a link back to my blog, no reposting, no back catalog. Exactly one ever received a warning, and I changed how I posted after it. They ended the same way regardless, and the reason given is the same boilerplate every time: financial gain and spam.

There is exactly one thing behind the financial gain claim. One account posted that the app cost a dollar a month, and that account was deleted. A dollar a month did not cover the hosting bill — I was donating plasma to keep it online — and the app has been permanently free ever since. Every account after it mentioned no cost at all, because there is no cost. Those were deleted for financial gain too.

Quora runs ads between the posts and has a paid tier writers earn from. Charging people to read me there would be permitted. Giving it away is not.

When you change the variable and the outcome does not move, the variable was not the cause.
