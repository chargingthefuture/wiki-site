The feed you read on a platform is arranged by somebody else. Not written by them — arranged. What sits at the top, what gets buried, what arrives on the day it would land hardest. Nothing ranks it, nothing is inserted, and nothing knows what you opened. There are two ways to use this. The first needs no account anywhere and nothing from me — paste https://chargingthefuture.github.io/chargingthefuture/feed.xml into any reader app and every post turns up on its own. It is the same mechanism every podcast runs on, so most people have used one without knowing. This blog and my channel of demo videos of the app are in it already and are the only things put there; everything else you add by pressing the + beside Subscriptions management and pasting a site address, which is usually enough because the reader goes looking for the feed itself. What each person subscribes to is theirs — no shared list, no comments, no way for one account to see another. The part I use most is the cutoff. I used to open YouTube and type the thing I needed with a cutoff after it — Sterling pacific before:2024-12-31 — because that date is my 24/7 online psyop date and I read what was published before it. Find a channel worth following and it got worse: sort the channel page oldest to newest, scroll past everything after the date by hand, then watch each video on a page built to put other things in front of me. Now it is set once per feed and I do nothing. My sidebar has two kinds of source: a category I called Psyop free, for the ones that will never carry psyop content — this blog is one — with no filter at all, and everything else, which carries its own date. Open the feed's settings, Filter actions, and one line reading pubdate:2024-12-31/ marks everything from that day onward as read on arrival. The filter sits on the feed rather than the account, so a source I trust is never filtered by mistake. It runs on articles as they arrive rather than on what is already sitting there, so set it when you add the feed, or clear the backlog once by searching the same line inside that feed and marking it read. Nothing is deleted — switching to all articles still shows what it caught. The search box takes the same syntax turned around, pubdate:/2024-12-31, for a one-off look. A YouTube channel is where that filter has nothing to bite on, because a channel's feed carries only the newest videos and no filter reaches what was never delivered — so I collect a channel's upload list and publish it as a feed that does carry the catalog. Each entry carries the player, so the video plays inside the reader with no comments, no column of recommendations, and nothing from elsewhere on the site. Keep it to your date with the filter, or book me on Foundation with the channel address and your date and the feed goes out already cut, so nothing after it ever reaches the reader; I run it and send the feed address back. Those feeds live in a repository of their own, apart from the blog, with its own note that a channel being collected says one person reads it and nothing else. No. 390 · 2026-09-22 · What actually comes back

Somebody asked how a person recovers from this and returns to a normal life. I am going to answer it, and the answer starts by disagreeing with the middle of it. Once a name is on the list it is never taken off, no method I know of stops this, and a quiet stretch usually means the people doing it are laying low. So there is no before to return to. That is not despair and not a counsel to give up — it is the difference between a plan that works and a plan that cannot. Measuring your life against a normal that is not coming back does real harm, and it is worth naming what the harm is: a person working toward restoration reads every ordinary incident as a failure of the plan, proof that they did something wrong or that it is hopeless. It is not. It is Tuesday, and the conditions did not change. A plan that only succeeds if the harassment ends is a plan that will tell you that you failed, every week, forever. Here is what does come back, and every item is specific, which is what makes it usable. Income — not the career that was taken, which usually does not come back the way it was and costs people years of waiting, but the ability to earn, which is not the same thing. You still know what you know. Housing, including rooms offered by survivors to survivors, which is the answer to the scheme that walks a destitute person back to relatives who were reached first. People who know you, which changes the most and the fastest: being findable is what lets two or three people who understand without being told turn up, and that is most of a recovery. The ability to ask, which is a skill rather than a circumstance and returns with practice — ask small, bring something, do not measure it against equal. And your own account of your life, in a dated record, because memory under stress rewrites the earlier version to match the later one. What does not come back should not be promised. Proof: the methods are chosen to be deniable one act at a time. The people who were turned, some of them approached before you knew anything was happening; expecting those relationships back keeps a person in the same week for years. And the time, which is a loss nothing on the list repairs — and which is not the most interesting fact about you. So recovery here is not restoration, it is rebuilding under conditions that are still running. The measure that works is whose hands your week runs through. Count them: how many of the things you need come through somebody who is part of this, and how many do not. That number moves, it moves because of things you can do, and it does not require the harassment to stop first.

Somebody moving every eight months is doing to themselves, at their own expense, what the Forced Homecoming is engineered to do to them. Keep a dated record across the move, the same way on both sides of it, because memory rewrites the earlier version to match the later one. The move was not wasted, and moving again is not the answer to it following you.

No. 388 · 2026-09-22 · No one to traffic

I have written that nobody who joins owes me anything and that I owe nobody either. That still stands. This is what follows from building the thing anyway. The network runs on people who are destitute and alone. Destitution is what makes somebody cheap to reach: no money means one route, one meal you can afford, one person who will still rent to you, and each of those is a place to put somebody who is taking part. Isolation is what makes the reaching stick, because a person with four people left in their life can be predicted and can be cut off from all four. Neither is a side effect; they are produced on purpose, because they are the working conditions. So an economy where survivors are neither is not a comfort, it is a supply problem for them. Some of that reaches past us. I have written elsewhere about the three places this road ends and what I expect if a fourth route exists — people driven to those endings do not only harm themselves, and fewer of them is a benefit to everybody standing nearby, targeted or not. I cannot prove that in advance and will not pretend otherwise; it is what I expect, and it is part of why I am doing this rather than something easier. It is not a favor to anybody and nothing is asked in return: a person who has never been targeted gets the benefit without joining, without agreeing with me, without knowing this exists. And there is the other side, which I have not written down before. The people doing this are not all enjoying it. Some are. The rest are in because of what the structure already gave them — the job, the apartment, the promotion, the thing they took before they understood what it was for — and because walking away would cost them those things. That is a reason and not a justification: losing a home is traumatic to the person it happens to and I am not going to tell anybody otherwise, but none of their lives are on the line — what is at stake for them is property and their own good opinion of themselves, and what is at stake on the other side is somebody's life. That difference is not incidental: across these networks there is an understanding that a target can be robbed, injured or killed and nobody will answer for it, and that the people who did it will be rewarded instead. No such cover extends to them — ordinary crime still applies to a person who is not a target, which is why what they risk is losing things and never losing their life. Nobody in that position can simply walk away, because the debt is held over them and there is always another target to be assigned. Unless there is not. If this works there is no one to traffic: not fewer, not harder, none, because the material a person needs to be worth trafficking is what the economy takes away. A trafficker with no target has nothing to trade on and nothing being asked of them, and the people who never wanted to do it stop having to. That is not forgiveness and not a deal. Following orders is not better than enjoying it, because the order is the same debt collected one level up, and somebody who trafficked a person under pressure still did it. What it is, is the exit existing at all. Right now every route out of that network runs back through the network, which is the same trap from the other side, and the people held there have as much reason as anybody to want this to succeed. It does not need anybody to be grateful for it. I am not owed. Neither is anybody else here. The work is worth doing on what it produces, which is fewer people with nothing left to lose, on both sides of the thing.

Traditionally a stalker is one person; this involves multiple perpetrators using various techniques to severely bother a target or make them conform to so-called rules of society. Refusing is the wrong shape for it, though. Refusing suggests a plain question and a plain answer; what happens is a test of whether a person can be moved, usually without them knowing a test is underway. Twice, decades apart, somebody who did not know I was a target tried to get me to slander two other people, and both got upset when I would not. Getting one person to slander another is how the dividing is done, and not taking that bait is what declining to traffic actually looks like — as often instinct as decision. What it is good for is smaller and real: it tells somebody who has been told they are imagining it that the thing has a shape, a name, and other people who recognize it on sight. After that, the move is not to study it further but to get a ride, a room, an hour of a trade arranged with somebody who is not part of it.

No. 386 · 2026-09-22 · They are not psychic

Scott Rowell (https://www.quora.com/profile/Scott-Rowell-26) asked on Quora, on November 24, 2024, whether the people doing this are psychic. The answer is no. They watched what I bought when I had almost no money, saw the same cheap hot meal over and over, and concluded it was my favorite food. They were wrong, and the way they were wrong is the proof: somebody reading a mind would have known it was a budget, somebody reading behavior guesses the reason and gets it backwards. Second, and larger, they arrange the events they then appear to have foreseen. A prediction is impressive when the person making it has no hand in the outcome; take that away and it is not a prediction. The canceled ticket, the job that evaporates, the landlord who will not renew, the delay that makes you miss the thing — those are staged, and somebody who stages an event knows how it ends. One bus trip of mine ran at least seven named schemes in sequence, and one of them canceled my ticket while I was sitting on the bus it was for. That is not foresight, it is scheduling. The plainest version is provocation: somebody runs up and punches me in the face, and afterward they knew I would react. That is not a reading of me, it is an event built to produce the reaction it then claims to have foreseen — and the reaction gets used twice, once to look prescient and once as evidence for the story they were already telling about me, see, I told you she is violent. And they tell you about it, before or after, because the telling is the point. Third, destitution does the rest. Take away somebody's income and their choices go with it: one hot meal they can afford, one route they can walk, one person who will still rent to them. The Forced Homecoming produces that on purpose — destitute enough that the only door left leads back to relatives who can be reached, in a place where everybody around them is taking part. Surrounded and broke, a person becomes easy to call in advance, and every scheme after that gets cheaper to run. Destitution is not the only route to that. A self-identified Targeted Individual, commenting under a different question on Quora, said in substance that they were fortunate: they had progressed far enough in their profession that holding a job, and being fulfilled in it, was achieved. That is my paraphrase, and the knock-on effect is mine to add — destitution at the traffickers' hands never arrived for them. Destitution can be dodged; isolation is not dodged by anybody I have met or read about, because turning the people around somebody is what the slander is for and it is run on everybody. So isolation does the same work by another road, and it compounds with the staging. The chaos around a person is arranged, the reaction to an arranged event is the obvious one, and the few people left to turn to afterward were selected. Predict the event, predict the reaction, predict who gets called — three guesses, and not one of them is a thought being read. When the people around somebody have been turned or were placed there to begin with, their week runs through a short list of known names, and a person with an income and four people left in their life is not hard to call either. Who benefits from the other explanation is worth asking. If they are psychic they are unbeatable, and there is nothing to do but endure it and interpret it, which is exactly the position all of this is built to produce. The same goes for every story that lifts them out of the human category. What they have is money, technology, a network and the willingness to commit crimes — real advantages, none of them insight. So: write down what happens, dated, as it happens, because the record shows the pattern and the times they got you wrong. And work on the options, because options are material rather than a mindset — somewhere to cook, an hour of a trade you do not have, a ride from somebody who is not reporting on it. That is not a defense against being watched. It is a way of being harder to predict.

UPDATE · 2026-09-13 · Dictionary — Fireside added to the capability list

There is now a place to talk underneath a post on my blog, and it belongs to the app rather than to a platform. It is called Fireside, and it is in the Dictionary's capability list as of today.
